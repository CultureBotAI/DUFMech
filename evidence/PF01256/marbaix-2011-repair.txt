Figure 2C shows NADHX dehydratase activity with ATP for yeast YKL151C and ADP for E. coli YjeF; reciprocal nucleotide controls differ. Figure 2D shows intact YjeF converting R-enriched NADHX to NADH with ADP, while without ADP it interconverts epimers without comparable NADH production. No-enzyme controls and three-replicate variability are reported.
Supplement S4A separately shows YKL151C plus ATP and YjeF plus ADP increasing A340 with mixed NADPHX, compared with no enzyme. Different enzyme masses prevent a rate comparison. Intact YjeF reaches a larger conversion extent than the standalone yeast dehydratase in this comparison.
Recombinants are N-terminal His fusions expressed in BL21 Star(DE3); YjeF received additional ion-exchange and size-exclusion purification. Substrate stocks are mixtures or 70-85%-pure enriched fractions. HPLC assignments use retention/UV comparison to prior work, not new atomic chemistry. Nucleotide dependence, pH, temperature and substrate contexts are retained separately; no cellular flux or isolated-domain sufficiency is established.
