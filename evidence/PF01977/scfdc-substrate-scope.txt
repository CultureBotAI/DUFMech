A: ASSAY AND READOUT
Sec3/4/8/9/14/18, Table 1, Figure 3 and supplement S1-S3.
Expression: 37 C to OD600 about 0.6, 0.2 mM IPTG, another 4.5 h; wash in
100 mM sodium phosphate pH 7, resuspend to OD about 1, pellet and store at -20 C.
Assay: 1 mL in PTFE-sealed 1.5-mL glass vials; substrates supplied from DMSO.
Optimized time series: 2 mM acid, OD600 about 1, 35 C, 100 mM phosphate pH 7;
sampling at 1,2,3,4,8,24,30,48,72 h. Do not combine with the initial 1-mM,
30-C, 24-h Table S1 screen, or treat differently timed maxima as initial rates.
HPLC uses authentic acid calibration and anisole internal standard; conversion
means acid disappearance. GC-MS is EI 70 eV with NIST/Wiley spectral analogy,
not authentic product standards for every substrate. Whole-cell endpoints do
not isolate ScFdc catalysis, cofactor supply, transport or PF01977 chemistry.
No-biocat and plasmid-negative host controls are text-reported in Sec4. The
cited S25/S26 figures are actually substrate-free matrix extracts (hexane/MTBE),
not plotted plasmid-negative-host or no-biocat assays. Preserve this miscitation.

B: FRESH-CELL SUPPLEMENTATION
Sec5/18 and Table 1 note [a]: 1b,e,h,i,j,q,s,t,u are reported to reach complete
conversion after fresh cells (OD600=1) and an additional 24 h at the plateau.
The edge source includes both cell addition and additional incubation; matched
no-addition controls for this extra interval are not established. This is not
proof that loss of viability caused the plateau, nor a measured viability rescue.
For 1n/o, Table 1 note [b] reports no increase, whereas Sec5 reports a moderate
increase. No unqualified positive/negative fresh-cell edge is adopted for n/o.
Product toxicity/inhibition are interpretations, not directly discriminated tests.

E: COMPUTATIONAL GATE HYPOTHESIS
Sec6/20, Figure 4c/d and S71 alignment: rigid AutoDock Vina 1.1.2 docking uses
AnFdc 4ZA7, not a measured ScFdc ligand complex. A 20-A box includes a surface
pocket; 10-16-A boxes restrict the search. The text and figure use Sc numbering.
Q192/I330 are proposed to narrow access; replacing I330 with Ala is modeled to
reduce a clash and favor 1w double-bond orientation relative to prFMN. This is
one separate hypothesis edge, not an observed causal intermediate or proof of
domain sufficiency. Actual model coordinates were not retrieved or rerun.
ScQ192/AnQ190 and ScI330/AnI327 reference mapping is verified in M1.
Gas-phase B3LYP adduct models for 1a/v/w and structural overlays do not measure
the in-enzyme trajectory. Planarity/LUMO associations do not establish a general
causal rate law; several modeled nonplanar substrates do complete after fresh cells.
The I330A discussion contains an F133A binding-energy label; no F133A experiment
or corrected numerical affinity is invented. Q192A remains primer-only here.
