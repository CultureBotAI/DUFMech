Interpretation safeguards
The 2015 prose mentions R172 but names the tested variant R173A; sequence
position 172 is Ala and position 173 is Arg. The 2018 paper consistently uses
R173. The 2019 computational section's F439L spelling conflicts with F437L in
the experiments, supplement and exact deposited 6R2Z sequence. Use F437L for
that experiment; do not invent an F439L construct or a PF01977 F437 residue.
UbiX/Pad is the separate cofactor-producing partner, not this Pfam domain.
S. cerevisiae Fdc, AroY, bacterial UbiD and VdcCD require their own mappings
and assay contexts before transfer; none is inferred from the AnFdc example.
