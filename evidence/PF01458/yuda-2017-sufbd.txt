Curator interpretation and factual assay metadata:
Purified SufBCD variants come from pGSO164-derived constructs expressed in
YT2512 (MG1655 delta-sufABCDSE), purified with Phenyl FF, Q XL and Sephacryl S-200.
SufS is separately purified, and SufE is expressed without His tag in
HMS174(DE3) from pET21a-sufE. No isolated PF01458 preparation was assayed.
Supplemental S3 shows similar complex elution and constituent protein bands;
the authors assign the normal SufB:SufC:SufD ratio of 1:2:1. These controls do
not resolve subtle folding changes or exact catalytic intermediates.

Figure 4a: 0.5 uM SufS, 2 uM SufE, 2 mM L-cysteine, 2 mM DTT, varying SufBCD;
25 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.8, 150 mM NaCl, 30 C, 10 min. Methylene-blue detection
measures released sulfide and the plotted activity is normalized per mg SufS.
SufB C254A abolishes added-complex stimulation, but basal SufSE output persists;
Q285A and W287A lower stimulation without eliminating it. Other tested variants,
including C405A, retain roughly WT stimulation. Each panel has its own plotted
concentration series and WT comparison; the retained comparisons use 3 uM
SufBCD, which is present in all three selected panels. Means/SD reflect at least
three measurements; independent protein preparations are not specified.
Figure 4b shows SufB variants co-purifying with SufE-His in separately prepared
cell extracts. The interaction control is not itself a directed catalytic edge.

Figure 4c: 10 uM SufBCD, 0.5 uM each SufS/SufE, 2 mM L-cysteine, 50 mM
Tris-HCl pH 7.8, 150 mM NaCl; 125 ul, 30 C, 3 h without DTT. Pre-existing
DTT is removed from protein preparations before the reaction. The complex is
retained with a 100-kDa ultrafilter, separated from smaller components, and
normalized by A280 using epsilon 130 mM-1 cm-1. Bound sulfur is released using
2 mM DTT, then quantified as methylene blue at 670 nm after 20 min development
and precipitate removal. Reported retained sulfur per complex: WT 4.8 +/- 0.9;
C254A none detected; C405A 1.0 +/- 0.1; SufD C358A 4.6 +/- 1.0. The detection
limit and raw measurements are not given. This endpoint does not localize each
retained sulfur atom, prove a C254-to-C405 transfer trajectory or measure Fe-S
cluster formation. At least three measurements underpin means/SD.

SufD H360A, supplemental S2: UT109 (MG1655 delta-iscUA-hscBA delta-sufABCDSE)
harbors pUMV22 Sp-r and the complementary pRK-sufABC-SE/pBBR-sufD pair.
H360A does not restore WT-SufD-supported plate growth without mevalonate (MVA)
at 37 C, 24 h on LB. The figure includes WT, vector and other SufD substitutions.
This is failed complementation in a bypass-dependent background, not universal
lethality. The general method discusses MVA/glucose supplementation; the
S2 plate caption does not separately resolve glucose supplementation there.
The Fdx/protein panels instead use liquid Superbroth with glucose and MVA,
37 C, early stationary phase. General supplemented medium uses 0.4% glucose
and 200 uM racemic mevalonolactone. Lysates normalized by cell density are
analyzed by native PAGE and anti-Fdx immunoblot. H360A has less detected holo-Fdx
than WT; undetected apo-Fdx does not quantify all cellular Fe-S clusters.
SDS-PAGE/Western controls show somewhat lower H360A SufD abundance, retained
as an alternative contribution rather than equal-expression proof.

The proposed internal sulfur tunnel, exact Fe/S ligands, FADH2 mechanism and
ATP-driven structural sequence are interpretations or other-study hypotheses,
not observed microsteps added from these experiments. Cellular and purified
results remain distinct. Other mutants, organisms, proteins and source studies
remain incompletely curated, so family status stays IN_PROGRESS.
