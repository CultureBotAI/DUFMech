Inspected 2026-10-11: relevant Results/Discussion, Methods, Table1, original
Figures6-8 and captions. Minimal exact quotations: "decrease",
"dephosphorylation". Other figures were not visually audited.

Figure6A/B: delta-pstB/MSMEG_5779 in mc2 155, separately overexpressing
C-terminal His6 PhoU1/MSMEG_5776 or PhoU2/MSMEG_1605 from pMH201
derivatives. Each reduces phoA-lacZ and phoU1-lacZ beta-galactosidase relative
to empty vector, particularly at10mM K2HPO4. MOPS/glucose0.2%,Tween80
0.02%,pH7.2,37 C/200rpm,OD6000.45-0.5,acetamide0.1%; three biological
replicates,SD. Original brackets/starred comparisons support high-Pi effects.
The50uM-Pi response is weaker; no invented significance is assigned to its
unstarred contrasts. Figure6C expression blots use7H9, not the reporter medium.
This does not prove direct repression of either promoter by PhoU protein.

Figure7A: purified C-terminal His6 proteins from E. coli BL21(DE3), partner
SenX3SD1(51-384)46pmol, RegX3150pmol, PhoU1275pmol,+/-Pi500uM.
RegX3 is already partly phosphorylated; mid-exponential OD0.4 expression
provides the less-phosphorylated preparation. Buffer300mMTris-HClpH8,
50mMKCl,10mMMgCl2; ATP1mM initiates30 C, samples0/1/2min.
PhoU1 together with Pi lowers the net phosphorylated RegX3 readout. Neither
PhoU1 without Pi nor Pi without PhoU1 produces the corresponding reduction.
One Results sentence names less-phosphorylated SenX3; Figure7A/caption and
Methods instead identify RegX3, which is used in the curated scope.

Figure7B: distinct preparation from late-exponential OD1 E. coli, more
prephosphorylated RegX3144pmol, SenX3SD139pmol,Pi500uM,+/-PhoU1200pmol,
same buffer/ATP/temp, samples0/1/2/3min. PhoU1 addition decreases P-RegX3
over time. SenX3 and ATP remain present, so this is a net reaction balance,
not proof that isolated PhoU is a phosphatase or a measured phosphatase kcat.
Protein amounts are pmol; reaction volume/concentrations are not invented.
Phos-tag gels are loaded without boiling to preserve phospho-Asp. Global
Methods mentions biological replicates, but independent purification counts
for the pictured gels are not specified.

Figure8: with SenX3SD146pmol, PhoU1275pmol,+/-Pi500uM, ATP1mM,30 C,
0/1/2min, no reduction in SenX3 autophosphorylation is shown. Thus the
RegX3 result does not establish autokinase inhibition. Reduced phosphotransfer,
enhanced SenX3 phosphatase activity, or both remain possible. Figures9/10's
Pi-binding/conformational model is not a measured Pi Kd or residue-level
binding site; neither modeled alpha6 motif nor phosphate occupancy becomes
an observed edge. Redundant-paralog deletion is reported without shown data;
selection only at low Pi does not identify a toxic molecular intermediate.
