Exact Results quotation: "increased transport activity"

Figure 6a compares intact MtCorB and TtCorB, each at protein/lipid 1:30
wt/wt, with empty liposomes. After a one-minute baseline, 5 mM external
MgCl2 produces intravesicular magnesium accumulation in both protein samples,
with substantially less accumulation for MtCorB in this lipid mixture.
This difference is not a measurement of intrinsic turnover or native direction.
Tt means Tepidiphilus thermophilus; Mt means Methanoculleus thermophilus.
Cloning names MtCorB 1-426 and TtCorB 1-445, pET29a C-terminal His6 constructs.
The shorter MtCorB delta-C crystallization construct is not the full-length
construct named in Figure 6. Neither isolated transmembrane domain was tested.

Methods: POPE/POPG 3:1, initially 5 mg/ml in 20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.5,
100 mM KCl, 50 mM NaCl, 0.5 mM EDTA and 35 mM CHAPS. Mix protein with
0.5 mg lipid for 20 min at room temperature, remove detergent with Sephadex
G-50, load 50 uM mag-fura-2 using one freeze/thaw and 0.8-um extrusion,
then remove external dye. Dilute 10 ul vesicles into 190 ul buffer without
EDTA at 25 C. Read excitation 330/369 nm and emission 509 nm every 6 s
for 10 min after addition, calibrating with 0.1% Triton and 12.5 mM EDTA.
Empty controls receive matched HPLC buffer containing 0.05% UDM.
The reconstitution Methods call both CorBs UDM-purified, whereas purification
specifies DDM for MtCorB and UDM for TtCorB; the Mt detergent discrepancy
remains unresolved. Figure captions specify three independent measurements,
not explicitly three independent purifications or reconstitutions.

Figure 6d: S26A, S30A and N76A increase magnesium accumulation versus WT.
E33A and N80A are described as near WT; no zero-effect claim is adopted.
Figure 6e: S117A, E118A, P121G and K122A decrease accumulation versus WT,
but each retains signal above empty liposomes. These are not null proteins.
Supplementary Figure 8c shows earlier/broadened size-exclusion peaks for
several variants, particularly S26A, S30A and N76A. Figure 8d shows strong
approximately 50-kDa bands for all reconstituted mutants. These controls
support incorporation and purity, not identical folding, oligomeric state,
orientation or active fraction. Do not infer an elementary coordination or
helix-motion step from a substitution's net transport effect.

Supplementary Figure 8a: with 5 mM external MgCl2, increasing TtCorB loading
from 1:810 through 1:270 and 1:90 to 1:30 raises accumulation. Figure 8b:
external MgCl2 of 0.75, 1.5, 3 and 5 mM gives increasing accumulation in
the separate substrate series. Neither series establishes a Michaelis constant.
Figure 6b: valinomycin under an outward K gradient increases the TmCorA
control response but not TtCorB. Electroneutral exchange is an interpretation;
counterion identity and stoichiometry were not measured. Figure 8e uses
different ion concentrations and dye affinities, not directly comparable
absolute selectivities. Structural models and simulations are not observed
atomic transport trajectories. Other CNNM/CorC members, cellular assays and
isolated CBS-domain ATP binding do not inherit these domain-level claims.
