Inspected main Methods/Results and Figures 2C, 4 and 5.
Minimal quotation: "the residue in Alba acetylated by Pat was Lys-16"

Paraphrased assay ledger:
Purified S. solfataricus Alba plus SSO2813 Pat and acetyl-CoA produces
acetylated Alba in vitro. Pat is a separate enzyme, not part of PF01918.
Radiolabel protocol: 10 mM Tris pH8, 150 mM NaCl, 65 C for 1 h.
Figure 4A compares 3 ug Pat plus 2 ug WT, K16E or K17E Alba:
K16E strongly reduces incorporation; K17E retains incorporation.
Figure 4B tandem MS localizes an acetyl group to K16 after Pat treatment.
For MS/EMSA preparation, Alba/Pat mixture is split into two aliquots,
with or without acetyl-CoA 100 uM, then incubated 1 h at 60 C.
Figure 5 compares these paired preparations on DNA and dsRNA 20mers:
0, 0.32, 0.63, 1.25, 2.5, 5, 10 pmol protein with about 1 fmol probe;
binding 20 ul, 10 mM Tris pH8, 150 mM NaCl, 10 min at 60 C.
Acetylation-treated material has about twofold lower binding affinity
on each probe, not the 30-fold native/recombinant difference in Bell2002.
Thermal nonenzymatic acetylation is a measured background. K16E is not
an acetylated-lysine mimic proof. This in-vitro chemical reaction is not
invalidated by Cao2018 finding no native K16 acetylation in tested cells.
ChIP and nuclease-release association are not encoded as transcription
causes. Personal-communication estimates are not independent experiments.
