Figure5:E234-derived NZ1 replaces chromosomal sis10b promoter with
arabinose-inducible promoter; slow growth in ACVy with arabinose.
WT pSeSD-sis10b rescue restores OD600 toward parentalE234.
K17A,deltaN12 and quadruple-mutant complementation give lower OD600
than WT-complementedNZ1; do not call every mutant complete non-rescue.
Total Sis10b immunoblots include residual chromosomal and plasmid protein.
Similar/higher abundance does not prove identical folding or activity.
K16A/K17R provide substantial rescue; double mutants partially rescue;
triple-mutant transformants were not obtained, not a measured growth curve.
Native growth effects are INDIRECT and are not joined to in-vitro unwinding
as a demonstrated causal chain. E. coli RL211 chloramphenicol reporter
supports a separate heterologous phenotype, not directly observed RNA
unfolding or a native intracellular substrate. Not encoded in this pass.
