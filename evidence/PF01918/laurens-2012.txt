Paraphrased assay ledger:
S. solfataricus Alba1, Alba1 F60A, Alba2; 1:1 Alba1/Alba2 mixture
is treated by the authors as heterodimer based on earlier work.
AFM uses nicked pRD24, 90 ng, 10 ul 40 mM HEPES pH7.5/25 mM NaCl,
30 min about23 C; tenfold dilution in 1 mM MgCl2 for mica deposition.
Dimer:bp ratios, not monomer:bp: WT Alba1 bridges at 1:60/1:30,
condenses at 1:15, and appears open/stiff at 1:7.5.
Heterodimers bridge at lower ratios and condense at 1:7.5.
F60A still bridges, including 1:30. Morphologies are visual classifications,
not a complete quantitative per-molecule response distribution.
Optical experiments use lambda DNA, about23 C, 10 mM Tris pH7.9,
1 mM EDTA, 3.5 mM DTT, 60 mM KCl, 10 mM MgCl2; about2-min
untensioned incubation before force-distance measurements.
WT at 2 uM increases fitted persistence length to 260+/-30 nm
versus 49+/-2 nm bare DNA (SEM). At matched 1 uM, F60A and
Alba1:Alba2 mixtures have lower persistence length than WT.
Low-concentration WT bridges differ from high-occupancy stiff filaments.
F60A and heterodimers retain binding/bridging; not loss of all function.
Cooperativity fits and Figure 4 contact arrangements are models, not
directly observed atomic relays. Questionable association-constant units
are not converted to new Kd values here. At Alba2:Alba1 1:20, the study
reports no detectable cooperativity effect, a context-specific negative.
Purification follows cited earlier protocols; exact residual tags and
historical clone sequences remain unverified.
