Minimal quotation: "not an intermediate on the enzymatic reaction pathway".

This is a follow-up interpretation boundary, not a new canonical graph of
the entire chemical mechanism. B. subtilis Pdx1 is His-tagged; the original
construct is cited to Hanes 2008 JACS, PMID:18271580. Its complete clone
identity/strain was not verified against the Raschle preparation. The main
paper was initially unavailable on the publisher page, then read successfully
from the author's public copy. No claim of inaccessible full text remains.

The early R5P-derived Pdx1-Z1 adduct was examined after 7 M urea denaturation.
NMR chemical shifts support a C2 ketone, not the earlier C2-imine assignment;
15N protein labeling and decoupling support a C1-N linkage. These observations
qualify the 2007 structural interpretation without undoing its mass shift or
K81 peptide localization. Neither paper directly images every catalytic step.

Figure S5 and main Results pp427-428 compare 15N protein/15NH4Cl with
15N protein/14NH4Cl. The C2-N coupling disappears with 14NH4Cl, supporting
incorporation of ammonia nitrogen. This is inconsistent with adopting the
2007 nitrogen-free proposal as settled. It does not by itself verify the
exact chemical state of every earlier 315-nm sample.

Crucially, Pdx1-Z2 is obtained by 50 mM HCl quenching and 7 M urea
denaturation of I320. The authors explicitly identify Z2 as an off-pathway
quench product, not a native enzymatic intermediate. The likely shared
protein nitrogen linking C1 and C5 is based on narrowband decoupling.
Do not turn the acid-induced cyclic Z2 structure into an observed native
catalytic step or call the proposed 1,5 migration a directly tracked movie.

Methods p429 use 12 h at 15 C for labeled-sugar loading, 1 M NH4Cl for
approximately 1 h at 22-23 C, gel filtration, and 1.5 mM racemic G3P for
2 h before the distinct Z3 preparation. Z3 is reduced with 50 mM NaBH4
for 15 min before urea. Supplemental S8 observes conversion of I320 to a
408-nm species; denaturation liberates PLP. These doses, quenching steps
and spectra must not be merged with Raschle's 0.4-mM/0.4-uM Figure 2C.
The full proposed reaction sequence remains a hypothesis-constrained model.
