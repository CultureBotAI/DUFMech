Main Methods, Results and Discussion inspected; original Figures2-6
visually inspected. Figure7 was read through its caption and text only.

Figures2/3: purified N-terminal His-tagged TM0486 produced in E. coli
without thiamine shows thiamine and TPP binding by WaterLOGSY and HSQC.
HMP and taurine are negative in the tested NMR conditions. NMR was at47C;
protein0.1mM, ligand0.5mM, phosphatepH6/NaCl buffer. HSQC line broadening
does not independently determine assembly stoichiometry. Figure3 ITC
at25C, protein25uM, gives a reported one-site fit n0.916, Kd1.58uM and
enthalpy-11kcal/mol. Methods print titrant500mM, inconsistent-looking
with the plotted molar ratio; retain the fit as reported, do not silently
replace the concentration with500uM. Dilution controls and duplicate
experiments are reported. No binding-site mutagenesis establishes a
residue-level binding relay, and TPP affinity was not quantified here.

Figure4 compares protein produced with and without thiamine in minimal
expression medium. Supplemented production gives a larger native-PAGE
species and DLS particle-size distribution. This is a whole-production-
condition effect, not an isolated acute ligand-addition experiment.
The larger DLS population does not prove exact tetramer stoichiometry;
aggregation remains an alternative. Figure5 extracted cofactor proton
NMR resembles the thiamine reference. Figure6 shows unresolved density
and docking, not a crystallographically identified thiamine complex.
PDB1VK8 retains UNL. Docked T44 contacts are not observed causal residue
effects. Crystallographic oligomers do not establish native cellular
stoichiometry. The alternate structural preparation/construct is kept
distinct from the binding experiment.

Expression Results and qPCR Methods: adapted T. maritima cultures show
approximately11-fold TM0486 transcript increase in anaerobic medium
without Na2S and approximately12-fold in the oxygenated oxidative
condition, relative to anaerobic Na2S-supplemented reference cultures.
Three independent cultures, triplicate measurements, 16S reference.
The oxygenated treatment is retained as a complete culture condition,
not an isolated oxygen-only factor comparison. Changes in RNA do not
show protein abundance, direct promoter activation or stress protection.
Co-transcription with TM0483-0485 is not a direct transport assay.

Excluded observed claims: thiamine transport, cytoplasmic cargo delivery,
oxidative-stress protection, family-wide ligand specificity and fungal
thiamine binding. These require evidence beyond this inspected study.
