Main ECM15 Results/Discussion and Methods, original Figures4/5, FigureS5
and TableS5A visually inspected. TableS5B/C text parsed, not independently
validated against clone sequences. Other supplementary PDFs downloaded
in the archive but not inspected. No full-study replication is claimed.

Figure5A: BY4741 S. cerevisiae ecm15 deletion lowers spot growth on
calcofluor-white plates relative to WT; C. neoformans ECM15 expression
restores growth toward WT. YPD comparison is similar among strains.
The assay is qualitative, not a growth-rate measurement or direct cell-
wall chemistry. TableS5A lists deletionYP738 and heterologous rescueYP744;
an empty-vector strainYP745 is listed but not displayed in Figure5A.

Figure4B: tagged Ecm15-mCherry is nuclear-enriched with Cac2-GFP in YPD;
starvation and rapamycin treatment reduce nuclear enrichment. Starvation
also weakens visible signal, so abundance/fluorescence changes remain
an alternative to movement alone. Figure4D: ECM15 deletion lowers
Cac2-GFP/DAPI PCC from0.77 to0.57 under nutrient-rich conditions.
Residual nuclear association remains. Methods calculate PCC from at
least20cells, not necessarily20independent cultures. Direct physical
binding and an Ecm15-mediated Cac2 transport step are not demonstrated
by colocalization. FigureS5A predicts D63-A91 NLS; no mutant test validates
that segment. Do not attach the Figure1 STL1 rapamycin dose/time to
Figure4 without an explicit source link.

Figure4C: ecm15 deletion increases apparent capsule under glucose-free
ASN relative to H99; own-gene complementation lowers it toward WT.
The retained graph concerns qualitative India-ink morphology, not an
absolute synthesis rate. Methods state complementation retains the
original deletion. TableS5A identifies ecm15 deletionYP573 and own-gene
complementYP612. Its ecm15-deletion-plus-CAC2 row instead prints a cac2
deletion genotype andYP617, also used for cac2 own-gene complement.
This conflict is not silently repaired. CAC2 cross-rescue epistasis is
excluded from exact-clone causal edges pending resolution.

Figure5B: ecm15 deletion reduces norepinephrine-dependent pigmentation
and mating-associated filaments relative to WT; own-gene complement
restores both qualitative outcomes. Pigmentation is not a direct laccase
activity assay, and filaments are not a complete mating mechanism.
The caption's printed100mg/ml norepinephrine is suspicious; no corrected
unit is asserted. Figure5C deletion lowers spot growth on SDS and ASN
without glucose; complementation restores it. RichYPD is a comparison,
and spots do not resolve death from slower growth. SDS concentration
was not specified in the inspected Figure5/Methods; do not invent it.

Figure5D left: deletion prolongs mouse time to the moribund endpoint;
own-gene complementation shortens it relative to deletion. Right:
ECM15 overexpression also prolongs this endpoint relative to its matched
WT/empty-vector control. These separate experiments use different
inocula and cannot be pooled as one dose-response or a monotonic
ECM15-to-virulence relationship. Ten animals per group are reported.
This is an assay-level endpoint, not a human clinical survival claim or
a new instruction for pathogen manipulation. STL1 patient associations
elsewhere in the paper are not transferred to ECM15.

Figure5F: VAD1 deletion increases ECM15/ACT1 steady-state RNA under
starvation relative to WT, with three independent experiments and
reportedP<0.05. Figure5E RNA immunoprecipitation is supportive association,
not by itself proof of a causal RNA degradation reaction. No direct
transcriptional activation is encoded; RNA stability can change.

Authoritative molecular mapping is in identity.txt. The H99 sequence
displayed in FigureS5 matches J9VTN7/AFR95954.1/XP_012050770.1; the Sc
reference mapping remains qualified to the BY4741 experimental system.
No fungal thiamine-binding assay or thiamine-to-TOR chain is established.
