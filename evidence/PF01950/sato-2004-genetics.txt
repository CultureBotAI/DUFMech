KW128 is KOD1-derived delta-pyrF delta-trpE::pyrF. Delta-fbp-8J replaces the
entire1128-bp fbp coding region with the same-orientation trpE cassette.
External/internal PCR, locus sequencing and Southern blotting support the
replacement. Delta-imp-2A is a distinct comparison strain; its construction
retains27bp at the imp3-prime end to protect the neighboring ORF.

Growth experiment: anaerobic85C, preculture in rich ASW-YT, then synthetic
ASW-AA (20 amino acids, sulfur), plus5g/L starch or5g/L sodium pyruvate.
Figure3 shows OD660 over approximately30h: delta-fbp-8J lacks the pyruvate
growth increase seen in KW128 and delta-imp-2A, while all grow with starch.
An amino-acid-only medium gave the same qualitative growth defect according
to Results; its curve is not shown. These are conditional optical-density
phenotypes, not direct viable counts or universal gene essentiality.

Cell-extract experiment is DIFFERENT: strains were grown in rich ASW-YT
with pyruvate at85C for15h to early stationary phase. FBPase activity was
0.37+/-0.02 U/mg in KW128 and0.34+/-0.01 in delta-imp-2A, but undetected in
delta-fbp-8J. The mutant CAN grow in this rich medium. The authors suggest
yeast-extract carbohydrates as an explanation, without directly measuring
that rescue substrate. Do not join rich-medium extract measurements to
the synthetic-medium growth curve as a measured causal intermediate.

No fbp reintroduction/complementation experiment was found in the inspected
full Methods/Results/Discussion. Native imp did not compensate; this is not
a failed engineered imp overexpression rescue. A monocistronic fbp Northern
band makes polarity less likely, not exhaustively excluded. Since later work
assigns dual aldolase/phosphatase activity, the deletion cannot isolate the
contribution of either chemical step. Retain UNRESOLVED phenotype edges.
