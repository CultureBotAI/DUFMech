Fbp from Thermococcus KOD1 was expressed in E. coli and purified separately
from Imp. Fbp purification follows Rashid2002; SDS-PAGE homogeneity is
reported, with the gel not shown here. At85C and2-mM FBP, the Fbp preparation
gave18.9+/-0.7 U/mg in three independent experiments. Product was measured by
glucose-6-phosphate isomerase and NADP-dependent glucose-6-phosphate
dehydrogenase coupling, with NADPH read at340nm. FBP-minus blanks and thermal
substrate decomposition were accounted for. This supports whole-protein
FBP hydrolysis yielding F6P, not an isolated-domain or residue mechanism.

The table's Fbp Km0.1mM and kcat17/s were imported from a95C experiment;
they are not measured at the85C activity endpoint. Exact Fbp assay buffer,
metal concentration and purification details referred to the earlier paper
were not independently verified here. Do not borrow20-mM MgCl2 from the
separate IMPase protocol. Phosphate is chemically expected from hydrolysis,
but this FBP product assay specifically detects F6P by coupling.
Imp also hydrolyzes FBP in vitro, but is a different protein/family.
The small IMP activity of Fbp is not proof of an absolute zero.
No aldol-condensation experiment was performed in this2004 comparison.
