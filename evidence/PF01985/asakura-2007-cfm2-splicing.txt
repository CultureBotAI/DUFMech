Arabidopsis At3g01370 alleles: SALK_000703=Atcfm2-1, a central insertion;
SAIL_67_E05=Atcfm2-2, a near-3prime insertion. Main text reports cosegregation
and failure of allelic complementation. This is not transgenic WT rescue.
AtCFM2 protein levels were not assayed because the maize antibody did not
detect the Arabidopsis protein; null/truncation/residual-activity assignments
are therefore not measured protein properties. Supplemental genotyping was
not separately inspected; Figure5 and main-text genetic descriptions were.

RNA assays use leaves from2-3week plants on MS with2% sucrose,pH5.7; general
growth14h light(23C,200uE/m2/s)/10h dark(21C). Rare Atcfm2-1 homozygous leaves
came from10h-light/14h-dark growth. Figure5C includes parallel short-day plants,
but exact matching of all RNA/protein assay controls to that regimen is not
specified. Strong homozygote effects are not adopted as genotype-only edges.
An ecotype and exact per-blot age/growth assignment are not asserted here.

Figure7A/D poisoned-primer extension uses10ug total leaf RNA. Both homozygous
Atcfm2-2 and heteroallelic Atcfm2-1/Atcfm2-2 show lower spliced-to-unspliced
readouts for ycf3-int1,ndhA and clpP-int2 versus WT. ycf3-int2,atpF,clpP-int1
and rpoC1 are unaffected in the shown comparisons. Atcrs1-1 controls selected
effects of impaired photosynthesis. Figure7B's5ug RNA blots also show lower
fully spliced ycf3 and excised intron1 with increased precursor; these favor a
processing defect over only mature-mRNA instability, not an elementary step.
The extra ycf3-int1 band is consistent with edited unspliced RNA; an unlabeled
clpP-int2 product may be a structure-induced RT stop, not a new spliced form.

Figure7C uses1ug leaf RNA with an exon/intron trnL-UAA probe, cytosolic25S/18S
loading controls and phosphor-imager U/S ratios. Heteroallelic Atcfm2-1/Atcfm2-2
has a higher U/S ratio than WT and the shown photosynthesis-impaired controls.
The paper reports about4fold versus the other impaired mutants, not a universal
4fold WT comparison. The weak homozygote effect is modest; the larger strong
homozygote effect is retained as a differently sampled context, not pooled.
No kinetic splicing rate, complete abolition or numerical replicate/error
estimate is inferred from these steady-state RNA assays.

Figure6 compares10ug leaf protein with serial WT dilutions and PonceauS/RbcL
loading context. The reported Atcfm2-2 preparation has much lower PsaD and PsaF
signals (authors estimate greater than10fold), unlike most other monitored
proteins. Exact per-lane growth/age and densitometric replication are not given.
The graph retains this sample comparison, not a growth-matched gene-only cause
or a proven ycf3-splicing-to-photosystem-I mediation chain.

Maize CFM2 RNA association is a different taxon/assay from Arabidopsis genetics;
the maize antibody detects multiple protein bands. Neither direct Arabidopsis
RNA contact nor CAF1/CAF2/CFM2 joint catalytic sufficiency follows from co-IP.
clpP-int2 is absent in maize; its direct AtCFM2 binding was not tested here.
The distinct CFM2 mRNA RT-PCR recipe must not be assigned to primer-extension
splicing assays. Supplemental Figures5/6 remain uninspected.
