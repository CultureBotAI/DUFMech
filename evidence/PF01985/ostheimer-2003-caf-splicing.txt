Methods explicitly identifies the assayed CAF1 tissue as caf1-1/caf1-2 from
a heteroallelic cross, despite generic homozygous wording in Results. CAF2 is
caf2-1 homozygous. The insertions are Mu8(caf1-1), Mu3(caf1-2), Mu1(caf2-1).
The first two are near the ORF start and may retain expression; the internal
caf2 insertion is considered likely null, not a measured absence of protein.
Lines were propagated through inbred crosses; the exact assayed background,
age and growth regimen are not assigned by the inspected Methods. The14-day
B73 seedling conditions describe the library, not these mutant RNA assays.

RNase protection uses6ug WT versus10ug mutant total leaf RNA. Spliced and
unspliced products support qualitative processing comparisons, not absolute
normalized transcript counts, numerical kinetic rates or equal raw-band loads.
Controls include WT, crs2-1, fully albino ribosome-deficient iojap and tRNA.
No assay replicate/error/statistical summary or transgenic WT rescue is supplied
for the displayed protection gels in the inspected material.

CAF1 heteroallelic tissue: lower splicing readouts for petD,rps16,ycf3-int1
are displayed in Figure5B/D; rpl16,trnG,ndhA effects are reported in Results and
Figure7's summary but their raw gels are not displayed in Figure5.
CAF2 homozygous tissue: lower ndhB,petB,ycf3-int1 readouts are displayed in
Figure5C/D; rps12-int1 and ndhA effects are reported in Results/Figure7 only.
Figure5E also shows lower atpF and rpl2 splicing readouts in CAF2 mutants.
These subgroup-IIA effects are explicitly separated: iojap phenocopies them,
and the authors favor secondary plastid-ribosome deficiency over direct CAF2
splicing action. That proposed mediation is not itself an observed causal edge.

ycf3-int2 is unaffected in the shown CAF comparisons. One co-IP Results
paragraph incorrectly names ycf3-2 as jointly CAF-dependent; the earlier
Results, Figure5D and Figure7 consistently identify ycf3-int1. The graphs use
intron1 and retain this discrepancy rather than silently reproducing the typo.
CAF1 does not show the displayed CAF2-specific or subgroup-IIA defects, and
CAF2 does not show the displayed CAF1-specific defects. These are scoped
negative controls, not evidence that either protein has no other functions.

CRS2/CAF and intron co-IP/two-hybrid results supply context, not a demonstrated
direct RNA contact, simultaneous single CAF1/CAF2 complex, catalytic step or
isolated CRM-domain requirement. No all-family transfer is made.
