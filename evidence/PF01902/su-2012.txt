dependent fluorescence signal on deletion-derived eEF2. Omitting enzyme or

quantitation. Main Methods:30C,30min,pH8,3nM enzyme,0.5uM eEF2; replicate
counts and exact assembled protein sequence were not established.

also yields ADP signal. Standards are enzymatically generated. No measured
PPi stoichiometry or isolated-domain activity is established.
Figure4C proposes adenylation followed by ammonia displacement; neither
intermediate capture nor atomic trajectory is observed.
Figure3D: full yeast or human protein restores fluorescence versus empty
vector. Yeast1-264 and PH1257 do not restore it under these conditions;
misfolding,abundance or cross-species compatibility remain alternatives.

product of Figure4A. Supplement PDF was inaccessible through tried endpoints.
