Minimal quotation: "two extra N-terminal residues (GS)"

The pET15b construct supplied by Geoffrey Chang encodes N-terminal His6
EmrE; thrombin cleavage leaves GS. The method describes BL21(DE3)
expression and nickel-affinity purification, followed by cleavage and
size-exclusion chromatography. The 2017 paper cites this protocol and
thanks Chang for the expression plasmid. This is a traceable construct
method, not independent sequencing of every later transport-assay batch.
The 2011 structural/NMR assays are not adopted as additional causal edges.
