Figure1 and S2A: CeMUT-7 CTD633-910 plus CeMUT-8 CTD322-578 form an
isolable complex after coexpression,tag cleavage and chromatography.
SEC-MALS gives about60kDa versus components32/29kDa; 8Q66 resolves the
slightly trimmed633-899/322-567 pair. S2 SEC-MALS:5.4mg/ml,20mM Tris
pH7.5,150mM NaCl,2mM DTT. This supports a direct physical assembly,
not an association constant or a native intracellular reaction rate.
Structural contacts are not automatically catalytic microsteps. The main
text's cross-link residue wording differs from the S2E table; no individual
cross-link is promoted to a causal edge.

Figure4C: double substitution R853E/T855E within Ce CTD633-910 decreases
recovered MUT-8 CTD in MBP coexpression pulldown versus WT CTD. S3H:
CTD-C773-910 yields poorer coprecipitation than633-910. TableS2 defines
Ce285/Ce302/Ce349 and Ce299. Lysate,supernatant,eluate and prey-only lanes
are present. MUT-8 solubility depends on its partner. These are observed
assay effects with UNRESOLVED directness: not measured binding affinities
or proof that the substitutions affect only a contact and not folding or
coexpression solubility. Both substitutions are outside current PF01927.
Human/zebrafish CTDs do not solubilize MUT-8 CTD in Figure6C; insolubility
limits the negative binding interpretation. No all-species nonbinding claim.

Figure3B/S5: purified full-length MUT-7/MUT-8 associates with GST-MUT-16
584-724, whereas MUT-7 alone and the two-CTD complex do not. Beads without
bait are negative controls.5uM proteins,20mM HEPES pH7.5,150mM NaCl,
10%glycerol,0.05%IGEPAL,2mM DTT;30min preincubation and1-2h resin
incubation at4C. Record the joint complex, not direct MUT-7/MUT-16 binding.
MUT-8 NTD1-235 directly binds the MUT-16 fragment, supported by SEC and ITC
(S5D12.5+/-1.67uM); this partner-only assay is context, not a PF01927 edge.

Figure3F: jointly adding MUT-16 584-724 and FL MUT-7/MUT-8 produces visible
droplets at10uM proteins,20mM Tris pH7.5,150mM NaCl,2mM DTT,5%PEG6000,
100min incubation. Fragment alone,FL pair alone and MUT-8 NTD/MUT-16
fragment lack comparable droplets. This is a crowded in-vitro assembly
phenomenon, not proof that any component alone is sufficient or that the
observed droplets establish all liquid-liquid-phase-separation criteria.
