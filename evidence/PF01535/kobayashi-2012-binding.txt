Inspected: production, probe and gel-shift Methods; relevant Results and Discussion;
Figures 2 and 3 visually, including Figure 3B's numerical table.
Quotation: "partially recovered"
Curator interpretation: Mutant 5-K12N and mutant 6-N12R each have apparent
50%-binding concentrations above 2500 nM, versus 9.7 nM for HCF/5&6.
Adding 6-N12K to 5-K12N gives 384.6 nM; adding 5-K12M to 6-N12R gives
454.5 nM. Both are partial recoveries, not WT restoration. K12H is 27.0 nM,
not literally equal to WT. Multiple gel-shift species make these operational
apparent values, not microscopic equilibrium constants. The text's description
of a reduced K_D accompanying reduced affinity has the direction reversed;
the adopted effects follow the figure and assay definition.
The tested mini-proteins retain N-terminal thioredoxin and C-terminal six-His.
