Fluorescence anisotropy compared apo and Cu(I)-loaded protein on 4 nM labeled
30-bp copZA operator DNA, in 10 mM MES, 0.4 M NaCl, 2 mM DTT, pH 6.5, 25 C.
Five readings per addition were averaged, not five independent experiments.
Figure 7A shows weaker occupancy for Cu-loaded wild type. Fits assume two
nondissociable tetramers per DNA. The unsaturated Cu curve uses apo maximum
anisotropy; the second binding constant is an upper bound. Individual step
constants are inversely correlated and not uniquely resolved.
Figure 7B's E90A control loses negative regulation. This supports coupling,
not an observed atom-by-atom allosteric path. Low-micromolar tetramers do not
exclude different nanomolar assembly. Ni/Zn coupling is weak, not strictly zero.
No transcription assay, family-wide metal specificity or cellular copper flux
is inferred from this purified-protein comparison. Raw data were not reanalyzed.
