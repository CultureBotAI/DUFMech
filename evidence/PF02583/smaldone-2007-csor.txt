Curator assay notes (paraphrase):
CU1065 is a B. subtilis W168 attSPbeta trpC2 derivative. The reporter is
SPbeta(PcopZA-cat-lacZ): HB7358 control, HB7352 csoR::spc and HB7356
csoR::spc amyE::csoR-cat complemented strain. Null alleles use replacement.
Overnight cultures were diluted 1:100 in LB, with or without 0.5 mM CuSO4,
and sampled at mid-exponential growth. General growth conditions are 37 C,
225 rpm. Figure 2A reports beta-galactosidase activity; its caption specifies
three experiments and total N=7, not seven independent biological experiments.
Without added copper, null-mutant reporter output exceeds control; ectopic
csoR restores regulation. The graph retains this genetic reporter effect,
not direct polymerase exclusion or measured native transcript abundance.
The authors correct their earlier YhdQ/CueR claim: the wrong reporter had
been transduced. New yhdQ nulls retain copper inducibility. This is a source
correction, not evidence against CsoR. Figure 2B's growth phenotype is separate
and not an adopted edge. Raw data were not reanalyzed.
