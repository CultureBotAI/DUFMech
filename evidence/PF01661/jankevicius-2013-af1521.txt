GST-AF1521 reduces the PARP10-associated32P signal by roughly75% in Figure4d.
No replicate count/error bars are given there. Human MacroD2, yeast Poa1p,
PARG, MacroH2A1.1 and Ymx7 are separate comparators, not duplicate Af1521 assays.
GST-AF1521 was expressed in Rosetta(DE3)pLysS,18C18h,200uM IPTG and eluted
from glutathione Sepharose with20mM reduced glutathione. No Af GST cleavage
is stated. Construct cited to Dani2009; exact vector residues remain unresolved.
Substrate is GST-PARP10 catalytic domain818-1025. About30pmol is bound to10ul
glutathione beads30min4C, washed3x in50mM HEPES pH7.2,150mM KCl,4mM MgCl2,
0.2mM DTT,0.1%NP40, then labeled with0.625uM32P-NAD15min37C.
The general procedure washes free NAD away, but the explicit GST-macrodomain
exception omits that wash. GST-AF1521 therefore receives unwashed substrate/NAD,
0.5uM GST-macrodomain15min37C. The retained endpoint is protein-associated
label decrease, with UNRESOLVED directness: continuing automodification, NAD
turnover and tag effects are not independently separated in this Af experiment.
Af-specific released-product MS and exact bond/site chemistry are not shown.
The MacroD2 mass-spectrometry/isotope assays on p20 and its proposed catalytic
water/substrate-assisted mechanism cannot be reassigned to Af1521. Structure
alignment and residue conservation are contextual evidence, not observed Af1521
chemical intermediates or proof of activity for every PF01661 member.
