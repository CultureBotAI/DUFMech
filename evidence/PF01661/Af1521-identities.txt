Karras2005 describes thrombin cleavage of His6-Af1521 and refers to Allen2003,
PMID12842467, for expression details. That earlier paper's complete Methods
were not retrieved; exact vector-derived terminal residues remain unresolved.
Jankevicius2013 explicitly uses GST-AF1521 from Dani2009, PMID19246377,
doi:10.1073/pnas.0900066106; its complete construct supplement was inaccessible.
Rosenthal2013 describes pET28-GST-derived macrodomains, but explicitly describes
tag cleavage for MacroD2 only. Do not call either 2013 Af1521 preparation tag-free
or sequence-verified from the 2005 structure. All current graph mappings use the
Af1521 native reference with these construct qualifications.
