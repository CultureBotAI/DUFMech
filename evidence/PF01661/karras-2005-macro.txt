weak at20pmol; BSA/lysozyme at200/400pmol are low controls. H2A and BAL/PARP9
are other binding comparators, not Af1521 orthologs. Polymer was generated using
0.4U bovinePARP1,6ug/ml activated calf-thymus DNA,150uM NAD plus tracer,25mM
Tris pH8,10mM MgCl2,0.1M NaCl,0.5mM DTT,1h25C. Dilute to10mlTBS-T;
bind1h25C, extensive washes including1M NaCl. Loading stain is reported but
not displayed. NAD-only and freed-PAR controls are data not shown. The displayed
experiment does not resolve free-PAR versus PARP1-conjugate binding, polymer
end versus internal binding, polymer cleavage, or recruitment in living cells.
