Figure1A-C: pCA24N SO_3166 expression in E. coli BW25113 reduces growth
and recoverable CFU versus vector. Coexpressed SO_3165-3166 restores CFU
relative to SO_3166 alone. LB, 37C, 1mM IPTG at OD600 about0.1; two
independent cultures. Figure1D-F separately tests MR-1 with pHGE, at30C
in the strain Methods and with1mM IPTG. Its CFU loss and coexpression
rescue are not the same experiment as the E. coli result. These are induced
whole-protein effects, not basal physiological killing or direct RNA assays.

Figure5A tests pCA24N constructs in DH5alpha, not BW25113. R97G and
H102A relieve induced growth inhibition; Y104A retains toxicity. Clones
were sequence-verified. Protein folding/abundance was not established by
this plate comparison, so substitutions do not by themselves prove catalytic
roles. Figure5C also shows C15R,H56P,R70H,V94G,L107H,H118P and the
combined N98T/Y104C variant. Do not attribute the double mutant to N98 alone.
