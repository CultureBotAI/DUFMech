from pHGE has lower recoverable colony formation than full fusion or vector.

stability differ. FigureS2 separately shows lower growth/CFU with fragment

Do not turn either into evidence of endogenous proteolytic activation.

Figure6A andS5A: isolated HEPN fragment cleaves MS2 RNA under reducing
conditions; the full fusion retains little detectable activity. The fragment
has stronger cleavage after reducing preparation/1mM DTT than without it.
Methods:1ug MS2 RNA with0.1ug protein,37C20min; short49nt FAM RNA
uses100nM RNA,500nM protein,20mM Tris pH8,150mM NaCl,5mM MgCl2.
The DTT comparison includes purification history, not a clean single acute
ligand titration. Do not infer cleavage sequence, intracellular substrate,
antiviral defense, or exact redox chemistry solely from degradation gels.

Figure6B,C,E/S6: reducing preparation decreases high-MW SDS-PAGE bands;
C163T/C270W decreases such bands without reducing agent. NEM/TCEP
controls support pre-existing intermolecular assemblies but show that some
monomer disulfides can arise during denaturation. Band shifts are not a
direct in-cell demonstration of a particular bond topology or activity relay.
Figure6F: native MR-1 expressing the double-mutant fragment has fewer
recoverable colonies than the wild-type fragment under0.5mM H2O2 and
0.5mM IPTG for2h. FigureS6B shows toxicity without H2O2 is retained.
This is a genotype-by-condition phenotype, not proof that H2O2 simply
activates an RNase or that all oxidative-stress survival changes are mediated
by the measured in-vitro oligomers. Both cysteines lie outside the mapped
PF01934 core; do not attribute the switch to the matched core alone.
