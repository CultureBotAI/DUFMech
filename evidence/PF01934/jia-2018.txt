Figure1A: purified SO_3166 produces shorter products from ompA RNA
1-306nt; RNA-only and heat-inactivated controls retain intact substrate.
Methods:8ul,0.5ug RNA,2ug protein,20mM Tris pH8,300mM NaCl,
25mM EDTA,60mM KCl,30mM MgCl2,37C,20/40min. The co-purified
SO_3165/3166 complex is less active, but this does not isolate noncovalent
binding as sufficient inhibition: Yao2020 identifies covalent modification.
The EDTA-containing assay also contains excess Mg; it is not a clean
demonstration of metal independence. Its roughly16.6uM protein condition
must not be quantitatively pooled with the2020 0-160nM assay.

Figure1B gives an assay-specific total-tRNA negative. Figure1C compares
rRNA isolated from MR-1 after expression, not purified rRNA in the same
in-vitro assay. Neither establishes family-wide RNA substrate exclusion.
The complex structure, SEC and SAXS support6 HepT:2 MntA, contrary to
the frozen description's reversed order. Proposed RNA-path occlusion is
structural interpretation, not observed substrate occupancy.

Helix-deletion growth phenotypes and reported similar CD spectra need a
separate construct/figure review before curation; the2018 binding-only
interpretation must be reconciled with the2020 AMPylation experiments.
