Curator's assay notes, not quotations:
E. coli b0637 was expressed with an N-terminal His6 tag in BL21(DE3), purified
by nickel affinity and anion exchange. RsfA is the paper's name for RsfS/YbeB.
Preincubation: 18 pmol 50S, 180 ug poly(U), with/without 360 pmol RsfA in 90 uL,
10 min at 37 C; then 10 pmol 30S for 10 min at 37 C. Binding buffer: 20 mM HEPES
pH 7.6 at 0 C, 4.5 mM Mg acetate, 150 mM K acetate, 4 mM beta-mercaptoethanol,
2 mM spermidine, 0.05 mM spermine. Sister aliquots underwent 10-30% sucrose
gradients/A260 or precharged radiolabeled Phe-tRNA/EF-Tu/EF-Ts/EF-G/GTP-dependent
oligo(Phe) synthesis, 2 min at 30 C. Both endpoints decrease with RsfA addition.
Preassembled programmed 70S did not show the same strong inhibition: Figure 4A
right is modestly below its control, not literally identical. Atomic steric
contacts were modeled; they are not separate experimentally observed edges.

The whole-gene deletion phenotype uses BW25113 derivatives. Overnight LB cells
were diluted to OD600 0.005 in M9 and followed at 37 C, 200 rpm, up to 40 h.
Figure 3B shows a prolonged growth plateau after initial growth, unlike WT.
For complementation the kanamycin cassette was removed. Native-promoter RsfA-His
did not rescue under this condition, while tac-driven expression induced with
0.2 mM IPTG about 3 h after transfer did. This supports a gene-dependent phenotype,
not a demonstrated direct metabolic route from ribosome inhibition to growth.
The paper's separate competition, stationary-phase reporter, mitochondrial and
interaction assays are not substituted for these adopted endpoints.
