Assay notes: Figure 1C uses purified E. coli YbeY and 5'-32P-labeled synthetic
30-nt RNA at 5 uM each. The RNA contains 18 nt from the mature 16S 3' end and
12 nt of the precursor trailer. The image shows cleavage fragments at 30, 90
and 120 seconds versus buffer. Figure 1D's duplex substrate is not comparably
cleaved. This is not an intact pre-16S maturation assay or a turnover estimate.

Figure 3B/C: 70S preparations (5.5 uM) from MC4100 delta-ybeY or MC4100 treated
with 200 ug/mL kasugamycin lose their rRNA bands after joint YbeY and RNase R
treatment (5 uM each). Each enzyme alone and the YbeY H114A/H124A-plus-RNase-R
control do not show that loss. Matched 30S preparations and untreated wild-type
70S remain resistant under the tested conditions. RNA was extracted after
incubation and analyzed by Synergel/agarose electrophoresis; this measures rRNA
degradation, not complete destruction of ribosomal proteins. Figure 3D examines
polysomes separately; those endpoints are not yet graph edges.

Main methods: 20 uL reactions, 50 mM HEPES pH 7.5; commercial RNase R from
Epicenter. YbeY purification uses affinity, ion exchange and size exclusion.
The text describes efficient RNA cleavage at 37 C, but the precise temperature,
duration and additional buffer components of Figure 3 are delegated to extended
methods and have not been verified. Tag, expression construct and purification
host are also unresolved in the inspected main text. The supplement URL returned
HTML, and Europe PMC's supplementaryFiles endpoint reports this article outside
its open-access subset. The .doc and extended methods were NOT inspected.

Identity: Figure 2A explicitly cites former Swiss-Prot P77385 for E. coli YbeY.
