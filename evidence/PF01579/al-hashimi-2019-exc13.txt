Table 2: 79 canals; 19% affected; affected-canal length score 3.5, SD 0.32;
BK540 control: 100 canals, 0% affected, length score 4.0, SD 0.0.
Length is a 0-4 ordinal anatomical score, not micrometres. The 3.5 mean is
conditioned on affected canals, not the mean across all 79. Fisher 3x2
P=1.2e-6 is just outside the authors' high-significance cutoff of 1e-6.
Figure 4D and Results show focal basal vesicles/cysts and irregular swollen
cytoplasm. The VC40373 C44Y strain is a separate, multi-mutation background;
its enlarged short lumens do not reproduce the RNAi's large lateral cysts.
It is not evidence for an isolated C44Y or disulfide-mechanism causal edge.
