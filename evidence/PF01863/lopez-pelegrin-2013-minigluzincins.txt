Curator's factual notes:
Synthetic proabylysin Q9V1Y2 scaffold, pCRI7, E. coli BL21(DE3), no tag.
Paper K2V label conflicts with reference/PDB L2V (see identity ledger).
Deletion preparations lack V105 or M104/V105. Thermofluor/Sypro Orange,
iCycler iQ: reported transitions 85 and 65 C, versus full-length 95 C.
Deletion purification uses 50-65 C heat treatment, versus 85 C full length;
replicates, uncertainty, ramp and exact dye-assay buffer not established.
This is a preparation comparison, not an isolated-domain mechanism.

Figure 3A: proabylysin Q101-V105 tail occupies the cleft in reverse substrate
orientation; terminal V105 carboxylate coordinates Zn (about 2.02 A).
Figure 3B-D: projannalysin dimer has partner E102-I110 helix across the cleft;
I110 carboxylate is 4.1-4.2 A from Zn, not the proabylysin ligand geometry.
Tail-mediated latency is a proposed explanation, not a successful activation
experiment. Deletions and limited proteolysis did not yield active enzymes;
inactive-ancestor interpretation remains possible. Projannalysin construct
has K2A and residual GP after tag cleavage; native numbering is retained.

Negative proteolysis was reported across tested protein/peptide panels,
including elevated temperatures/chaotropes. This is not universal inactivity.
Aquifex insolubility/refolding failure is not a catalytic negative result.
