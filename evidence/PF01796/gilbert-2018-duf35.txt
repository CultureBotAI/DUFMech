Curator interpretation:
The copurified M. tuberculosis H37Rv Ltp2-ChsH2 C-terminal DUF35 complex
turns over 17-HOPC-CoA in a TTHB247/NADH-coupled assay. Figure 5 and methods
use 100 mM sodium HEPES pH 7.5, 16 uM TTHB247, 200 uM NADH,
2.5-30 uM substrate, 25 C, 1 mL, at least triplicate measurements.
The substrate preparation also contains 3-OPDC-CoA; no detectable spontaneous
hydration occurs during the 5-15 minute kinetic assays. DUF35 alone is inactive;
Ltp2 alone has low activity and possible folding/stability limitations.
Product LC-MS belongs to a separate larger-complex assay, not this kinetic
experiment. No isolated-OB-fold catalysis or host virulence effect is inferred.
