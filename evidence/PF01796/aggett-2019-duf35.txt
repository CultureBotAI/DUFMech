"After a 10-min incubation of a 1:5 ratio of Ltp2/ChsH2"

Curator interpretation:
Adding the T. curvata DSM 43183 ChsH2 C-terminal construct to Ltp2 increases
specific aldolase activity on 21 uM 17-HOPC-CoA from (4.2 +/- 2.6) x 10^-4
to (1.4 +/- 0.079) x 10^-1 umol/min/mg after 10 minutes at a 1:5 ratio.
DUF35 alone has no detectable aldolase activity. The coupled assay uses
100 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 200 uM NADH, 16 uM TTHB247, 25 C, 1 mL,
at least triplicate measurements. Genes are Tcur3479 and Tcur3482;
the coexpression construct places a C-terminal His tag on Ltp2.
The tested DUF35 region includes both modern PF01796 and PF12172 matches.
Neither isolated domain's sufficiency nor an allosteric molecular mechanism
is proved by this addition experiment. Bile-acid physiology and residue
mechanisms are not adopted from analogy or docking.
