Curator interpretation and factual assay metadata:
The tested unit is a synthetic 708-828 fragment of A. silvestris ANC4999
B9T28_05395, not its full-length adhesin. pOPINF adds an N-terminal His6/3C
sequence; BL21(DE3) expression uses 1 mM IPTG, 18 C, 16 h. Nickel affinity
and SEC purify the preparations. Methods explicitly remove the tag for
crystallization, but do not unambiguously establish that processing for the CD
and proteolysis aliquots. Their tag status remains unresolved.

CD: 10 mM sodium phosphate, 100 mM sodium fluoride, pH 7.4; 300 ul in a 1-mm
cuvette; WT 0.36 mg/ml, K715A 0.38 mg/ml. Heating 5 to 95 C and subsequent
cooling to 5 C in 5-C increments; 180-280 nm spectra. Each displayed spectrum
averages four repeat scans, not four independently prepared protein batches.
Initial WT/mutant spectra both resemble folded beta-sheet proteins. Figure 6a/c
shows the mutant transition at lower temperatures than WT (source describes
approximately 45 versus 80 C, not independently fitted melting constants).
Figure 6b/c shows less return toward the initial CD spectrum for K715A after
cooling. The comparisons concern normalized spectral behavior, not cell survival
or an observed unfolding/refolding pathway for a single molecule. Independent
replicate uncertainty, thermal equilibration time and detailed scan rate are not
established by the inspected method.

Proteolysis: mix 10 ul of 1 mg/ml polypeptide with 10 ul of 1 mg/ml Proteinase K
in PBS, incubate 1 h at 20, 30, 40 or 50 C, then SDS-PAGE. Calculated final
concentrations are 0.5 mg/ml each in the 20-ul mixture. PBS recipe: 137 mM NaCl,
3 mM KCl, 10 mM Na2HPO4, 1.8 mM KH2PO4, pH 7.4. Figure 6d includes separate
K715A, WT and protease lanes and shows less intact mutant-domain band remaining
than WT in the matched protease-treated lanes. This is a qualitative gel result;
there is no independently quantified degradation rate or replicate confidence.

Table S6 coding sequences independently confirm the intended K715A substitution
as the only protein difference. Both retain N748 and D749, contrary to Figure 5
and the main text's Asp748 label. S8 shows similar principal SEC elution peaks
and dominant domain bands for both preparations; low-temperature CD supplies
an additional folding control, not proof of identical atomic structures.

The mutation removes the reported crosslinking lysine. Both proteins initially
show similar secondary structure, and the WT crystal is reported to contain a
K715-N806 isopeptide bond. Nevertheless, mutation effects need not be exclusively
due to that bond; local packing and the construct's separate disulfide can matter.
No D748/D749 catalytic mechanism, formation-time course, force-resistance assay,
native adhesion or family-wide bond presence is inferred. Methods' K517A labels
conflict with K715A in the Results/figure and the independently translated
Table S6 sequences; this discrepancy is retained rather than silently corrected.
