Inspected 2026-10-11: main Methods, Results and Discussion; Figures 2-5 and 8
visually inspected. Minimal quotations: "flavin reduction"; "dihydrouridine".

Recombinant yeast Dus2 is expressed in BL21(DE3), cobalt-affinity purified and
further separated by DEAE to remove RNA/chromophore contamination. WT storage
DTT is removed immediately before assays. Exact tag junctions are unresolved.
Figure 2: anaerobic oxidized Dus2, 15 uM, mixed with NADPH at 4 C in 100 mM
HEPES pH 7.5, 100 mM NaCl, 10 mM MgCl2 and 10% glycerol loses A450 as its
flavin is reduced. Minor slower phases may reflect damaged protein; NADP
dissociation is proposed rather than independently determined by this trace.
Figure 3 and Results: dithionite-prereduced Dus2 with synthetic pre-tRNA-Leu
at 25 C yields flavin reoxidation and colorimetrically detected dihydrouridine.
Results specify 60 uM enzyme/700 uM RNA and 457 nm, whereas the caption
specifies 35 uM enzyme and 450 nm. The printed pre/intronless template strings
also differ by a promoter-containing prefix rather than an evident intron.
The adopted effect is qualitative product formation in the reported substrate,
not an exact reconstructed RNA sequence, site assignment or precise rate.
Figure 4: 20 uM prereduced WT Dus2 with affinity-purified tRNA-Leu from a
YNR015w knockout is reoxidized at 4 C, measured by A450. Caption specifies up
to 800 uM RNA but the image axis says mM; no silent unit correction is made.
The mutant comparison uses 25 C, not WT's 4 C, and has conflicting primers.
No C117-specific proton-transfer or equal-condition 1600-fold edge is adopted.
Reverse-reaction Results: 40 uM Dus2 with 90 uM bulk E. coli tRNA aerobically
for 18 h reduces the measured dihydrouridine content. General kinetics default
to 4 C, but this endpoint paragraph does not explicitly restate temperature.
Figure 8 is a separate anaerobic 4-C assay: 20 uM oxidized Dus2 (after mixing)
is reduced by bulk E. coli tRNA, measured by decreasing A450. This mixture
does not establish a specific yeast tRNA, nucleotide position or native reverse
flux. Separate reaction vessels and substrates are not a tracked cellular cycle.
Colorimetry uses ring opening and an A550 standard curve, not site-resolved MS.
Raw spectra, unshown NMR stereochemistry, clone sequence and exact tRNA
modification inventory were not independently verified. The paper's critical
prior modification remains unidentified; do not generalize to every Dus member.
