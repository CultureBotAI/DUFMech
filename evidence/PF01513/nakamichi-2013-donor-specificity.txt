Figure 2 compares purified, untagged E. coli NADK and N75T using a G6PD-coupled
assay at 37 C: 5 mM NAD+, 5 mM MgCl2, 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 5 mM glucose
6-phosphate, 0.5 U glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase. ATP or tetrapolyphosphate
is 5 mM; hexametaphosphate or metaphosphate is 1 mg/ml. The latter two are
heterogeneous chain-length mixtures, not equivalent molar substrates.
ATP-associated specific activity falls with N75T; poly(P)4, hexameta(P)n and
meta(P)n activities rise. WT activity with these donors is small but nonzero.
Tripolyphosphate does not share the increase. Means/SD are reported for three
independent experiments; independent purification batches are not established.
Specific assay enzyme doses, reaction volume and initial-rate window are not
fully specified in the inspected 2013 main protocol.

Figure 3 supplies separate product support: 5-ul reactions, 37 C, 10 h, 5 mM
NAD+, 5 mM MgCl2, 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 1 mg/ml meta(P)n and 0.43 ug/ml
enzyme. N75T yields a stronger NADP+-comigrating TLC spot than WT. No-enzyme
and nucleotide standards are displayed. The plate uses isobutyrate/500 mM NH4OH
5:3 and UV254 detection. This is qualitative endpoint evidence, not a fitted
rate, exact product mass/structure, complete conversion or all-donor activity.
