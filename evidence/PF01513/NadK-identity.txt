Nakamichi 2013 names the inherited pET-14b_ecoNADK plasmid and describes untagged
WT/N75T expression in BL21(DE3)pLysS, with sequence confirmation by the authors.
The cited 2001 cloning paper identifies MG1655 as genomic donor. Its printed
forward primer translates to MDNHFKCIGI, differing at N2/D2 from the current
reference and 2013 Figure S1's displayed MNNHFKCIGI. The printed reverse primer
has a locally translated RLVKKLF-stop terminus, whereas the reference ends
GWSKKLF. These historical-primer discrepancies remain unresolved; a full clone
sequence is not available in the inspected material. They do not relocate the
explicitly identified N75 but prevent an unqualified sequence-identical claim.
