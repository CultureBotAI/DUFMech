Inspected: full-text Results, Discussion and Methods; Figures 5, 7 and 8 were
visually inspected. NMR and structural findings below are text/caption evidence,
not independently reanalyzed spectra or coordinates. Supplementary S5/S6/S7
images were not retrieved: the linked DOC returned HTML and publisher access
was restricted. Statements about those controls are the authors' reports.

Construct: Ginkgo biloba mature Gnk2 Ala1-Phe108, expressed periplasmically with
pET26b and pDsbABCD1 in E. coli BL21(DE3). Methods explicitly retain an extra
N-terminal Met. The C-terminal Leu-Glu-His6 fusion is used for NMR interaction
screening; it is not assumed for the untagged purified SPR/growth preparations.
Untagged protein uses SP Sepharose, Mono S and Superdex 75 purification.

SPR: WT/N11A/R93A/E104A immobilized from 50 ug/ml acetate pH6 onto a
noncarbohydrate GLH surface by amine coupling, approximately 10000 RU. Running
buffer: 10 mM HEPES pH7, 150 mM NaCl, ProteOn XPR36. Analyte is alpha1,2-
mannobiose, not monomeric mannose (insufficient SPR sensitivity). Methods list
1.25/2.50/5/10/20 mM; Figure 5 labels 0.625/1.25/2.5/5/10 mM. This unresolved
twofold discrepancy prevents a precise independently verified affinity claim.
Figure 5 shows dose-ordered WT responses and smaller responses in all three
mutants; N11A retains visibly nonzero responses. Text reports WT KD 5.9+/-0.2
mM from steady-state fitting, triplicate assays; mutant affinities were not
fittable. No zero KD, infinite KD or complete absence of binding is established.
Text reports similar CD spectra for all three mutants (S5), and WT-like R93A
thermal stability (S6). Neither controls equal active immobilized fractions.

Yeast: S. cerevisiae BY4741 (MATa his3delta1 leu2delta0 met15delta0 ura3delta0),
with a separately supplied delta-mnn2 variant. Potato dextrose broth, 25 C;
pre-culture OD600 1, inoculate fresh cultures at OD600 0.01, add 2 uM WT or
R93A. Turbidity monitored up to OD600 2.55; assays repeated twice. Figure 7A
shows a large WT growth delay, a much smaller R93A delay, and strong attenuation
of WT delay by 0.5% w/v yeast mannan. Adding 50 mM D-mannose changes the WT
response little. Soluble-saccharide-only controls are not displayed. These data
do not uniquely prove competitive sequestration or measured cell-wall damage.
Discussion identifies lag extension, not a log-phase growth-rate decrease.
No measured spore-germination or viability endpoint is adopted.
Figure 7B's delta-mnn2 treatment curve retains a small delay relative to its
untreated control, far smaller than the BY4741 treatment delay. Text's strong
no-effect wording is qualified accordingly. MNN2 loss alters N-linked alpha1,2
mannosylation; this is not deletion of all mannan. No genetic rescue is shown.
Delta-och1 was too growth impaired to assay (unshown), not proven resistant.

Filamentous fungi: F. oxysporum IAM5009 and F. culmorum IFM50210. On potato
dextrose agar at 25 C, 6-mm discs placed 5 mm from a roughly 30-mm colony carry
30 ul of 10 mg/ml WT/R93A (300 ug), or 10 mM Tris pH7 buffer. Figure 8A/B
visually shows local growth exclusion around WT discs and much less around
R93A/control. Methods score 3 days after disc placement; caption says 8 days
after seeding. These are different timing origins, not a proven contradiction.
No zone diameter, fungicidal activity or unique wall-disruption step is measured.
Liquid F. culmorum: potato dextrose broth, initial OD600 0.005, 25 C, 2 or 10 uM
WT/R93A. OD600 measured after 36 h; endpoint inhibition is not a time-resolved
specific growth rate. Figure 8C means and SD, n=3; WT inhibition 22.1% at 2 uM
and 68.2% at 10 uM, R93A 7.6% at 10 uM. R93A is not completely inactive.

Scope/gaps: NMR text also reports glucose and N-acetylglucosamine binding, not
exclusive mannose monosaccharide recognition. The mannan screen does not prove
universal glycan specificity. Mannobiose docking is a model, not a bound crystal
structure. Static mannose contacts do not directly measure a causal trajectory.
N11A/E104A growth phenotypes were not measured in this study. Binding assays,
yeast lag, Fusarium disc responses and liquid OD endpoints stay separate.
Cell-wall disruption, biofilm prevention and plant protection remain unmeasured
here. Kinase-containing CRKs/PDLPs are not automatically Gnk2-like lectins.
PMID:26315821 is a later actin-remodeling lead, not independently reviewed here.
