Authored evidence assessment:
Figure3A: the joint delta-mamO/delta-R9 background has diminished MamE and
MamP processing; adding WT or H116A MamO restores shorter products. Figure3B
separately tests single delta-mamO, which retains limO, and WT complementation.
The single mutant has diminished processing despite LimO; rescue restores it.
Sigma70 is a loading control; the MamE NS band is nonspecific. These are cell
lysate processing patterns, not a purified MamO-mediated proteolysis reaction,
direct MamE binding assay or measurement of an ion-transport intermediate.
S4 names AK94 as delta-mamO/delta-R9 and AK30 as WT. S5 identifies pAK605 as
pAK253 with the mamAB promoter replacing tac, pAK845 as MamO and pAK856 as
H116A; pAK823 and its derivatives encode N-terminal M2/3xFLAG/BamHI-tagged
MamO. Exact tagged-versus-untagged construct assignment for every Figure3
lane remains unresolved; the S3 asterisks/anti-FLAG identify tagged alleles.
Main Methods pAK243 versus S4/S5 pAK241 concerns an additional mamE deletion
and is not silently normalized or assigned to the MamO comparisons.

Standard cultures use MG with ferric malate30uM,HEPES25mM pH7.2,10%oxygen,
30C; lysates are late-log. Temperature-comparison cultures were anaerobic
and are not merged into this graph. Single-copy neutral-site complementation
uses the mamAB promoter. In S3C the single delta-mamO background loses magnetic
response; WT,H116A,D149A,T225A,H148A,H263A tagged alleles restore response when
LimO is present. This is conditional complementation, not proof that the
mutated residues are dispensable without LimO or that the Pfam segment alone
suffices. H116/D149/T225/H148/H263 are all outside PF01925(357-633).

unresolved: no transported solute, physical MamE interaction or native metal
