Authored evidence assessment:
CR018 deletes cg3112/cysZ in C.glutamicum ATCC13032. Table2 scores no growth
after48h on solid MMS with2mM sulfur as sulfate or sulfite; WT grows. CR018
retains growth with thiosulfate,sulfide and cysteine. The complementation
paragraph reports WT-like growth when each missing gene is supplied in trans,
including pCR018e/cysZ, supporting gene-level effects rather than a polar-only
explanation; the rescue itself is text-only, not a displayed quantitative panel.
The Methods specify agarose16g/L,5%glucose,MMS with only trace sulfur.
Table4 names pEC-XK99E as the pCR018e backbone, whereas the cloning Methods
name pZ8-1. Preserve this discrepancy; exact primer-defined deletion endpoints
remain unresolved. A guessed PDF endpoint returned404; the actual linked EPS
supplement returned challenge HTML and was not inspected as a primer table.

Table3 liquid cultures use MMES,2.5%glucose,30C,initial OD6000.01,100uL/well
and15-min orbital-shaking cycles. At2mM sulfur, CR018 does not yield sulfate/
sulfite generation or lag times; WT generation times are2.1/2.2h. The same
mutant retains thiosulfate/sulfide growth; their lag values are not identical
to WT and are not blanket no-phenotype proof.
Figure3 compares CR018 at10,15,20,30,40mM sulfate with WT at5mM, not matched
dose controls. Increasing mutant sulfate restores turbidity;10mM still shows
limited output,20mM remains lower and30/40mM approaches WT5mM. Each time point
is18measurements from3independent cultivations with6parallels, not18biological
replicates. No transport rate,Km,ion coupling or purified-protein assay was
measured. High-affinity uptake is the authors' supported hypothesis, not an
observed biochemical edge. Numeric growth thresholds do not measure affinity.
