Curator notes (paraphrase):
PGA1_c13340 is the DUF1638 locus studied in Phaeobacter inhibens DSM 17395,
also called BS107 and formerly P. gallaeciensis. Figure 4B shows negative
pooled mutant fitness in ammonium/sucrose and ammonium/L-lactate conditions,
with relief in methionine/sucrose and casamino-acid conditions. The graph keeps
the sucrose comparison; it does not treat all media as an identical background
with a single extra supplement. Methods allow nitrogen-source replacement.
General fitness methods use rich-medium pool recovery, OD600 0.02 transfer
into 5 mL, growth to saturation and barcode sequencing before versus after
growth. P. inhibens was grown aerobically with shaking. Exact plotted doses,
temperature, elapsed time and insertion-level data were not recovered here.
There is no adopted clean deletion or gene complementation. The authors argue
that an operon-terminal location makes polarity less likely; this is not a
direct polarity control. They state that the specific DUF1638 function remains
unresolved despite the figure's B12-reactivation heading. Cofitness and matching
phylogenetic profiles with RamA-like proteins do not establish a direct physical
complex or a measured cobalamin-reduction reaction. Raw data were not reanalyzed.
