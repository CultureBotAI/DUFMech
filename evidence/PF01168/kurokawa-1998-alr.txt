Racemase assays: 1 ml, 100 umol CHES pH 9, 30 umol D- or L-alanine,
37 C; heat stop 100 C/10 min. D-to-L product measured with alanine
dehydrogenase/NAD+, L-to-D with DAAT/lactate dehydrogenase/NADH.
Protein amount and reaction duration are not specified for each comparison.
Figure 3A supports both directions; K39A's loss is reported in Results.

Table I inactivation: 10 ug holoenzyme, 0.5 umol D-alanine,
10 umol potassium phosphate pH 7, 0.01% 2-mercaptoethanol, 100 ul,
25 C/12 h; retained D-to-L activity decreases. Reactivation uses
50 ug apoenzyme, 1 umol PMP, 2.5 umol pyruvate, 10 umol K2HPO4-KOH
pH 10, 0.01% reductant, 100 ul, 25 C/12 h. It is a separate
preparation, not tracking the same enzyme molecules through a cycle.

Figure 5: 100 ul, 10 umol CHES pH 9, 8.7 nmol apoenzyme,
8 nmol randomly [4'-3H]-labeled PMP, 8.5 umol pyruvate, 30 C;
with/without 50 umol methylamine hydrochloride. K39A lowers volatile
tritium release; methylamine increases it. WT plateaus near 50%, rescued
mutant near 80%, not equivalent absolute catalytic turnover.
No formaldehyde product was identified; the proposed recycling scheme
is not adopted as an observed elementary pathway.

Figure 4's 50 umol methylamine in 500 ul implies 0.1 M, whereas its
associated prose says 0.5 M. Figure 5's Methods independently specifies
0.5 M and is the adopted rescue experiment. The opposite PLP-to-PMP
mutant reaction was not rescued under the tested conditions. No in-vivo
regulatory chain, isolated-domain sufficiency or universal family function
is established. Complete clones, exact kinetics, detection limits and
replicate counts remain incompletely resolved.
