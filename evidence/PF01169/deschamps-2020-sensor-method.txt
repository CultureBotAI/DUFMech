Inspected 2026-10-11: pH calibration and in-vivo measurement Methods
referenced by the 2023 Gdt1 study. No independent mechanism edges adopted.
Mnn2-HA-pHluorin** calibration uses 0.16% digitonin-treated yeast,
blank subtraction and a four-parameter curve. Yeast measurements use
400/480-nm excitation, 507-nm emission, 28 C and 300-rpm stirring.
Its glucose-starvation protocol uses an ice incubation, unlike 2023's
explicit 60 min at 28 C; use the later experiment's own stated conditions.
This yeast pHluorin protocol is distinct from bacterial sfpHluorin with
390/470-nm excitation and nigericin calibration.
