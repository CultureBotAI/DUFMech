Figure S1 uses blank-subtracted sfpHluorin spectra and 0.5 ug/ml
nigericin for calibration. It does not repeat the main Figure 1
caption's digitonin statement. Figure S2's acidification comparison
uses paired time-point means; it is not the adopted matched-baseline
Figure 2B experiment.
Table S1 reports p<0.001 for pH 6.8 versus 8.0 in GDT1 cells for both
calcium and manganese uptake. After nigericin those pairwise p values
are 0.13 and 0.37, respectively. Empty-vector between-pH comparisons
are not significant. Calcium 6.8 versus 7.5 is 0.70; manganese 7.5
versus 8.0 is 0.095. Do not claim every pH increment changes uptake.
