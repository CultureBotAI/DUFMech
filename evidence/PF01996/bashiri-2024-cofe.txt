Curator interpretation:
Recombinant, tag-cleaved A. fulgidus CofE converts F420-1 plus L-glutamate
to F420-2 in a GTP-containing assay. Figure 2 gives substrate mass 644.1389
and predominant product mass 773.1616. LC-MS, not binding alone, supports
the reaction. No exclusive endpoint or cellular chain-length claim is made.
Shared FbiB/CofE methods specify 20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.5, 50 mM KCl/NaCl,
2 mM MgCl2/MnCl2, 2 mM GTP, 50 uM F420-1, 2 mM L-glutamate and 37 C.
The slash-separated salts are not independently resolved for this assay here;
incubation duration and an enzyme-omission control are not specified there.
The nitro analogue and FbiB results are distinct experiments, not additional
CofE edges. Structural activation, proton-transfer and processivity models
are not relabeled as directly observed chemical intermediates.
