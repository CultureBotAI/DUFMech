Curator notes (paraphrase):
The focal locus is DvMF_1464 in D. vulgaris Miyazaki F, not Hildenborough.
Figure 3 measures pooled transposon-mutant relative fitness by barcode abundance,
not a clean deletion, purified reaction or individual growth curve. Minimal
lactate/sulfate conditions give negative fitness; adding L-methionine rescues it.
The caption specifies 1 or 10 mM methionine. D,L-cysteine at 1 mM gives partial
rescue; the reported mean -1.0 pools three genes, not just DvMF_1464.
General fitness methods recover pools in rich medium, transfer at OD600 0.02,
and compare barcode abundance before growth and at saturation by sequencing.
Miyazaki-specific methods specify anaerobic, unshaken 10-mL Hungate cultures
with 1 mM sulfide; generic fitness methods say 5 mL. Exact Figure 3 volume,
temperature, insertion-level contributions and incubation duration remain open.
Nutritional rescue is not gene complementation. Cross-feeding and insertion
polarity are not excluded. Cofitness with other pathway genes does not establish
direct physical partners or a resolved catalytic route. The homoserine-bypass
argument involves another locus and is not an observed DvMF_1464 reaction.
