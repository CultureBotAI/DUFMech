Curator notes (paraphrase):
Figure 4C compares D. alaskensis G20, JK00771 (Dde_3007::Tn5), mutant with
empty pMO9075, and mutant with pJK2 carrying Dde_3007. Mutant/empty-vector
growth is deficient; pJK2 restores visible growth. Figure 4D shows nutritional
rescue by homocysteine. This is not a biochemical conversion measurement.
Important source discrepancy: plate images say MOLS4; their caption says LS4D.
Both recipes occur in methods, but the exact plate formulation cannot be chosen
from this material. General culture conditions are anaerobic 30 C; plate assay
duration and homocysteine concentration remain unverified. Figure 4B's separate
pooled assay uses 1 uM methionine, not the 2018 study's millimolar doses.
pJK2 uses a revised start at genomic position 2993462 and was sequence-verified
by the authors. It must not be described as the unchanged 421-residue reference.
Supplement Table S1 and Data Set S1 returned HTML from PMC and HTTP 403 from
ASM; exact insertion position and complete plasmid junctions were not inspected.
The proposed O-phosphohomoserine route in Figure 4E is not an observed reaction.
Later work questions homoserine participation; this does not negate the plate
phenotype. No raw growth or fitness data were reanalyzed.
