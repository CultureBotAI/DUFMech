Curator notes: DBP1 is delta-pdaD delta-bpsA::pdaD delta-pyrF, constructed
from DAD by pdaD replacement at bpsA, not a clean single deletion in KU216.
KU216 is delta-pyrF. Figure 6 compares these strains in ASW-YT-S0
(2 g/L elemental sulfur), 85 or 93 C, OD660 over time, triplicate SD.
At 93 C DBP1 fails to show the parental OD increase; 1 mM added
N4-bis(aminopropyl)spermidine gives a delayed, lower recovery, not full rescue.
At 85 C a lag delay converges toward a similar final OD. This endpoint is not
viability, a direct transport assay or isolated PF01861 function. No bpsA
genetic complementation of this endpoint was established in the inspected study.
The marker relocation and upstream pdaD function qualify causal attribution.
2014 polyamine-isomer reassignment also cautions against inheriting older labels.
