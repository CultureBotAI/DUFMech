Curator notes: 100 uM Tk-BpsA with 1 mM dcSAM, no acceptor, 50 mM PIPES
pH 7.6, 80 C, 30 min, SDS/PAGE and trypsin LC-MS/MS gives a terminal peptide
mass of 2477.099 versus 2362.981 Da, compatible with two aminopropyl additions.
