Curator notes: pET28a N-terminal His6 TK1691 from KOD1, expressed in
BL21-CodonPlus(DE3)-RIL, heat treatment and Ni-affinity purification. Standard
HPLC reactions: 200 ul, 50 mM PIPES-NaOH pH 7.5, 80 C, 0.5 mM dcSAM,
1 mM acceptor unless varied for initial-rate fits. Spermidine and the mono-
aminopropylated acceptor yield the corresponding mono- and bis-branched products.
Table 3 reports WT kcat 0.12 +/-0.02 and 0.049 +/-0.005 s^-1, respectively;
D159A and D160A products are not detected in both assays. Triplicate SD is
reported; numerical detection limits and matched folding controls are not given.
These are net preparation effects, not observations of an elementary relay.

Parallel reciprocal plots and structures motivated the earlier Asp159 carrier
model, but the proposed ester intermediate had not been identified. The 2019
study changes that interpretation. C91A/C94A retains dimerization and activity,
so iron occupancy alone is not an essential-catalytic-metal argument. T350Y
prose says both efficiencies decrease, whereas Table 3 increases the spermidine
value; that disputed endpoint is not adopted. Full raw data and figure images
were not independently reanalyzed.
