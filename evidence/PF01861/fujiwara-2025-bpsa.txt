Curator notes: KU216 delta-pyrF, historical DBP1, and separately constructed
KPS delta-bpsA::Pc-speE delta-pyrF are compared; KPS is not bpsA complementation
of DBP1. In ASW-YT plus 0.5% sodium pyruvate at 93 C, DBP1 shows no OD660
increase while KPS grows more slowly and to lower yield than KU216. At 85 C
KPS approaches KU216; DBP1 grows with a delay. Distinct sulfur/pyruvate assays
are not interchangeable. KPS accumulates thermospermine, not restored BCPA.

Cold protocol: 17 h ASW-YT-S at 85 C, then 24 h ASW-AA-S at 85 C; four
cycles of 30 min ice bath and 30 min at 85 C. Plate CFU relative to each
strain's untreated baseline: after two cycles DBP1 is near zero and KPS about
50%; KU216 remains about 80% through four cycles. KPS collapses after the
third cycle. Figure 6 accessibility text reverses this ordering; plotted curves,
legend and Results agree and take precedence. CFU does not establish a direct
membrane, transcription or DNA-protection mechanism; precise replicate details
and raw count uncertainty remain open.
