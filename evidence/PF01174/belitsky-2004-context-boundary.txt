Minimal quotation: "conditional auxotroph"
This earlier study used Bacillus subtilis SMY, not the strain-168 reference.
BB2256 carries a 135-bp pdxT deletion replaced by a ble cassette. Its growth
depends on medium ammonium; 60/120 mM NH4Cl rescue relative to 1 mM in
Figure 2. Figure 4 compares C-terminal His6-PdxT/PdxS preparations at room
temperature, pH 8.2, using an APAD/glutamate-dehydrogenase coupled assay.
These are distinct from the later untagged, 37 C, pH-8.0 preparations.
The pBB1181 caption describes a penultimate Gly-to-Ser change, incompatible
with the current strain-168 reference's penultimate Leu if numbering is assumed
identical. pBB1252 is separately amplified from SMY genomic DNA and yields
the tagged expression derivatives; the pBB1181 mutation is not assigned to it.
The supplied reverse primer matches the reference's C-terminal region locally,
but complete SMY sequences and the caption discrepancy remain unresolved.
No SMY edge is added in this batch. The paper did not identify the synthase
product, so its physical association alone is not used to establish PLP formation.
