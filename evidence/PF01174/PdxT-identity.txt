P37528/PDXT_BACSU is the 196-aa Bacillus subtilis strain-168 reference,
sequence version 1, entry version 158. UniProt and InterPro sequences match
exactly; MD5 c26e28a76c39a7911775f8ce619ecc0f. Names pdxT and yaaE refer to
this protein; YaaD/PdxS is the separate synthase partner, not PF01174.
P37528 Pfam:PF01174 interval: 6-188, inclusive.
Current SNO integrates with IPR002161; the frozen previous-name snapshot
retains UPF0030. Imported description is preserved despite later experiments.
Raschle 2005 cloned strain-168 genomic yaaE and reports construct sequencing
and N-terminal sequencing of purified protein. The forward primer encodes
MLTIGVL; the stop-containing reverse primer matches the EYKQKALV terminus.
Untagged YaaE is distinguished from the C-terminal His6 version used for
pull-out assays. The reported peptide PMFGTCAGLIILAK matches residues 74-87,
including C79 inside PF01174. Complete historical clones are not independently
resequenced. Belitsky 2004 uses strain SMY and different His6 preparations;
current strain-168 mapping is not proof of exact SMY clone identity.
