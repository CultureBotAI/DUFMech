Figure5: purified human238-510 includes PF01936; it is NOT the352-500
crystallization construct. WT protein with U30 and Mn yields shorter labeled
RNA products. The paired5-prime/3-prime label patterns support internal
cleavage without identifying physiological targets or an atomic catalytic
trajectory. GST was removed. Assay1uM protein,10pmol unlabeled plus0.1pmol
labeled U30 in10uL,20mM HEPES pH7.5,150mM NaCl,10%glycerol,1mM DTT,
3mM MnCl2 (or MgCl2 in5B),room temperature,up to60min. No-protein and
no-added-metal lanes are shown. No-added-metal retains partial activity;
do not say metal-free catalysis is absolutely absent. WT and mutant purity
appear in5A. D426A/D427A jointly lowers5-prime-U30 processing in5C, but
residual products remain; this is not complete abolition or direct proof
of a particular metal/water coordination scheme. Protein folding is not
independently excluded as an alternative mechanism of the mutation effect.

Figure3: lambdaN-HA tethering in HeLa reduces RL-5BoxB RNA with full MARF1
or its1-1563 delta-C-terminal construct versus LacZ. Removing NYN raises
RNA relative to WT but does not restore every readout fully to LacZ.
Figure3C checks fusion-protein expression. Northern RNA is normalized to
firefly control; luciferase activity is a separate readout, not proof of
translation repression independent of RNA loss. Measurements at24h,
minimum3 experiments. Artificial tethering is not native target recognition.

Figure6A-C: WT N-terminal fragment lowers reporter RNA versus LacZ;
NYN deletion and D426A/D427A each raise RNA relative to that WT fragment.
Do not relabel these as full-length mutant tests. Expression controls are
shown; the exact deletion junction is not sequence-verified here.
Figure6D-G: full MARF1 and delta-C-terminal construct lower RNA from the
internally polyadenylated A114-N40-HHR reporter versus LacZ. GW182-SD lowers
luciferase without lowering RNA in this comparison. The protected internal
poly(A) context supports bypass of usual terminal deadenylation but does
not directly measure MARF1 cut sites or every downstream decay enzyme.
