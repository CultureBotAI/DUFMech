Constructs use SOURCE numbering. Local NYN mapping through PDB5YAA is
documented in identity.txt. Do not silently replace source D272 by canonical
D451 or assume a constant offset throughout every MARF1 isoform.
Functional158-1381 was expressed in Sf9, affinity/size-exclusion purified;
shorter158-320/158-407/158-690 constructs were expressed in E.coli BL21(DE3)
with cleaved His6-SUMO tags. Expression-host differences are a potential
alternative in comparisons of these preparations, not proof of LOTUS-only
chemistry. Figure1F158-690 gives much less RNA cleavage than158-1381 at
matched0.1/0.5uM. Purified isolated NYN is therefore not interchangeable
with the active multidomain mouse preparation or human238-510 assays.

1mM MgCl2,37C. Standard5min; dose0-1uM in1B,timecourse0.05uM in1C,

Figure2D: each D178A,D215A,D246A,D272A lowers H49 cleavage relative to WT.
The panel lacks an explicit enzyme concentration, so none is invented.
S5 gel confirms purified variant preparations, not identical folding.
S5 printed caption calls the gel C while the visible panel is B; Figure2
Results also points to2C where the actual activity panel is2D. Cite the
visible gel location, not an unexamined caption label.

Figure3C: in Marf1GT/GT fully-grown GV oocytes, microinjected WT Marf1
mRNA lowers Ppp2cb steady-state RNA versus untreated gene-trap cells;
D272A mRNA does not reproduce that reduction. Supplement p6: approximately
10pL at500ng/uL per oocyte,40h culture with5uM milrinone to retain GV arrest.
Three qPCR sample sets; RNA-seq has four80-oocyte sets. Restrict the graph
to the validated Ppp2cb readout. Decreased RNA abundance is not direct proof
that MARF1 cleaves that transcript in cells; the paper itself leaves direct
targets unresolved and does not completely exclude transcriptional effects.

Figures4,S6: B6DBA2-background D272A knockin has two additional synonymous
marker mutations. S6 sequencing supports the engineered allele. Female
homozygotes produce no offspring in the mating assay and ovulated oocytes
retain GV-stage features rather than WT mature features. Main Figure4C says
8months; supplemental methods specify8-10months,three females/genotype
starting6weeks,proven-fertile B6D2F1 males. Compound GT/D272A females share
the phenotype, reducing but not eliminating genetic-background/off-target
alternatives. Ovulation and follicle development are not reported abolished.
Mutant MARF1 protein abundance increases despite unchanged Marf1 RNA;
do not model a simple protein-abundance-loss mechanism.

Figure5A: Line1 and Iap RNA levels rise in knockin GV oocytes versus WT.
Figure5B: gammaH2AX-positive focus counts rise; this is a DNA-damage marker,
not a direct count of transposition events or chemically measured DNA breaks.
These observations are parallel genotype effects, not a demonstrated chain
from LINE1 mobilization through damage to meiotic arrest and infertility.
TUT4/7 transcript-set overlap is not a demonstrated ordered catalytic pathway.
No raw RNA-seq reanalysis, clone resequencing, or experimental replication.
