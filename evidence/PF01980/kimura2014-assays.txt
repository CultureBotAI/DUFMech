S1. Native RNA deletion phenotype (Figure2A/B, PDF6/p9355)
Quote: "no m6t6A-containing tetramer was detected"
Deletion screen: OCR36 removes23ORFs, so that initial result alone is not
gene-specific. Ten individual uncharacterized-gene deletions were examined;
the yaeB/trmO deletion lacked the total-RNA m/z427 peak. The graph uses the
individual-deletion comparison, not the large deletion as isolated attribution.
For isolated Thr1(GGU) and Thr3(GGU), RNaseT1/LCMS detects m6t6A-containing
Um6t6AAGp in WT but not delta-yaeB; ct6A-containing Uct6AAGp appears instead,
with a small t6A fraction. Doubly negative ions742.11,726.11,735.10 respectively.
Two RNA substrates are distinct observations, not independent gene knockouts.
ct6A can hydrolyze to t6A during handling; no quantitative in-cell flux or direct
TcdA activation by TrmO deletion is inferred. General growth LB37Covernight.
RNA isolated with RCC oligo-DNA probe purification; T1 digest1pmolRNA/50U,
20mM ammonium acetate pH5.3,37C30min. Tetramer identification is not atomistic
mechanism or isolated-PF01980 necessity. Figure3G provides separate rescue.

S4. Native mixed-modification substrate (Figure3D, PDF7/p9356)
Quote: "m6t6A was formed in response to a reduction in t6A"
Same methylation conditions, using isolated delta-trmO Thr3 containing both
t6A37 and ct6A37. UponTrmO addition m6t6A fraction51% versus7% in comparison,
t6A23% versus65%,ct6A26% versus27%. These are displayed normalized fractions,
not error-estimated rates or mass balances of independently calibrated species.
Critically, the asterisk-marked7% m6t6A in the no-TrmO preparation is identified
by the authors as WT-tRNA carryover on the oligo-DNA probe. It is not mutant
residual synthesis. The supported change is increased product with diminished
t6A; unchanged ct6A argues against detectable ct6A methylation under this assay.
Do not assert absolute zero ct6A turnover at every condition or native competition
kinetics. This preparation is separate from synthetic Thr3 in Figure3B.
