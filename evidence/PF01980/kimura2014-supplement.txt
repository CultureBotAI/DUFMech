T1. Reference and construct support
S5 caption explicitly names E. coli NP_414737.1 and human NP_057565.3, with
235-aa and441-aa sequences in the alignment. Visual endpoints match P28634
and Q9BU70 reference ends; no full residue-by-residue alignment to the physical
insert is established. S5 labels EcQ25/R92/D125/K136 and D194/R196. It also
shows mammalian internal insertions and shorter archaeal proteins. This is
sequence-context evidence, not proof of identical function across the alignment.
Human second-PCR primers from Table S2:
hTrmO_2ndPCR_f AGTCAGTCgctagcATGCGCGGCTTGGAGG
hTrmO_2ndPCR_r AGTCAGTCgtcgacTTAAGACCCTAGAGACACCAAGG
Biopython translation: forward coding segment startsMRGLE. Reverse binding
segment reverse-complements to CCTTGGTGTCTCTAGGGTCTTAA; its matching reading
frame endsLVSLGS*, agreeing with the current441-aa human reference tail.
Endpoint agreement supports a full-body design, not complete internal sequence,
HeLa isoform, physical insert or tag/linker verification. No truncation boundary
was inferred merely from the internal insertion shown in S5.
Table S2 explicitly labels Q25A,R92A,D125A,K136A and D194A_R196A primer pairs;
the latter supports a joint intervention, not separate single-mutant assays.
Full genomic primer alignment and mutant protein sequencing remain unfinished.

T2. RNA control construct discrepancy
Table S2 lists these two primers with IDENTICAL nucleotide strings:
Thr4_body
GCCGACTTAGCTCAGTAGGTAGAGCAACTGACTTGTAATCAGTAGGTCACCAGTTCGATTCC
Thr4_U34G_body
GCCGACTTAGCTCAGTAGGTAGAGCAACTGACTTGTAATCAGTAGGTCACCAGTTCGATTCC
The labeled U34G-only negative control therefore has unresolved sequence
documentation. Do not repair the primer silently or claim its physical sequence
was verified. Figure4's reported low U34G-control signal remains a reported
assay result, but the isolated U34G intervention is not independently confirmed.
The listed triple-mutant primer differs and reads
GCCGACTTAGCTCAGTAGGTAGAGCAACTGCCTGGTAAGCAGTAGGTCACCAGTTCGATTCC.
The A31C/U39G pair-only primer reads
GCCGACTTAGCTCAGTAGGTAGAGCAACTGCCTTGTAAGCAGTAGGTCACCAGTTCGATTCC.
Table S2 U3C_A70G labels agree with Figure4, while Results says U3-A71.
Neither this table nor the displayed assay proves a general identity rule.

T3. Homolog structures are not Ec atomic measurements
S3 labels AF0241/2NV4 (Archaeoglobus fulgidus), RPA0152/3OKX
(Rhodopseudomonas palustris) and HI0510/1XQB (Haemophilus influenzae).
AF0241/RPA0152 show AdoMet; HI0510 has an additional domain. S7 is their
best-fit superposition with positions labeled in Ec-equivalent numbering.
No E. coli or human structure is measured in these figures. The PF01980
mutation graphs therefore retain UNRESOLVED directness. A ligand contact in
a homolog is not proof of Ec methyl transfer or a reaction transition state.

T4. Downstream decoding hypothesis, not an observed TrmO reaction
S9 caption quote: "The N6-methyl group was added to the crystal structure coordinates"
Main Discussion quote: "might have a function in stabilizing the anticodon"
Main source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC4132733/fullTextXML
The model modifies PDB1XMO: T. thermophilus30S, mRNA and tRNALys anticodon
stem-loop recognizing AAG, not the EcThr3/ACC reporter substrate or a TrmO-RNA
cocrystal. S9B displays methyl-carbon distances3.3A to U36O4 and3.2A to A38N6.
The authors propose favorable van der Waals interactions, stronger stacking
and less U36 fluctuation. No measured contact energy, RNA dynamics or decoding
rate follows from these modeled distances. S9C's ct6A model is separate and
does not turn the two independently normalized reporters into matched kinetics.
The graph retains only the hypothesized m6t6A-dependent stabilization step as
COMPUTATIONAL/HYPOTHESIZED, with unresolved directness and explicit scaffold
transfer limits; it is not joined to observed biochemical edges as one pathway.

T5. Historical tsaA mapping is not mutation-level proof
Supplementary Result uses the earlier75% cotransduction frequency and Wu
distance formula to place tsaA9-10kb left of yafC near proS/yaeB/rcsF/metQ.
The selection of yaeB as the sole then-unknown gene is a candidate argument,
not sequencing of the historical tsaA lesion. No exact nucleotide or amino-acid
change is reported in this supplemental paragraph. Reassess the original1998
PMID9537379 experiments before adding their separate physiological claims.
