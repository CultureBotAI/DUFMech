Flp-In 293 T-Rex reporters express RFP-SQS or RFP-VAMP2 from GFP-2A-RFP
cassettes, and OPRK1-GFP or TRAM2-GFP from GFP-2A-RFP configurations with
the substrate fused to GFP. RFP is mCherry. Ratios are RFP:GFP for tail-anchored
reporters and GFP:RFP for multipass reporters, not interchangeable axes.
Lentiviral pHAGE2/CMV constructs express WT or mutant EMC3-TagBFP-3xFLAG;
analysis selects BFP-positive cells. This is exogenous variant expression, not
an endogenous point-mutant knock-in or an EMC3-null complementation experiment.
R31/R180 and first-loop mutant effects were tested in at least three independent
replicates in all cell lines; plotted histograms are representative distributions,
not quantitative insertion rates or statistical tests of exact fold changes.

R31A and R180A, and joint M101S/M106S/M110S/M111S (MCyt-1-S), reduce SQS
and OPRK1 ratios relative to matched WT EMC3. VAMP2 and TRAM2 controls show
little/no corresponding shift. R31E and R180E also reduce both client ratios.
Replacing E with K at R180 improves SQS and OPRK1 relative to E, but does not
establish complete restoration for every client. At R31, K improves SQS relative
to E, but OPRK1 remains impaired; no R31K OPRK1 rescue edge is curated.
S13A FLAG-IP at 72 h recovers EMC2/EMC4 with variants, limiting a gross assembly
loss explanation. It does not prove identical complex abundance, folding,
stoichiometry or kinetics. F173A and MCyt-2-S (five methionines at 198/199/
203/208/210) show no appreciable reporter defect in the displayed assays.
No individual effect is assigned to each methionine within the fourfold mutant.

Reduced fluorescence is a substrate stability/abundance readout consistent with
impaired biogenesis, not a direct observation of insertion intermediates. The
loop-capture and membrane-thinning model is not turned into observed edges.
S9 uses Sec61beta F85-BpA, CaM, EGTA and detergent-solubilized EMC; tag-dependent
crosslink shifts identify EMC3 contact but not translocation. Supplement p4 calls
an amber codon UGA, an internal terminology inconsistency (amber is UAG);
historical construct sequence was not independently recovered. No crosslink
edge is required to infer the distinct cellular perturbation effects.
