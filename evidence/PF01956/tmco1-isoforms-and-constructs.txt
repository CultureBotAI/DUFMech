PF01956 TMCO1 identity and construct assessment
Accessed 2026-10-12. Curator-authored factual notes; not a primary-source transcript.

Identity sources:
https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UM00.json
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/api/entry/pfam/PF01956/protein/uniprot/Q9UM00/
Human TMCO1 Q9UM00 has sequence version 2, 239 residues, last sequence update
2019-04-10. Current displayed isoform 1 is Q9UM00-3. Isoform 3, Q9UM00-1,
lacks canonical residues 1-51 and is therefore 188 residues long. Isoform 2
Q9UM00-2 instead lacks residues 115-133. Names and accession suffixes are not
interchangeable. PF01956 maps canonical residues 59-216; the arithmetic interval
in the shorter Q9UM00-1 sequence is 8-165, not a separately run profile scan.
The historical D140 label would map to canonical D191 under this exact deletion
mapping. UniProt annotates D191A/E using PMID27212239. This database evidence
does not independently verify the historical plasmid sequence or channel assay.

McGilvray2020 DOI10.7554/eLife.56889, PMID32820719:
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7505659/fullTextXML
The HEK293 Flp-In T-REx TMCO1 knockout derives from Anghel2017. Nicalin and
CCDC47 deletions were made in parental and TMCO1-knockout backgrounds, with
clonal isolation, genomic sequencing and immunoblots reported. These are
whole-gene perturbations, not isolated-domain or residue tests. Endogenous
EAAT1 abundance experiments are distinct from tagged TMCO1 affinity-purification
and structural preparations. Stable overexpression lines are described, but no
EAAT1 reintroduction-rescue endpoint is shown in the selected Figure5 experiments.

Sundaram2022 DOI10.1038/s41586-022-05330-8, PMID36261522:
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9630114/fullTextXML
The same source lineage supplies the knockout backgrounds. Reporter tags and
N/C-terminal orientation vary by assay; whole-cell knockout, microsome-derived
cell-free reactions and siRNA depletion are not interchangeable. Constructs
were DNA-sequence checked by the authors; raw clone sequences were not inspected
here. Current Q9UM00 mapping grounds the gene/family, not the exact historical
isoform expressed in every clone. No new residue-level edge is assigned.

Anghel2017 DOI10.1016/j.celrep.2017.12.006, PMID29281821:
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5868721/fullTextXML
Inspected main TMCO1/ribosome interaction and topology/co-IP methods. Purified
TMCO1 pelleting with ribosomes and cell/microsome co-IP are binding evidence,
not by themselves insertion or ion-conduction effects. Supplementary construct
and knockout details were not obtained: the PMC PDF endpoint returned HTML.

Access and coverage limits:
Wang2016 PMID27212239 abstract and CSU postprint landing metadata were inspected.
https://engagedscholarship.csuohio.edu/scichem_facpub/535/
The CSU PDF and publisher PDF returned HTTP403; publisher full-text endpoints
failed. No challenge was bypassed and no inaccessible figures were treated as
read. Channel selectivity, tetramerization, D140 assays and mouse physiology
remain uncurated pending direct inspection. The 2020 authors' proposal that
calcium phenotypes reflect client-biogenesis defects is not a demonstrated
refutation of the 2016 channel model. Both possibilities remain distinguishable.
Archaeal Ylp1 PMID26256539 remains a lead: PubMed was read, PMC returned a
challenge and the Europe PMC XML endpoint returned HTTP500. This is an access
limit, not evidence that no archaeal causal function exists.
