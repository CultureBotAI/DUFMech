Human EMC purified from EMC5-3xFLAG T-Rex HEK293 cells supports SQS reporter
insertion in reconstituted vesicles. Figure4C uses native rabbit reticulocyte
lysate substrate; Figure4D uses isolated PURE-system SQS-calmodulin complex.
Keep these two substrate preparations separate. Empty liposomes, no-membrane
reactions, WT and EMC6-deficient microsomes are controls; VAMP2 is poorly
inserted by EMC vesicles but efficiently by WT/EMC6-deficient microsomes.
The readout is a C-terminal 3F4-tagged, proteinase-K-protected fragment,
immunoprecipitated and detected by autoradiography, not SQS enzyme activity.
Each Figure4 graph summarizes four experiments, mean +/- SD. EMC titration
changes protein at constant lipid, and about one-third of reconstituted EMC has
the productive orientation. Do not report equal total EMC as equal active EMC.

Reconstitution uses deoxy-BigCHAP and bovine PC:PE:rhodamine-PE at mass
8:1.9:0.1, BioBeads, 18 h at 4 C. Figure assays use fresh vesicles or once
freeze-thawed cholesterol-containing vesicles; assignment to each displayed
lane is not specified. Uncontrolled freeze-thaw increases background insertion.
Insertion: 5.5 ul isolated CaM-SQS, 4 ul vesicles or 2 ul microsomes in 10 ul;
0.5 mM EGTA starts release, 32 C for 20 min; 0.5 mg/ml proteinase K, 1 h on
ice, then PMSF quench and 3F4 fragment IP. This explicit recipe applies to
the defined-complex assay; the general RRL targeting protocol specifies 15 min.
The precise Figure4C timing should not be silently equated to Figure4D.

S19 reports reduced protected product after trypsin pretreatment of EMC vesicles.
The authors refer to native-microsome shaving; a specific trypsin dose/duration
was not established from the inspected Methods. The effect is not an EMC3-only
cleavage experiment and does not identify the indispensable subunit or residue.
Whole-complex sufficiency in this assay does not mean EMC3 alone suffices,
nor that every PF01956 member inserts every membrane protein.
