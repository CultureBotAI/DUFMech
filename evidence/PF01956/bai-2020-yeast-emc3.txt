BY4741 WT, EMC3 deletion and K26L are separate cellular comparisons. Spot assays
start with equal OD, 7 ul of tenfold serial dilutions, YPD, 30 or 37 C for two
days. Deletion and K26L impair colony growth at 37 C; 30 C is permissive.
Do not infer universal essentiality, direct lethality or fitted liquid growth rates.
The K26L construction used pFA6a-His3 in BY4741; exact historical junctions and
clone sequence were not independently verified. EMC structural purification
starts from W303-1a Emc5-3xFLAG, a distinct background from the growth assays.

In EMC3-deleted cells, C-terminal EGFP fusions of Fet3, Mrh1, Pdr5, Ftr1, Hxt3,
Ena1, Dip5, Fet4 and Pma1 have reduced membrane-associated signal compared
with WT. The readout combines abundance and localization; loss of signal does
not directly measure insertion, protein folding or flux. Hxt3 mRNA falls about
half in the paired ACT1-normalized qPCR; therefore its protein phenotype is not
assigned exclusively to a post-translational mechanism. Other mRNAs need not
be numerically identical to WT and RNA abundance is not transcription rate.
Images are equally adjusted; water-washed cells are imaged in water at room
temperature. The precise culture temperature/time for those image panels is
not explicitly resolved from the Methods and is not assumed to be 37 C.
Figures1a-e are reported repeated three times with similar results; qPCR plots
show independent-experiment dots and mean +/- SD.

K26L reduces membrane-associated Mrh1-EGFP and Fet3-EGFP signals. An intact
mutant EMC can be purified (Figure3g), arguing against gross complex collapse
but not proving equal cellular abundance or a specific ionic mechanism. Figure3e-g
is reported repeated three times. No reintroduction rescue is shown for these
selected assays; the observed changes are retained without a causal growth-to-
localization or localization-to-growth chain. A single K-to-L change cannot
separate charge removal from hydrophobicity or all structural effects.
The pooled membrane proteomics, other EMC-subunit mutants, predicted client
pocket and client orientation model are not additional EMC3-specific causal
steps here. Supplementary raw tables and gels remain follow-up.
