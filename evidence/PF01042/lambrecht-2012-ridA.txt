The following is a curator's factual assay ledger, not reproduced full text.

Salmonella YjgF/RidA and IlvA carried C-terminal His6 tags. Proteins and
QuikChange variants used pET20b+ in BL21AI, 0.2% arabinose induction,
9.5 h at 37 C, nickel affinity purification, and dialysis through
HEPES/EGTA, HEPES, and HEPES/20% glycerol. Detailed lineage cites the 2010
purification paper. Complete plasmid sequences were not reconstructed.

Figure 2B: adding 1.6 uM RidA to 0.9 uM IlvA increases 2-ketobutyrate
formation across the threonine series at pH 9.5. Assays contain 10 uM PLP,
50 mM each TAPS/CHES/CAPS, 200 uL, 22 C; initial rates use 30-60 s at
pH above 9.0, not the first 30 s. Protein concentrations are monomer-based.
Two replicates with SE; representative of more than five independent
experiments. Separate TLC and mass spectrometry support product identity,
not additional intermediates directly observed in this kinetic trace.

Figure 4B: 10 mM threonine, 0.9 uM IlvA, 10 uM PLP, 1.6 uM RidA when
present, 50 mM each TAPS/CHES/CAPS pH 9.5. At 5 mM ferricyanide and 2 h,
RidA decreases the amount of ferricyanide reduced and increases the
2-ketobutyrate yield. A420 measures ferricyanide and DNPH derivatization
measures product, since ferricyanide interferes at A230. Two replicates
with SD, representative of four independent endpoint experiments. Exact
endpoint incubation temperature is not specified in that dedicated method;
the kinetic assay's 22 C and TLC's 37 C are not silently transferred.
Ferricyanide trapping is evidence for accessible reactive intermediates,
not direct structural observation of enamine hydrolysis or proton transfer.

Figure 6: 15 mM threonine, 0.9 uM IlvA, 1.6 uM WT or variant RidA.
Adopted gray bars use 50 mM CHES pH 9.5; separate white bars use HEPES
pH 8.0. General kinetic conditions supply PLP, 200 uL and 22 C as above.
Y17F, R105A, E120K and E120A each reduce the product-rate enhancement
relative to WT; R105A has the lowest enhancement. None abolishes all
activity. Three replicates with SD; representative of three independent
experiments. Structural water positioning and absence of an essential
acid/base catalyst are interpretations, not directly observed microsteps.

Figure 7: serine replaces threonine in the coupled IlvA assay at pH 9.5.
RidA increases pyruvate production rate across the plotted serine series,
with the same monomer doses, PLP, mixed buffer, volume and kinetic timing.
Two replicates with SE; representative of three independent experiments.
No precise rate was digitized from the image. RidA alone is not asserted
to dehydrate threonine or serine. Homolog tests and cellular complementation
are additional contexts, not independently mapped graphs in this curation.
