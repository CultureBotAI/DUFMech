Methods and Discussion read; Figure 1 visually inspected.
Minimal quotation: "dramatically decreased the negative effect"

Figure 1B adopts only the Salmonella RidA comparison. Reaction components:
50 mM potassium phosphate pH 9.0, 50 uM PLP, 12.5 mM 2-ketoglutarate,
0.75 ug IlvE, 10 ug IlvA, 50 mM serine and 16 ug RidA where indicated.
Methods specify these mixtures preincubated 2 h at 37 C, then 5 mM
[U-14C]isoleucine (20 nCi), followed by 15 min at 37 C. TLC separates
isoleucine and 2-keto-3-methylvalerate (2KMV); specific activity is reported
per mg IlvE. RidA increases retained activity relative to the IlvA/serine
mixture lacking RidA. The uninhibited comparator omits IlvA/serine.
Means and ranges of two replicates; independently repeated at least twice.
Figure 1A tests omitted components, threonine substitution and pyruvate.

Important order ambiguity: Figure 1B's caption describes adding RidA to
IlvE that had been preincubated, whereas Methods list RidA among components
present for preincubation and Results interpret prevention. The graph
retains the endpoint comparison, not proof of reversal of established damage.
The 3-chloroalanine experiment uses a different nonradioactive method and
is not an identical control reaction. Its lack of RidA protection limits
generalization to every route of IlvE inhibition.

RidA/IlvA purification cites earlier procedures. IlvE is C-terminally
His6-tagged from pET28b(+) in SB300A1-derived strains. Complete clones and
preincubation-order reconciliation remain open. The 317-Da adduct and
proposed nucleophilic site are separate evidence; raw MS was not reanalyzed,
and the paper did not identify the modified peptide. Other homologs and
cellular phenotypes require separate curation.
