Verbatim excerpt: "S140 did not require either AroAAs or PABA for growth"

S140 is M. maripaludis S2 with a 0.5kb internal MMP0006/aroB' segment
replaced by a 1.3kb pac cassette. Reported EcoRI Southern fragment changes
3.8kb to4.6kb. Growth resembled wild type in McNA minimal medium with
acetate. Extract shikimate/dihydroshikimate labeling was reported as96%,
versus about90% wild type. Fractional labeling is not absolute flux.
The extract assay uses labeled glucose6P, homoserine, NAD/NADP,30min37C
under argon and derivative GC-MS. No essentiality edge is warranted in
this tested context; neither universal dispensability nor an identified
replacement enzyme is established. AroA' deletion/rescue strains S170/S176
target MMP0686, not PF01959 MMP0006. Do not transfer their positive
phenotypes to the AroB' record. No invented null-effect graph predicate.
