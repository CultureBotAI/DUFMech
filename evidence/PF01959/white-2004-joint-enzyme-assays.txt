Source qualifications, not silently corrected:
1. Methods uses 3,4-dihydroxybenzoic acid; Table2 uses 4,5-dihydroxybenzoic
   acid. Reversing ring numbering maps these locants onto one another;
   the wording difference is not evidence of distinct chemical products.
2. Rows7-11 are described as in air; the same footnote ends with all
   samples under argon. Oxygen conditions cannot be assigned confidently.
3. Curator calculation: 125ul x 1.6mM ASA = 200nmol; DKFP = 275nmol.
   The reported 330nmol exceeds both inputs for the proposed 1:1 pathway.
   Concentrations, units or product quantitation require reconciliation.
4. Rows1-3 and7-11 differ in buffer and readout; do not derive a clean
   cofactor fold-stimulation or isolated-MJ1249 kinetics across them.

Graphs retain qualitative joint-reaction product observations, not validated
stoichiometry. Figure3 assigns oxidative deamination and cyclization to
MJ1249; neither transient keto intermediate nor a residue-specific sequence
is directly established by these assays. Cell-extract downstream shikimate
reactions and partner MJ0400 chemistry are not separate PF01959 edges.
