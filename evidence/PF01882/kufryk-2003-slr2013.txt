Curator's factual notes, not a transcription:
The PCC 6803 T192H parent carries the D2 mutation in psbDI and lacks psbDII.
The Rg2 suppressor retains those changes and has slr2013 Y294Stop
(UAU to UAA). Genomic-fragment mapping and PCR-amplified mutant-region
transfer restored photoautotrophic growth to T192H. The mapped region
includes slr2011/slr2012/slr2013/sll1924; possible cotranscription is noted.
This is not an independently recreated single-base allele with reciprocal
full-length Slr2013 rescue, nor a measured purified-domain reaction.

PS I-retaining cells were grown in BG-11 at 30 C, 40 umol photons m^-2 s^-1,
120 rpm in liquid culture; photomixotrophic conditions include 5 mM glucose.
Selection of Rg2 used glucose-free BG-11 plates. Solid medium includes
1.5% agar, 0.3% sodium thiosulfate and 10 mM TES-NaOH pH 8.2.
The parental T192H maintenance plates include kanamycin, spectinomycin and
atrazine; atrazine is not silently transferred to the oxygen assay.
Table 2 reports means +/- SD from three independent experiments:
                        photoautotrophic doubling (h)   O2 evolution
wild type                       12.8 +/- 0.4             256 +/- 28
T192H                           no growth                 0
Rg2                             13.3 +/- 0.5             275 +/- 35
T192T/Y294Stop                   13.4 +/- 0.7             248 +/- 32
O2 units: umol O2 per mg chlorophyll per hour, not per cell or per protein.
O2 assay: intact cells, Clark electrode, 5 ug chlorophyll/mL, 0.1 mM
2,5-dimethyl-p-benzoquinone and 0.5 mM potassium ferricyanide; illumination
above 580 nm at 1 mmol photons m^-2 s^-1. Assay temperature is not explicitly
specified in this method; the growth temperature is not substituted.

Figure 1: 77 K fluorescence, 435-nm excitation, normalized at 725 nm;
Rg2 restores the 695-nm feature absent in T192H. This supports the cellular
PS II interpretation, not a direct Slr2013 folding or protease mechanism.
Figure 7 shows the 435-residue Slr2013 reference and Y294 stop position.
An expected 293-residue product is inferred from the codon, not verified by
a direct measurement of mutant protein length or abundance here.
WT-D2 restoration in Rg2 gives T192T/Y294Stop with near-WT growth/O2.
The slr2013 mutant PCR did not restore photoautotrophy to E69Q or
V247M/A249T D2 mutants. The suppressor is condition-specific.
Deletion constructs failed complete segregation; this is not a measured
viable null-mutant phenotype or proof of a specific essential chemistry.
PS I-less fluorescence/EPR comparisons also change the D2 background and
are not assigned solely to Slr2013. Chaperone and protease roles remain
alternative interpretations. Raw traces, microscopy and fits were not reanalyzed.
