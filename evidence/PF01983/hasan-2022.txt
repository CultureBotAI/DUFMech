Construct and assay context
Synthetic or previous-study constructs carry N-terminal His6 tags; proteins
were produced in E.coli BL21(DE3) or LOBSTR-BL21 (individual host allocation
not resolved), induced at OD0.7 with1mM IPTG, then18C for18h. Ni-NTA/PD10
purification is described; no tag cleavage is assumed. Mutations use native,
not tagged, numbering. Methods report Sanger verification of constructed
plasmids; that statement is not independent access to all clone sequences.
The standard coupled assay has Mjan-CofD as the partner. In50uL:
100mM HEPES pH7.4,2mM GTP,2mM MgCl2,34uM CofD,26uM CofC, and0.5mM EACH
competing substrate3-PG,PEP,2-PL. Fo is printed as0.14nM in s4-5; retain the
unresolved unit rather than silently substitute a plausible concentration.
No assay temperature is supplied in this subsection; do not import37C from
Bashiri2019. Products are measured by LC-MS extracted-ion chromatogram AUC,
normalized relative formation over the linear0-20min interval, n=3 biological
replicates with SD. These fractions are not absolute rates, turnover numbers,
binding affinities or direct measurements of CofC's activated intermediates.

Graph mrhiz-mutant-product-fractions-2022, Figure3B
WT pFS03 is the comparison, with Mjan-CofD held as partner. Individual S162A
(pMH25), S162E(pMH22), S162G(pMH24), S162Y(pMH23), C95L(pMH66), M91L(pMH77)
and combined S162G/C95L(pMH68) lower the measured3PG-derived product fraction.
These are distinct tested interventions, not a chronological causal chain.
Each input means replacement of WT in the same coupled reaction.
The visually inspected WT profile is mostly3PG; the alternatives favor2PL,
with differing PEP fractions. S162G still has a visible3PG contribution, so
do not describe every S162 replacement as absolute loss. No bar values were
digitized. The combined S162G/C95L profile has little visible3PG and sizable
2PL/PEP components; no synergy statistic or absolute zero is inferred.
Controls/comparators: S162T(pMH26), C95A(pMH67), M91A(pMH76), H145A(pMH74),
H145T(pMH75) retain predominantly3PG-derived relative profiles. Retained
fractions do not prove unchanged absolute catalytic activity. See identity
ledger: paper S162 is E5ASS2 S167; M91/C95/H145 likewise have+5 offsets.
The paper's model-based binding/pocket explanations are not established by
these coupled product profiles. Folding, abundance and partner coupling are
not independently excluded as explanations for mutation effects.

Graph msmeg-double-mutant-product-fractions-2022, Figure3C
WT pMH10/YP_886732.1 versus G169S/T152H pMH80, with Mjan-CofD and equal
competing substrates. The double mutant yields a reported19.6%3PG-derived
fraction; WT has no visible3PG bar. This is not19.6% total conversion.
Comparators G169S(pMH32), L98C(pMH70), G169S/L98C(pMH69), T152H(pMH82)
do not acquire the double mutant's3PG output. No unperformed complementation,
direct binding assay, evolutionary reconstruction or mutant structure is
invented. Triple G169S/T152H/L98C(pMH81) was reported insoluble; it is NOT a
purified catalytic negative or evidence that L98C specifically blocks3PG.

Graphs mrhiz-partner-product-fractions-2022 and b3-partner-product-fractions-2022
Figure6A/B changes ONLY the assayed CofD homolog while retaining the specified
CofC protein and general standard reaction. Mjan-CofD is replaced with the
cognate Mrhiz-CofD(pFS02) or B3-CofD(pMH60), respectively. Both comparisons
increase the relative3PG-derived product fraction and decrease the2PL- and
PEP-derived fractions. Residual non3PG products are traces, not assumed zero.
B3 CofC is pMH43/KQH55_09515; do not ground it as sequence-identical E5ASS2.
Different WT baselines in Figures3 and6 are separate experiments; do not pool
their relative percentages into a single replicated quantitative estimate.

Graph msmeg-partner-product-fractions-2022, Figure6C
With Msmeg-FbiD fixed, replace Mjan-CofD(pFS04) with cognate Msmeg-FbiA(pMH59).
The2PL-derived fraction drops almost entirely while the PEP-derived fraction
increases to the dominant product. The variable is the downstream partner,
not an FbiD mutation. This experimentally demonstrates context dependence
of the coupled assay; it does not directly measure intrinsic FbiD affinity.

Unadopted atomic and transfer interpretations
Figure3D/FigureS1 are homology/docking models. S162 side-chain direct3PG
contact in that model is not established by the B3 product-bound structure,
whose discussed S162 interaction is through backbone. Crystallographic ligand
contact is not a mutant mechanism or an observed catalytic trajectory.
Figure6G's channeling cartoon is an explanatory model, not direct evidence of
a stable complex or transport of a labeled intermediate. The structural,
nanoDSF and other-family-member branches remain follow-ups. Do not transfer
the observed fractions to all PF01983 proteins or a native whole-cell pathway.
