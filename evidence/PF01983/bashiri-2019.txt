Controls, alternatives and excluded edges
Figure2 shows separate2-PL and ATP conditions. With Mtb-FbiA as partner,
2-PL does not yield the detected product in either enzyme pairing. Mtb-FbiD
does not yield the ATP/PEP signal seen with Mjan-CofC; the latter is lower
than its GTP/PEP output according to Results. No rates or substrate-affinity
rankings are inferred from these illustrative traces. Results also report
no FbiD/CofC activity without FbiA; corresponding supplement controls were
not visually recovered, so no separate necessity edge is adopted here.
EPPG is a proposed unstable activated intermediate, not directly observed
in these Figure2 reactions. Neither a stable CofC/FbiA complex, intermediate
channeling nor a partner-induced nucleotide-binding conformation was directly
demonstrated. Hasan2022 partner replacements show why the coupled2-PL
negative cannot establish universal archaeal or PF01983 substrate exclusion.
Protein-bound PEP structural contacts and the absence of observed GTP binding
are not sufficient to assign a catalytic residue intervention. The paper's
text attributes comparator2I5E to M.mazei, while Figure3's caption says
M.jannaschii; this unresolved comparator discrepancy is not normalized away.
