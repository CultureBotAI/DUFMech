Specific follow-ups, not unsupported graph edges
1. Grochowski2008 PMID18260642 / doi:10.1021/bi702475t is the imported
   family's original CofC biochemical citation. Inspect original Methods,
   constructs, nucleotide conditions and product confirmation before adding
   its2PL activation results. Database annotation is not a replacement.
2. Braga2019 PMID31469543 / doi:10.1021/acschembio.9b00605 concerns3PG-F420
   in the rhizoxinica lineage and supplies the earlier pFS03 construct.
   Its original full experiments and all exact mutant inserts remain to be
   inspected. The verified RefSeq/E5ASS2 suffix mapping does not authenticate
   the physical clone or establish the native translational start site.
3. Lopez2007 PMID17660436 / doi:10.1099/mic.0.2006/009241-0 concerns
   MSMEG_2392/FbiD, dye decolorization and F420 biosynthesis. Inspect actual
   deletion/complementation and cofactor controls before native phenotype
   or antibiotic-response causality is represented. Other native genetic
   FbiD studies and Grinter2020 PMID32430409 are not reviewed for FbiD here.
   The preprint lead was resolved to Rifat et al., PMID33077652,
   doi:10.1128/AAC.01948-20, PMCID:PMC7927868 (2020 online/2021 issue).
   Its citation and abstract identify native cofactor and susceptibility
   experiments; their controls and full biological scope are not assessed
   here. No allele-specific resistance mechanism is imported from snippets.
4. Hasan2022 additional archaeal/bacterial homologs and Figure6D-F remain
   outside the adopted member contexts. Their TableS3 entries are leads, not
   completed mapping/experimental reviews. Braga2020 redox-pathway work is
   another source, not automatically independent proof of CofC chemistry.
5. Hasan2022 prints Fo0.14nM in s4-5. Resolve with the authors' original
   assay records or an authoritative correction, not a guessed unit change.
   Obtain absolute conversion data and mutant folding/abundance controls
   before converting relative product fractions to affinity or rate claims.
6. Complete the structural/nanoDSF branch, including B3 product-bound7P97,
   TableS1 ligand contacts, TextS1/TableS5, and source-specific residue maps.
   Homology-model predictions and a static product complex do not establish
   S162 side-chain contact, ligand motion or a catalytic sequence. B3 is not
   sequence-identical to deposited reference E5ASS2 despite that mapping.
7. The2019 main supplement remains incomplete; exact synthetic inserts,
   Figure2 incubation endpoint and per-condition replicate counts need
   stronger evidence. The main-paper no-partner assertion is recorded as a
   source statement, not promoted to a separate observed requirement graph.
8. Historical MJ1117 and modern MJ0887 are distinct proteins/families.
   Revisit the original2008 CofD assay context separately rather than repair
   its locus name by assumption. Q58517/CobY/PF12804 is not PF01983 CofC.

Representational boundary

purified-pair product formations, seven separate Mrhiz mutation effects,

three partner exchanges. Only the two composite reconstitutions are marked
direct MECHANISTIC; mutation/partner effects are PHENOTYPIC with unresolved

substrates, cofactors and assay context. No family-wide physiological effect,
residue-binding mechanism, mature-cofactor reduction, channeling or isolated-
Pfam-domain sufficiency is inferred. No graph has been added to satisfy a
count when its causal evidence was missing.
