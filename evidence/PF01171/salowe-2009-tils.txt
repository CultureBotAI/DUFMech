E. coli K12 tilS from Z50870 was cloned in pET26B+, expressed as a
C-terminal His6 fusion in BL21(DE3), purified by Ni-NTA and Superdex
200. The paper reports clone sequencing and intact-protein MS.
The E. coli tRNAIle2 transcript is made by T7 transcription, purified
with phenol/chloroform and Superdex 75, and checked by PAGE and MS.

Preparative modification: 75 ul, 50 mM TAPS pH 8.5, 3 mM MgCl2,
0.5 mM TCEP, 0.1 mg/ml BSA, 20 uM tRNAIle2, 100 uM ATP,
500 nM TilS, 37 C for 4 h. Table 4 reaction B uses 0.1 mM lysine;
reaction H uses 20 mM ethanolamine. Control A has no amine.
Reported observed intact-RNA masses: A 24669.4, B 24795.3, H 24710.0 Da,
consistent with corresponding predicted products within reported mass error.
Figure 2 shows shifted B/H bands; residual unmodified substrate is
estimated by a subsequent radiolysine assay. Intact-RNA mass and migration
alone do not independently map the modification or every atomic step.

The reaction mixtures were frozen without further workup. For subsequent
aminoacylation they were diluted to 100 nM tRNA in 30 ul with 50 mM
HEPES pH 7.3, 10 mM Mg(OAc)2, 100 mM KCl, 0.5 mM TCEP,
0.1 mg/ml BSA, 100 uM ATP and 100 nM tritiated amino acid. Use
40 ug/ml E. coli BL21(DE3)-derived S100 fraction or, separately,
0.1 nM N-terminal FLAG-tagged B. subtilis 168 IleRS purified by
M2-FLAG/Superdex 200. Incubate 2 h at room temperature; acid SPA
capture measures coupled amino acid in tRNA. Replicate counts, error
estimates, matched carryover controls and complete clone scars remain open.

Table 5 relative endpoints (A control, B lysine, H ethanolamine):
E. coli MetRS activity in S100: 23, 0, 0 percent of tRNAMet reference;
E. coli IleRS activity in S100: 2, 100, 123 percent of B reference;
B. subtilis purified IleRS: 0, 100, 99 percent of B reference.
The respective 100% signals are 3240, 2210 and 4600 net cpm.
Columns are not one shared absolute scale. Enzyme concentrations were
chosen in the assay's linear-response range, not saturating substrate kinetics.
The graph retains B/H versus A directions, not a statistically established
23% ethanolamine improvement over lysine or a universal numerical zero.

Other amines were tested. N-alpha-methyllysine and ornithine products
instead matched the lysine product, attributed to about 0.1% lysine
contamination. These are not adopted as successful alternative substrates.
Lysine's apparent inhibition in the radiolabel assay is isotope dilution;
alternative incorporation is not proof of a dead-end inhibitor or an affinity Kd.
No in-vivo decoding, drug efficacy or family-wide substrate rule is inferred.
