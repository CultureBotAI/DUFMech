W3110 coaE was cloned into pET28b(+) through NcoI/HindIII with an added
alanine after the start codon. Production used BL21(DE3), 100 uM IPTG,
6 h at 37 C. DEAE and Q anion-exchange purification yielded an SDS-PAGE-
pure preparation; its sequenced N terminus was ARYIVALTGGIG. No His6 tag
is specified. Complete sequenced plasmid bytes were not independently read.

Adopted preparative reaction: 3.0 umol dephospho-CoA, 0.1 umol ATP,
1.0 umol MgCl2, 1.0 umol KCl, 3.5 umol phosphoenolpyruvate, 5 U
pyruvate kinase and 2 U recombinant CoaE in 5 ml water adjusted to pH 8,
30 min at room temperature. ATP regeneration is part of the joint system.
HPLC-purified product had a proton NMR spectrum reported identical to
authentic CoA. The spectrum/raw data and a preparative no-enzyme control
are not supplied in the inspected material; the reaction scheme is not
itself experimental proof. Separate ADP-coupled and phosphotransacetylase
assays corroborate activity but have different doses and pH 8.5.
Minor ADP formation with alternative substrates does not independently
identify their phosphorylated products. Neither a catalytic base nor a
family-wide substrate repertoire is directly established by this graph.
