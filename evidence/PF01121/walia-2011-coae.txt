Minimal quotations: "completely abolishes kinetic activity";

"increasing the enzymatic turnover"; "Table 2".

Full-length tagged mycobacterial CoaE derives from the 2009 pET28a+
construct. Mutants were sequenced by the authors; production in BL21(DE3),
0.4 mM IPTG, about 12 h at 18 C. Exact experimental clone bytes were
not independently reconstructed. Reference P63826 is now strain-split.

Single-coupled alpha-ketoglutarate-dehydrogenase assays at 25 C follow
A340 for five minutes, with no-DCoA blanks subtracted. Base conditions:
50 mM Tris pH 8, 10 mM ATP, 0.5 mM DCoA, 10 mM MgCl2, 20 mM KCl,
2 U coupling enzyme, 2 mM alpha-ketoglutarate, 0.3 mM NADP and 0.3 mM
the pyrophosphate cofactor (the source calls it thymine pyrophosphate).
Substrate series vary DCoA or ATP; fitted parameters are not binding Kd.
Exact CoaE dose for each spectrophotometric series is not stated.
Figure 5 caption reports two independent experiments performed in duplicate.
ITC kinetic assays at 20 C are a separate corroborating method, not the
same incubation: 1 uM protein preloaded with DCoA, 10 mM Tris pH 7.8,
150 mM NaCl, 10 mM MgCl2, 20 mM KCl, 5% glycerol.

Table 2 DCoA-varied kcat, min^-1: WT 1.758; R140K 0.453; D32A 0.1026;
L114A 3.414. These support lower apparent turnover for R140K/D32A and
higher apparent turnover for L114A in that series. ATP-varied kcat is
WT 2.886 versus L114A 1.152, the opposite L114A direction. Do not join
these conditions into a universal activation or inhibition claim.
Table 2 marks R140A nr and the text says activity abolished; Figure 5A
nevertheless plots R140A Km=240 uM. This is a CONTESTED activity-loss
claim, not an unqualified observed zero. Table 2 gives D32A ATP Km=57.5
uM but Figure 5B labels 575; G8A Km values also differ. R140K prose says
518-fold Km increase while the table gives 181 versus 34.9 uM. D32A's
printed DCoA kcat/Km ratio does not equal its displayed kcat divided by Km.
These conflicts are retained, not silently corrected or used for affinity.

CD/fluorescence and proteolysis were used as structural controls, but K14A
has an approximately 18% secondary-structure change and D32E altered
fluorescence. They do not prove every mutant is identically folded. Neither
the docking model nor altered kinetics directly observes a catalytic base,
phosphoryl-transfer geometry or transition-state stabilization. Magnesium-
omission results have unresolved buffer/reporter-control details and are
not adopted as a residue-specific metal-coordination graph.
