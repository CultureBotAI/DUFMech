The study identifies Rv1631 as a 407-aa protein, with a 196-aa kinase
region and a 211-aa C-terminal UPF0157 region. Full-length genomic PCR
was cloned through NdeI/HindIII into pET28a+ and expressed as a
hexahistidine-tagged protein in BL21(DE3), using nickel affinity and
imidazole elution. This is not the 2001 E. coli Ala-added construct.

Unresolved source discrepancy: the printed forward-primer NdeI junction
contains ATGGTGACCGACCGCGAT, which translates to MVTDRD, whereas the
archived P63826 and both current successors begin MLRIGL. Reference
mapping is strong through accession, Rv1631, length and internal mutation
positions, but this primer cannot independently verify the complete clone.
Do not silently repair it or infer separate H37Rv/CDC1551 experiments.

remain follow-ups, not newly curated graphs here.
