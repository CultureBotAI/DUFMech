Kang et al., Structural study of TTR-52 reveals the mechanism by which a
bridging molecule mediates apoptotic cell engulfment.
PMID:22713871; doi:10.1101/gad.187815.112

Accessed 2026-10-11. Relevant Results and main Methods read; Figures 3 and
4 visually inspected. Minimal quotations:
"number of cell corpses"
"greatly decrease the PtdSer-binding ability"

Figure 3 upper panel: WT full-length TTR-52::mCherry under heat-shock
promoters in ttr-52(tm2078) reduces persistent corpse counts compared
with the condition without transgene expression. Assayed at the 1.5-fold
embryonic stage; two independent transgenic lines, n >= 15 per line,
SEM. Heat shock is 1 h at 33 C, recovery at 20 C, examination 3.5 h after
the treatment. tm2078 is not the 2010 sm211/V43M allele. These counts do
not directly measure a single cell's clearance time or prove prevention
of apoptosis. Other variant rescue results are not additional adopted edges.

Figure 4: separately affinity-purified WT, D51A, D80A and N85A full-length
TTR-52-mCherry-Flag from transfected 293T culture medium; lipid-strip
binding to PtdSer is markedly reduced by each substitution. Protein
presence is checked by anti-TTR-52 immunoblot, not an independently
verified exact equimolar loading or folding measurement. P-6001 strips
are blocked 1 h at 22 C in 25 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.0, 150 mM NaCl,
0.1% Tween 20, 1% dried milk, 0.1 mM ZnSO4 and 0.2 mM CaCl2.
Protein incubation is overnight at 4 C in buffer without milk, with
three washes and anti-Flag detection. Two independent experiments.
Exact protein dose and affinity constants are not supplied in the inspected
method. This is an immobilized-lipid endpoint, not bilayer insertion or
an observed atomic ion-binding mechanism.

Source qualifications: the 2012 Discussion describes M5 corpse labeling
as unaffected while its earlier Results and the 2010 study describe loss.
The C-terminal double mutant is I113R/L132K in text but I113R/L132R in
Figure 3. Neither ambiguous construct is an adopted substitution graph.
The crystallized protein is the six-alanine M5 mutant, and reconstructed
WT coordinates are modeled. Do not label its ion-mediated binding proposal
as a directly observed WT trajectory. Exact tm2078 deletion boundaries,
supplemental constructs, raw microscopy and quantitative binding analysis
remain follow-up work.
