Curator's factual ledger follows; the article is not reproduced here.

sm211 was backcrossed four times to N2 Bristol; mapping, genomic/cDNA
rescue and sequencing identify ttr-52/F11F1.7 V43M. It is not a gene null.
Figure 1C: 33 corpses from three N2 embryos and 32 from three sm211 embryos;
displayed mean durations 28 +/- 2 versus 55 +/- 6 min (SEM). These are
nested corpses, not 65 independent embryos. The method captures 300-min
embryos on agar in 118 mM NaCl/48 mM KCl egg buffer, a 26-um stack at
1.3-um spacing, every 5 min for 150 min. Exact imaging temperature and
handling of the >110-min histogram bin are not independently resolved.
Persistent corpse duration supports impaired clearance, not more apoptosis.

Figure 4B: smIs34 Pced-1CED-1::GFP embryos with and without sm211.
The adopted 1.5-fold-stage comparison decreases the fraction of corpses
with CED-1::GFP rings from about 64% to 34%; 15 embryos per stage/genotype,
SEM, stated P < 0.0001. Corpses identified by Nomarski morphology in
20-um serial stacks at 1-um spacing. Comma stage is a separate comparison.
Receptor ring labeling is not a direct molecular binding constant or
proof of an observed recruitment-to-engulfment trajectory.

Figure 2 confirms rescue of the sm211 corpse-count phenotype by intact
ttr-52 constructs, but does not separately demonstrate rescue of every
adopted lifetime or ring endpoint. V43M and secretion-signal variants have
altered localization; do not assign V43 a unique ligand-contact mechanism.
The 2010 phospholipid-strip buffer uses 1 mM ZnSO4/2 mM CaCl2, unlike the
2012 protocol's tenfold lower concentrations. Pull-down/co-IP show physical
association, not automatically another causal activation edge. Full movies,
complete transgene insertions and raw statistical calculations were not
reprocessed. Later tm2078/M5 contexts are not interchangeable with sm211.
