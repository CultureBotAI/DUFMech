Curator paraphrase:
Native strain and nucS source are C.glutamicum ATCC13032, NCgl1168;
Q8NR49 is the matching reference, not strainR A4QDH7. Biochemical NucS
is N-terminally His-tagged. The deltaCter construct removes five native
residues ELTLF226-230, all within current PF01939 110-230.

Figure3: adding cognate beta-clamp increases WT cleavage of G/T duplex;
clamp alone does not cleave. DeltaCter retains low basal activity but not
clamp stimulation. Figure3C SPR detects WT-clamp binding while deltaCter
responses are absent under the tested concentrations. Binding and cleavage
are different measurements, not two independently established sufficient
causes. Whole-protein deletion can perturb more than a contact interface.

Figure4: both labeled strands produce products assigned to a five-nucleotide
5-prime overhang, with and without clamp. Model panelsD/E are homology
models, not new Cgl co-crystal evidence; their residue numbers refer to the
Tko template. Default cleavage conditions are30C,pH6.4,2.5mM MnCl2.
The text reports narrow pH preference and no Mg-supported activity under
its tested conditions. This conflicts with the other2018 study if promoted
to a context-free rule; no such universal metal requirement is curated.

Figure1C measures selected-colony mutation FREQUENCIES despite some
Results wording calling them rates: deletion raises the readout; WT
plasmid complementation lowers it; five-residue chromosomal truncation
raises it. Complementation approaches but is not numerically identical to
WT. Figure1D is transcript abundance, not proof of equal protein abundance.
Native repair intermediates and individual biochemical mediation steps
were not measured by the frequency comparison.
