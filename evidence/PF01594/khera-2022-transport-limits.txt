Exact short excerpt: "unable to demonstrate the transport activity of TqsA"

Curator assessment: The authors explicitly report unsuccessful attempts to
demonstrate transport with the methods used, not proof that TqsA cannot
transport. AI-2 remains a probable substrate and other substrates remain open.
Cryo-EM structure, docking and the proposed elevator-like motion are not a
directly observed transport cycle. Native-MS interaction experiments use
60 uM DPD/AI-2 and 4 uM protein; binding is not transmembrane flux. Preliminary
substitution effects require further biochemical support. The text's reported
mass-shift unit is suspect; no corrected number is adopted without inspecting
the original supplementary spectra. Figures and supplements were not visually
assessed in this session. The reported negative sodium/lithium antiport tests
are condition-specific and do not transfer Upf0118 function to TqsA. No new
OBSERVED molecular transport edge is supported by this inspected material.
