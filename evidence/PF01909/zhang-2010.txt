Inspected main Methods, Results and Discussion, Table 1, figure captions,
and original Figure 3, 4, 5 and 8 images. Figure 3 defines E. coli His-tagged
full-length GlnD (890 aa), GlnD-N (1-371), deltaNT (329-890), G93L/V,
G94L/D, D107A/V/Y and HD-AA (H514A/D515A) or HD-QN (H514Q/D515N).
The study's NT domain drawing is 74-169; current PF01909 coordinates are
85-144. Neither is the complete 1-371 experimental N-terminal construct.
Most purified proteins were at least 90% pure; historical clones were not
independently sequenced or tested for equal folding by this curation.

Methods UTase endpoint: 50 ul, 100 mM Tris pH 7.5, 100 mM KCl,
25 mM MgCl2, 1 mM DTT, 0.3 mg/ml BSA, 1 mM ATP, 10 mM alpha-KG,
60 uM E. coli GlnB; preincubate 2 min at 30 C, start with 1 ul of
50 mM UTP, incubate 60 min, stop with EDTA. The paired Mg/Mn condition
adds 1 mM MnCl2. Native PAGE resolves GlnB trimers with 0-3 modified
subunits and yields an average UMP count, not an initial reaction rate.
Figure 5 includes enzyme-free controls and concentration series from
0.025 to 2 uM as plotted. Full-length and 1-371 constructs increase
modification above the enzyme-free background in both conditions;
1-371 is less effective than full-length in Mg alone. DeltaNT, G93L,
G93V and G94D give lower modification than full-length, close to the
background. G94L is an important exception to a blanket glycine-mutant
loss claim. No precise bar-derived numerical effect or error estimate
is asserted. Residue changes need not identify the chemical transition
state or rule out folding/abundance effects.

Figure 4 separately tests loss of UMP from fully modified GlnB, without
UTP. Its inverted y-axis is remaining modification, not removal rate.
HD-AA and HD-QN greatly reduce removal relative to full-length under
both plotted protocols (0.1, 0.5 and 2 uM enzyme). Figure 5 shows that
these variants retain transfer activity, a useful functional control.
Mg-UR: 50 mM MgCl2, 0.5 mM ATP, 0.5 mM alpha-KG, 2.5 mM glutamine,
40 uM GlnB-UMP, 30 C/60 min. Mn-UR: 1 mM MnCl2, 10 mM glutamine,
omit MgCl2, ATP and alpha-KG; other common buffer components as Methods.
This is a comparison of complete assay protocols, not an isolated
Mg-to-Mn substitution experiment. UR effects belong to the HD region,
outside PF01909. DeltaNT retains substantial Mn-supported removal.

Figure 8A: with Mg only and 2 uM enzyme, 10 mM glutamine reduces the
modification endpoint for full-length, HD-AA and HD-QN versus their
matched no-glutamine controls; 1-371 lacks this visible inhibitory
response. Figure 4's UR-deficient HD variants help separate inhibited
transfer from competing removal. Figure 8B is a distinct Mg/Mn context:
glutamine decreases the full-length endpoint, especially at 2 uM, but
not the UR-deficient variants comparably. Competing removal can explain
that result; do not encode it as directly observed NT inhibition.
ACT-dependent glutamine binding, metal occupancy, allosteric structures
and physiological futile cycling are models, not directly measured
steps in the retained graphs. No universal family/species transfer.
