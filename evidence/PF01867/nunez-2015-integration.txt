Curator's assay facts, not a full-text reproduction:
Donor: MG1655 Cas1/Cas2, purified separately with tags removed by the cited
2014 procedure. Target pCRISPR: pUC19 plus BL21-AI CRISPR insert. Donor DNA:
33-bp M13-derived duplex. Buffer:20mM HEPES pH7.5,25mM KCl,10mM MgCl2
(or MnCl2 where specified),1mM DTT,10%DMSO;37C,1h.
Radiolabel default:200nM complex,20nM protospacer,approximately5nM target;
EDTA/SDS/proteinaseK deproteinization. Unlabeled Figure1:75nM proteins,
50nM protospacer,7.5nM target. Other unlabeled assays normally200nM donor.
At least three assay repetitions; representative gels, not fitted rates.

Original Figures1,2,3 and ExtendedData2/3 visually inspected.
Fig1B-E/Fig2B-C: joint WT proteins produce radiolabeled relaxed/linear DNA;
component-omission and deproteinization controls retained. BandX is not
radiolabeled integration product. Half-site species predominate; relaxed DNA
does not uniquely establish full-site integration, and linear DNA can arise
from independent half-site events. No completed chromosomal repair is inferred.
Fig1C/E and ED3B/C: Cas1H208A and D221A lower integration; Cas2delta-beta6-beta7
also loses activity. Cas2E9Q and Cas1K224A retain integration. The latter differs
from cellular K224A in the 2014 study; do not label it universally inactive.
ED2B:10mM Mn,0/50/100/200nM proteins. Cas1 alone has low integration and Cas2
addition raises product. This is enhancement, not an absolute Cas2 requirement.
Fig3A/B:both3prime ends phosphorylated lose detectable product; either isolated
single strand also fails relative to the duplex. Single-end capping differs
from dual capping. 5prime phosphate remains compatible.
Fig3C:linearized pCRISPR lacks the supercoiled target's integration under this
IHF-absent protocol. Empty supercoiled pUC19 supports integration. Do not infer
that CRISPR sequence is required or that linear DNA is universally unusable.
Fig2G/H:50nM Cas1,5nM synthetic Y-DNA,1h,37C releases33-bp labeled donor;
Cas1 alone works in Mg and Mn, Cas2 alone fails. Optional Cas2 is not required
for this assay. Minor smaller bands do not define a unique cleavage mechanism.
