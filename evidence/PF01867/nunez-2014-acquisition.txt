Fig1B:joint expression, unlike either alone or empty vector, produces expanded
locus bands. Clonal sequencing identifies inserted spacers; complete raw
sequence reanalysis and SupplementaryTable1 inspection are not claimed.
Fig4C:E141A,H208A,D218A,D221A and K224A separately lack detectable expanded
bands relative to WT. These cellular endpoints do not isolate chemistry;
K224A retains integration in the distinct purified 2015 ED3 assay.
Fig3D:R245D,R252E,R256E separately reduce expansion; corresponding alanine
substitutions retain it. Fig3C:Cas1delta-P282-S305,I291G,I291R retain expansion;
Cas2delta-beta6-beta7 does not. No universal tail requirement is assigned.
Fig3F:R252E reduces co-precipitated Cas2-HA after Cas1-FLAG IP, with expressed
input controls. Separate lysate assay,400mM KCl washes; not purified binding
affinity or proof that loss of association is the sole cause of acquisition
loss. Only R252E among these three charged Cas1 variants was tested here.
SupplementFig2d is described as WT-like SEC retention of purified R252E;
original supplementary figure not inspected, so normal folding of all variants
is not established. Protein abundance, folding and cellular context can matter.
