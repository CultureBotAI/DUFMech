Curator assessment: Table 5.1 maps metagenomic ORF1 to DUF29 at 9-109 by
MOTIF/Pfam, score 85.4, E=4.7e-24. The functional discussion and Figure 5.12
combine sequence/structure prediction, gene-neighborhood analysis and PFP
predictions to suggest nucleotide interaction or regulation. Their arrows are
an inference workflow, not measured biological state transitions. The selected
conclusion retains low confidence and only a broad predicted binding class.
No demonstrated DUF29 substrate reaction or directed perturbation effect is
provided in these inspected sections. The separate TC11pol enzyme experiments
must not be transferred to ORF1; ORF2/Usp is also a different sequence.
ORF1 was not mapped here to a modern exact protein accession or taxon.
The thesis's neighborhood organism labels are not independently verified.
