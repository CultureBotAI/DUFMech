The purified GltSHis complex contains wild-type alpha and C-terminally His6-tagged
beta. Figure S3C compares 1 mg/ml samples after 17 h at 15 C in 25 mM HEPES/KOH,
pH 7.5, 1 mM EDTA and 1 mM DTT. The DLS radius is lower at 1 M added NaCl than
without it. Salt-dependent assembly is a whole-complex effect, not proof that
PF01493 alone senses salt. DLS-derived sizes and SAXS mixture fits are distinct.

S3D starts with 1.2 mg/ml GltSHis exposed to 1 M NaCl for 17 h. Tenfold dilution
into salt-free buffer increases measured radius, whereas dilution into 1 M NaCl
does not. Residual NaCl is calculated as 0.1 M, not zero; final protein is 0.12
mg/ml. Ten-second DLS acquisitions are not independent biological replicates.
Some irreversible subunit loss is reported elsewhere; complete recovery is not
asserted. No measured cellular regulatory pathway follows from this comparison.

Main page 8245 and supplemental Methods report failure to detect production of
the delta40 alpha polypeptide in BL21(DE3) coproduction experiments, including
25 C and 15 C/0.1 mM IPTG conditions. Delta7 production and purified behavior
were retained. The endpoint is reported expression, not direct folding evidence.
No displayed delta40 gel, detection threshold or rescue was established here.
The proposed exposed-hydrophobic-core explanation is not an observed causal edge.

Table 2 holds assay salt at 1 M while comparing different salt-preincubation
histories; its apparent kinetic differences do not isolate oligomerization as
the unique causal mediator. Figure S8's correlation is not an observed atomic
electron-transfer pathway. These kinetic and NADP-ligand contexts are not added.
