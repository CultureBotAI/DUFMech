Q05755 Pfam:PF01493; domain interval: 1249-1496, inclusive.
Q05755/GLTB_AZOBR, gltB, Azospirillum brasilense, taxon 192, is 1515 aa;
sequence version 1, entry version 133; sequence MD5
2EC0E500A134FE2E2E4A0AB348DBE4F8. UniProt and InterPro full sequences agree.
UniProt annotates propeptide 1-36 and mature chain 37-1515. Its genomic-sequence
reference PMID:8428988 names Sp7; this does not independently establish every
2008 expression clone's donor strain.

The deposited 1EA0 polymer is exactly Q05755 residues 37-1515. The mmCIF
struct_ref_seq mapping independently gives author/polymer 1-1479 to reference
37-1515. Therefore PF01493 corresponds to mature positions 1213-1460, not
the same numbered interval in the 1479-aa protein used in the 2008 paper.
The reported delta7 removes reference 1509-1515, wholly outside the Pfam match.
The reported delta40 removes reference 1476-1515, overlapping PF01493 at
1476-1496 but also deleting the terminal tail outside that match.

Independent reverse-complement translation of printed supplemental Oligo 2
gives the locally matching LNRLEVPVHL (reference 1499-1508) followed by two
stops. Oligo 3 gives EEHVTETQSRF (1465-1475) followed by two stops. This agrees
with the named protein deletions, not a complete historical-clone verification.
The supplemental stop-position prose (4524/4437) is not substituted for these
protein mappings; exact nucleotide coordinate conventions remain unresolved.

PF01493 is GXGXG, integrated into IPR002489. The frozen EX_DUF classification
is retained; neither that status nor the structural model proves isolated-domain
sufficiency. FwdC/FmdC and other glutamate-synthase members need their own assays.
