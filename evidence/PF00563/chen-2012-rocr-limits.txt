Curator assessment:
The crystallized protein was R286W, not wild-type RocR. The study compared
solution assemblies using SAXS and proposed a regulatory model; it described
phosphorylation-dependent modulation as putative. The metal-site structure
does not settle the alternative catalytic interpretations. Neither an observed
phosphotransfer-to-hydrolysis chain nor a definitive E352 proton-transfer edge
is adopted here. No claim about the entire later literature is made.
