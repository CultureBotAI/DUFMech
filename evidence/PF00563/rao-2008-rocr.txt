Curator transcription of measured facts, not a quotation:
PAO1 PA3947 was cloned into pET-26(b+) and expressed as His6-tagged
full-length RocR in E. coli BL21(DE3). Final tagged sequence was not verified.
HPLC monitored 5'-pGpG formation; MALDI-MS gave product mass 708.108
(calculated 708.11). Initial-rate measurements kept conversion below 10%.
Table 1 conditions: 100 mM Tris pH 8.0, 23 C, 20 mM KCl, 25 mM MgCl2.
WT kcat = 0.67 +/- 0.03 /s; E352Q = (1.1 +/- 0.1) x 10^-5 /s;
reported decrease 6.1 x 10^4-fold. Higher mutant enzyme concentrations
permitted quantification. cAMP/cGMP negative substrate tests are reported.
Figure 4 was visually inspected: WT and E352Q have distinct log-kcat
axis scales. Figure 3 uses different metal conditions, 20-min endpoints
and E352A, not E352Q; its bars are not the Table 1 initial rates.
No obvious aggregation was reported; subtle structural effects remain possible.
These observations support a reaction and substitution effect, not proof of
the proposed general-base microstep, phosphorylation or cellular phenotype.
