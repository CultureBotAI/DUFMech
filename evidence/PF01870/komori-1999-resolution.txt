Figure4: untagged P.furiosus Hjc,10 nM, cleaves synthetic immobile4J and mobile4Jh
substrates at10 nM,56 C1 h,10 mM Tris pH8.8,10 mM MgCl2,200 mM KCl,1 mM DTT.
Denaturing gels map predominant symmetrical cuts in opposing strands2 and4;
minor cuts are retained, particularly for the mobile substrate. Protein-absent
lanes and chemical sequence ladders are present. No ligase is added here.
This is a purified-protein preparation effect, not proof of isolated-domain
sufficiency, an exclusive substrate repertoire or cellular recombination.

Figure5: E.coli RecA2.7 uM acts on gapped circular pUC1186 uM nucleotides
at37 C5 min; labeled linear PstI-pUC1183 uM nucleotides initiates20 min exchange.
The buffer is20 mM Tris pH7.5,15 mM MgCl2,2 mM DTT,2 mM ATP,100 ug/ml BSA.
Subsequent Hjc treatment is55 C10 min at0,1,2.5,5,10,50 nM. Deproteinized
agarose-gel products show decreasing intermediate signal and increasing linear
dimer/nicked-circle products. RecA-minus and Hjc-minus controls distinguish
substrate from intermediate processing. RuvC control uses37 C1 h, not matched
Hjc temperature/time; no comparative catalytic-rate claim is made.

Figure3: anti-Hjc immunodepletion reduces synthetic-junction cleavage by crude
Pfu extract; anti-PCNA and anti-intein PI-PfuI do not produce that reduction.
Reaction uses4 ul extract plus10 nM junction, standard buffer,56 C30 min.
Western blot confirms Hjc in the anti-Hjc precipitate. This is a cell-free
extract endpoint with potential co-depletion, not an in vivo knockout or an
isolated biochemical step. No add-back rescue is reported. Anti-PCNA also
co-precipitates Hjc, but that association is not evidence for PCNA activation.

Fraction V appears selective for four-way substrates; more highly purified
Hjc also weakly cleaves three-way/loop substrates. The paper's ligase result
is data-not-shown; use the displayed 2000 Figure3 for ligation compatibility.
