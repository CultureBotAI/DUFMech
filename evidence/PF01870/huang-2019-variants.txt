Figure2: tag-free SiRe_1431 Hjc or variant5 uM with the indicated kinase,
65 C30 min,25 mM Tris pH8,10 mM NaCl,5 mM MgCl2,2 mM DTT,50 uM ATP plus
8.3 nM gamma-32P ATP. Methods allow1 or2 uM kinase as specified, Figure1 uses
2 uM; Figure2 does not independently label its kinase concentration. Avoid
inventing a uniquely resolved Figure2 dose. SDS-PAGE/radiolabel comparisons:
S34A reduces Hjc labeling by SiRe_0171; S9A reduces it with SiRe_2030;
T138A reduces it separately with SiRe_2030 and SiRe_2056KD. KD is a kinase
domain construct, not full-length SiRe_2056. Residual labeling remains.
These mutation-dependent readouts are not mass-spectrometric site localization,
in vivo occupancy measurements or proof of site-exclusive kinase specificity.
S136A is weaker than T138A; other mutants retain substantial label. Figure1
shows three active kinase preparations, but kinase-alone/kinase-dead details
in supplementS1 were not visually verified; no extra addition-sufficiency edges
are inferred here from that uninspected control.

Figure3: 10 nM Hjc or each indicated variant with1 nM radiolabeled junction,
25 mM Tris pH8,25 mM NaCl,5 mM MgCl2,1 mM DTT,0.01% BSA,65 C30 min.
Denaturing PAGE compares WT,D42N,S9E,S9A,S34A,S34E,T138A,T138E, with no-protein
and boiled-substrate controls. S9E reduces cleavage; S34E and D42N leave very
little/undetectable product. WT is about70% cleaved and S9E about20% by displayed
bars, not an exact kinetic fold-change. S34A remains appreciably active, while
T138A/E are WT-like. S9E/S34E are phosphomimetic substitutions, not purified
proteins with chemically verified phosphoserine. Similar gel-filtration elution
and no precipitation do not prove identical folding. Actual kinase-treated Hjc
cleavage in supplementS5 was reported in main text but not independently
inspected here, so no corresponding direct phosphorylation-to-inhibition edge.

Figure5: chromosomal Hjc substitutions in E233S (REY15A delta-pyrEF delta-lacS)
with Hje intact. Early-log OD6000.2-0.3 cultures, tenfold dilution series,
10 ul spots,75 C7-10 days. S34E supports greater spot outgrowth at25 J/m2 UV
and15 ug/ml cisplatin, not an apparent increase at15 J/m2 UV or10 ug/ml cisplatin.
No analogous benefit under3/4 mM MMS or2/3 mM HU. This measures growth/tolerance
after damage, not direct repair kinetics, viable-cell percentages or a general
growth-rate increase. Methods say at least three trials; caption says2-3.

S34E was not recovered twice in delta-hje background, but no universally lethal
allele claim follows. SiRe_0171 deletion may affect other substrates; it is not
Hjc-specific epistasis. Figure4 Mg-coordination effects are homology-model
hypotheses. Figure7 pathway switching is a proposed model, not observed fork
trajectories or demonstrated stimulation of repair. Main Discussion notes that
these phosphorylation positions are not conserved across archaeal Hjc proteins.
