Li et al., Structure and regulation of the BsYetJ calcium channel in lipid nanodiscs.
PMID:33208533; doi:10.1073/pnas.2014094117
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7720206/
Accessed 2026-10-11; main Results, Discussion and Figure 5C inspected,
including visual inspection. Minimal quotations from calcium-flux Results:
"decrease substantially with decreasing pH";
"largely impaired within the pH range studied"

Proteoliposomes containing BsYetJ were preloaded with Fura-2; calcium was
added externally. Figure 5C compares WT, D171N and separate spin-labeled
constructs at pH 6, 7 and 8. Bars measure calcium influx in nM/min,
with SEM and n=3. WT influx decreases at pH 7 and 6 relative to 8;
D171N has markedly lower influx than WT across those pH conditions.
These are transport endpoints, not directly observed protonation or
salt-bridge-breaking steps. Nanodisc DEER experiments are a distinct system.
No numerical rate is transcribed from bar height. No empty-liposome
control is displayed in panel C; purified-protein sufficiency is not claimed.
