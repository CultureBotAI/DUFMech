M1, standard assay/product-identification paragraphs: MJ0056 was PCR-amplified
from M. jannaschii genomic DNA, cloned into pT7-7 and expressed in E. coli
BL21-CodonPlus(DE3)-RIL. The authors verified the insert by sequencing. Heat
treatment and MonoQ purification yielded >95% purity; measured mass 15218.1
+/-10 Da agrees with the 132-aa calculated 15213.8 Da reference. No affinity
tag is described for this preparation. The retained reference mapping is not
independent sequencing of the historical physical insert.
The 60-ul assay used 47 mM TES pH 7.2, 1.2 ug RibK, 1.7 mM CTP and 0.37 mM
riboflavin, 70 C for 10 min. Reported conversion was 42%. HPLC separation and
positive/negative-ion MS and MS-MS matched authentic FMN. This supports direct
phosphorylation, not an experimentally resolved proton-transfer trajectory.
CDP production is the reaction interpretation, not separately quantified here.
No enzyme-minus time-course is described in the inspected paragraph; do not
invent one. Kinase specificity is not family-wide proof.

M2, extract paragraph: lactose-induced expression in the bacterial host yielded
8.7 uM FMN in the extract, six times comparator extracts expressing unrelated
enzymes. This is a whole-expression intervention with indirect cellular effects,
not native archaeal flux, a knockout/rescue, or an empty-vector comparison.

M3, nucleotide-specificity paragraph: replacing CTP with an equal concentration
of ATP or GTP yielded 30% or 11% of the CTP activity. These reported assay
contrasts are retained separately. IUBMB EC2.7.1.161 cites Ammelburg2007 for
UTP but not ATP/GTP use. The original 2007 full text was inaccessible (403);
its abstract was inspected, not its raw donor assay. Do not erase the2008
positive residuals, call them contamination, or assert universal strict CTP
specificity. Purity, donor contamination, metal state and conditions are
possible discriminating questions, not established explanations.

M4, metal-addition paragraph: separate 1.6 mM additions gave specific activities
(umol/min/mg) none 0.70, Mg2+ 0.60, Mn2+ 0.35, Zn2+ 0.12, Ni2+ 0.40,
Cu2+ 0.02, Co2+ 0.27. Mark stronger Mn/Zn/Ni/Cu/Co decreases as assay effects,
not a known inhibitory binding site; no uncertainties or statistical tests were
given. The small Mg difference is retained here without claiming inhibition.
ICP analysis measured 4-5 mol Mg/mol purified protein and 0.0013 mol Zn/mol.
Activity without ADDED metal therefore does not demonstrate metal-free catalysis.

M5, acid/thermal paragraph: 0.1 M HCl (~pH1.05), 10 min at room temperature,
then dilution in pH6 acetate left 0.13 versus control 0.32 nmol/min (~40%).
The loss could reflect unfolding or other damage; neither was isolated.
Heating up to100 C for10 min caused no significant activity change under the
reported conditions (DTT used; detection limit ~2%). This negative is not a
growth optimum or a complete unfolding curve. Monomer size-exclusion estimate
and genomic neighbors are context, not causal edges. MJ1179/RibL FAD synthesis
and other riboflavin pathway enzymes are not reassigned to this family.
