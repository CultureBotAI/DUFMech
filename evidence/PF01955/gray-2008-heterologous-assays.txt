Both figures were visually inspected, with each donor row kept separate.
Black curves are0mM arabinose; gray curves are2.5mM. Triplicate OD650
time courses run24h at37C, oxic NCE,22mM glycerol,1mM MgSO4,trace minerals,
15nM indicated cyano-corrinoid. Overnight NB starters inoculate at5percent;
microplate wells are200ul. Methods antibiotics are100ug/ml ampicillin and
50ug/ml kanamycin, while captions print ug/ul, a1000fold unit conflict.
No exact growth rate, significance or protein abundance is inferred from plots.

Figure3 host JE8312 is a TR6583-derived metE205 ara-9 cob1315(delta-cobU)
ycfN112(delta-ycfN) strain carrying pCOBY38 (M.mazei cobY). Donor pBAD24
plasmids are pCbiZRs,Rr,Bh,Mm,Fa,Pf,Mj, in that displayed row order after vector.
Five donors support robust Cbi-dependent growth without added arabinose:
Rs,Bh,Fa,Pf,Mj. Rr andMm require2.5mM arabinose for robust complementation.
Each donor comparison uses the vector at the SAME arabinose condition, with
CobY and host downstream functions already present. No CbiZ-alone sufficiency.
Vector Cby/Cbl arms grow, while vector Cbi and no-corrinoid arms remain low.
The observed phenotype is host optical density, not directly detected
intracellular Cby/Cbl, CbiZ expression level or Cbi kinase activity.

Figure3 induction comparisons within the same donor and substrate:
Rr andMm induction increase Cbi-supported growth relative to no inducer.
Rs andBh induction reduce or delay Cbi- and Cby-supported growth across the
displayed time course; their24h Cbi endpoints can approach the uninduced
plateau, especially Rs. Thus these are time-course suppression/delay effects,
NOT necessarily lower final biomass. Cbl curves are largely preserved.
The six curated induction contrasts use OD650 near the12h midpoint, not a
fitted growth rate, exact digitized value or the possibly convergent24h endpoint.
No-corrinoid controls do not show a general arabinose-fed growth effect.
Higher protein abundance is not measured; intermediate hydrolysis or host
interference is a proposed explanation, not an observed downstream mechanism.
Fa/Pf show altered time-course shape with induction; no precise rate is assigned.

Figure4 host JE7864 is metE205 ara-9 cbiP236::Tn10d(tet)
cobD1302::Tn10d(cat) cobU330 with pCOBY38. Actual perturbation is cobD,
encoding Salmonella threonine-phosphate decarboxylase, NOT a cbiB deletion.
CbiB-dependent pathway placement is the authors' interpretation of AP rescue.
Compare15nM Cbi plus10mM1-amino-2-propanol with Cbi alone in each donor row:
Rs,Bh,Fa,Pf,Mj at0mM arabinose; Rr,Mm at2.5mM arabinose. AddedAP supports
robust growth conditional on donor,CobY and the remaining host machinery.
Vector plusAP does not restore robust growth; Cbl supports all rows.
Some Cbi-alone arms drift upward late; they are not asserted to have zero growth.
The figure does not directly measure AP phosphorylation, CbiB catalysis,
amidohydrolase activity of isolated domains or all intracellular intermediates.
The three negative donor constructs are text-reported,data not shown, as
retained in the construct ledger. No universal loss of native function is inferred.
