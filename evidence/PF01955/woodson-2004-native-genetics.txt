Halobacterium sp. NRC-1-derived strains, defined liquid medium at37C,
corrinoids100pM supplied as cyano forms; readout absorbance650nm over~170h.
With Cbi, JE6812(delta-cbiP delta-cbiZ) has much lower OD than JE6738
(delta-cbiP cbiZ+). The double mutant's late residual OD rise is visible;
do not translate the author's growth-negative interpretation into zero cells
or lethality. Both lack substantial growth without exogenous corrinoids.
In JE6812, adding Cby gives robust growth in Figure2A (reported27h doubling).
Cbi-GDP and Cbl rescue are described but not plotted.

JE7210(delta-cbiP delta-cbiZ ura3::cbiZ+) regains Cbi-supported growth
(Figure2B, reported22h doubling), compared with JE6812; without corrinoid
the same restored strain stays low. The result is sufficiency within the
remaining salvage machinery and supplied precursor, not CbiZ-only synthesis.
JE7002(delta-cbiP delta-cbiZ ura3::cbiP+) grows without supplied corrinoid;
this restores cbiP, not cbiZ, and is a pathway-control result.
JE6811(delta-cbiZ, cbiP+) and wild type grow similarly without corrinoids
(reported30h and34h doubling). The experiment does not support general
CbiZ essentiality or a requirement for de novo synthesis under those conditions.

No intracellular corrinoid time course or direct native-enzyme reaction was
measured here. Whole-gene phenotypes are not isolated-domain activities.
Deletion endpoint/polarity details require the original supporting constructs;
chromosomal cbiZ restoration substantially supports the gene-specific effect.
Replicate counts/error bars are not stated for these plotted curves.
