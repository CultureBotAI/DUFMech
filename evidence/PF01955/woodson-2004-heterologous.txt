S. enterica JE824(metE205 cobU330), aerobic defined liquid medium at37C,
dicyanoCbi15nM; OD650 readout. The Methods state DMB3uM and AP10mM additions,
but Figure3B explicitly separates AP-positive and AP-negative arms.
The per-arm interpretation is retained rather than assuming AP in every arm.

Figure3A: M. mazei pMmCBIZ2 carrying BOTH cbiZ+ and cobY+ restores robust
growth, as does S. enterica cobU+ on pJO52. pMmCBIZ1(cbiZ+ alone),
pCOBY10(cobY+ alone) and pT7-7 vector do not provide comparable rescue.
The cobY-only curve has a small late rise; no claim of absolute zero growth.
The joint intervention is represented as one subject, not independently
sufficient CbiZ and CobY edges. No molecular intermediate was directly assayed.

Figure3B adds cobD1272 to metE205 cobU330. The caption names JE6894,
whereas Results name JE6984. Retain the genotype and both labels unresolved.
In this background, pMmCBIZ2(cbiZ+ cobY+) stays low without AP; AP addition
restores growth. cobU+ rescues with or without AP; vector+AP stays low.
Thus AP is a conditional rescue with both donor genes and remaining host
machinery, not AP-alone synthesis and not direct catalysis by CbiZ.
The exact per-arm recipe and strain name require supporting Table1/Methods.
No native M. mazei cellular salvage experiment is established by these curves.
