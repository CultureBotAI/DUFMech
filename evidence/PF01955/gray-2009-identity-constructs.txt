Table3 forward primers replace the initial GTG with ATG; their 3-prime regions
match CDS bases2-20 (purification) and2-18 (rescue). Reverse-primer mapping was
rechecked against the CDS plus downstream sequence, not just the coding region:
purification AGCTTTGTCGACTCACAGGAGGTCTCAGCCC has a 20nt 3-prime suffix whose
reverse complement GGGCTGAGACCTCCTGTGAG matches genomic1035826..1035845.
It spans the native stop and 13bp downstream. Rescue
AGCGAAGGATCCATGTCAACCCATTGTCCCG has a 22nt suffix whose reverse complement
CGGGACAATGGGTTGACATGGA matches1035851..1035872, ending40bp after the CDS.
These exact matches support the RSP_2406 assignment and the reported +40bp
rescue construct. The earlier CDS-only nonmatch is resolved, not a mutation.

Purification uses pRsCBIZ3 in pKLD66, amplified with KpnI/SalI primers and
expressed in BL21 (Table2 specifies BL21(DE3)). N-terminal MBP and His6 tags
remain in the measured enzyme. TEV removal yielded insoluble inactive material;
the study does not establish equivalent activity of isolated untagged CbiZ.
Rescue uses pRsCBIZ6 in pRK404, the coding region plus40bp downstream, using
HindIII/BamHI cloning. Both inserts were reported sequence-confirmed, but full
historical insert/tag/linker sequences were not retrieved in this assessment.
Domain coordinates refer to the native parent, not fusion-residue numbering.
Protein-normalized rates refer to the measured fusion preparation.
