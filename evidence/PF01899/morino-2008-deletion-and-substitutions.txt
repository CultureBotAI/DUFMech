Transport: everted vesicles from LBK+50mM NaCl cultures,37C;2ml reaction,
50mM BTP-sulfate,140mM choline chloride,5mM MgCl2,1uM acridine orange,

This is coupled vesicle delta-pH collapse, not isolated MrpE transport.

Figure1B displays pH6.5-9.0, using the Methods-default2.5mM NaCl addition.
Delta-mrpE reduces the signal at pH7-9 but retains a small response at alkaline
pH. WT is also near zero at6.5, so do not claim a decrease at every tested pH.
Figure2 lane7 retains all other six subunits. Figure4B lane3 mainly shows A-D;
a weak fuzzy upper region does not identify an active six-subunit complex.
The authors' six-subunit explanation remains a hypothesis, not an observed edge.

Figure5B instead uses10mM Na,pH8.5. T113A loses the detectable antiport response;
P114G retains a much smaller response. Empty vector and tagged parent are controls.
Figure5C WT-normalized T113A membrane signals: A0.52,B0.60,C0.66,D0.48,
E0.07,F0.12,G0.02. The E/F/G reductions are retained as three measured endpoints,
not proof that E directly stabilizes each partner. SDS-PAGE uses20ug protein.
Figure5D loads30ug solubilized protein for BN-PAGE, displaying A/D probes:
T113A lacks the full-complex bands while retaining the A-D subcomplex.
P114G retains both full-complex forms, with E/F/G signals1.35/0.81/0.73.
Detergent-dependent migration does not directly measure assembly kinetics.

Figure5B inset: apparent Na Km(mM) at pH8.0/8.5/9.0 is0.084/0.079/0.060
for parent and1.67/1.30/1.29 for P114G (about19.9/16.5/21.5-fold).
These are half-maximal fluorescence responses, not equilibrium binding Kd.
Growth-complementation loss for T113A/P114G is reported without shown data;
no P114G growth edge is curated. One Results sentence prints T114G, but
construct definitions and Figure5 identify P114G. Do not invent a third allele.
Other six substitutions are reported to retain complementation without shown data.

The four-TM MrpE cartoon is a historical prediction, not structural evidence.
Later structures show two short TM helices and a cytoplasmic domain. Whole-complex
activation and MrpE/F/G-to-A/D conformational coupling are proposed interpretations.
Read the2010 ledger for reused-row numerical and growth discrepancies; do not
pool those values or count them as independent replication.
