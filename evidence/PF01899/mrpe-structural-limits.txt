The OF4-related7QRU structure also shows two short MrpE TM helices and a
cytoplasmic domain, with inter-protomer contacts. Its MrpE sequence differs
from the2008 parent at54,62,158 (see identity ledger). The authors found no
strong evidence for bound Na or Mg in the structure after inspecting candidate
densities. Competing sodium pathways and conformational gating remain proposed;
do not infer an observed H131-sodium binding edge from the structural picture.
