Figure 2A titrates recombinant Sso1441/Sso1442 Cas5/Cas7 into fluorescent
crRNA and increases anisotropy. Fit Kd 1.38 +/- 0.14 uM; the +/- value is
curve-fit error, while plotted error bars are SEM of triplicate experiments.
The type of triplicate independence is not specified. Do not treat this Kd
as a Cas7-alone value or compare it quantitatively with the 2011 EMSA.
The protein genes are coexpressed using the cited 2011 method. The probe
is spacer 26 from CRISPR A of P1, not the 2011 P2 spacer-1 probe.
Methods: 20 nM fluorescent crRNA; 20 mM Tris pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl,
5% glycerol, 1 mM EDTA, room temperature; excitation/emission 490/535 nm.
The general titration range is 0.04-11 uM; Figure 2A's displayed x-axis
extends to about 8 uM. No assay incubation time is established here.

Figure 2B/C lacks convincing Csa5-alone binding to RNA/DNA/heteroduplex
or binding to a preformed Cas5/Cas7/crRNA complex. These negatives do not
prove Csa5 dispensability in all systems. Cellular co-IP uses P1 lysate and
is not an RNA-directed causal step or a measured affinity of isolated Cas7.
