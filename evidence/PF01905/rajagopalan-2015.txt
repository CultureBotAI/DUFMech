DK1622-derived in-frame devR deletion; Discussion explicitly distinguishes
it from earlier potentially polar devR transposon insertions. Exact removed
codons and Table S1 catalog identifier remain unverified. Reference Q07765
matches three versioned deposits; PF01905 is only 19-176 within 302 aa.
Whole-gene perturbation is not a domain-specific or direct biochemical test.
Submerged starvation in MC7 (10 mM MOPS pH 7.0, 1 mM CaCl2), 32 C.

Figure 2A: the devR mutant forms mounds but has reduced darkened fruiting-body
appearance at 96 h relative to DK1622; not a complete aggregation defect.
Figure 2B: at 48 h the mutant has fewer visibly ovoid cells in gently dispersed,
settled aggregate samples; residual shape change appears by 72/96 h, so no
permanent absolute block or exact developmental-delay duration is inferred.
At least two biological replicates for the qualitative images.
Figure 2C: at 72 h the mutant yields far fewer mature-spore CFU/ml than DK1622,
but not zero. This is a composite heat/sonication resistance, germination and
colony-formation assay, not total microscopic spore counts or pure viability.
Log10 CFU, at least three biological replicates, SD bars. No bar heights were
digitized or universal fold loss assigned. The same panel shows adding either
the promoter deletion or devI deletion to devR restores high CFU relative to
the devR single mutant. This is suppressor genetics, not restoration of DevR.

Figure 3: at 72 h, both double mutants regain darkened fruiting bodies relative
to the devR single mutant, a qualitative endpoint separate from the 96 h panel.
The promoter deletion is -38 through +19 relative to the dev start; devI
deletion is in-frame. These suppressors establish conditional requirements,
not absolute DevR indispensability in every genotype or natural isolate.

Figure 4B: devR deletion increases dev transcript abundance roughly tenfold
at 24 h. PdevF-2/PdevR-3 spans +237 to +440, not the Figure 4A junction probe.
qRT-PCR normalized to 16S rRNA and WT, with standard curves and no-RT controls;
three technical replicates for at least three biological replicates, SD bars.
Steady-state abundance does not distinguish initiation, elongation, termination
or RNA stability, and it is not direct measurement of DevI protein.

DevI complementation, premature-stop and extracellular tests primarily use
devS backgrounds; do not transfer them to devR as separate experiments.

as evidence that DevT has an indispensable separate developmental mechanism.

exact assay-clone sequences remain limits. The 2015 supplement was not obtained
