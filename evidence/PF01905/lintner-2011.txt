Figure 3B: isolated SSO1442 Csa2 and coexpressed SSO1442/SSO1441 Csa2/Cas5a
each shift radiolabeled crRNA-A1, compared with RNA alone. Protein series
0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 uM; RNA 100 nM. Similar-looking curves are not
a precise equivalence test or separate quantitative Kd estimates.
Binding Methods: 20 mM MES pH 6.0, 50 mM potassium glutamate, 0.5 mM DTT,
5 mM EDTA, 5% glycerol, 55 C for 10 min, then native 10% PAGE.
Supplement pages 10-11 identify sequenced dual-expression constructs with
native Csa2 and His/TEV-tagged Cas5a. Do not silently apply the structural
Csa2 tag or cellular tandem Strep/His8 tag to every recombinant preparation.

Figure 3C: with the recombinant complex and Target-A1f ssDNA, adding cognate
unlabeled crRNA-A1 increases the protein-bound DNA band relative to no crRNA.
Replacing Target-A1f with reverse-complement Target-A1r under crRNA-positive
conditions removes the strong bound-DNA signal. The free RNA-DNA hybrid is
distinct from the more slowly migrating protein-bound species. Protein
titrations are matched qualitatively; exact Figure 3C protein/crRNA doses
were not established beyond the general Methods and displayed titration.
Table S3 supplies all three named oligonucleotides and strand identity.
Neither binding result proves dsDNA targeting, cleavage or cellular immunity.
PAM-independent binding is mentioned as data not shown; it is not a displayed
experimental graph or proof that a complete effector ignores PAMs.

Figure 4E: the H160A Csa2 preparation has less shifted crRNA than WT across
the displayed 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3 uM series. Signal remains at higher doses;
do not call complete loss. No fitted affinity change, separate mutant-folding
measurement, direct H160/RNA contact or catalytic proton-transfer event was
established. H160 lies within current PF01905 5-273, but whole 321-aa protein
was tested. Crystal 3PS0 contains no RNA and resolves H160 only in A/C.

S1 shows RNA retention under RNase treatment in a heterogeneous cellular
aCASCADE preparation, without a matched unbound-RNA arm establishing a
unique Csa2 protection effect. Co-purification, helical appearance, flexible
loops and modeled hexamers remain context. Cas6 cleavage in Figure 2 is not
Csa2 catalysis. Reported purification-force differences are not assay outputs.
No raw gels, densitometric reanalysis, exact mutant sequencing or 3-D dynamics
were reproduced. 2011 and 2013 assays use different probes and temperatures.
