Figure 4D retains ordinal yeast two-hybrid outputs, not affinity constants.
Full-length FOG-2 (327 aa) gives ++++ with GLD-1. Truncation to 318 aa
gives ++, to 299 aa gives +/-, and to 263 or 251 aa gives no detected
interaction. Every displayed construct remains ++++ with SKR-1, a positive
F-box interaction control. The 314-aa FTR-1 comparator is negative for
GLD-1, whereas the 327-aa FTR-1/FOG-2 exon-4 chimera gives +++.
Thus transfer confers a reporter interaction but does not fully match
the ++++ FOG-2 score, and does not establish an isolated peptide's sufficiency.

Methods place GLD-1 in pAS1 (DNA-binding fusion), FOG-2 derivatives in
pACTII (activation fusion), with 20 mM 3-amino-triazole and sequencing
of constructs. FOG-2 autoactivation in pAS1 motivated this single assay
orientation. Figure 4D instead labels the depicted derivatives "Bait";
the Methods orientation is retained with this discrepancy explicit.
The inherited Clifford 2000 protocol used Y190 yeast, histidine selection
and beta-galactosidase filter-lift readouts, with 25 mM 3-amino-triazole
in that original screen. Do not substitute 25 mM for the 2005 modification.
The 2005 article does not independently enumerate every earlier reporter
step or define quantitative calibration of the plus signs. No replicate
count, binding constant or purified-protein concentration is adopted.

Figure 4B shows FOG-2 residues 254-327 (74 aa) as exon 4, and FTR-1
as 314 aa. Methods instead calls full-length FTR-1 318 aa and its
251-aa FOG-2 truncation an exon-4 removal (76 aa deleted). These
discrepancies are not silently resolved into a fabricated clone junction.
The current PF01827 interval in 327-aa FOG-2 is 144-271; the 318/299
truncations delete only sequence after that interval. The 263 construct
removes domain 264-271 and the tail; the 251 construct removes domain
252-271 and the tail. FTR-1's independently mapped interval is 146-283.
Legacy schematic shading is not the current Pfam interval. Whole-construct
effects can involve folding, expression or geometry as well as interface
contacts; retained SKR-1 activity does not prove a correctly folded FTH.

Phylogeny and sequence divergence are context, not a causal sexual-fate
experiment in other family members. The separate C. briggsae gld-1 RNAi
experiment is not a PF01827 perturbation. Supplemental alignments and
raw yeast growth/reporter measurements were not independently reanalyzed.
