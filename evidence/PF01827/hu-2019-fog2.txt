Table 2: families with tra-2(e2020); fem-3(q95), segregating fog-2(q71),
are shifted as embryos from permissive propagation at 15 C to 25 C and
grown to adulthood. Compound microscopy scores oocyte presence.
The full scored population is 85 Mog and 24 Fog (109 total). A selected
subset is recovered for PCR/Sanger genotyping: of nine Mog, four are
fog-2(+/+), five q71/+ and zero q71/q71; of twelve Fog, zero are +/+,
one is q71/+ and eleven q71/q71. Fog explicitly includes self-fertile
animals. Do not call all 11 mutants sperm-free, or use 11/11 as an
unbiased population penetrance estimate: sampling followed phenotyping.
The retained effect is increased oocyte-producing/feminized outcome in
this sensitized genotype, not a direct repression-rate measurement.
Methods briefly places fem-3(q95) on I; the detailed crosses and strain
list place the linked unc-24/fem-3/dpy-20 interval on IV. Retain the latter
with the source inconsistency noted. Marker/background effects and direct
FOG-2 biochemical steps were not isolated by this segregating-family test.

Results near Figure 3: the endogenous tra-2(nm75) allele inserts a
nine-residue HA tag four residues before TRA-2's stop. In fog-2(q71)
XX animals the homozygous tag yields 35% self-fertility (N=40), compared
with the reported sperm-deficient q71 baseline. Exact denominator for a
contemporaneous untagged comparator is not supplied. This is partial
genetic suppression by altered TRA-2, not FOG-2 complementation.
CP153 is derived by crossing JK574 males to CP139/CP141; q71 and nm75
are confirmed by sequencing/PCR. Default culture is 20 C on NGM;
the precise scoring day and replicate breakdown of the 40 are not given.
The tagged allele is therefore not a neutral reporter of wild-type biology.
Figure 3B image says nmIs4 where Methods/caption identify nm75; Figure 6
uses nm75. This image-label discrepancy remains unresolved.

Figures 3C and 6A: neither whole-worm anti-HA blots nor germline imaging
shows a clear TRA-2 increase in q71. L3 gonads, n=12 per genotype,
have mean intensity 41.17 versus 41.2 (P=0.99). This does not prove
identical translation rates or absence of a smaller undetectable effect.
The positive gld-1 loss effect is a different perturbation and must not
be assigned to FOG-2. OMA-1:GFP remains absent from the distal germline
in both fog-2 backgrounds (Figure 6B); there is no universal target
derepression claim. Somatic staining and detection limits matter.

RNAseq compares first-day gravid N2 adults with successfully mated q71
XX adults, three samples of 100 animals per condition. tra-2 abundance
is similar (mean FPKM 48.9/49.6); most GLD-1 targets are unchanged.
Mating/sperm composition differs, and active XX spermatogenesis is absent
at collection. Reduced sperm-transcript signals are not direct FTH-mediated
transcriptional repression. Raw GEO GSE110998 data and supplemental tables
were not reanalyzed. Text says 304 shared targets while Figure 2 says 302;
the random-primed 81/<10000 transcript result is mislabeled 0.08%.

tra-2(e2020) removes 108 nt of the 3-prime UTR and abolishes known
GLD-1 binding in prior in-vitro assays. The phenotype does not prove
no binding anywhere in vivo, a particular alternative RNA target,
GLD-1 independence or direct ubiquitination of nascent TRA-2.
The Table 1 quantitative model uses assumed parameters, not measured
repression constants. Table 3 RNAi percentages also need caution:
19/179 is about 10.6%, and 32/75 about 42.7%, rather than transferring
all narrative percentages literally. No such percentage is a new edge.
