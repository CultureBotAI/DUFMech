Figure 3D-F compares GM12-derived Mmc 1.1, MIB-MIP deletion control,
ATPase-cluster deletion (0581 initiation codon through 0575 stop codon),
0575-recode (G3S/K4R) and 0575-K152A (G3S/K4R plus K152A).
Relative to Mmc 1.1, ATPase deletion increases immune-serum agglutination
and removes the detectable supernatant IgG ~44-kDa and IgM ~68-kDa products.
The triple-substitution strain has the same three effects relative to the
G3S/K4R recode control. MIB/MIP abundances remain broadly WT-like; that does
not prove their normal localization or assembly. S13 confirms dispersed
cells without serum and antibody-signal-negative no-serum controls.
Methods: pellet 2 ml late-log culture into 1 ml fresh SP5 plus 2% B4J12
immune goat serum, incubate 1 h, image and separate supernatant/cell pellet.
Incubation temperature is not specified in this assay paragraph. No numerical
agglutination effect size or replicate statistics are inferred from images.

Limits: one clone per mutant, other genomic SNPs/INDELs, and no complementation.
DeltaATPase has extra passages and 23S-rRNA changes. Predicted Walker-A
function is not an ATP-hydrolysis measurement. Cell-pellet cleavage products
expected under the proposed ATP-driven opening/reset model were not clearly
detected; altered localization, assembly and detection limits remain alternatives.
These are indirect cellular effects, not observed energy-transduction steps.
