Frozen PF01732 is Myco_protease, Mycoplasma immunoglobulin protease,
InterPro IPR022382, EX_DUF. Do not rewrite imported identity or counters.
The 2016 primary study identifies DUF31 in MMCAP2_0582 (MIP82/r-0582).
Versioned GenBank ACU78687.1 has 861 aa, gene MMCAP2_0582, GM12,
NCBITaxon:436113, complement(CP001668.1:712279..714864), translation table 4.
The source genome is labelled deltatypeIIIres/transgenic, not an independently
sequenced historical expression plasmid. MIB83 is ACU78312.1, 750 aa,
MMCAP2_0583, complement(CP001668.1:714887..717139).

the reference polymer sequence in PDB 7ADK entity 2. Sequence SHA-256:

Do not independently certify a historical r-0582A sequence from this reference.

C7LKT2 is retired. 7ADK's reference A0A446C0V0 is also retired and maps to
UPI00101941AD, which has X810 rather than GM12 L810. It is not the exact
whole GM12 sequence, although the PF01732 interval matches. Use the exact
ACU/UPI0001793D5F mapping above instead. PDB sequence/mutation metadata do
not resolve the published inactive-protein construct by themselves.
MIB's exact-sequence archive UPI0001793D5E has other Pfam domains, not MIP's
PF01732. MIB, Protein M and MIP must not be conflated. Whole MIP, paired
systems and cellular phenotypes do not prove isolated PF01732 sufficiency.
