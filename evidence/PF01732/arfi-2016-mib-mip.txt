Evidence assessment (not a verbatim extract):
Figure 2C compares purified goat IgG with recombinant MIP r-0582 plus MIB
r-0583, MIP without MIB, and r-0582A plus MIB. The joint WT preparation
generates an approximately 44-kDa heavy-chain fragment; the omission and
mutant lanes retain intact heavy chain without that product. Nonspecific
MIB detection is explicitly explained by antibody binding, not IgG cleavage.
Figure 3B separately shows heavy-chain cleavage products from Fab and
F(ab')2 in joint reactions. Figure 3C reports Edman product sequence VSSAST.

Both recombinant proteins replace the N-terminal transmembrane segment
with a His tag and are expressed in E. coli. PBS pH 7.4; MIP added last.
Main Methods give 30 C; SI gives room temperature. Figure 2B is specifically
15 min at 30 C, but numeric doses/time for Figure 2C are not established.
Do not borrow the distinct 2021 protocol. Exact 2016 clone termini remain open.
