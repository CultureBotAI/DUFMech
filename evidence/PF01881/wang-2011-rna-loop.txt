Figure5 reactions use 1 uM enzyme and 50 fmol 5'-labeled RNA in 20 ul,
20 mM HEPES pH7, 250 mM KCl, 1.5 mM MgCl2, 5 ug E. coli tRNA,
10% glycerol, 70 C. Printed DTT is 0.075 M (75 mM), versus 0.75 mM in
2008; discrepancy unresolved. Incubation duration is not stated in 2011 Methods.
Binding/cleavage use 10% native/15% denaturing PAGE. Do not import a time
or silently repair DTT. WT repeat is GUUACAAUAAGACUAAAAUAGAAUUGAAAG.

Figure5A/B G: U2/U3-to-AA; H: C5/A6/A7-to-GUU. Each loses detectable
binding and cleavage compared with WT. K (delete1-3) also loses both; I/J
(delete1 or1-2) retain them. Do not infer individually necessary bases from
these combined changes. B (2'-deoxy A22/A23) binds but is not cleaved.
D (delete25-30) binds but is not cleaved; C (delete27-30) retains cleavage,
so U25/G26 or their structural context matter without individual isolation.
Figure5A/C M/N delete respectively five/six linker residues and lose cleavage.
Their retained binding is reported without shown gels. E/L/O shorter linker
deletions and F two-base insertion retain processing. The corrected linker
interpretation supersedes the 2008 nt9-12 claim, not the underlying assays.

Figure5D Q (A23U) shifts the 5' product from22 to21 nt; P (A23U+dA22)
also yields21; R (A23U+dG21) restores22. Deoxy modification therefore can
reroute rather than universally abolish cleavage. MALDI confirms WT22 and
A23U21 product masses, consistent with cyclic-phosphate ends; no rate is derived.

RNA-bound structure resolves only nt2-10; the cleavage-site segment is not
observed. C-domain K188/K190/R191 contacts are structural, not mutational
causal tests. The positive-surface/G-loop wraparound path and its proposed
alignment of the scissile bond are hypotheses. Functional dimer necessity,
order of repeat processing, exact catalytic acid/base roles and conserved
family-wide chemistry are not established by these inspected experiments.
