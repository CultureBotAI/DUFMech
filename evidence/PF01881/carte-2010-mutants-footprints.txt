PF1131 N-terminal His6 preparations: WT, separately Y31A, H46A, K52A.
QuikChange DNA sequences were confirmed by the authors. Figure3B shows
purified proteins by Coomassie SDS-PAGE, not a native-fold/stability control.
Figure3A uses 10,50,500 nM protein, 50 fmol uniformly labeled T7-transcribed
repeat substrate, 20 mM HEPES pH7, 500 mM KCl, 20 mM EDTA, 65-70 C,
30 min. Do not substitute the 2008 lower-salt/Mg recipe. Tables1/2 distinguish
T7 template oligos7+8 from synthetic 30-nt RNA oligo9; do not silently use the
synthetic sequence for every uniformly labeled assay. Reaction volume is not
explicitly repeated in the brief 2010 Methods; the protocol refers to 2008.

Y31A and H46A have no detectable cleavage; K52A has greatly reduced but
nonzero output, reported about 40-fold lower at 500 nM. This is an endpoint
yield comparison, not kcat. Figure4 doses are 1,50,200,500 nM. All mutants
retain stable shifted RNP; K52A has somewhat reduced binding. WT reactions
can bind products. Y31/H46/K52 map outside PF01881, in PF21350.
Their acid/base/transition-state roles are proposed, not experimentally assigned.
The Discussion's alternative sentence repeats the same acid/base assignments;
no opposite assignment is inferred from that wording.

Figure2: 0.1 pmol synthetic end-labeled repeat preincubated with 0.001,0.05,
0.2,1 uM Cas6 for 30 min at 65-70 C, in 20 mM HEPES pH7/500 mM KCl.
Pb(II) acetate 15 mM then 10 min room temperature, quenched with 20 mM EDTA;
or RNase A 10 pg then 15 min at 37 C. Strong protection from Pb cleavage
at nt2-8, and from RNase A at U3/C5/U8. Cas6's own cleavage products remain
visible. Absence of detected protection elsewhere is not proof of no contact.

Figure5A: anti-Cas6 immune resin, unlike preimmune resin, carries repeat-cleaving
activity; possible copurifying enzymes are not excluded by the IP. Figure5B
coIP/Northern associates Cas6 with 1x/2x processing intermediates, whereas Cmr2
recovers mature species. Association is not a causal stability/retention assay
or proof that Cas6 performs the later trimming step. No such graph edge added.
