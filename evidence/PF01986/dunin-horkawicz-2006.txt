Interpretation: the authors offer two alternatives for cooperation between
Endonuclease III and a COG1833 GIY-YIG region: lesion-flanking cleavage or
exonucleolytic extension of an AP-lyase nick. These are computationally motivated
proposals, not perturbation results. This record instantiates them only for the
named MJ0613 fusion, with joint prerequisites kept together. It does not assign
standalone DUF123 glycosylase activity. Other repair roles remain possible.

The discussion warns against transferring an Endonuclease III annotation to
COG1833-only proteins. C-terminal Cys/His conservation motivates a structural
metal-binding proposal, not a measured metal identity or catalytic requirement.
Neither graph asserts active residues, strand polarity, a particular lesion,
reaction conditions, physiological repair flux, or a completed repair pathway.
The relation between these two proposals is alternative, not sequential.
