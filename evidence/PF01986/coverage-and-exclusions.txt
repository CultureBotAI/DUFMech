PF01986 scoped search, exclusions and discriminating follow-up
Research date: 2026-10-16 UTC

Local discovery used rg --no-ignore --hidden in curation, evidence, history,
reviews, reports, docs, .claude and .agents. Before this edit only the structural
inventory matched the exact family; DUF1231 and longer numeric names were not
treated as DUF123. This is a bounded local search, not literature completeness.

Web queries included exact PF01986, DUF123, COG1833, MJ0613 and MMP0538 paired
with nuclease, enzyme activity, assay, glycosylase or experimental. Inspected
the 2006 publisher full text/PDF, computational methods, COG1833 subsection and
Figures 1-2; UniProt, InterPro, ENA and RCSB public metadata. These inspected
sources did not supply a measured MJ0613 DUF123 causal reaction. This does not
establish that no experiment exists in all literature or for every member.

Other exclusions and access/coverage limits:
- Clan membership, predicted structure, conservation and protein networks alone
  do not supply causal edges. Vendor annotations and AnnoDUF UI examples are not
  assays. Other GIY-YIG families' experimental activities are not transferred.
- The 2006 paper's M. maripaludis GI45358100 / MMP0538 pairing has not been
  reconciled to current sequence/locus data; no M. maripaludis graph is adopted.
- Aravind et al. 1999 (PMID:9973609; doi:10.1093/nar/27.5.1223), cited for the
  original domain prediction, remains a full-text coverage gap. PMC returned a
  browser challenge and the attempted publisher page failed; no bypass used.
- The JBC 2004 Endonuclease III engineering search lead and other primary member
  studies were not fully inspected. No E. coli residue experiment is assigned
  to the archaeal appended DUF. Searches are not a systematic citation census.
- dbfetch returned HTML rather than FASTA; it was rejected. ENA's EMBL endpoint
  supplied the parsed CDS instead. A successful HTTP response is not validation.

Open work:
1. Find and authenticate primary activity/perturbation studies and distinguish
   full-length MJ0613 from its N-terminal Endonuclease III and appended DUF.
2. Discriminate lesion-flanking cleavage from exonucleolysis using substrate and
   product mapping, matched protein integrity controls and domain-specific tests;
   establish metal dependence and residues only with appropriate interventions.
3. Resolve additional member identities, standalone-domain partners and original
   literature, without extrapolating one fusion's proposal to the full family.
These are research gaps, not observed negative biological results.
