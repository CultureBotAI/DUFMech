Figure2A-B: pMID-herA-T E233S derivative transformed with pSSR vectors and
12uM simvastatin selection, then5-FOA/uracil/simvastatin counterselection.
E233S is REY15A delta-pyrEF delta-lacS, cultured75C. WT herA and D176E plasmids
permit recovery of chromosomal herA deletions, confirmed by flanking PCR and
plasmid sequencing. Empty vector survivors instead retain the chromosomal gene
and show marker escape. The two positive rescue edges are indirect cellular
effects under this scheme, not direct proof of a particular repair intermediate.
Other five mutant transformants were not obtained in the initial transformation:
their failure is not a clean comparison among established deletion strains.
Overexpression/dominant-negative alternatives and selection context matter.

Figure2C-D: WT HerA alone has basal ATPase activity, DNA increases it, NurA does
not further stimulate it under these conditions. D176E and E356D retain reduced
ATPase activity, but only D176E yielded the successful deletion-complementation
outcome. Therefore residual ATPase alone does not predict the cellular result.
Do not import the S. solfataricus NurA-dependence to this species unqualified.

Figure4B-C and Figure5A: in a34/84nt3'-overhang DNA assay, D176E/NurA produces
more exonuclease-class products than WT, whereas E356D/NurA produces fewer.
E356D retains endonuclease-class activity; it is not a complete nuclease-null.
Figure5B blunt34/34 substrate gives D176E approximatelyWT exonuclease activity;
do not transfer the overhang increase to blunt DNA. Figures4 and5 separate native
gel products and denaturing controls supporting the product assignments.
Methods:20ul,50mM Tris-HCl pH8,50mM NaCl,10mM MgCl2,1mM DTT,1mM ATP,
0.001percent BSA,1nM radiolabeled DNA,65C30min (timecourse up to30min).
Figure4 caption27.8nM HerA hexamer plus83.4nM NurA dimer. This is a mixture
in excess NurA, not evidence of a6:6 complex stoichiometry.

Whole-protein biochemistry and complementation are separate graphs: no observed
edge directly links one in-vitro product fraction to cell viability. Folding,
assembly and cellular protein amounts are not resolved for every variant.
Main text reports no detectable unwinding with HerA alone or D58A NurA; this
differs from the solfataricus assay. It is an assay-specific negative, not proof
that all PF01935 domains lack unwinding capacity.
