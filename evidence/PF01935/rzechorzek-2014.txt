Inspected publisher full text, primary XML, main PDF and supplement text.
Visually inspected Figure4A-D and supplement FiguresS4-S5. Not raw-data reanalysis.
Untagged HerA and NurA, native expression clones; functional variants generated
from wild type. All graph claims concern full proteins and their complexes.

Figure4A: phosphate-release ATPase assay with D58A nuclease-inactivated NurA.
Supplement pp13-14:200ul,20mM Tris-HCl pH8,100mM NaCl,5percent glycerol,
5mM MgCl2,400uM ATP,400nM DNA when supplied,30nM complex (HerA6:NurA2).
Preassembly20min60C. Blunt or15nt 5'/3' overhang duplex substrates; three repeats.
K154A and R142A/R381A lower release to undetectable levels. R381A reduces it,
not completely for blunt DNA. R142A,K363A,D471A increase it for tested duplexes.
Graphs select the5' overhang comparison so different substrates are not pooled.
Increased turnover is not evidence of increased translocation or repair.
The R142A/D471A no-DNA result and other double mutants remain uncurated followups.

Figure4B and S4: intact5.3kb PCR DNA decreases over0,5,10,20,40min with complete
WT HerA/WT NurA/ATP reaction. Quantified triplicates and S4 controls support
activity of the joint preparation, not HerA-intrinsic nuclease chemistry.
K154A,K363A,D471A reduce processing. R381A delays early processing, with the
40min endpoint approaching WT; do not describe it as complete nuclease loss.
Supplement p12 specifies5mM MnCl2,3mM ATP,20mM Tris pH8,100mM NaCl,1mM DTT,
5percent glycerol,BSA,60C and1.25nM final DNA. Figure/S4 report2uM complex;
Methods use2.5uM preassembly followed by dilution into40ul. Main Methods say
5.5kb, but Figure/S4/primer-based supplement say5.3kb. Preserve those discrepancies
instead of inventing one reconciled concentration. Only directional claims made.

Figure4C/S5: native full HerA with D58A NurA unwinds54bp duplex plus25nt5' tail.
Boiled and no-protein controls,30min60C,10nM DNA; caption0.46uM complex,
Methods0.5uM. Supplement p12 includes3mM ATP,10mM magnesium acetate plus
5mM MgCl2,Tris acetate,potassium acetate,NaCl,DTT,glycerol,BSA.
K154A and R381A strongly reduce unwound product; K363A reduces it modestly.
D471A is NOT an unwinding-loss result here: plotted product is above WT despite
lower triplex displacement and destruction. R142A also increases unwinding.
Do not collapse these distinct readouts into one generic helicase-activity edge.

Figure4D: K154A,D471A,G465STOP lower released triplex-forming oligonucleotide
compared with WT HerA/D58A NurA. 5nM triplex,25mM MES pH5.5,50mM NaCl,
10mM MgCl2,0.5mM spermidine,3mM ATP; preassemble30min60C,assay50C.
Methods use SwaI-linearized7500bp pLKS5 and22nt TFO; figure cartoon describes
approximately5kb duplex. No quantitative translocation speed is assigned.

Mutation effects are observed; their direct molecular explanations are unresolved.
Static AMP-PNP-bound HerAm10 structure plus these assays suggest, but do not
directly film, a brace/arginine/pore-loop chemo-mechanical cycle or its ordering.
Supplement primer heading K142A conflicts with R142 in sequence/results;
S6 demonstrates mosaic rings using G465 truncation, not directly K154A doping.
These caveats constrain later doping/structure graphs. No such graph added now.
