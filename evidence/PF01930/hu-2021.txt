Object: GSU0057/Q74H36v1 full-length559aa natural Cas4/Cas1 fusion from
G.sulfurreducens DSM12127, with GSU0058 Cas2. PF01930 matches15-197 only.
Recombinant proteins are expressed in E.coli, affinity tags cleaved, purified
by SEC. Methods pET28a versus supplement pET28b naming is unresolved; exact
residual scar is not claimed. Purified fusion/complex effects are not isolated
Cas4-domain sufficiency. Neither Cas1 integration nor ExoI trimming is
misattributed to the Pfam domain's catalytic center.

Figure3f: matched PAM-free, single-PAM and dual-PAM prespacers, EMSA timecourse
0.5,1,2,5min,2nM DNA/50nM complex at4C;50mM Tris pH8,100mM KCl,
5mM TCEP,5mM MnCl2,10%glycerol. PAM-bearing substrates favor full shifted
complex relative to no-PAM control. This is complex assembly, not proof that
binding alone activates cleavage. Density snapshots do not measure docking rates.

Figure3b andED8a: E.coli ExoI trims the non-PAM overhang to approximately7nt
in the Cas4/1-2 complex, while PAM-side is protected relative to ExoI alone.
10nM prespacer,250nM complex,37C preincubation;50mM Tris pH8,100mM KCl,
10%glycerol,5mM TCEP,5mM MnCl2 plus10mM MgCl2. ExoI titration and time
controls are shown. Native Gsu processing nuclease identity is not established.

Figure3d andED8b: composite Cas4/1-2, ExoI and supercoiled pCRISPR context
produces non-PAM-side leader-proximal integration; restriction-release product
sizes resolve orientation. Controls omit ExoI or target. This is a joint
reconstitution, not Cas4 alone sufficient for integration. ED7 reports failure
on tested linear leader-repeat DNA; that conditional result is not universal.

ED8c-e: annealed half-integration mimic compared with free prespacer gives more
PAM cleavage, with temperature dependence and three independent experiments.
It is a synthetic mimic, not proof that a preceding integration actually occurred
in that sample. ED8m supports full integration in the prepared complex, but
cryo-EM states do not by themselves establish live Cas4 dissociation kinetics.
Figure4d: shortening repeat from a reach-capable30bp Sub3 to19bp Sub2 reduces
PAM-cleavage enhancement. Sub3 promotes cleavage despite no full integration;
longer Sub4 supports integration. Retain measured length/context effects,
not a resolved allosteric signal or proven K102 side-chain switch.

ED4f: E18Y changes cleavage from PAM-selective to distributive; S191A largely
retains Gsu specificity with weakened activity. DoubleE18Y/S191A shows partial
Pfu-like preference on a distributive background. Structure contact predictions
do not make every contact residue an experimentally established causal step.
ED4g has a material source conflict: Results says the double mutant does NOT
support Pfu-PAM spacer integration; the caption says it does. The original gel
and near-baseline bar agree more closely with Results. The publisher caption
also contains the affirmative statement. No unqualified cellular PAM-switch
edge is adopted; exact corrected result remains a follow-up.

Figure1b/c: E.coli BL21-AI containing Gsu module pTU338 and pCRISPR pTU368,
PCR array expansion and sequencing after supplied prespacer challenge, n3 for
PCR. Correctly spaced ssPAM supports expansion; noPAM, adjacentPAM anddsPAM
substrates do not show comparable expansion. Single-PAM inputs bias orientation;
dualPAM yields mixed orientations. Keep indirect cellular effects separate from
purified-protein mechanisms and native Gsu function. There is no infection
challenge or measured increased antiviral fitness in this experiment.
