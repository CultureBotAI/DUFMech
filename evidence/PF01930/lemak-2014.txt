Tested full-length 6His-tagged Pcal_0546, A3MTK6 sequencev1, not an isolated
57-210 domain. Cloning refers to an earlier method; exact retained tag/scar
and oligo table sequences were not independently resolved in this pass.

Figure2A: protein dose series0.12-2.5uM, 0.1uM end-radiolabeled linear ssDNA,
15min45C, default50mM Tris pH8,5mM MgCl2,1mM DTT; protein-free laneC.
Different product ladders from 3'- versus5'-labeling support the authors'
5'-to-3' exonuclease interpretation. The directly retained edge is substrate
cleavage, not an independently filmed directional translocation mechanism.
Figure2E uses a distinct high-concentration assay:55uM enzyme and10ug dC10,
LC-MS against dC/dCMP standards. Retention/mass data support phosphate-bearing
products, assigned by the authors to3'-phosphate/5'-hydroxyl termini. Do not
claim a CRISPR-integration-ready3'-OH from these Pcal products.

Figure2C: circular M13mp18 ssDNA5nM, Pcal0.9,1.9,3.7,4.6uM at45C,
protein-free laneC, agarose/SYBR endpoint. Circular-substrate processing supports
endonucleolysis. Methods default lists2mM MgCl2 for endonuclease assays, whereas
Results describes Mn-dependent activity. The exact Figure2C metal assignment is
not resolved here; no metal-specific necessity edge is retained.

Figure3A: splayed-arm substrates with21bp duplex and21/22nt overhang models,
one3'-radiolabeled strand;0.25-9.8uM Pcal,45min45C, laneC no protein.
Products extend below the duplex-length region. Retain net processing, not a
separate observed ATP-driven helicase step; transient duplex breathing and
coupled processing are not excluded. The ATP comparison is in uninspected S3.

Figure5A/C: individual D123A,E136A,Q149A,Y153A andY166A proteins show strongly
reduced exonuclease and circular-substrate processing versusWT. Exo246nM15min;
circular assay2.2uM10min45C. Figure5E uses1.9uM45min45C and shows residual
processing for several motif mutants, so they are not universally inactive.
F141A remains active; its proposed product-exit-channel widening is a hypothesis,
not a directly observed elementary mechanism. K138A andH146A did not yield
soluble protein and were not assayed as purified inactive enzymes.

Individual C64A,C200A,C203A,C209A variants lose visible color/320-550nm
absorbance according to Results, supporting disrupted assembled cluster signal.
Figure5A/C/E retains DNA cleavage, with nonidentical band patterns. In5C Cys
variants are1.1uM versus2.2uM other variants; do not infer identical rates from
these gels or general Fe-S dispensability in cells. The proposed stabilizing
extra beta sheet is not an observed causal rescue. Cluster mutation can affect
folding and metal occupancy as well as a specific catalytic step.

The study does not demonstrate Pcal-dependent cellular spacer acquisition,
its native partner complex, PAM selection or a native physiological DNA target.
Pcal's locus is outside the Cas operon; CRISPR/non-CRISPR roles remain open.
SSO1391 andSSO0001 results are deliberately excluded from Pcal graph edges.
