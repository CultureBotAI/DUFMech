Assay facts: pHIS8-ygbP, BL21(DE3); purified kinetic preparations undergo
thrombin cleavage. TLC/phosphorimaging measures labeled CDP-ME.
Kinetics: 23 C, 5 ul, 398 nmol Tris-HCl, 40 nmol MgCl2, 79 nmol NaOH,
0.7 U pyrophosphatase; 6/12-min samples. Substrates vary across nine
concentrations; cosubstrate amounts differ by mutant. CTP-series turnover
per second: WT 54.1, K27S 2.05, D106E 0.93; T140V 1.8 and R109A 0.26
include the source's MEP-unsaturation correction. Independent assay sets
number at least two; individual fits and between-run variability differ.
The assay's final pH is not independently established from these amounts.

Crude screen: 217 nmol labeled MEP, 81.3 nmol CTP, 400 nmol Tris-HCl,
40 nmol MgCl2, 79 nmol NaOH, nominal 1 ug YgbP-containing crude sample,
5 ul; 23 C, 1/15 h. The exact protein-normalization basis is unclear.
D106A, D106N, K213S, K27A, T165V gave no detected activity and were
not purified. Blank lacking YgbP and calibrated TLC are described.
Expression, aggregation and folding remain alternative contributors.

Pulse-chase facts: labeled CTP preincubation versus label-in-chase control;
2 mM CTP/2.2 mM enzyme pulse; chase 10 mM each CTP/MEP, 100 mM Tris
pH 8, 10 mM MgCl2, 2 mM DTT, 4.48 U pyrophosphatase; 5-s sampling.
Early labeled product is higher after preloading CTP; MEP does not show
that separation. Figure 3's percent axis and prose >70% are inconsistent;
do not adopt an exact trapped fraction. Its displayed series ends at 40 s,
while Methods describes 50 s. The MEP-pulse concentration/mass pair is
also inconsistent with a 25.7-kDa reference. These do not establish a
literal atomic binding trajectory or a universal absence of MEP binding.
Table footnote T104V differs from T140V elsewhere. K27S corrected MEP
and CTP turnover values differ materially; do not silently average them.
The manuscript expressly leaves CTP inhibition's physiological role open.
