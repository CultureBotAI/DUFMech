ProBiS binding-site comparison generated a proposed DNA complex from 1VPQ
and endonuclease IV structure 2NQJ. No experimental Tm1631 DNA-binding or
cleavage assay is reported. Modeled Y47/Y48 interactions and an unpaired base
are not mutagenesis or demonstrated lesion specificity. The controls are
simulations of unbound Tm1631 and the endonuclease IV complex.
Methods describe 90/60/15 ns simulations at 310 K, not native high-temperature
activity. The last 20 ns supplied MM/GBSA estimates with entropy omitted under
an equal-entropy assumption; these are not measured affinities.
Supplement methods explicitly describe temporary base-pair restraints at
21 and 43 ns. A later illustrative active-site model additionally forced the
phosphate toward R145/R191 before restraint removal. Alternative poses had
clashes. These assumptions limit treating trajectory stability as validation.
The beta-clamp connection is motif-based inference, not a demonstrated DUF72
catalytic pathway. The 2016 work extends this same modeled system, not an
independent experimental replication. Other family members remain unassessed.
