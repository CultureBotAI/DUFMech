Table S1 identifies all 21 donors as non-essential and from This Study;
Table S16 identifies their deletion::Cm alleles in Hfr Cavalli. Protocol S16
identifies the recipient deletion collection as Keio, with Kan markers.
Reference b1868/JW1857 maps to YecE P37348, but exact historical recipient
clone sequences and all donor junctions were not independently sequenced here.
The main screen used rich LB with dual selection and 36 h outgrowth at 32 C.
Protocol S2 specifies two screens, four biological recipient colonies, eight
measurements per pair, averaged GI scores, and exclusion within 10 kb of donors.
The full dataset also combines 39 earlier screens; these 21 donors are explicitly
identified as new in Table S1 and are not called independent repeated studies.
Protocol S2 gives thresholds <=-3.3 and >=3.1 after Z-score filtering;
main prose simplifies this to magnitude >=3. All 21 rows meet both criteria.

The comparison is normalized double-mutant colony growth versus a multiplicative
expectation from individual-mutant fitness, NOT growth versus WT or a measured
change after adding just one deletion. Sixteen pairs aggravate and five alleviate.
Positive S-scores do not mean WT rescue, faster absolute growth or binding.
Each pair is one joint perturbation; there are no gene-to-gene causal arrows.
No row-specific replicate dispersion, raw colony images, exact fitness fractions,
allele-specific rescue or independent growth validation was inspected for YecE.
Potential marker, polar and secondary-mutation effects remain. These cellular
effects do not demonstrate nuclease chemistry, a specific biochemical pathway,
or a common mechanism shared by all partner genes or all DUF72 members.
