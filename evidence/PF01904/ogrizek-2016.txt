The downloaded ZIP contains 48 coordinate subsets; five representative files
were parsed with Bio.PDB, not rerun as quantum calculations or full trajectories.
Minimal quotations: "pentacovalent intermediate";
"Lys73 (here numbered as 72:A)" from the Figure S2 caption.

The starting protein-DNA pose comes from the 2013 simulation. Two waters were
replaced with Mg2+ and a preorganized 42-atom QM region was treated using
B3LYP/6-31G* within CHARMM36. Sixteen interpolated replicas link constructed
reactant/product endpoints. The reported path is a potential-energy profile,
not a measured rate or calculated activation free energy with complete sampling.
Figure 1 proposes nucleophilic attack on DNA phosphate, a pentacovalent state,
then P-O3' cleavage. Figure 3 places replica 5 at a modeled energy minimum;
this is not experimental intermediate trapping. Figure 4 removes the ions;
Figure 5 removes the wider environment and uses a pure-QM setup according to
Methods, despite its QM/MM caption. The latter is not an isolated protein-only
deletion. Each plot uses a relative energy scale. Authors report a 17 kcal/mol
internal barrier without ions and noise below 3 kcal/mol with ions; neither
quantity is adopted as an experimental kinetic parameter or absolute rate gain.
Initial neutral-lysine chemistry, ion count/type and hydroxide generation
remain assumptions or future work. Predicted pKa 9.55 does not establish the
actual protonation population without pH/context or an experimental measurement.
The main-text Lys73 corresponds to native K72 by the explicit supplemental
caption; the PDB/reference has T73. Tables use ASN45/GLN115, consistent with
model numbering shifted from reference N44/Q114. No residue-specific mutation
experiment, physical DNA binding, biological DNA repair or family-wide nuclease
activity was established in this theoretical work. Two hypothetical graph steps
retain the proposed chemistry without adopting the paper's stronger conclusion.
