The following is a curator's factual ledger, not reproduced article text.

Chicken E1C3A0 BEST1 residues 1-405 plus EGEEF tag, expressed in Pichia;
TripleA means the joint I76A/F80A/F84A construct. Reconstituted into 3:1
wt/wt POPE/POPG, with 0.2-1 mg/ml protein and 10 mg/ml lipid for bilayers.
Electrical recordings use 20 mM HEPES-NaOH pH 7.6, 0.21 mM EGTA,
0.19 mM CaCl2 (about 300 nM free calcium), 22-24 C. Symmetric conditions
are 30 mM KCl on both sides. Channels insert in both orientations.
Pulses span -100 to +100 mV for 2 s, from a 0-mV holding potential;
steady currents are averaged over the final 200 ms. Empty-bilayer currents
are subtracted. Error bars use at least three separate bilayer experiments.

Figure 1B-D: adding 10 mM EGTA to both sides strongly decreases WT current.
The displayed B-D traces are from the same representative bilayer.
Figure 1E/F: after cis-side 10 mM EGTA silences that orientation, trans
perfusion varies free calcium from near zero to 350 nM. Calcium increases
current magnitude at -100 mV; n=3, SEM. The fitted K1/2 of 17 +/- 3 nM
is assay-specific, not an independently measured calcium-binding Kd.
Figure 1I/J: DIDS added to both sides decreases current magnitude at
-100 mV, dose series through 1 mM, n=3. Up to 2% DMSO control had no
noticeable effect. This does not identify a binding site or clinical effect.

Figure 3C: TripleA, F80A and F80N each retain more current at +100 mV
after bilateral 10 mM EGTA than WT, normalized to their own calcium-present
current. Three independent bilayers, SEM. This is a retained fraction,
not absolute conductance or single-channel open probability. F80I retains
much stronger calcium dependence than TripleA; the paper describes it as
nearly WT-like. Macroscopic protein incorporation is not precisely known.

Figure 4A/C/D: D301A/D304A decreases the chloride-flux-associated ACMA
quench under 2 uM external free calcium, compared with WT; adding TripleA
to that double-mutant background restores quench. Protein 0.1 mg/ml and
lipid 10 mg/ml before 100-fold assay dilution. Reconstitution uses
100 mM Na2SO4, 10 mM HEPES pH 8.2, 1 mM EGTA; assay uses 125 mM NaCl
and 1 mM EGTA or calcium-EGTA. CCCP initiates the coupled proton/anion
readout. Preparations and comparisons are performed in parallel on one day.
Exact replicate count and recording temperature for these traces are not
specified. Empty vesicles were prepared; Figure 4 does not display them.
The cited 2014 protocol supplies dye/ionophore details, not a new 2016
empty-control trace. Sensor-facing orientation limits WT reactivation.
Size-exclusion similarity argues against gross misassembly but does not
independently rule out every folding or incorporation difference.

Neck substitutions do not substantially switch anion/cation selectivity.
Open probability and conductance cannot be separated by these measurements.
Proposed quadrupole interactions and gating movements are not directly
tracked microsteps. Aperture V205, later inactivation, cryo-EM and GABA
experiments remain additional contexts, not adopted edges in this review.
