The described construct is chicken BEST1 residues 1-405 plus EGEEF.
MS and Edman degradation identify Thr2 after initiator-methionine removal.
The 2014 gating flux buffer uses 125 mM NaCl, 10 mM HEPES-NaOH pH 8.1,
0.5 mg/ml BSA, 2 uM ACMA and mixtures of 1 mM EGTA/calcium-EGTA.
CCCP is 1 uM, added after 120 s; fluorescence readings every 30 s use
410/490-nm excitation/emission and initial-reading normalization.
The signal is acidification coupled to anion influx, not a direct
chloride-concentration measurement. The 2016 source states that it uses
this protocol but specifies changed reconstitution conditions separately.
Do not borrow a 2014 empty-vesicle trace, replicate count, or exact recording
temperature for the 2016 comparisons, or claim that structural models show
an experimentally tracked sequence of gating motions.
