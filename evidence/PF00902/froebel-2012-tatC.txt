Curator factual extract, not a full-text copy:
Figures 1A, 2A, 3A and 4A compare radiolabeled TorA-MalE335 processing
and proteinase-K protection. MalE lacks 35 C-terminal residues. Tat-positive
inverted membrane vesicles yield protected mature product; TatAC vesicles yield
processed but accessible product. RR-to-KK replacement blocks TatAC processing.
CCCP (0.1 mM; DMSO comparator) removes the protected Tat-positive response.
TatC-only vesicles generate processed, unprotected MalE335; Tat-deleted vesicles
do not produce its properly migrating mature band. Other degradation is present.
Vesicles derive from BL21(DE3) DeltaTat with p8737 (tatABCD), p8737-tatAC,
or pFAT588 (TatC-His6), respectively. They retain native membrane components
and signal peptidase. Vesicles join translation after 10 min, then incubate
25 min at 37 C. Fig. 1B checks Tat protein content.
Processing does not establish mature-domain transport or purified TatC sufficiency.
Fig. 4B is a topology model, not tracked hairpin insertion. Fig. 5 shows substrate
context dependence; natural SufI/AmiC differ from TorA fusions. Their signal/linker
and NapG assays are not additional adopted edges. No raw densitometry or
supplementary datasets were reanalyzed; complete assay buffer and PK protocol
require the cited methods.
