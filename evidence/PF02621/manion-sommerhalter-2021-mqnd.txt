Curator assessment, not an adopted canonical edge:
The reported His6-MqnD reaction supports naphthoate formation and release of
a three-carbon fragment identified by trapping, coelution and isotope labeling.
Unprotected product oxidizes readily; the anaerobic assay and trapping assay
are different contexts. The supplement provides enzyme preparation and assay
conditions but no clone accession, sequence or locus for MqnD in inspected text.
Q9K9F8/BH2689 is a candidate reference, not a verified identity of that clone.
An accession-bearing construct record, sequence or unambiguous primary mapping
is needed before this experiment is attached to a protein-grounded causal graph.
This is an identity gap, not a claim that MqnD chemistry lacks evidence.
