Curator's experimental ledger, not additional quotation:
- Results and original Figure 4D: recombinant Pyrococcus furiosus PF0930
  produced 6-hydroxymethyl-7,8-dihydropterin diphosphate from 6-HMD with ATP.
  The measured chromatogram follows iodine oxidation. Product assignment uses
  retention, optical spectra, and desalted LC-ESI-MS ions at 354.1/352.1 plus
  MRM fragments matching a standard. Crude-mixture MS initially failed through
  suspected ion suppression. A minor monophosphate peak has uncertain origin;
  the authors suggest workup hydrolysis, not a demonstrated second enzyme step.
- Figure 4D caption reports zero-time, no-added-enzyme and matching MonoQ
  fractions from a non-PF0930-expressing E. coli extract as negative controls.
  The displayed trace concerns PF0930. The same caption reports similar
  results for MJ1634; do not relabel this trace or its approximately 90% yield
  as separately displayed MJ1634 data.
- Methods: 200 uL, 3.5 ng PF0930 or MJ1634, 100 uM 6-HMD, 1 mM ATP,
  8 mM MgCl2, 16 mM DTT, 40 mM TES/KCl pH 7.4; argon, 70 C, 10 minutes.
  Other tested nucleotides retain PF0930 activity (CTP 41%, UTP 40%, GTP 12%
  relative to ATP). ATP preference is not absolute donor specificity.
- Figure 4B and complementation Methods: pBAD24-borne MJ1634 rescues growth
  of E. coli C600 delta-folK::tetB on LB without thymidine after 48 hours.
  Empty vector, E. coli folK and thymidine supplementation are controls.
  Rescue occurs without arabinose induction. This is a heterologous cellular
  result, not an archaeal knockout or a direct measurement of cofactor pools.
- MJ1634 genomic PCR clones were Sanger verified. Biochemical expression used
  pT7-7 in BL21-Codon Plus (DE3)-RIL; PF0930 used modified pET24d with its
  expression/purification referred to Sugar et al. The latter protocol and
  exact construct-derived extra residues remain to be traced.
- Original supplementary Figure 4 (page 9) shows MJ1634 purification and
  identifies the major bands in MonoQ lanes 6/7 by MALDI TOF/TOF. Other lanes
  contain chloramphenicol acetyltransferase. The main text reports >95% purity
  and tryptic MS identity. Results say heat treatment at 80 C, whereas Methods
  and the supplement specify 70 C; retain the discrepancy, not a silent choice.
- No isolated PF01973 truncation, catalytic residue test, quantified kinetics,
  native archaeal flux, or bacterial Maf kinase activity is established here.
  MJ0408/MptD, PF1278 and their aldolase reactions are different targets.
