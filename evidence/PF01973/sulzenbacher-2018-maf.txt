Curator's experimental ledger, not additional quotation:
- Results, original Figure 1, Figure S1 and Figure S4: in AMB-1, deletion of
  amb0685/maf loses the approximately 70-kDa flagellin band, nonulosonic-acid
  modifications, detectable supernatant flagellin, flagellar filaments and
  motility. The approximately 55-kDa intracellular flagellin remains. A short
  linker replacement was designed to minimize polarity. In-trans wild-type
  maf on pAK22 restores the 70-kDa band, filament and motility. Figure S1
  compares wild type and deletion only; export rescue is not shown there.
- MG medium, 30 C and microaerophilic conditions are specified for AMB-1
  manipulation/complementation. The deletion is PCR checked and mutation
  plasmids are sequence verified. Figure 1 uses anti-Fla Westerns and electron
  micrographs; the text describes optical-microscope motility observations.
  No quantitative motility rate or universal effect size is extracted.
- DMB chemistry, LC-MS and tandem peptide MS support a nonulosonic acid.
  Mass alone does not distinguish pseudaminic from legionaminic stereoisomers.
  Biosynthetic context and amb0715 deletion/rescue support pseudaminic acid
  or a related derivative, not an unambiguous stereochemical determination.
  The amb0715 result is not a perturbation of PF01973 and is not a Maf edge.
- Original Figure 5B: E324A and D415A complementation constructs lack the
  70-kDa anti-Fla band; text reports failure to restore motility. S283A,
  D318A and N491A retain those readouts in the tested conditions. A caption
  says N494A, but the actual figure, Methods, Table S2 primers and the current
  sequence support N491A; position 494 is D. The native negative results do
  not prove these other residues dispensable under every condition.
- E324A and D415A are separate whole-protein variants, not a double mutant
  or isolated-domain assay. Maf abundance, folding and localization controls
  are not shown in the inspected mutation experiment. Anti-Fla is not an
  anti-Maf expression control. Loss of complementation does not identify the
  molecular cause or establish catalytic necessity independently of stability.
- Results, Discussion, Figures 4/S7: Glu324's catalytic role and Asp415's
  proposed donor-pocket organizing role derive from structural comparisons
  and docking. No Maf-bound donor was crystallographically observed; soaking
  and CMP-Neu5Ac cocrystallization did not yield the proposed complex. The
  figure's sulfate is not an experimentally bound sugar donor. These causal
  proposals are hypotheses, distinct from observed mutation phenotypes.
- The authors' central structural domain spans 273-530, not the current
  PF01973 profile interval 259-419. The other terminal domains remain part
  of the tested 666-residue protein. Crystal dimer contacts do not establish
  a physiological dimer: SEC-MALLS supports a solution monomer.
