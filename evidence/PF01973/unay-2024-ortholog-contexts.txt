kurthia-glfm-mr1-2024, two edges:
Figure5D lanes1/3 (WT) and5/6 (delta-maf1 delta-maf2) compare pJU0047
Maf/Hag with pJU0046 Maf/GlfM/Hag. The GlfM-containing cassette gives
stronger slow-migrating Hag-HA immunoreactivity in each background.
The host-Maf-null result excludes a requirement for the two native Mafs
in that displayed condition, not all host contributions. The cassette,
Kurthia Maf, acceptor and donor-capable host are joint inputs. There is
no Hag/GlfM-positive but Kurthia-Maf-negative comparator in this panel.

kurthia-pse-mr1-2024, one edge:
Figure5D lanes3/4 compare the same triad in WT and delta-pseI. Loss of
the host Pse-synthesis gene reduces Hag-HA migration retardation; signal
remains detectable. This is not a no-protein control or a direct glycan
structure, donor stereochemistry, occupancy or catalytic-rate measurement.

kurthia-glfm-caulo-2024, one edge:
S5A lanes3/4 compare pair and triad in delta-flmG. GlfM inclusion increases
the migration retardation of Hag-HA. The pair alone retains a partial
shift. GlfM is therefore not represented as universally required.

kurthia-pse-caulo-2024, two edges:
S5A lanes3/5 compare the Maf/Hag pair in delta-flmG versus
delta-flmG delta-neuB; lanes4/6 compare the triad in those same hosts.
Loss of neuB reduces migration retardation in both comparisons.
The Pse-deficient host in S5A is the DOUBLE mutant, not single delta-neuB.
Both backgrounds lack endogenous FlmG. Empty-vector lanes1/2 establish
tagged-signal background, not a Maf-minus/Hag-positive control. Thus no
independent observed Maf-necessity edge is inferred from the caption.

irchel-glfm-caulo-2024, one edge:
S5B lane1 versus GlfM-positive WT lanes3/5 shows increased FLAG-FliC
migration retardation when cognate GlfM is co-expressed with the Irchel
FliC/Maf system. The host is WT Caulobacter, not delta-flmG.

irchel-pse-caulo-2024, one edge:
S5B GlfM-positive WT versus single delta-neuB pairs (lanes3/4 and5/6)
show reduced retardation after loss of neuB. The no-GlfM pair has fast,
detectable bands in both hosts (lanes1/2); vector-only lanes7/8 are
background controls. Endogenous FlmG is present, and no Maf-minus but
FliC/GlfM-positive control is shown. Host enzyme contributions and exact
physical expression-construct sequences remain unresolved.
