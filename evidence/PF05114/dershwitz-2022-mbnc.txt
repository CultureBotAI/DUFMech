Quoted fragment:
MbnC is not required for the formation of the N-terminal oxazolone group

Curator reading: The study's mbnC-deficient system is an mbnABCMN deletion
background complemented with mbnABMN on pWG104, not simply a clean chromosomal
single-gene deletion. Its product retains the N-terminal ring, limiting universal
claims that MbnC is necessary for both rings. This does not itself negate the
scoped purified-complex activity observation.

Source discrepancy: One Results sentence on page 2 says the N-terminal ring is
absent, conflicting with the abstract, neighboring text, NMR interpretation and
Discussion. Do not silently propagate that sentence as a separate causal claim.
