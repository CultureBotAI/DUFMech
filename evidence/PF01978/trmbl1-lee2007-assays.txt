L2 / MALTOTRIOSE COMPETITION READOUT
Locator: Results maltotriose-binding subsection and Methods sugar-binding assay.
At5uM TrmBL1 and0.47uM radiolabelled maltotriose, about180cpm specific signal
out of120000cpm input is reported, removable with5mM unlabelled maltotriose.
The edge represents this competition-associated reduction of retained tracer,
not the addition of protein causing binding without an inspected blank series.
General method:100uL,25mM HEPES pH7.5/300mM KCl;5min70C,2mL ice-cold saturated
ammonium sulphate,10min on ice,0.45um cellulose-nitrate filter,2mL95%-saturated
wash, scintillation counting. The chase's exact timing is not specified.
General titrations use2.5or5uM protein, fixed radiolabel with5-1000uM total sugar;
the separately reported5mM chase lies outside that titration range. The source
describes the small specific signal as reproducible but supplies no raw replicate
counts/errors here. Do not interpret180cpm as a separately reported post-chase
count or claim complete loss of all filter radioactivity. Signal was inadequate
for a fitted affinity constant; the proposed mM-range Km is an extrapolation,
not a measured Kd/Km. Likewise the discussion's >500uM half-saturation estimate
is not a measured binding curve.
Results report no specific maltose or fructose signal, while Methods list
radiolabelled maltose, maltotriose and SUCROSE. Preserve that fructose/sucrose
conflict. A negative precipitation assay does not prove no binding in other
conditions; SEC/EMSA responses cannot repair the missing affinity measurement.
