R0. SHARED IDENTITY AND ASSAY LIMITS
The tested regulator in all experiments below is full-length pSL152-derived
TrmB, not TrmBL1. The thesis calls it P. furiosus PF1743; Lee2003 attributes
pSL152 to T. litoralis. Reference Q9HGZ9 sequence version 1/entry 109 and Q7LYW4
sequence version 1/entry 91 have identical 338-aa bodies. This does not resolve
the physical insert or strain. Reference Pfam intervals are PF01978 11-77,
PF11495 110-234 and PF31112 249-335. All graph claims concern the whole protein;
none establishes isolated PF01978 sufficiency or an atomic DNA contact.
Versioned reference/deposition provenance and construct discrepancies are retained
unchanged in trmb-primary-assays.txt, SHA256
e15a096c12490afde76bbd6367bcdabd42b7103faac3a2db1147f79093aaed62.

Preparation: methods printed pp.39-41/PDF45-47 describe C-terminal His6 protein,
heat treatment and Ni-NTA purification, storage in glucose-containing elution
buffer without dialysis. Added sugar concentrations below are not total free
sugar concentrations. Final carryover, active protein fractions and complete
matched stock compositions remain unresolved. Salt compensation is described,
but it does not establish that every carried component is matched.

Transcription methods printed p.51/PDF57: 25 uL reactions, 150 fmol PCR template,
167 nM TBP, 118 nM TFB, 32 nM P. furiosus endogenous RNAP, 1 uL NTP stock
(ATP/CTP/GTP each 11 mM, UTP 0.67 mM) and 2 uCi alpha-32P-UTP; 80 C for
30 min. These are reconstituted runoff endpoints, not cellular expression,
transporter flux, binding constants, recruitment kinetics or allosteric motion.
Buffer I (printed31/PDF37): 32 mM NaHEPES pH7.3, 0.08 mM EDTA, 250 mM KCl,
4 mM MgCl2. Buffer II: 40 mM NaHEPES pH7.3, 250 mM NaCl, 0.1 mM EDTA,
2.5 mM MgCl2, 5 mM beta-mercaptoethanol, 0.1 mg/mL BSA.
TM: pMLP-derived 484-bp template, usual 156-nt runoff; MD: pSL195-derived
348-bp template, 168-nt runoff. Operator changes can change runoff length.
Quantifications below are published percentages, not independently remeasured
gel intensities. Each panel's no-regulator baseline is normalized separately.
Reported zero is a quantified assay endpoint, not proof of molecular absence.
Replication/error estimates were not established for Figures18-20,22-23,33-35.
Figure21 explicitly averages four experiments; its error-bar definition remains
unresolved. Line joining does not establish a monotonic mechanistic dose curve.

Buffer I; 0.3 uM TrmB. WT core TACT-3nt-AGTA, shown TACTtttAGTA.
TM2 replaces AGTA with GACT; TM4 replaces TACT with CTGA; TM6 expands the
three-T spacer to six T residues. Figure18B lanes1-5 use mutant DNA, lanes6-8
WT DNA. TrmB absent in lanes1/6, present in lanes2-5/7-8.
Added maltose (mM), lanes1-8: 0,0,1,2.5,5,0,0,5.
TM2 panel output: 100,83,100,83,85,100,27,45.
TM4 panel output: 100,110,115,89,89,100,26,115.
TM6 panel output: 100,110,91,50,80,100,0,115.
TM2/TM6 yield shorter products than WT's 156 nt; TM4 retains that length.
Supported: under no-added-maltose conditions, each operator change increases
the fraction of its own basal output retained with TrmB (83 vs27, 110 vs26,
110 vs0). This is a promoter perturbation phenotype, not an absolute output
comparison or direct binding-affinity measurement. Altered basal initiation or
product stability are not separated by those normalizations. TM6's maltose
series 110,91,50,80 is nonmonotonic; do not call all mutant sugar responses null.

R2. MDTA ENGINEERED PROMOTER: FIGURE19, PRINTED67/PDF73
The MD background's TrmB site is replaced with the TM site; the original diagram
was inspected, not a complete independently verified plasmid insert. Buffer I.
Order: no regulator, TrmB, TrmB+25mM maltose, TrmB+25mM maltotriose.

describes the maltotriose change as a 26% decrease; the table establishes a

Figure21 has NO TrmB or maltose and uses buffer II, not Figure20's buffer I.

it does not fully exclude a machinery/buffer contribution under Figure20's

promoter occupancy or DNA-affinity changes. Panel baselines differ; no potency
ranking or universal sugar-to-DNA mechanism is inferred.

REMAINING GAPS
Complete physical construct/taxon identity, active fractions, glucose carryover,
matched controls and raw replicated quantification remain open (issues228/229).
Promoter/anti-inducer coverage is extended, not finished (issue230). TrmBL1 as
the tested regulator, other paralogs/taxa, native physiology, direct DNA contacts
and isolated-domain function still require independent primary assessment.
The observed transcription endpoints must not be joined to hypothetical RNAP
recruitment, uninspected older EMSAs or other protein members as a proven chain.
