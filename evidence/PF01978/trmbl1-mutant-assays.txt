remain unresolved; promoter assignment here follows the title/results,not
independently verified physical fragments. No silent TMMD-to-FBPase correction.

Figure53A,source-assigned PFK,all proteins0.7uM:
lanes1:none,2:WT,3:E40N,4:D50N,5:E81A,6:ED85AA.
WT gives an upper retarded-DNA band. Replacing WT with E40N or D50N at the
same dose reduces that distinct upper band and leaves predominantly free DNA.
This supports qualitative retardation loss,not proof of zero affinity.
E81A and ED85AA retain upper bands; no exact WT-equivalent affinity is measured.
Figure53B,source-assigned FBPase,all proteins0.5uM:
left image1:none,2:WT,3:E40N,4:D50N;right image5:none,6:E81A,7:ED85AA.
E40N/D50N reduce the distinct WT-associated upper band in the left image.
E81A/ED85AA show retarded species in the separate right image,which lacks
its own WT lane. Do not fit or rank affinities across the image panels.
FBPase template cloning discrepancy (EcoRI/HindIII versus EcoRI/BamHI) remains
in the prior ledger. Neither EMSA nor promoter identity alone proves TGM-specific
base contacts or a causal effect on native metabolism.

M4. FAILED OWN-PROMOTER BINDING ASSAYS AND MODEL LIMITS
Printed119/PDF125 explicitly reports unsuccessful own-promoter EMSA/footprint
experiments due to DNA/TrmBL1 aggregation retained in gel wells. This is an
assay limitation,not an observation that E81A/ED85AA cannot bind the promoter.
The authors infer two region-dependent recognition modes from the combination
of transcription,EMSA and footprints (printed115-120 and136/PDF121-126,142).
The proposed first region includes E40/D50 (predicted32-53);the proposed
second includes E81/E85/D86 (predicted80-99,outside reference PF01978).
Keep regional contributions to occupancy HYPOTHESIZED,not observed atomic
recognition helices. No cis-TGM replacement control,isolated-domain sufficiency,
direct residue-base contacts or intact complex structure is established here.
Printed136 discusses a TrmB dimerization helix,not direct experimental proof
of identical TrmBL1 contacts; altered folding/assembly is an alternative.
Do not turn the two substitutions in ED85AA into separate sufficient causes.
