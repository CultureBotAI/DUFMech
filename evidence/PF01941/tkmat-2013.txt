Minimal quotation: "Activity assay of TkMAT."

Figure 3 HPLC traces show SAM in the complete recombinant TkMAT reaction,
absent from the no-enzyme control; ATP and SAM references identify peaks.
Methods: N-terminal His-tagged enzyme expressed in E. coli; 37 C, 3 min,
100 mM Tris-HCl pH8, 20 mM MgCl2, 200 mM KCl, 10 mM ATP and methionine,
0.5 mg protein/ml, acid quench and SCX-HPLC. The measured product is SAM;
Figure 1's triphosphate-hydrolysis scheme is not a TkMAT phosphate assay.

Supplement S3/Table 1: apparent Km(Met)=0.31+/-0.07 mM,
Km(ATP)=6.54+/-2.49 mM; Vmax=1.95+/-0.26 umol/min/mg. The other substrate
is held at 10 mM. Different-temperature literature comparators are not a
controlled enzyme comparison. Figure 3 and supplement page 2/S2-S3 visually
inspected; supplemental PDF has four pages and all captions were read.

Apo crystallography and cross-protein superpositions suggest active-site
roles and loop gating; no residue-specific catalytic perturbation is measured
here. The open helix is resolved, not an observed opening trajectory.
