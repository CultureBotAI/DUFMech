Exact short extract: "very low level"

The experiments use synthesized, codon-optimized genes expressed in E. coli;
purified KpnAspDH (K. pneumoniae34618) and DelAspDH (Delftia sp.Cs1-4)
carry C-terminal His6 tags. Table2 reports oxidative and reductive activity;
Methods define these by NAD(P)H absorbance at340nm, not product-specific MS.
OAA reduction uses ammonium chloride at pH8.2; oxidation uses L-Asp atpH10.
These conditions must not be merged with the2003 Thermotoga conditions.

Figure2 visibly assigns pH8.5 optimum to Kpn andpH8.0 toDel, agreeing
with Results; Discussion reverses those assignments. Figure3b shows
markedly greater Del thermal stability under the20-minute preincubation.
Figure1A includes separate C/C-prime lanes plus1-6/1-prime-6-prime;
its legend lists seven identities against six numbered lanes. Figure1B's
purified lanes1/2 are expresslyKpn/Del. Do not silently normalize theA-lane
legend or infer quantitative purity from the image alone.
Reu/Bja low crude NADPH rates are reversed between Results andTable2,
and the Results pointer toTable1 points to primers rather than activity.
Kpn crude NADH activity was not detected, whereas Table4 reports NADH
kinetics for purifiedKpn; this is a context-dependent discrepancy, not a
demonstration that all Kpn preparations cannot useNADH.
