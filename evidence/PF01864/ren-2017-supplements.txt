The following are numerical facts and curator interpretation, not copied prose.

N=65, Figure S3: chromatogram and spectrum visually inspected. Product extracted
at m/z1020.54-1020.56, retention time28.46 min, spectrum1020.55. No independent
PPi measurement is established by this product-ion assignment.

N=66, Figure S4: visually inspected. Ion additions 10 mM each. Adding KCl or
LiCl enhances the Mg-containing reaction versus Mg alone; NaCl does not show
similar enhancement. Mn supports activity, but monovalent ions do not enhance
it similarly. Ca has low residual signal, Zn near baseline; avoid literal-zero
claims. The plotted signal is DDM-normalized product ion counts. CTP series
with Mg plus K gives reported Km1.28 mM and Vmax2.1 umol/min/mg, but 1-hour
endpoint Methods do not independently verify an initial-rate regime.

N=67, Figure S5: visually inspected. D41A and G54A, as well as R51A/D55A, have
lower product output than WT. R51A output differs numerically from Figure4;
do not pool separate panels or calculate cross-panel fold changes.

N=69, Figure S7: visually inspected. Each D8A/W9A/D12A/E16A/N82A/Y139A has
lower product signal than WT; W9A retains substantial output. D8/W9 are outside
Pfam12-174. These are whole-protein perturbation effects, not evidence that the
DUF domain itself contains those sites or that every site is chemically catalytic.

N=74, Table S2: text and original table image inspected. Kd in uM:
WT+Mg 0.2 +/-0.02; WT-Mg 1.6 +/-0.18.
N28A, P31A, K57A, D100A, K107A, D125A: n.d.
D41A 2.1 +/-0.16; R51A 3.4 +/-0.34; G54A 1.05 +/-0.11;
D55A 0.76 +/-0.07; T58A 8.85 +/-1.41; R108A 1.95 +/-0.25;
R114A 4.6 +/-3.39; D122A 1.4 +/-0.07.
Table reports representative parameters from three technical replicates, not
three independent protein preparations. Error statistic is not independently
established. n.d. is an assay detection result, not infinite Kd or zero binding.
WT fitted sites N=0.36/+Mg and0.31/-Mg; endogenous CTP co-purification explains
substoichiometric values. Do not infer occupancy or protein stoichiometry from N.
Main Figure1 caption has a concentration-unit typo; use TableS2 uM, not M.
Main prose rounds WT-Mg differently (1.67 versus table1.6).
