Methods: intact ApCarS-His6, pET21b, E. coli BL21(DE3), membrane extraction in
1% DDM, Ni-NTA and size exclusion. Activity: 0.5 uM protein, 50 mM Tris pH7.5,
10 mM MgCl2, 75 mM NaCl, 0.1% DDM, 125 mM imidazole, 5% glycerol, 2 mM CTP,
synthetic DGGGP. Its concentration is not specified in the 2017 paragraph;
do not silently borrow a concentration from the 2014 study.
WT: 1 h at 37, 60 or 90 C; substitutions: 1 h at 90 C. EDTA: 20 mM.
Figure 1D: product signal increases across those temperatures; EDTA lowers it.
No-added-CTP and EDTA traces have small residual signal, particularly at 90 C.
Purified protein carried endogenous CTP. Neither comparator is an absolute null.
HPLC-MS product assignment: CDP-archaeol [M-H]- m/z1020.55, also Figure S3.
The endpoint signal is not an initial rate or a separately measured PPi yield.

Figure 4A/B: each N28A, P31A, R51A, D55A, K57A, T58A, D100A, K107A, R108A,
R114A, D122A and D125A lowers product signal versus WT. P31A retains product
despite undetected ITC binding; R51A/D55A/R114A also have appreciable residual
product. Low plotted values are not proof of zero turnover. Two plotted points
per activity condition do not establish biological replicate independence.

Figure 6C, inspected in complete publisher image:
https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fcr.2017.122/MediaObjects/41422_2017_Article_BFcr2017122_Fig6_HTML.jpg
Lipid strips: 0.4 ul of 0.005 mM DGGGP on Hybond-C; 0.08 ug protein overnight
at 4 C, anti-His and HRP/ECL detection; WT and lipid-alone comparators.
Buffer: 10 mM Tris pH7.5, 100 mM NaCl, 0.02% DDM, 0.01% Tween20; milk blocking.
Each D122A/D125A: no detectable strip binding. Each N28A/K57A/D100A: weaker
signal. Each A29W/L124W/F126A/L156W: no detectable binding. Each Q123A/H157A:
stronger signal. Intensities are not Kd; protein folding/tag exposure can matter.
Figure 6D/E: all six tail-site variants have lower product signal than WT,
including Q123A/H157A. Stronger lipid-strip binding is NOT increased catalysis.
L156W and L124W retain visible product; do not describe all four as strict nulls.

Discussion and Figure 7 caption: proposed phosphate-head attack on CTP alpha-P,
with Mg/N28/K57/D100 assistance and putative leaving-group stabilization.
Another proposed role is TM5 motion allowing lipid entry/product release.
These are models based on structure, mutagenesis and docking/100-ns MD, not
observed chemical intermediates or measured release events. Temperature can
affect diffusion/collisions as well as conformational motion.
The lipid is modeled, not present in the experimentally solved CTP-bound crystal.
Figure 7 caption and discussion were read; its original image was not inspected.
