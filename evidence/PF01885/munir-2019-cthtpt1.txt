Minimal quotation (primes and subscripts normalized to ASCII):
"Removal of the 2'-PO4 and 3'-PO4 groups by CthTpt1 depended on NAD+."

Curator interpretation:
Purified full-length His6-CthTpt1 removes either terminal phosphate from
5'-32P-labeled AUCACGCUUA 10-mers. Figure 1 includes no-enzyme, no-NAD+ and
T4 Pnkp product-mobility controls; CIP challenges distinguish capped products.
Conditions: 0.5 uM enzyme, 0.2 uM RNA, 1 mM NAD+, 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5,
37 C, 30 minutes, 10 uL. Denaturing PAGE/autoradiography resolves products.
The terminal reactions are approximately stoichiometric under these conditions,
unlike the much more efficient internal-phosphate reaction. Do not describe
terminal removal as demonstrated multiple-turnover catalysis. The proposed
ADP-ribosylated intermediate is not itself an independently proven causal edge.
Runella and human homologs do not show equivalent terminal removal in this assay.
No endogenous substrate, isolated-domain sufficiency or cellular role is proved.
