Figure5E-T: HEK293T PRRC1 siRNA versus control on day3 increases cellular
SEC31A and SEC24C immunofluorescence; Golgin97 signal and SEC31A protein
abundance controls do not show corresponding increases. This supports a scoped
localization phenotype, not direct proof of altered coat disassembly kinetics.
Three experiments; >125cells from nine fields per experiment.
Figure5U-X: separate SBP-EGFP-EGFR and SBP-EGFP-ShhN RUSH reporters show
fewer Golgi-positive cells at15/30min following release in knockdown cells.
Methods:40uM biotin,100ng/ul cycloheximide; day3 knockdown, reporter introduced
24h after siRNA. Three experiments,>100cells each. No rescue is shown here.

Figure5A-B contrasts mixed rat-liver-cytosol vesicle recruitment with a negative
GST-Sar1A(delta1-17) pulldown for PRRC1 despite Sec23A/B recovery. Sec23A/B
co-IP with PRRC1-FLAG is association, not purified direct binding. These are
not converted into causal PRRC1-to-Sar1 binding or activation edges.
GTP/GMPPNP permeabilized-cell observations in S4 are described in main text;
the supplement itself was not obtained and no new S4 edge is adopted.
Whole-protein siRNA effects do not identify the causal domain, nucleotide
substrate or catalytic residues. Exact isoform/siRNA details remain open.
