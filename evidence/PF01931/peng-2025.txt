Read PRRC1 Results, Methods and figure7 legend; visually inspected figure7.
Minimal quotations from figure7: "PRRC1 KO" and "GST-PRRC1".

Figure7C-F: immunoblot-validated HeLa knockout increases ATRAP Golgi/total
above-threshold fluorescence, while steady-state cell-surface EGFR localization
looks unchanged. This is not the synchronized EGFR RUSH assay of2021.
Figure7H-I: ATG9A Golgi/total signal decreases with ER-marker colocalization
in knockout cells; PRRC1-HA expression restores the localization readout.
The panelI y-axis incorrectly says ATRAP while the images, legend and Results
identify ATG9A. Retain that discrepancy rather than calling the plotted label
independent proof of a second ATRAP rescue.

Figure7J-K:0.01mg/ml GST-PRRC1 added to semi-intact-cell budding reactions
with0.4mg/ml rat liver cytosol increases vesicle-fraction ATG9A,ERGIC53,SEC22B
signals (n3). The2mg/ml cytosol lanes are distinct conditions. This mixed
reconstitution does not prove direct cargo binding, isolated-domain sufficiency,
or cargo-specific hydrolysis. A GST-only control is not shown in this panel.
Figure7L-N: following60min EBSS starvation, knockout cells have fewer and
smaller LC3 puncta thanWT. These are morphology readouts, not a flux assay
or a demonstrated causal chain through ATG9A trafficking.

Co-IP with AP4 is not direct purified binding or proof that all PRRC1 effects
use AP4. Supplement-dependent guide, clone, purification and isoform details
remain unverified; publisher access403 and PMC asset challenge returned HTML,
not valid PDFs. Family-level or domain-level catalytic transfer is not justified.
