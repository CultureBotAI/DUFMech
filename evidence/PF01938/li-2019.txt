Figure4D,S4: in native S0001-derived deletion strain at37C, pMEV2 single
R35A,F39A,F42A,F63A,F65A variants fail to restore WT growth kinetics;
K62A partially restores. Compare variants to WT complementation, not to
zero growth. I33A restores native growth despite its RL211 phenotype;
K28A,G30A,G32A also restore. Do not assert that all eleven variants have
the same outcome or that the two assay hosts have identical protein levels.

Figure5C: pMEV4 UTR-mCherry reporter fluorescence normalized per mL*OD,
575/610nm,triplicate early/middle/late cultures. Deletion reduces the
MMP0127 signal modestly and1515/1697 more strongly versus parental.
Figure5B,D: changing the1515 leader to predicted hairpin-disrupting
UTR1515MT removes the parental fluorescence advantage; bars are not
identical at every stage and mutant-host fluorescence can exceed parental.
This is a genotype-by-leader reporter effect. Translation, RNA stability,
fluorophore maturation and other cellular effects are not all separately
resolved; a complete native transcription-antitermination chain is not
directly measured by fluorescence alone.
