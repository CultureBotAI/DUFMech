Minimal quotations: "Surface Analyses"; "Quaternary Structure".

The authors compare four archaeal structures and calculated surfaces with
known RNA-binding folds. They propose low RNA-binding likelihood from
nonconserved contacts and varying electrostatic surfaces, not from a matched
negative binding assay for PAB1135. Their gel-filtration and crystal analyses
support dimeric preparations, with different interface types, but do not test
a dimer-disrupting intervention against a functional readout.
PAB1135 is not one of the four experimentally solved proteins. Its later
binding assay does not demonstrate that every earlier member binds RNA.
Conversely, the earlier computational prediction does not negate that assay.
Do not encode this as experimentally proven inhibition of RNA binding or a
universal dimer-to-function causal edge. Resolve accession/target/PDB labels
against deposited sequences before member-specific structural transfer.
