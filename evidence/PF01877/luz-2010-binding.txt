Figure3: 1 pmol labeled RNA in 20 ul; trypsin-treated PAB1135 at
50/100/200/400 pmol, 30 min, 37 C. Thus nominal RNA is 50 nM and
protein 2.5/5/10/20 uM, assuming the stated protein amounts.
Buffer: Tris-HCl pH8, 20 mM KCl, 5 mM MnCl2, 1 mM DTT,
100 ug/ml BSA, 0.8 U RNasin; Tris concentration is not stated here.
Native 8% gels include no-protein controls for each probe.
C8U5G8 produces increasing RNP bands and a bound/free ratio rising
approximately 0.17 to 0.74 over the tested protein range. U8C5A8 and
UUA/C have weak nonzero shifts. At matched doses C8U5G8 gives the larger
ratio, but both RNA sequence and predicted structure differ.
Figure3B is bound/free, not fraction of total RNA; 0.74 is not 74% bound.
No fitted KD, saturation, replication/error bars or statistical test is supplied.

Figure1 shows tagged-protein purification and proteolytic tag removal;
trypsin also removes unspecified native N-terminal residues. Printed trypsin
0.1% and 0.1 mg/ml are inconsistent. Figure2 CD/SEC support an organized
preparation and apparent dimer, not exact termini or dimer necessity for binding.
The low-stability and single-stranded probes are not strictly nonbinding.
Three probes do not prove universal sequence independence. No RNA processing,
protection, cellular target, RNase P activation or exosome catalysis is measured.
