Assessment: ProtCNNSim computes normalized embedding similarity using Pfam 35
and ProtENN2. The DUF6647/DUF4157 score, conserved HEXXH motif and comparison
with mini-zincin structures support a proposed relationship to clan CL0126.
The benchmark uses predicted structures and TM-align, not activity measurements.
Figure 11A overlays an AF2 model with collagenase T (4ar9); the image labels the
model PF20352 DUF6647, despite the caption's DUF4157 wording. The accession's
current Pfam match independently supports PF20352. Preserve this naming
discrepancy; do not silently reassign the model to PF13699 or declare an erratum.

No member-specific substrate cleavage, metal-loading perturbation or biological
phenotype is measured for PF20352 in this study. A drawn zinc and aligned side
chains are not observed metal binding or catalysis by the modeled protein.
Neither the paper's metallopeptidase analogy nor InterPro's YbeY-like fold
description demonstrates RNA versus protein substrate specificity. No causal
edge is warranted from these sources alone; this is not evidence of inactivity.
