Figure 1 image, caption, Results and desulfurase Methods: purified IscS-TusA
has higher sulfide-production activity than IscS alone. TusA-only and IscU-only
controls have no detected activity. Precomplex formation uses a 1:3 ratio,
20 min at 30 C, 50 mM Tris-HCl/200 mM NaCl, pH 8; gel filtration isolates
complexes. Activity incubation is 10 min at 30 C with 1 mM DTT and 1 mM
L-cysteine; acidic DMPD/FeCl3 and an Na2S standard quantify sulfide. Figure 1
also shows intermediate activities after adding TusA or IscU to the reciprocal
precomplex. These are not direct cellular sulfur-flux measurements.

Figure 5A/D images and caption: 50 uM IscU, 25 uM Fe2+, 1 uM IscS,
250 uM L-cysteine; TusA at 0, 1, 5, 10 or 50 uM. Anaerobic 50 mM Tris-HCl,
100 mM NaCl, pH 8. Either 2 mM DTT or 1 uM Fdx + 1 uM Fpr + 100 uM
NADPH supplies reduction. After 30 min preincubation, IscS and cysteine are
added; A456 is followed every 5 min for 40 min, independent triplicates.
Only the 50 uM TusA series shows the clear decrease under both reductant
conditions. This is an assembly-associated optical readout, not direct proof
of sulfur diversion or a particular inhibited elementary reaction. The assay
temperature is not specified in this caption. Caption B/E refers to a yellow
line while the no-TusA line key is red; no spectrum-specific comparison is
adopted from those panels.
