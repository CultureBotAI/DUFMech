Comamonas sp.E6 tctA insertion EME018 (not a clean domain deletion)
loses citrate-dependent growth (Fig3B) and the induced 14C-citrate
retention increase (3C); TPA uptake remains similar to WT (3A).
There is nonzero background signal: no absolute zero-radioactivity claim.
Main text reports kan insertion and Southern verification; supplement
details were not retrieved as a valid PDF. No TctA rescue was inspected.

E6 tpiA insertion EME019 has little 14C-TPA accumulation (5A);
pJBtpiA restores it. Citrate uptake rate is about54% of WT (5C),
not absent. Growth is lost on TPA/IPA (4A/B), delayed on OPA/PCA/citrate
(4C/D/E); delayed cultures ultimately grow. The graph represents
increased time to growth for those three conditions, not reduced final
yield or demonstrated transport of each unmeasured substrate. Main text
reports only partial growth rescue in FigS3; no S3-derived edge is added.

Uptake: succinate pregrowth,4h with10mM succinate plus inducing TPA or
citrate; washed cells OD600=2,20uM carboxy-14C-TPA or1,5-14C-citrate,
50mM Tris-MES pH7.5,30C,10-180s,filter/wash/scintillation. Rates use
10-60s and normalize to cell protein. Cell-associated radiolabel does
not identify intact intracellular substrate or isolated permease flux.
Growth: Wx with10mM indicated sole carbon source,30C,initialOD0.1.
These figures report means and SD from three independent experiments.

P.putida PpY1100 is a non-PCA-growing resting-cell host, not a growth
rescue assay. Fig7 compares constructs with/without tphCII, tpiBA and
tphA2IIA3IIBIIA1II. Only their joint pJCBAtG construct substantially
lowers extracellular TPA. TpiA does not chemically degrade TPA itself.
Induction:1mM m-toluate,10h30C; assayOD20,1mM TPA,50mM Tris-HClpH7.5,
30C shaking. Methods describe60min; original plotted endpoints stop
at48min. HPLC measures remaining TPA, three independent experiments.
Control constructs remove transport partners or catabolic enzymes;
main-text extract results support the separation of entry and chemistry.

Remaining alternatives: insertion polarity, expression/folding effects,
unmeasured membrane interactions, identity of bound/retained metabolites.
The coexistence of residual citrate uptake in EME019 but its near-loss
in EME018 is unresolved, not proof of two independent sufficient routes.
CCCP sensitivity is whole-cell energy dependence, not measured proton
stoichiometry. Competition is not direct physical partner recognition.
