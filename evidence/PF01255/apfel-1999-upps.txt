The recombinant untagged E. coli preparation produces radiolabeled prenyl products from FPP and IPP. After phosphatase treatment, reported major TLC products comigrate with undecaprenol and decaprenol; shorter products also occur. This TLC is reported but not shown.
Figure 6 measures normalized phosphate output using UppS plus yeast inorganic pyrophosphatase and malachite green. Adding 0.01% Triton X-100 or 0.5 mM MgCl2 increases output relative to omission, but residual output remains. The assay does not isolate which coupled step accounts for each increase. Higher concentrations do not imply monotonic activation.
