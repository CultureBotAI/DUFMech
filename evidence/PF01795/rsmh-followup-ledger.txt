2012 E. coli RsmH structure, PMID:22561317
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22561317/
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1047847712001141
Abstract and returned article snippets only; full paper not inspected.
Reported SAM/cytidine structure 3TKA and SAXS are leads. Dimerization or
ligand occupancy does not prove a required catalytic dimer or a captured
productive complex. A quoted introductory 12% doubling-time increase
for rsmH disagrees with the 2010 table's approximately 15% (12% is rsmI).
Use the primary growth table, not this secondary recap.

2019 O157:H7 study, PMID:31798540, DOI:10.3389/fmicb.2019.02520
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC6863780/fullTextXML
Selected Results and Methods inspected from full XML: DNMT assay,
expression/complementation, and bisulfite verification. Reported DNA
methylation, flagellar effects and colonization require separate review.
The enzymatic readout uses a commercial colorimetric anti-5-methylcytosine
kit. Reported preparation purity is 93.4%, and the authors explicitly
allow activity from the remaining impurity. This is not direct chemical
identification of a particular DNA product or proof that the known rRNA
N4 enzyme universally catalyzes DNA C5 methylation. Do not discard the
reported phenotype or adopt its mechanistic attribution without resolving
protein accession/construct provenance, controls, substrate identity,
contamination and orthogonal product-specific evidence. Figures and SI
not independently inspected in this pass; no O157:H7 graph is adopted.

2026 B. subtilis MraW: distinguish final article from accessible preprint
Final PMID:42632017, DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117887
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42632017/
Final publisher full text returned 403; final abstract/metadata inspected.
Accessible preprint PMID:42388804, DOI:10.64898/2026.04.20.719615,
PMC13317604.1, bioRxiv April 20 2026 version:
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13317604/fullTextXML
The PMC page links the final article but its content is still the preprint.
Selected preprint Results and Methods were inspected, including ribosome
purification and coupled transcription/translation. PY79 WT and ZP1
deletion-derived ribosomes differ in GFP output in the reconstituted
system, despite similar bulk RNA/protein measures. The selected Results
also report no growth difference in three tested media and no comparable
in-vivo GFP deficit. Methods specify biological triplicates, 4 ul of
ribosome preparation per 25-ul reaction, 37 C for 4 h. Equal volume plus
bulk similarity is not independently established identical active-ribosome
dose. This is a promising context-specific follow-up, not grounds to
transfer an E. coli growth edge to all Bacillus conditions.
Other leads are suppression of spoIVA defects, CmpA regulation and an
early mRNA stem-loop. Retrieve final Methods, map the B. subtilis member
and exact strains, inspect figures/SI and review assay normalization
before adopting final-version causal claims. No preprint graph is saved.
