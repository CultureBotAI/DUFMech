Identity and constructs
BW25113 is the cellular parent. The rsmH::kan strain is from the Keio
collection; rsmI::Cm was constructed by one-step deletion and P1-transduced
into rsmH::kan to make the double knockout. No genetic complementation is
presented in the experiments adopted here. Whole-gene perturbations can
have indirect/polar effects; these are not isolated-domain perturbations.
Recombinant RsmH and RsmI came from ASKA pCA24N constructs with N-terminal
His tags, induction with 0.1 mM IPTG and nickel-affinity purification.
They were treated with 2 mM AdoHcy for 1 h at 37 C and dialyzed to remove
endogenous SAM before reconstitution. Figure 3A shows Coomassie-stained
preparations, not a quantitative guarantee of absolute purity.

Figure 3 and reconstitution Methods
Substrates were isolated from the double deletion strain. The reaction
was 100 ul, 37 C for 1 h, 100 mM NH4Cl, 20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.6,
10 mM magnesium acetate, 6 mM beta-mercaptoethanol, 1 mM SAM when supplied.
The Methods specify 10 pmol recombinant proteins, RsmH and/or RsmI;
do not infer an independently verified total dosage for the mixed sample.
Substrate amounts differ: 5 pmol 30S or tightly coupled 70S versus
2.5 pmol naked 16S RNA. After extraction, RNase T1 digestion and LC/MS
detected the C1402-containing tetramer.

RsmH plus SAM and 30S gives the N4-methyl product m4CCCGp at m/z 645.1,
with low apparent efficiency. RsmI alone gives the isomeric CmCCGp.
Both enzymes with SAM and 30S give m4CmCCGp at m/z 652.1, alongside a
substantial monomethylated signal. The displayed joint-enzyme assay lacks
detectable methylated product when SAM is omitted, 70S replaces 30S, or
half as much naked 16S replaces 30S. The last comparison changes both
substrate state and nominal amount; no equal-dose specificity inference.
The negative traces do not establish universal inactivity, an absolute
detection limit, separate tests for every enzyme/substrate combination,
or an experimentally observed order of the two methylations.
DIRECT here means the net purified-preparation assay effect, not observed
atomic transfer, isolated PF01795 sufficiency or absence of contaminants.

Figure 2 and cellular methylation Results
Total RNA analysis and an excised 16S fragment spanning 1378-1426, with
RNase T1/RNase A digestion and collision-induced dissociation, support
assignment at C1402. WT carries m4Cm. Deleting rsmH removes the N4 mark
while retaining the 2'-O mark (Cm). The dimethyl and monomethyl tetramers
give 652.1 and 645.1 respectively. The RNase A fragment changes from
Gm4CmCp 1000.2 to GCmCp 986.2. In the rsmI-only deletion, nucleoside m4C
overlaps the m5C1407 contribution and is not a uniquely separated peak.
The curated cellular edge concerns loss of the N4 modification, not loss
of both marks or a directly isolated methylation-to-fitness mechanism.

Figure 4 and luciferase Methods/Results
Renilla/Firefly constructs measure Fluc/Rluc (F/R), normalized separately
to WT for each construct. Initiation reporters use AUG-driven Renilla
and a Firefly start codon on the same transcript. AAA is the negligible
negative comparator. Readthrough/frameshift reporters instead contain
a short window between the two luciferase coding sequences.
Cells grow at 37 C in LB with 100 ug/ml ampicillin and are harvested at
A600 0.5. Freeze/thaw lysates are clarified and assayed with the dual
luciferase system. Full window/primer sequences need the unavailable SI.
Figure 4's caption does not establish the replicate count or definition
of its error bars; do not borrow n=3 from the growth table.

Single rsmH deletion versus WT: AUU reporter approximately 1.8-fold,
UGA approximately 0.5, UAG approximately 0.8, +1 frame approximately 0.9,
and -1 frame approximately 1.2. Double rsmH/rsmI deletion: AUU about 1.8,
UUG about 0.8, GUG about 0.9, UGA about 0.5. These are normalized reporter
ratios, not measured molecular rate constants. Single rsmH deletion does
not justify a claim that every non-AUG start is increased. RsmI-only UGA
increases about 1.3-fold, so it cannot be substituted for the RsmH result.

Important internal contradiction: Figure 4B and the Results show UGA
reporter reduction after rsmH deletion, whereas the Discussion says
UGA readthrough increases when the N4 mark is absent. The curated edge
uses Figure 4B plus Results and retains this unresolved textual conflict.
No edge asserts that the reporter measures RF2 affinity, direct P-site
geometry changes, IF3 feedback or a complete modification-to-growth chain.

Table 1 and growth Methods
LB 1-ml cultures in 96-well format, OD600 every 15 min. Three independent
experiments; reported mean +/- SD in minutes. At 37 C: WT 20.4 +/- 0.3,
rsmH deletion 23.5 +/- 0.8, rsmI deletion 22.9 +/- 0.6, double deletion
26.3 +/- 2.5. The curated edges therefore increase doubling time, not
growth rate. RsmH's increase is about 15%, not the 12% for RsmI.
At 33 C the corresponding four values are 30.5, 34.5, 31.4, 36.5;
at 42 C they are 20.9, 22.7, 22.9, 26.8. Only the 37 C effects are graphed.
No cell-division-specific lesion, killing or essentiality is established.

Discussion-only models and limits
The authors propose a late 30S assembly intermediate and changes to
P-site interactions; these were not directly tested in the retained
assays. No mechanistic arrows are added for those models. This ledger
is a partial assessment of one member and selected contexts, not an
exhaustive PF01795 dossier or experimental replication.
