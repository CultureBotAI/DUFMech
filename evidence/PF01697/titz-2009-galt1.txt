Construct and assay boundary (Methods, expression and transferase assays):
Full coding cDNA from Bristol N2 was expressed untagged in baculovirus Sf9;
an N-terminal FLAG version served expression detection and reportedly gave
similar activity (comparison not shown). Cells incubated 72 h at 28 C were
lysed in TBS pH7.4 / 2% Triton X-100, EDTA-free protease inhibitors, 15 min
at 4 C, then clarified at 2000g for 5 min. All activity work used raw
supernatant, not purified GALT-1. Standard reaction: 0.5 ul extract in
2.5 ul, 1 mM UDP-Gal, 40 uM acceptor, 2 h at room temperature, MES pH6.5.
HTML prints MES 40 uM and MnCl2 10 uM. These unusual units remain unresolved;
do not silently change them to mM. Kinetics alone used 0.1 ul extract.
Donors were exchanged equimolarly. Metal chlorides or Na2EDTA REPLACED
MnCl2 at equal nominal concentration; EDTA was not added on top of MnCl2.

Figure 2: dabsyl-GEN[GnGnF6]R parent m/z2207 becomes m/z2369 with GALT-1
extract and UDP-Gal, a +162-Da product. Empty-vector extract with donor and
GALT-1 extract without donor lack that product. Product MS/MS m/z1273
supports a Hex-dHex-HexNAc glycopeptide fragment. Aspergillus beta-galactosidase
returns the parent mass. Its digestion protocol is 27 mU, citrate 50 mM,
pH4.5, 2 days at 37 C, not the GALT-1 reaction temperature. Figure 2B's
caption reverses the displayed order: the upper panel is parent2207 and
the lower is parent2369. Assign by precursor mass, not the caption's order.
Laser fragmentation and residual Sf9 hexosaminidase peaks are annotated.
The authors' exact beta1,4 assignment also uses permethylation MS/MS in
supplement S3, which was not retrieved/inspected. Retain the measured
core-fucose-associated beta-galactosylated product without claiming a new
independent determination of the 4-position or elementary chemistry.

Figure 3A: dansyl-N[GnGnF6]ST HPLC substrate at 9.09 min shifts to a product
at 8.06 min with UDP-Gal. Substituting UDP-Glc yields a minor (<10%) peak
at that retention time; UDP-GlcNAc and UDP-GalNAc do not show the product
peak. These comparisons support lower product-associated signal, not a
universal exclusive donor rule. The small UDP-Glc peak is not chemically
identified and may reflect contamination or extract-mediated conversion.
The apparent 84-uM UDP-Gal Km derives from two crude-extract datasets; it
is neither a binding Kd nor purified-enzyme catalytic efficiency. Negative
free-fucose/pNP-fucoside tests are reported without displayed data.

Figure 4: GnGnF6 glycopeptide shows product2369 without visible parent2207.
Removing antenna GlcNAcs (MMF6) leaves both parent1801 and product1963;
MMF3, with alpha1,3 instead of alpha1,6 core fucose, retains parent1801
without the +162 product. These are qualitative product/parent patterns,
not absolute rates or equal MALDI response factors. Nonreducing GlcNAcs
are not strictly required since MMF6 retains measurable conversion.

Figure 5: free GnGnF6 glycan, not glycopeptide, and radiolabeled UDP-Gal.
12-h TLC separates product32mm from donor60mm and hydrolysis-related78mm;
the product peak is absent with vector extract. Metal comparisons use a
2-h incubation and neutral-fraction scintillation after anion exchange,
3-7 independent experiments, mean +/- SD, normalized to maximal average.
Mn/Fe/Co each produce more counts than the water-added extract with
residual cofactors. EDTA replacing Mn gives counts near vector baseline,
not literally zero. Co/Fe/Mn error bars overlap; no significant ranking,
pure-enzyme binding constant, metal-free control, DXD coordination geometry
or exclusive Mn requirement follows. Some hydrolysis background can remain.

Figure 6A / Methods: 50-100 newly hatched L1, fraction reaching L4 after
72 h on BL21(DE3) CGL2 lawns, NGM with 1 mM IPTG and 50 ug/ml kanamycin;
three replicates, SD. The stated overnight37C is plate preparation, not
a measured worm incubation temperature. The unc-119(ed3);galt-1(op497)
double mutant has a much greater L4 fraction than unc-119 alone. Integrated
opIs444 and opIs445 lower it again, with opIs444 incompletely restored.
pAB15 contains genomic galt-1 (1843bp upstream, coding region without stop)
fused to mCherry/let-858 AND the unc-119 transformation marker; selection
rescues the unc-119 dauer defect. Therefore the intervention is the joint
construct, not an isolated single-gene rescue. The unc-119 single mutant
is itself CGL2 sensitive, an informative comparison but not a reason to
hide marker co-rescue. This is developmental sensitivity, not a direct
measurement of death, receptor occupancy or every glycosylation step.
Figure 6B shows intestine/coelomocyte expression, not marker-verified Golgi
localization. No observed arrow connects all assay contexts into one chain.
