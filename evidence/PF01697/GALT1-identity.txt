PF01697 spans residues 189-394 inclusive. Predicted membrane segment 9-29
and putative N-glycosylation positions N109/N152 are outside that interval.

by the retained GALT-1 substitution experiments: there were no such mutants.
Full-length protein, crude expression extracts and whole-gene insertions
must not be represented as experiments on an isolated PF01697 domain.

Historical protein records, retrieved through NCBI Eutils and parsed with

that every historical expression clone was sequence-identical. The 2009

uninspected. RefSeq transcript NM_072144.8 is a current cross-reference.
