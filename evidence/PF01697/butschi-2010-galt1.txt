Figure4A/B and Methods: op497 is a Mos1 insertion in M03F8.4 exon2,
identified by inverse PCR after selection in pmk-1(km25) and 2-6 outcrosses.
The protein product/null status is not experimentally established here.
50-100 newly hatched L1 were fed BL21(DE3) producing CGL2; L4 fraction was
scored at72h. Plates were prepared overnight at23C, distinct from the 2009
protocol. The specific assay's animal incubation temperature is not restated.
pmk-1 alone gives no L4 animals in the displayed comparison; adding op497
gives approximately0.7, without claiming an exact digitized value. Published
statistical comparisons were to N2, not to the zero-valued pmk-1 population.
Do not turn the higher developmental fraction into a direct binding effect,
an unconditional growth advantage or a quantitative mortality decrease.
The rescue in 2009 uses unc-119, not this pmk-1 background.

Figure7 and glycome Methods: animals for glycan isolation were grown in
OP50 liquid culture for five days at room temperature, not on CGL2 food.
Sequential PNGaseF and PNGaseA release separates the F-resistant/A-sensitive
fraction shown. PA-labeled glycans were separated by normal-phase followed
by reversed-phase HPLC, then MALDI-TOF MS/MS and glycosidase treatments.
pmk-1;galt-1(op497) has reduced late NP-HPLC peaks and lacks the selected
RP-HPLC peakA (approximately14.5min), compared with pmk-1 alone. The selected
NP fraction is34.0-35.5min. pmk-1;fut-8 is a distinct comparison, not a
GALT-1 perturbation. No total-glycome abundance or rescue measurement is
implied by the qualitative peak difference.

PeakA has composition Hex5dHex2HexNAc2-PA, sodium adduct near m/z1605,
and a core-associated fragment near753. Fucosidase alone does not remove
the capped fucose; Aspergillus beta1,4-galactosidase removes one hexose,
parent1443 / core fragment approximately591 and a retention shift; subsequent
bovine kidney fucosidase removes a fucose, parent1297 / fragment445.
These support the authors' core Galbeta1,4Fucalpha1,6 assignment. The 37C,
two-day glycosidase digestion is analytical, not the worm culture condition.
