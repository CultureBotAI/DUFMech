Minimal quotation anchors: "RNA-protein complex"; "mt-Co1 probe B";
"Protein synthesis". These labels occur in Figures3/6 and the Figure2 caption.

Figures3A and6C: mature mouse TACO1 aa27-294 NP_081622.1 was expressed as
N-terminal His/GST fusion in E. coli ER2566, nickel-purified and gel-filtered.
Protein was measured by BCA. The RNA-binding protocol does not describe tag
removal; TEV cleavage and heparin chromatography are explicit additional steps
for crystallization only. Do not borrow the crystal construct's tag state.

RNA EMSA uses ATTO680 RNA at100ng/ul, equimolar through eightfold excess
protein,30min room temperature,10mM HEPES pH8,1mM EDTA,50mM KCl,2mM DTT,
0.1mg/ml fatty-acid-free BSA and0.02% Tween20, then10% PAGE in TAE.
Figure3A maps probeB to mouse mtDNA5446-5632; its RNA/protein complex appears
with increasing TACO1. Other overlapping mt-Co1 probes have different binding
patterns; mt-Atp8/6 and mt-Co2 controls show no comparable shift. No universal
exclusive RNA specificity or translation activity follows from this assay.

Figure6C explicitly labels probeB in WT and all three grouped substitutions.
R74A/S75A/R76A/S79A/K80A sharply reduces binding. K112A/N113A/K116A/S117A
and K162A/Y163A/K167A give smaller reductions relative to WT at the displayed
concentrations. Do not split a grouped perturbation into individual-residue
causal claims. Mutant folding/active fraction and a tag-only binding control
are not established by these displayed gels. Results are qualitative, without
a fitted dissociation constant or displayed replicate uncertainty.

Figure2D: mitochondria isolated from adult male C57BL/6J-background mice,
30weeks, homozygous ENU Taco1 T491A/I164N versus age/littermate-matched WT.
The allele was backcrossed8-10generations. COXI pulse-label signal is reduced
in both liver and heart; Coomassie staining shows equal50ug protein loading.
Caption reports three independent biological experiments. Methods use500ug
mitochondria in750ul respiration-compatible buffer,150uCi35S-methionine,
60min37C. Figure caption/Results mention methionine and cysteine whereas
Methods specify methionine alone; retain this discrepancy rather than alter units.
Figure2B steady transcript abundance is largely unchanged; this is not an RNA
half-life measurement. Figure1 text reports TACO1 loss, but Figure1 was not
visually assessed in this pass. No mouse genetic rescue is adopted.

These genotype-to-pulse effects are INDIRECT: they do not demonstrate an
isolated PF01709 catalytic reaction or assign initiation versus elongation.
The older initiation proposal is qualified by the2024 elongation evidence.
Figure5's caption says Leu164 while Results and verified sequence say Ile164;
the curated allele follows the sequence/Results, not that inconsistent caption.
