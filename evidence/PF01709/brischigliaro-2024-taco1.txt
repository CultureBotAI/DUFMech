Minimal quotation anchors: "newly synthesized"; "Relative ribosome
occupancies". Figure1D and Figure2D captions supply these terms.

Human HEK293T ATCC CRL3216 cells at37C/5%CO2, high-glucose DMEM with10%FBS,
pyruvate/uridine/GlutaMAX. Exon1 CRISPR pool was cloned, screened by TACO1
immunoblot, and edited regions subcloned for Sanger sequencing. Exact lesions
are not enumerated in the inspected Methods/resources. OriGene TACO1 ORF
was used for rescue, with tagged/untagged and full/delta5-CMV6 variants in
Methods. Resources distinguish ordinary TACO1, TACO1-FLAG and TACO1-OE.
Figures1/2 label the ordinary rescue, but complete per-panel clone sequence
and promoter assignment remain unverified; do not identify it as FLAG-OE.

Figure1C/D compares WT, KO+empty vector and KO+TACO1. Thirty-minute100uCi
35S-methionine pulse after methionine-free preincubation, with emetine blocking
cytosolic translation;17.5% SDS-PAGE, autoradiography and beta-actin loading.
Methods print100ul/ml emetine without stock strength; do not silently replace
with a mass concentration. Newly made full-length COX1 is severely reduced
(roughly10%remaining) and COX3 moderately reduced in KO. Rescue restores both
toward WT. Quantification uses3biological replicates, mean/SD and two-way
ANOVA/Dunnett comparisons. No absolute synthesis-rate constant is assigned.
Figure1F COX1 RNA increases rather than decreases; no transcription activation
arrow or RNA-decay rate is established. Extra pulse bands do not identify
each exact cleavage/termination position. These are whole-cell INDIRECT effects.

Figure2D: mitoribosome profiling in WT, KO and reconstituted cells,80%confluent,
RNaseI footprints from55S monosomes. Reads25-45nt aligned to the mitochondrial
transcriptome; codon counts normalized within each transcript. Sliding5-aa
windows grouped by0,1,2,3prolines and summed occupancies normalized to WT.
The2-Pro and3-Pro bins rise in KO; both fall after TACO1 reconstitution but
the3-Pro bar remains above WT. These are normalized footprint occupancies,
not directly measured transit times, rates, collision frequencies or absolute
ribosome numbers. No plotted replicate dispersion in Figure2D is claimed.

Figure2C and FigureS3B support COX1 site-specific peaks and their alleviation;
TableS4 distinguishes affected and unaffected motifs. COX1 VYPPLA and COX3
HWPPTG are listed without a KO stall; some non-Pro-rich sites also pause
(TableS6 text). Two prolines per5-aa window does not necessarily mean an
adjacent PP motif. Do not universalize every polyproline site or transfer all
human motifs to mouse. The gene's transcript-level share is not a direct
initiation-rate assay. Proposed collision-triggered termination and PTC
stabilization are not represented as directly observed causal microsteps.

Methods describe P-site offsets15-18nt while Results discuss a12-nt offset
from a32-nt footprint. Aggregate bin observations are retained without raw
read reprocessing or claiming independently reproduced residue-level peaks.
The cybrid Methods/Results differ on codon542/342; no exact cybrid-stop edge
is curated. Interaction screens, bL27m substitutions and all additional
polyproline/non-Pro sites require separate member/context review.
