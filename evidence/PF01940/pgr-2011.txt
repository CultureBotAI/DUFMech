peaking at 3 h before declining. Figure 4c is a separate promoter-GUS assay.

cotyledon greening and RNAi decreases it relative to WT; untreated plants
all green. Three sets of 50 seedlings per condition; plotted significance
is versus WT. This endpoint is not a glucose-binding or transport assay.
