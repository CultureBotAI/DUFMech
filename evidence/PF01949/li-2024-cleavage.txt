Minimal exact Discussion quotation: "forming a 23-nt product."

Paraphrased observations and limits:
- Five separate His-tagged proteins were expressed in E. coli BL21 and
  purified: Afu/WP_010878930.1, Halo/WP_009487363.1, Gm/TLY50285.1,
  Loki/TFG01626.1 and Thor/TFG14500.1. Identity boundaries are in identity.txt.
- Figure2 and supplementS3 show a fluorescent product comigrating with a
  23-nt marker from 5'-FAM ssDNA with dU at position23. Main Methods use
  300-nM protein, 30-nM substrate, 37C30min; bufferA contains150-mMNaCl,
  5-mMMgCl2, Tris pH7.4, DTT and glycerol. FigureS3 caption specifies
  substrate/time/temperature and relies on main Methods for protein level.
- The alternative A, dI and AP substrates lack the analogous product in
  those gels. S3 does not include an enzyme-minus dU lane. Figure3 provides
  such controls for Afu/Halo, plus time courses and35/50/58-nt substrates.
  The35-nt substrate is visibly cleaved despite text saying longer than35.
- The product length supports a cut immediately3' of dU, not glycosylase
  excision of free uracil. Terminal phosphate/hydroxyl chemistry is not
  independently measured. No complete cellular repair sequence is observed.
- Figure6A shows Afu/Halo cutting the lesion strand of dU/G duplexes;
  5'-FAM label is on dU in panelA, on the opposite G strand in panelB.
  The latter has no corresponding cleavage. Conditions are37C60min,
  not the standard30min. Multiple upper bands are attributed by authors
  to incomplete denaturation; they are not independently mapped extra cuts.
- SupplementS6 includes dU positive and enzyme-minus controls, with no
  analogous product for the eight tested ordinary mismatches. S4 shows
  no product from the tested ssRNA after60min with any of the five proteins.
  Negative assays are condition-specific, not edges claiming universal absence.
- Assays were reported repeated at least three times; displayed gels are
  representative. No raw gel reanalysis, kinetic model, domain-only assay
  or independent replication by another group was performed in this review.
- S9 visually supports related full-length sequences for all five tested
  proteins. It labels the bacterial sequence GbEndo_dU where other sections
  use GmEndo_dU; accessionTLY50285.1 is the maintained identity anchor.
