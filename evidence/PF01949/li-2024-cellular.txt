Paraphrased findings and limits:
- H. volcanii H26 delta-pyrE2 is the experimental background. The
  mutant removes an internal part of Endo_dU, retaining upstream promoter
  and gene fragments. Source labels it delta-Endo_dU, not a documented
  full-gene null. S12 sequencing figure supports locus identity/partial
  deletion. Methods refers toS11 for this validation; S11 is actually the
  protein gel. FigureS12 termini/neighborhood support HVO_0443 mapping
  described in identity.txt; full allele reconstruction remains open.
- Cultures received0/0.1/0.5-mM sodium bisulfite (SB) atOD600 about0.5;
  RNA was collected48h later,45C Hv-YPC. Reads were mapped toDS2;
  Figure8B reports normalized TPM, not absolute transcription rate or
  UDG protein level/activity. BioProject PRJNA1072706 is given as the
  raw-read deposit, not reanalyzed here.
- Figure8B mutant UDG TPM at0.5mMSB exceeds WT at the same dose.
  Within the mutant,0.5mMSB also raises UDG TPM relative to noSB.
  These are two distinct controlled contrasts, not proof that UDG induction
  mediates restored repair. Replicate number and error-bar definition for
  RNA-seq are not established by the main caption/Methods read here.
- FigureS13 plots OD600 versus time for0/0.1/0.5mMSB, with three
  biological replicates andSEM. SB addition is at20h in this figure.
  At105h, the no-SB mutant has lower OD600 than no-SB WT. This is
  an optical-density phenotype, not separately measured viable cell count,
  generation time or DNA repair flux. Both strains grow more slowly with
  0.5mMSB and converge near similar late OD; do not claim mutant-specific
  SB hypersensitivity from a basal growth deficit. No genetic rescue shown.
- Adjacent UDG24bp downstream and alteredTPM suggest physiological
  cooperation, but do not establish an operon, direct protein interaction,
  a complete compensatory pathway or absence of polar effects.
- Afu/Halo purified assays do not demonstrate H. volcanii substrate specificity
  or its cellular intermediates. Do not join different species into an
  experimentally proven DNA-repair chain.
