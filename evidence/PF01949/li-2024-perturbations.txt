Paraphrased assay observations:
- Figure7E has Blank, WT, D10A and double-mutant lanes. D10A retains
  cleavage; the double preparation has much less23-nt product. Results
  and supplementS11 call it D10A-H166A, but Figure7E says D10A+H166R.
  S11 shows major bands of similar size for WT and mutants, not an assay
  of native folding, active concentration or correct mutation sequence.
  Preserve this preparation-level effect with unresolved H166 identity.
  No H166 single-mutant lane establishes an independent H166 requirement.
- Figure4 tests5-mM added metal salts and5/10/20/50-mM EDTA with
  300-nM protein and30-nM dU ssDNA. Zinc conditions lose product;
  authors report precipitation. This is not proven active-site zinc inhibition.
  EDTA/no-added-metal activity does not establish an apoenzyme or exclude
  tightly bound trace metal. No invented no-effect/activation edge for EDTA.
- Figure5A reaction at65C yields less product than37C for each protein,
  with little activity at still higher temperatures. These purified recombinant
  preparations do not prove inactivity of native AfuEndo_dU in its thermophile.
- Figure5B400-mM versus200-mMNaCl reduces product for both enzymes.
  Caption/Methods specify50,100,200,400,800,1000mM; first panel label
  appears5mM. The curated comparison does not depend on that first lane.
  BufferC lacks salt before addition and has no added magnesium.
- Figure5C/Methods testpH5-10, although Results/Discussion say4-10.
  Activity atpH4 is not demonstrated by the inspected panel.
- SupplementS5B tests65C protein preincubation followed by cleavage at
  37C60min. Ten-minute preincubation loses product relative to zero-time
  control. This is distinct from running the reaction itself at65C.
  Caption lists0,10,20,30,40,50min while panel endpoint says60min;
  the first post-zero interval is unambiguous within the reported series,
  but exact later intervals remain discrepant. S5A retains cleavage after
 37C preincubation. No direct denaturation/refolding measurement is shown.
- Except this preincubation assay, condition experiments use30-min
  reactions with300-nM protein/30-nM substrate; temperature, salt and
  zinc comparisons are separate controlled experiments, not a joint treatment.

All these effects retain UNRESOLVED molecular directness. Structural analogy,
motif conservation and predicted uracil pocket are contextual proposals,
not directly observed binding or a resolved catalytic residue mechanism.
