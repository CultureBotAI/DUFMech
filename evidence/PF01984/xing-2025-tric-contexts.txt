IL co-IP edge: Par9 phrase "reduced its interaction".
ED6f/g; Methods Par30/Par32. Human PDCD5-Flag WT versus I93G/L96G (IL)
transfected for48h in HEK293F. Clarified500ug lysate was bound to20uL
packed anti-Flag M2 beads for1h at4C, washed3times, then immunoblotted.
Lysis: PBS pH7.4,0.1%IGEPAL,5mM MgCl2,0.6mM PMSF and inhibitors.
ED6g defines co-IP CCT1 divided by co-IP PDCD5, normalized to WT100%.
The reported IL association is lower. Five source-image replicates are
present (MOESM16 image1:1/2, image2:3/4, image3:5), agreeing with the
ED6 caption's n=5 but not Methods Par32's n=4. Par32 gives IL11.04+/-9.68,
WT100+/-0; these are not reasserted as a resolved five-replicate estimate.
Input and IP anti-PDCD5, anti-CCT1 and GAPDH controls are visible.
Replicate4's raw CCT1 IP difference is less pronounced than the others;
do not claim complete binding loss in every replicate. RKK cellular
expression is much lower, so its co-IP ratio cannot establish an intact
interface or refute the recombinant result. No cellular RKK causal edge.
IL substitution is a combined perturbation, not two separately sufficient
residues. Interface contacts are model-supported, not observed atomistic
causal steps; altered fold/abundance/localization are not fully excluded.

RKK native-gel edge: Par9 phrase "severely impaired".
ED6h; Methods Par27/29; MOESM16 image4. Human recombinant PDCD5-His6
R55A/K63A/K66A versus WT at0,0.075,0.15,0.225,0.3,0.6,0.9,1.5,3uM,
with300nM recombinant human TRiC,20min at25C, without ATP. Buffer50mM
Tris pH7.4,100mM KCl,5mM MgCl2,10%glycerol,1mM TCEP. Anti-PDCD5 native
complex signal is strongly reduced for RKK; anti-CCT8 tracks TRiC.
Caption says two independent experiments; the source image shows the
displayed titration, not two separately labelled replicate series.
This is a binding-readout effect, not a measured Kd, complete absolute
absence of association, independent effects of each residue, or proof
that neutralizing electrostatics alone explains the loss.

IL native-gel edge: Par9 phrase "binding kinetics".
ED6i/j; Par29; MOESM16 image5. PDCD5 WT/IL3uM plus TRiC300nM,25C,
same ATPase buffer,10/15/20/30min. At10min the IL native-complex PDCD5
signal is lower than WT; later bands approach WT more closely. Graph
retains the early10min comparison, not permanent loss or a fitted
on-rate/off-rate. Caption specifies n=2, SEM for the plotted timecourse;
one displayed blot series is present. Anti-CCT8 is the complex control.

Bead-release edge: Par11 phrase "CCT1 enrichment".
ED7h-j; Methods Par30/33/35; MOESM17 image2, four labelled replicates.
PDCD5-Flag/TRiC complexes captured from HEK293F as above were exposed
on beads to lysis buffer plus1mM ATP,5mM Al(NO3)3,30mM NaF for1h at37C,
then washed3times in the same treatment buffer. Control beads underwent
the corresponding incubation/washes without ATP/AlFx. CCT1 measured in
supernatant relative to bead fraction rises: reported1.53+/-0.51 versus
0.38+/-0.06, n=4, SD. PDCD5 supernatant/bead values0.09+/-0.05 versus
0.10+/-0.04 are not detectably changed. Thus the endpoint is TRiC release
from immobilized PDCD5, not PDCD5 release from beads, substrate release,
or an ATP-alone effect. The combined closure-favouring treatment is one
input; chemical/structural microsteps are not independently resolved.

Native nucleotide-treatment edge: Par11 word "disrupted".
ED7k; Par29; MOESM17 image3. PDCD5-His6 WT3uM and human TRiC300nM,
20min at25C in ATPase buffer with1mM nucleotide/analogue. The ATP/AlFx
condition reduces PDCD5 signal at the TRiC complex relative to no
nucleotide. ATP, ATPgammaS, ADP and AMPPCP do not reproduce that loss;
TRiC-only lane and CCT8 blot are controls. Residual PDCD5 signal remains,
so do not claim zero binding. Caption reports two repetitions; one
source blot series is displayed. Exact Al/F concentrations for this
native-gel preparation are not specified in Par29; do not silently copy
the bead-release recipe. Effect retained at the measured complex-signal
level, not as a direct proof that ring closure causes dissociation.

Thermal-profile edges: Par12 word "stabilization".
Supplementary Fig.7e-h, PDF pages10-11; Methods Par36/37. Commercial
HEK293T WT ab255449 and PDCD5-knockout ab266229, DMEM/10%FBS,37C/5%CO2.
5e5 cells per aliquot in PBS were heated3min, lysed40min at4C, aggregates
removed, soluble fractions immunoblotted and normalized to GAPDH.
Actin temperatures37,44.1,49.9,55.5,62C; means WT100,85.3,73.8,46.3,26.3
and KO100,100.3,109,83,57.6. Tubulin temperatures56.8,58.3,59.5,60.7,62.1C;

Both panels report n=2 independent experiments, SD; not a formal proof
of significance at every point. Graphs retain higher measured mean
soluble-protein signal, not folding flux, intrinsic melting temperature,
substrate release or a demonstrated TRiC-dependent mechanism. Tubulin
starts at different normalized baseline signal; this prevents treating
its difference as an abundance-independent stability shift. Target
rescue/clone-specific controls were not established in the inspected
experiment. Figure7e and7f are different temperature series. The source
caption's tubulin panel letter slip is resolved using the actual panels,
not by merging the two assays. Antibodies: actinAM4302, tubulinT5168;
do not invent a single substrate gene/isoform identity from these blots.
