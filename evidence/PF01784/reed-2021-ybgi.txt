Curator's notes, not quotations:
Keio ybgI deletion and the preexisting folE deletion were transferred into
E. coli K-12 MG1655 by P1 transduction. The ybgI resistance cassette was removed
before making the double mutant; mutations were checked by internal/external PCR.
Liquid growth used LB with 0.3 mM deoxythymidine, starting OD600 0.1, 37 C,
40 h, five replicates. Figure 8b shows lower OD600 of the double mutant than
the folE-only mutant during approximately 5-15 h. This is the adopted conditional
phenotype, not an estimate of an enzymatic rate, survival or final biomass yield.
The article later describes better double-mutant growth on dT without clearly
resolving the liquid-versus-plate context. That wording is not reconciled by
inventing an experiment; the graph is restricted to the visually checked curve.
No added defect is claimed for ybgI deletion in a folE-positive background.

In plate complementation, pBAD24-ybgI did not replace folE, whereas pBAD24-folE
supported dT-independent growth in the folE-deficient backgrounds. This does not
prove zero YbgI enzymatic activity in every condition or refute all HP0959 assays.
Plate methods say 10 h; Figure 8 caption says images at 20 h. Neither time is
used to define the liquid-growth edge. Supplement download returned HTML instead
of a ZIP; the publisher supplement route returned 403, so Table S2 was not read.
The exact derivative strain identifiers and primers remain a coverage gap.
No ybgI-rescue experiment for the liquid-growth curve was established here.

Metal insertion is a proposed general role, not a demonstrated molecular edge
from this experiment. Primary HcgD studies (doi:10.1016/j.febslet.2014.05.059;
doi:10.1111/1574-6968.12141) and HP0959 (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0066605) are
identified leads, not reviewed experiments in this record. PMID:29625546 was
inspected as an abstract only; its cell-wall suggestion is not an adopted edge.
