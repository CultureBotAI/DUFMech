Minimal quotation:

Curator interpretation:
Purified, tag-cleaved Sare_1364 from Salinispora arenicola CNS-205 converted
commercial SAM to adenosine in aqueous phosphate buffer. HPLC retention against
standards and LC/MS identified products. The assay used 50 mM phosphate pH 7.9,
37 C, 10-2000 nM enzyme and 0.5-500 uM SAM. Scheme 1 reports an apparent
SAM Km of 1.6 +/- 0.2 uM and kcat of 0.46 +/- 0.03 per minute.
Chloride inhibited the reaction. SAH and MTA did not substitute as substrates;
slow iodination is not evidence for a physiological halogenation pathway.
The experiment tests intact protein, not isolated PF01887. It does not resolve
SAM sulfur stereochemistry or establish intracellular SAM homeostasis.
