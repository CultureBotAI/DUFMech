Curator assay facts:
Common conditions: 50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 2 mM MnCl2, 100 uM GTP, 1 uM RtcB,
0.1 uM radiolabeled 20-mer RNA, 37 C. RNase T1 digestion precedes urea-PAGE.
Figure 7 uses 5'-phosphorylated RNA with a 2',3'-cyclic phosphate end.
The 3'-phosphate signal rises before the 3'-ppG-assigned band. Starting material includes
about 10% 3'-phosphate; about 10% ligation is attributed to residual 5'-OH.
Its no-GTP control is described as pRNAp in the caption but pRNA>p in the
main text and appears cyclic in the image. The adopted processing edges use
the unambiguous plus-GTP time course, not that control's disputed identity.
Figure 2 separately uses 5'-OH RNA>p: WT plus GTP generates the ligation
junction T1 fragment; GTP omission or H337A prevents efficient sealing.
The panel time units differ (seconds versus minutes). Slow residual hydrolysis
without GTP is not a universal zero. These are enzyme-excess experiments,
not steady-state turnover, native tRNA repair or a mammalian cofactor assay.
The RNA 3'-ppG assignment uses mobility, GTP dependence and prior intermediate
chemistry; no independent chemical reidentification of this RNA product was
performed here. Its specific chemical-conversion edge is retained as INFERRED.
