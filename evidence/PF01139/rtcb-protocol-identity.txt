Protocol provenance (paraphrased from PMID:22045815 and PMID:21224389):
The 2012 studies cite the 2011 purification protocol. It expresses pET28b-
His10-Smt3-RtcB in BL21-CodonPlus(DE3), cleaves the tag with Ulp1, removes it
by nickel chromatography and gel-filters tag-free RtcB. Do not call the final
assayed protein a His-tagged fusion or infer an isolated PF01139 construct.
The original cloning supplement was not inspected. Biological graph taxonomy
therefore uses E. coli species taxon 562, retaining the reference strain limit.
