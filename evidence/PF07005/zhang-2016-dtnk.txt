Curator interpretation:
Purified ReDtnK Q0K4F6 phosphorylates D-threonate using ATP. Table 1 reports
kcat 45 +/- 4 per second, Km 0.12 +/- 0.03 mM. The kinetic assay at 25 C used
100 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10 mM KCl, 10 mM MgCl2, 2.5 mM ATP, 2.5 mM PEP,
0.16 mM NADH and PK/LDH coupling in 200 uL. ADP production was measured via
NADH loss, not direct product mass spectrometry in this assay.
The intact enzyme contains both current domains PF07005 and PF17042; neither
isolated domain was shown sufficient. 4-hydroxy-L-threonine has lower efficiency.
Other family members accept different substrates. Genome context and similarity
alone are not causal evidence. Supplementary tables, constructs and growth plots
were not independently inspected; no physiological or domain-only edge is added.
