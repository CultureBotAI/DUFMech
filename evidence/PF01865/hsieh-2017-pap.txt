Identity and context: Table2-1 (PDF93/printed78), Table2-4 (PDF98/printed83),
Methods2.8/2.10 (PDF39-40,45-48), Results3.2/3.3 (PDF57-66).
RmP3924 delta-pap and RmP3926 delta-pap-pit derive from the RmP3421
conditional Pho-regulon background. Assays without IPTG follow depletion of
PstSCAB-PhoUB/PhoR; this is not an otherwise WT strain. Growth uses MOPS-P2,
20 mM glucose, 2 mM Pi, 30 C, OD600 technical triplicates. Uptake uses 10 uM
33P-Pi, 30 C, biological triplicates and the 30/60/90-s slope normalized to
Bradford total protein. Toluene-treated cells supply the background correction.
Methods calls the additional transporter allele phnC; Table2-1 calls it phoC.
Do not silently resolve that discrepancy or assign effects to isolated Pap.

Original Figures3-15/3-16 visually inspected (PDF118/119, printed103/104).
RmP3924 with pap-only integration has poor growth and uptake velocity 0.78;
full pap-pit pTH2824 gives 3.13; operon-restoring pap-pit-truncated integration
gives 3.48; the alternative split-promoter architecture gives no growth rescue.
Other constructs pap-truncated-pit and pit alone give 0.07 and 0.14. Units
nmol Pi/mg protein/min. Figure3-15 groups 3.13/3.48 together and the three
lower values together by ANOVA/Tukey, p<0.05. Figure3-16 PCR distinguishes
the two recombinants; only the restored operon rescues growth. Polar effects
on downstream pit expression remain unresolved; pap alone is not sufficient.

Original Figure3-29 visually inspected (PDF132/printed117): variant Pap bands
can differ in size; E54A/D58A/D160A caption and D160A/D93A Results wording
are not fully consistent. Folding, abundance, processing and Pit translation
are not excluded explanations for reduced uptake. This is whole-cell function,
not a purified Pap activity or a defined metal-coordination mechanism.
