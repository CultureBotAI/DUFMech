Identity/genetics: TableI distinguishes W303-1B/CN3(pmp3::TRP1), YR93(pmr2
deletion)/YR93-3(additional pmp3::URA3), YR93-1(additional nha1::LEU2)/YR93-31
(pmp3 and nha1 deleted), and CY162(trk1/trk2 deleted)/CY162-3(additional
pmp3::URA3). Gene replacement is PCR checked. No native PMP3 add-back for
each measured endpoint is shown. RCI2A rescue uses a distinct triple-deletion
background and cannot serve as endpoint-specific rescue for every other assay.
YD is2% yeast extract(KAT),2% glucose, not a peptone-containing YPD recipe;
solid medium adds2% agar. MM is0.7% yeast nitrogen base without amino acids,
2% glucose and required amino acids, which are not enumerated here.

Figure3: seed10ul saturated culture into3mlYD plus0-1.5M NaCl,15h30C;
relative OD600 normalized to the same strain in YD without added salt. Three
repeats gave similar results; plotted error estimates not supplied. Deleting
PMP3 lowers normalized growth in W303-1B, in the pmr2-deleted YR93 background,
and with both pmr2/nha1 deleted. Do not compare different backgrounds as if
they were one paired intervention. Figure2A spot growth at1.3MNaCl supports
the W303 phenotype, but Figure3 is the retained liquid-growth endpoint.

Figure2B: CN3 accumulates more intracellular Na than W303-1B over30-90min;
not an initial Na-influx rate or direct voltage measurement. Protocol conflict:
caption says0.9MNaCl added to exponential YD cells; Results/Methods say1M,
with Methods describing water preincubation1h4C and challenge in10mMMESpH6,
50mMglucose at28C. Exact challenge medium/dose remain unresolved. Cells are
washed in20mMMgCl2/1.5Msorbitol, extracted with2mMCTAB and analyzed by atomic
emission. No calibrated cell-volume measurement or raw replicate file was
independently reanalyzed. Sorbitol growth control is data not shown.

Figure5B: washed MM-grown W303-1B/CN3 cells atOD600=5 in10mMMESpH6,50mM
glucose; after5min add2mM14C-methylammonium,2.5uCi/ml. At10-60min filter
samples and wash with20mMMgCl2 before scintillation. CN3 retains more label;
two repeats had similar results. This is uptake/accumulation, not a calibrated
membrane-potential trace or proof of a Pmp3 proton-conducting pore. Assay
incubation temperature is not explicitly restated in this uptake Methods.
Figure5A: tenfold serial spots onYD,2days30C. CN3 grows less at100ug/ml
hygromycinB and0.7MTMA; TMA retains growth at the most concentrated spot.
AtpH2.2 CN3 and W303-1B look similar, so acid protection is not a universal
single-deletion phenotype. Spots are growth endpoints, not quantitative killing.

Figure4A: CY162-3 grows better than CY162 in YD without addedKCl plus1mM
EGTA over the displayed15-30h. This is not potassium-free medium or a permanent
yield advantage. Text describes later48h adaptation/suppressors not displayed
in the30h graph. Replacing1mMEGTA by10mMCaCl2 lowers CY162-3 growth, with
residual growth visible; this jointly changes calcium and chelation.
Figure4C: inocula pregrown with100mMKCl;10ul into3mlYD with100mMKCl at
variedpH,15h30C. Normalize within each strain to pH5.5. CY162-3 has higher
relative growth atpH3-4 than CY162. Three repeats, not a direct proton-flux assay.
Figure4D: tenfold spots atpH3 with100mMKCl,2days30C; adding10mMCaCl2
impairs CY162-3 growth while W303-1B/CN3 remain strong. Separate from liquid
pH normalization and EGTA/no-added-KCl conditions.

Figure7B:10ul saturated YR93 or YR93-3 into3mlYD,0.2MNaCl,0-10mMCaCl2
or MgCl2,15h30C. Each series is normalized to its corresponding10mMCaCl2
culture. In YR93-3, Ca improves growth toward a plateau around7mM; Mg yields
partial improvement up to10mM. YR93 is near its reference across the range.
Two repeats had similar results. This does not directly measure NSC1 blockade
or membrane depolarization. Figure7A also distinguishes EGTA/Ca conditions.

Figure8A: plantRCI2A replaces pmp3::URA3 at the PMP3 locus in YR93-31 under
PMP3 regulatory regions, not a plasmid copy. In3mlYD salt series seeded with
10ul saturated culture,15h30C, it increases normalized growth relative to
YR93-31. The rescued curve approaches but is not identical to YR93-1; no
exact equivalence test is displayed. Two repeats had similar results.
Figure8B:100ug/ml hygromycinB,YD,tenfold spots,2days30C; RCI2A improves
growth but does not restore the complete YR93-1 dilution pattern. Rescue is
partial and in yeast, not evidence for a plant whole-organism salt mechanism.

Figure6: CN3 and W303 membrane ATPase/MgATP and ACMA-quenching relations
are broadly similar; triple pmp1/pmp2/pmp3 deletion has lower ATPase activity.
Do not attribute the triple-mutant effect uniquely to Pmp3 or infer activation
of Pma1 from the methylammonium phenotype. Authors' proton-leak/NSC1 proposals
and intracellular-pH/medium-acidification data-not-shown remain model/limits.
All retained perturbation edges are indirect cellular phenotypes. Neither the
paper's title nor conservation across homologs proves a direct proton pathway.
