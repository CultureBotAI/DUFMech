Minimal exact excerpt: "stronger suppressor activity"

Interpretation: Figure2 compares qmcA-ybbJ plasmid pSTD622 with qmcA-only
pSTD620 in AD1762 lacking ftsH and htpX; sfhC21 is present in all tested
ftsH-defective strains. Both inserts were amplified from MC4100 into pTWV228.
Figure2A used 3-ul serial-dilution spots on L agar with 1mM IPTG or 0.4%
glucose; incubation was 30C/17h or 42C/24h. Figure2B followed liquid growth
after a 30C-to-42C shift with 1mM IPTG. The joint construct improves both
growth readouts relative to qmcA alone. YbbJ alone did not suppress. QmcA
expression was reported similar between constructs, but those data were not
shown. No effect size, replicate count or isolated-domain sufficiency is assigned.

These are conditional whole-cell effects, not proof of YbbJ proteolysis,
direct chaperoning, FtsH activation or normal-strain heat resistance.
The two readouts are not sequential pathway steps. Full historical YbbJ
clone sequence and matched quantitative abundance controls remain unresolved.
