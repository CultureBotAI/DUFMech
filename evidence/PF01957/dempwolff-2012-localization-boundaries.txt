The NfeD2/YuaF-FloT recruitment and altered foci observations involve O32077,
currently PF31148/PF25842, and are excluded from PF01957 graphs. For the mapped
PF01957 member NfeD1b/YqeZ, the paper reports no visually discernible dependence
of localization on FloA/FloT/YqfB or vice versa under tested conditions. These
qualitative negatives do not establish independence under every stress.

Source labeling needs care: original Figure4 image labels N as NfeD2 loss,
R as NfeD1b loss, O as FloA truncation, and P as YqfB depletion. Main text and
parts of the caption swap some of those panel references. Original Figure6
image contains A-I, including YuaI panels C/D and YqeZ panels E-I, whereas
the accompanying caption describes only A-G. Do not silently map the caption's
NfeD1b labels onto the YuaI images or assign a molecular effect from those errors.

YqeZ loss used internal-plasmid truncation with a xylose promoter for downstream
genes; it was not an isolated PF01957 deletion. PY79/DML1541 derivatives and
rich/minimal media are distinct contexts. No new causal edge is supported by
the inspected PF01957-specific localization comparisons.
