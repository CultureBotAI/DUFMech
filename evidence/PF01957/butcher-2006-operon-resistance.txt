Figure4A: CU1065 (trpC2, no SPbeta immunity), versus HB5331
yqeZyqfAB::kan, exposed to JH642 producer on 0.7% LB agar. Operon deletion
increases inhibition. Producer sunA deletion and SPbeta restoration remove
the inhibition; yqfB-only deletion is similarly sensitive. This is a joint
operon effect, not a clean YqeZ-only or PF01957-domain knockout.
Figure4B: HB6161 expresses the entire operon from integrated Pxyl in
CU1065 sigW::kan; induction with 20mM xylose improves resistance relative
to the matched sigW control. Xylose itself reduces zones, requiring matched
xylose conditions. yqfAB and yqfB alone did not complement; the former also
changes the yqeZ locus, so this is not isolated YqeZ sufficiency.
