Escherichia coli K12 YbbJ is P0AAS3, sequence version 2, 152 aa,
loci b0488/JW5065; sequence last updated 2007-06-26. PF01957 maps 94-148.
Chiba2006 cloned from MC4100; this current sequence is an identity reference,
not a verified reconstruction of its pre-2007 plasmid sequence.
Bacillus subtilis 168 NfeD1/YqeZ is P54465, version 1, 437 aa,
locus BSU25390; PF01957 maps 382-435. CU1065 experimental alleles and
fusion/truncation junctions were not independently sequence-reconstructed.
These are full-protein or operon experiments, not isolated 54-aa domain tests.

B. subtilis NfeD2/YuaF is O32077 v1, 174 aa, BSU31020. Both current UniProt
cross-references and the complete two-entry InterPro/Pfam response assign
PF25842 at 1-101 and PF31148 at 114-168, not PF01957. Its historical generic
NfeD name does not justify importing FloT recruitment effects into PF01957.
PF31148 membership does not automatically add a record to the frozen corpus.
The unrelated B. subtilis gene named ybbJ (Q45583) must not be substituted for
E. coli YbbJ. Likewise the unrelated E. coli yuaF is not Bacillus NfeD2.

Pyrococcus horikoshii PH1510 is O59179 v1, 441 aa. Current PF01957 377-432
is separate from PF25145 24-196 and PF24961 238-359. The published isolated
N-terminal protease construct is not an isolated PF01957 activity experiment.
PH0471 is O58204 v1, 143 aa with a PF01957 cross-reference; its exact domain
coordinates and original structural/binding experiments need further review.

The frozen PF01957/InterPro:IPR002810 identity, EX_DUF seed status and counts
are preserved. Current API coordinates qualify experimental scope without
replacing the 2026-10-10 source snapshot or establishing universal function.
