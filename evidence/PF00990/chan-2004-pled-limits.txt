site, superseding the earlier competitive-binding suggestion. Its proposed

Its assay conditions differ from the earlier Genes & Development study.
Supporting Figure 6 and its raw fits were not visually inspected: the linked
PMC PDF returned HTML, the Europe PMC XML endpoint failed with HTTP 500, and
an institutional thesis PDF URL returned an HTML item page. The adopted
qualitative inhibition edge instead uses the accessible earlier Figure 6A.
No new Ki estimate, phosphorylation-independent universal feedback claim or
crystal-contact-based catalytic microstep is adopted.
