Mth RPR titration0.3-20 uM, ~2 nM internally radiolabeled E.coli pre-tRNATyr,
55 C; denaturing PAGE/phosphorimaging. Proteins supplied at twice RNA molarity.
At nominal pH5.8, optimized RPR-alone buffer2 M NH4/500 mM Mg gives
KM(STO)21.3 +/-2.6 uM and maximum kobs0.13 +/-0.01/min. RPR+RPP21/RPP29
at0.8 M NH4/120 mM Mg gives1.4 +/-0.2 uM and0.13 +/-0.01/min. These are
joint protein/buffer protocols, not a protein-only controlled affinity assay.
SupplementS4 dilution controls support rapid substrate escape relative to
cleavage; fitted KM(STO) is still not an independently measured protein Kd.
SupplementS5 shows incomplete (~40%) amplitude for the two-protein reaction.

At3 uM RPR,30 mM Mg/0.8 M NH4, adding RPP21/RPP29 to RPR+POP5/RPP30
changes the reported kobs0.016 +/-0.0004 to5.5 +/-0.18/min. Four-protein
measurements were actually atpH5.4 then multiplied by2.5 forpH5.8 comparison.
S6 supports near-unit log-rate/pH dependence but uses20 rather than30 mM Mg;
its drawn fitted slope is1.1. Retain this normalization and transfer limit.
RNA alone and RNA+RPP21/RPP29 at30 mM Mg are not reliably quantifiable.
