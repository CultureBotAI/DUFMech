Quotation: "reduction of peak current"
Curator interpretation: Enterokinase-cleaved, RP-HPLC-purified recombinant ShK
reduces mouse Kv1.3 and Kv1.1 peak currents in separate stably transfected L929
lines. Functional Hill-fit midpoints are 25 +/- 10 pM and 15 +/- 5 pM,
respectively. The article calls these Kd values, but the measurement is current
inhibition, not an independent physical binding assay. Figure 6C reports 3-5
determinations per point; 6B's example trace is synthetic peptide, not recombinant.
Synthetic-peptide comparisons give no statistically significant potency difference.
Mass and NMR comparisons support the recombinant preparation's identity/fold;
enterokinase leaves no extra N-terminal residue. Exact channel clone sequences,
independent peptide batches and underlying current data were not reanalyzed.
Curator sequence check: the two printed primers overlap exactly by 21 bases.
Their assembled coding translation contains DDDDK followed by the exact 35-aa
P29187 sequence and a stop codon. This verifies the printed design, not an
independent resequencing of the historical experimental clone.
