Preparation: full-length K-12 tatD PCR product, NdeI/XhoI into pET22b,
C-terminal His tag; BL21-CodonPlus-RIPL production, Ni-NTA then Q-Sepharose
purification. QuikChange point mutants used the same purification workflow.
The native 260-aa sequence is not the complete tagged experimental sequence.

Figure 2B: 40 nM 5'-32P-labeled 20-nt ssDNA, TatD concentration series
0-0.5 uM, 37 C for 1 h. General reaction buffer is 3 mM MOPS, 75 mM NaCl,
pH 7.2, 10 uL. DNA sequence: CAAACTCTCTCTCTCTCAAC (20 bases).
Mg2+ or Mn2+ at 2 mM supports digestion; no added metal, 1 mM Ca2+ and
100 uM Zn2+ lack detected digestion under this particular comparison.
The graph adopts the Mg-containing joint reaction producing shorter DNA.
Figure 2C compares 5'- and 3'-labeling, supporting 3'-5' exonucleolysis;
2D blocks the 3' end with biotin/streptavidin. These support direction but
are not additional quantified graph edges. Neither establishes rejection
of all duplex substrates or an absence of AP endonuclease activity.

Figure 5C: WT and H62A/H64A/H127A/H152A in the upper gel; WT and
E91A/E201A/D203A in the lower gel. H62A reduces 20-nt ssDNA digestion;
the acidic substitutions give little residual activity. H64A/H127A/H152A
retain digestion. Lanes show a 0.5/1/2 series but the figure and caption
do not give its units. The general assay method gives 37 C and 1 or 2 h,
but does not resolve this panel's exact duration, metal concentration or
DNA dose. Do not borrow Figure 2B's values to fill these gaps.
Mutant effects do not establish a unique catalytic base, metal stoichiometry,
or intact folding of every preparation. Discussion explicitly leaves the
two-/three-metal mechanism and possible cofactors unresolved.

Figure 3B: WT and DeltaTatD K-12 cells from the Keio collection; serial
spots under chronic 1 mM H2O2, overnight at 37 C. Deletion reduces colony
growth relative to WT; drug-free controls are shown. Separate MMS 3 mM,
MMC 120 nM and UV-C comparisons do not show the same defect. This is a
whole-gene phenotype, not direct measurement of a DNA-repair reaction.
No genetic rescue is shown for the adopted comparison. The exact deletion
strain identifier and basal spot-medium composition are not resolved here.
Acute peroxide outgrowth in panel C is a different protocol, not this edge.

Figure 3D's resistant 3'-terminal abasic substrate is not the internal THF
duplex tested in 2023. The two results are not a direct contradiction.
The general method prints a protease-K quench concentration of 20 mM;
this questionable unit is retained as unresolved, not silently corrected.
Supplemental and complete clone details remain follow-up work.
