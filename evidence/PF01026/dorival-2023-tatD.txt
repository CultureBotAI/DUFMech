EcTatD was untagged: synthetic gene in modified pET-21, BL21(DE3),
0.1 mM IPTG, 15 C for 12-18 h; PEI, ammonium sulfate, phenyl-Sepharose,
Q-Sepharose and size-exclusion purification. The His-SUMO construct and
tag-cleavage procedure described for other proteins do not apply to TatD.

Figure 6 TatD lanes: 10 uM protein, 100 nM DNA, 20 mM EPPS pH 8.0,

ssDNA and undamaged duplex comparisons use 10 mM MgCl2. The gel labels
