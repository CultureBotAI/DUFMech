Curator interpretation:
The N-terminal dGT1 construct (residues 1-272, S. parasanguinis FW213) transfers
glucose from UDP-glucose to Fap1 already modified by Gtf1/2/3. The radiotracer
assay used 20 ug enzyme, 0.2 uCi UDP-[3H]glucose, 50 mM Hepes pH 7.0,
10 mM MnCl2, 0.01% BSA, 200 uL, 37 C for one hour. The measured readout was
radioactivity retained with the washed acceptor. Figure 2 compares full-length,
N-terminal, C-terminal and GST constructs; heat-inactivated controls normalize
background. Metal omission loses activity; Mn, Mg and Ca are not equivalent.
This supports the isolated domain-containing construct's transfer reaction,
not synthesis of mature Fap1 by this construct alone. Figure 4 glycan profiling
and later C-terminal-domain functions were not independently visually reviewed.
