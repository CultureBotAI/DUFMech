cob(I)alamin or enzymatically reduced cob(II)alamin. Cob(I)inamide is

reducing system; SeCobA positive control works. Abstract wording is broader
than this oxidation-state-specific result. Figure5 product supports growth
and photolyzes like AdoCbl, but the no-added-ATP preparation partly rescues,
consistent with retained ATP rather than a perfect omission control.

Tris100mM pH7.5,KCl500mM,Mg2mM,HOCbl100uM,ATP3mM,Ti(III)1mM;
no-cobalamin control lacks this product. Not all EutT phosphate products
can be equated. Chelation/transition-metal additions do not significantly
alter activity; MgATP is still used. ICP limits do not establish zero atoms.
