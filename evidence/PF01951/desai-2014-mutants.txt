Figure 6A-C: 70 C, 30 min; 50 mM Bis-Tris pH 7.0, 300 mM NaCl,
0.25 mM MnCl2, 0.10 mM GTP or ATP, 5 uM PhRtcB, 100 nM PhArchease,
1 uM each 10-nt RNA half. Readout: 20-nt ligation product; two experiments.
GTP reductions versus WT: H9A, D12A, K117A, D141A, deltaI142.
ATP reductions: those five plus E8A. E8A has no detrimental GTP effect.
Native O59205 v1 positions independently checked: E8/H9/D12/K117/D141/I142.
Paper uses native numbering despite inserted L after the initiating M.
S5 shows comparable predominant bands for WT and all six variants (~4 ug/lane),
not a native-fold or thermal-stability test. Effects remain UNRESOLVED at the
molecular level; crystal Ca and static contacts do not prove metal delivery.

Figure 7A-C: same endpoint conditions, WT Archease added versus omitted.
GTP: RtcB F204A/E206A/K480A recover ligation; D95A recovers RNA-ppG,
not ligation. ATP: E206A/K480A recover ligation; F204A does not.
RtcB D65A/N202A/H203A/H404A are not rescued for ligation in these assays.
These are RtcB partner variants, not Archease residues. Archease addition is
the intervention; no new Archease catalytic chemistry is assigned.
Figure 7D is a 15-min titration; 7E/F use 800 nM Archease. Their kinetic
comparisons with WT RtcB are not pooled with the 100 nM endpoint contrasts.
Negative direct GTP-binding results do not establish a purine pocket in Archease.
S1 pH titration changes buffer species; no isolated pH-causality edge is inferred.
