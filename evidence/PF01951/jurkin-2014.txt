Figure1C: cells harvested 3 days after control siGFP, Archease, RTCB or joint
siRNA transfection. A 3'-labeled, LNA-stabilized XBP1 RNA fragment pre-cleaved
by IRE1 is incubated with extract for 15 min. Archease-only and joint depletion
both reduce the spliced product relative to siGFP. Standard Methods specify
0.1 uM RNA, 5-min IRE1 precleavage and 2 ug/ul extract; assay temperature is
not unambiguously specified in the inspected protocol. RTCB-only reduction
is a comparator, not an Archease knockout. No numeric yield inferred from gel.

S1D: lanes labeled Buffer, Archease WT, D39A, K144A and BSA, 2/5/10/30 min.
WT treatment increases the spliced product; both mutant preparations fail
to reach WT output. Reported 30-min normalized signals: WT 7.01, buffer 1.64,
D39A 1.51, K144A 1.08, BSA 1.71, all normalized to BUFFER AT TWO MINUTES.
This is not a sevenfold WT-versus-buffer comparison at 30 min. Replicate
number/statistical test is not supplied for this panel. Main Results/Methods
describe recombinant Archease addition (0.2 ug/ul final when added), whereas
the S1D caption describes lysates of cells overexpressing WT or mutant forms.
Both descriptions are retained: preparation-associated effects are UNRESOLVED,
not an unambiguous purified-protein mechanism or cellular rescue experiment.
S1E FLAG-Archease IP lacks detectable ligation unlike FLAG-RTCB/FLAG-DDX1;
no intrinsic Archease ligase activity is established.

Figure2A/B and S2A/B: Tet-ON HeLa, 1 ug/ml doxycycline for six days, then
300 nM thapsigargin (Tg), 0/2/4/8/16/24 h. Single Archease shRNA lowers
late-stress XBP1s protein signal in the representative Western. The mean
spliced RNA fraction trends lower, especially at 16-24 h, but S2B labels
the overall comparison NONSIGNIFICANT (N=3, SEM). Retain the trend in this
ledger, not as a directed causal edge or absence of all XBP1s. The fraction
is XBP1s/(XBP1s+XBP1u), not the XBP1s/XBP1u ratio or absolute abundance.
RTCB-alone shRNA preserves much XBP1s induction; its co-depletion of DDX1
and FAM98B (S2A) prevents treating it as a clean single-protein mechanism.

Figure2C-I/S2C: combined Archease+RTCB shRNAs versus TWO control shRNAs,
same six-day Dox and 24-h Tg regimen. XBP1s protein is strongly reduced;
do not claim absolute absence from every lane. Spliced RNA fraction is lower
in the aggregate time course (S2C, N=5, SEM, P<0.001), especially 2-16 h.
The representative Figure2D 24-h fraction does not follow the same ordering;
no every-time-point assertion. RT-qPCR is ACTB- and untreated-control-normalized:
XBP1s RNA induction is lower (Figure2E, P<0.05), total XBP1 RNA accumulation
lower (F, P<0.0001), EDEM1 induction lower (H, P<0.01), and DNAJB9 induction
lower (I, P<0.01), N=5, SEM, two-way ANOVA. These are separate joint-perturbation
outcomes, not an experimentally isolated feedback/mediation chain. Figure2G
XBP1u and S2D/E HSPA5/CHOP differences are NONSIGNIFICANT and remain controls,
not supported specific downstream causal requirements. S2F/G RIDD-target
BLOS1/PDGFRB responses are also nonsignificant between the depleted/control
groups; this does not directly assay IRE1 cleavage kinetics or prove all RIDD
targets unaffected. No apoptosis phenotype is inferred from CHOP expression.

S3A/B: after six-day Dox induction WITHOUT the Tg time course, Northern blots
show lower mature Ile and Arg tRNA signals with single Archease shRNA and with
joint Archease/RTCB shRNAs, compared with their respective one-/two-vector
controls. N=3, U6 loading control; no numeric fold or panel-specific significance
invented. Methionine tRNA is the splicing-independent comparison. S3C/D metabolic
labeling after Met/Cys starvation, then one hour 35S-Met/Cys, does not show a
global protein-synthesis decrease (N=2). S3D Archease-only lane also has reduced
RTCB protein, unlike the strong RTCB retention in S2A; do not assert universal
absence of partner co-depletion. Mature RNA levels do not isolate production
from turnover, and these assays do not prove a tRNA-to-XBP1 mediation chain.

Protocol/source-discrepancy ledger:
- Main Methods label a ligation buffer as 1x with 400 mM KCl, 125 mM spermidine,
  20 mM ATP, 20 mM GTP, 10 mM DTT and 25 mM MgCl2. No verified stock/dilution
  clarification was found in the inspected supplement; do not silently scale it.
- RNAi buffer lists 5 uM MgCl2 and 0.5 uM DTT, whereas extract lysis buffer
  has 5 mM and 0.5 mM respectively. Their actual preparation remains unresolved.
- The sentence assigning spliced/unspliced templates to genomic DNA/cDNA has
  an apparent ordering problem; the verified gel product assignments are used,
  not an invented corrected template sequence.
- HSPA5 forward and reverse primers are printed identically; no exact HSPA5
  amplicon or mechanism is inferred without resolving the protocol.
- S1D overexpression-lysate caption versus recombinant-addition Methods/Results
  remains explicit. This is not treated as a proven knockdown rescue.
