Identity and assay ownership:
Human Q8IWT0 v2 is the 167-aa reference with PF01951 31-167. ED4 uses this
numbering (D39, K144, T147). FLAG/pDONR forward primer starts MAQ..., whereas
the recombinant His6 pET28a primer starts MKGG... with an additional initiating
codon. Do not silently equate the N termini. Mutant names below are the paper's
D39A/K144A labels; complete recombinant mutant sequences remain unresolved.
See identity-turnover-human-20261012.txt. WT and mutant purification is shown
in ED3a, but purity is not native folding or thermal-stability verification.

FLAG-RTCB is an affinity-purified multiprotein complex from HEK293 cells,
including DDX1 and other partners, not isolated RTCB. Archease is recombinant
His6 protein from E. coli. Linear RNA is the intron obtained by endonuclease
cleavage of a hybrid pre-tRNA. Products include linear concatemer, circular
concatemer and circular intron. Quantified Pconcat includes linear AND circular
concatemer signals, not every ligated species. The intron preparation is
dissolved in 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 100 mM KOAc, 6 mM Mg(OAc)2 and 150 uM
spermine; do not assume those are final concentrations in every reaction.
Base ligation/maturation procedures cite Popow2011 PMID:21311021. Its detailed
supporting protocol was not recovered from attempted public publisher/MPG
endpoints in this follow-up. Missing final buffer/timing details are a coverage
limit, not permission to invent them or import Gerber2024 Mn conditions.

Biochemical edge ledger:
1. Figure 1d: 20 nM FLAG-RTCB complex, 100 nM RNA, 10 uM Archease versus
   buffer at 1 and 10 min. Added Archease increases ligated products and
   substrate consumption. This is joint complex activity, not Archease ligase.
2. Figure 1e: 20 nM complex, 500 nM RNA; 10 uM Archease added at 60 min
   restarts Pconcat accumulation through 120 min versus matched buffer.
   Three technical replicates, mean +/- SD. Separate from initial addition.
3. ED3b/c: after preincubation of 30 nM complex/300 nM RNA for 90 min at
   37 C, dilution gives final 22.5 nM complex/225 nM RNA. Archease 40 nM to
   10.25 uM, 4-min equilibration, samples 4/5.5/7/8.5 min. Formation rate rises
   around submicromolar/micromolar Archease; half-maximal response around 1 uM.
   The 10.24 uM point is below the plateau: no globally monotonic dose claim,
   binding Kd, Hill coefficient or cellular effective concentration inferred.
4. Figure 3c: 10 uM WT Archease enables RTCB labeling from alpha-32P-GTP
   compared with buffer; D39A/K144A do not. Alpha-32P-ATP is negative as donor.
   A 6.5-ul assay at 37 C contains 2.5 ul FLAG eluate, 0.5 ul Archease,
   3.25 ul nucleotidylation STOCK buffer, 0.25 ul radiolabel. The stock has
   20 mM Tris pH 7.5, 200 mM KOAc, 12 mM Mg acetate, 300 uM spermine,
   1 mM DTT, 1 uM each ATP/GTP, 1% Triton and 30% glycerol; these are NOT
   all final assay concentrations. Incubation duration not specified here.
5. Figure 3d: 20 nM complex, 500 nM RNA, 10 uM Archease +/- 0.5 mM GTP,
   0-60 min. Archease plus added GTP gives much greater Pconcat accumulation
   than Archease without added GTP or GTP without Archease. Three technical
   replicates +/- SD. Residual initial activity does not prove no nucleotide
   requirement: co-purified nucleotide is possible.
6. Figure 1f: 20 nM complex, 1 uM RNA, 10 uM WT or named D39A/K144A.
   Mutants fail to support WT-like product accumulation. This and Figure 3c
   are four distinct mutant/output comparisons; molecular reasons remain
   unresolved. Do not infer that substitution solely removes a metal contact.

Extract and living-cell edge ledger:
7. Figure 4a: HeLa RNAi targeting Archease lowers in vitro pre-tRNA maturation
   compared with control (5/10/30/60 min). RTCB-only and double depletion are
   distinct conditions. ED10a confirms mRNA knockdown; ED10b does not show
   RTCB protein co-depletion after Archease depletion. No knockdown-to-direct
   domain catalysis edge, and no newly inferred exact transfection duration.
8. Figure 4b/c: Archease-depleted HeLa extracts with empty vector, c-myc WT,
   D39A or K144A expression, assayed at 15 and 45 min. WT restores maturation;
   each mutant fails to restore WT-like activity. Western verifies expression
   of each construct, not matched native folding. This is Archease-specific
   rescue in HeLa, unlike the separate DDX1 rescue in Suzuki2025 U2OS work.
9. Figure 4e: HeLa T-Rex host cells, doxycycline 1 ug/ml induction of tagged
   S. cerevisiae pre-tRNA3-PheGAA transgene. Northern at 0/24/48 h after
   induction shows reduced mature tagged RNA after Archease RNAi, with U6
   loading control. This is a yeast RNA transgene in human cells, not an
   endogenous human-tRNA measurement or a yeast knockout. Synthesis/stability
   and processing alternatives are not fully isolated by this endpoint.

Excluded interpretations and remaining work:
- ED5c/d: added Archease does not measurably change tested TSEN cleavage;
  no cleavage-stimulation edge. ED5 binding/crosslinking is not activation.
- Figure 3a/b and ED6 show transient WT versus persistent mutant RNA adducts;
  extracted RNA in those adducts is substrate/concatemer, not final circular
  products. These do NOT directly establish accelerated final-product release.
- Figure 3e/f and ED7-9 suggest DDX1 nucleotide/ATPase contributions. Partner
  variants, label discrepancies (E271Q/E371Q, R577K/R557K), and controls remain
  open before adding residue-specific partner mechanisms. DDX1 is not Archease.
- Figure 4d plus ED10c include partner knockdowns and co-depletion, especially
  CGI99/DDX1; do not infer independent direct requirements for each protein.
- ED10d caption points to Figure 4b although Results cite it with Figure 4e.
  Do not silently reinterpret the source's cross-reference as exact provenance.
- No Archease-specific XBP1, neuronal, fly or plasma-cell effects are inferred
  from these tRNA assays. No independent expert or raw-data audit is claimed.
