Figure3e: With Archease and alpha-32P-GTP, ATP enhances FLAG-RTCB-associated
guanylate labeling versus no added ATP. Replacing ATP with AMPPcP reduces the
signal. FLAG-DDX1 preparations containing K52N or E371Q also have less
RTCB-associated label than the WT-DDX1 preparation; K52N is most affected.
The main nucleotidylation Methods give 10 uM Archease and 37 C, but do not
unambiguously give the added ATP/analogue concentration for this panel.
Do not borrow the 0.5 mM ligation concentration or treat stock buffer as final.
AMPPcP replacement does not by itself isolate hydrolysis from analogue binding.

Figure3f: WT/K52N/E371Q FLAG-DDX1 complexes, 20 nM complex, 1 uM linear
intron RNA, 10 uM Archease, 0.5 mM ATP versus AMPPcP. At 30/60/90 min,
WT complex plus Archease/ATP has greater Pconcat accumulation than its
buffer/ATP or Archease/AMPPcP controls. K52N and E371Q preparations yield
less product than WT under the matched Archease/ATP condition. N=3 technical
replicates, mean +/- SD. Pconcat includes linear and circular concatemer,
not isolated circular intron. These are intervention/output comparisons,
not independently observed ATP-to-guanylate-to-product mediation steps.

ED7 controls: K52N and E371Q reduce ATP hydrolysis in the separate 60-min
37 C assay (N=4, SEM). Both reduce ATP UV-crosslinking; loading is shown.
K52N has lower RNA retention, while E371Q is not similarly reduced in the
filter-binding assay (N=9, SEM). Do not claim unchanged RNA binding for every
mutant or a uniquely hydrolysis-specific molecular defect. Purity/loading and
assembly controls do not exclude all folding or complex-composition effects.

ED8c-e/ED9: 20 nM FLAG-DDX1 ligase complex, 10 uM Archease, RNA 100 nM to
4 uM. Rates come from the first 15 min (N=2, SEM), counting concatemerized
AND circular product species, unlike the main-figure Pconcat-only convention.
Reported apparent kcat values (10^-3/s, mean +/- SEM) are WT 4.32 +/- 0.24,
K52N 0.99 +/- 0.14, E371Q 0.88 +/- 0.09; each K/E comparison is marked
P<0.0004 by the paper's Bonferroni-corrected z-test. KM comparisons are
nonsignificant. Do not treat these fitted complex parameters as elementary
Archease catalytic constants or independently reanalyzed raw data.

Source discrepancies and negatives:
- ED8 caption says E271Q, but panels/table and main Figure3 say E371Q.
  Current Q92499 has H271 and E371; retain the main/panel E371Q label without
  claiming a verified historical clone sequence.
- ED7 schematic/ED8 table say R557K; ED8 gel and ED9 tables say R577K.
  Q92499 v2 has N557 and R577. No exact Arg-variant edge is added until its
  historical clone is resolved. Its reported lower apparent turnover remains
  a lead; a presumed typo is not silently repaired.
- ED8 caption says kcat is Vmax multiplied by enzyme concentration. This is
  dimensionally inconsistent; the plotted rates divided by 20 nM instead
  agree with the reported scale. Retain reported parameters and the conflict,
  not a silently corrected raw-data calculation.
- ED8b uses AMPPnP, whereas Figure3f uses AMPPcP; do not conflate analogues.
- H601Q has a smaller reported mean kcat change, marked nonsignificant;
  no established H601-specific requirement is claimed.
- ED7a is a tentative cycle model, not an observed release/transfer trajectory.

Figure4d/ED10c: HeLa extracts after DDX1 or CGI99 RNAi, all supplemented
with 10 uM recombinant Archease, show reduced pre-tRNA maturation at
10/30/60 min versus siEGFP control. CGI99 depletion co-depletes DDX1;
no independent direct CGI99 requirement or unique molecular mediation is
assigned. RTCB depletion and other subunit controls remain separate. Added
Archease does not justify assigning partner-only cellular defects to its domain.
