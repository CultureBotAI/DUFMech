Curator paraphrases and measured facts:
Full-length LarCMt was expressed as a synthetic N-terminal His6-TEV pLW01
construct in E. coli BL21(DE3). Autoinduction at20C normally included1mM
NiCl2 after4h, harvest around20h. His60 Ni Superflow purification applies
also to the preparation from culture without added nickel. That preparation
is not proven apoprotein or nickel-free; both medium and resin can supply
nickel. Actual occupancy was not established by the inspected experiment.

Methods synthesize P2TMN using NaAD0.2mM,LarBLp10uM,LarELp200uM,
bicarbonate50mM,ATP2mM,MgCl2 20mM,Tris100mM pH7,1h room temperature.
An aliquot is mixed with an equal volume of CTP0.2mM,MgCl2 10mM,
beta-mercaptoethanol10mM,LarCMt2.5uM,MES100mM pH6,room temperature.
These are reported recipe concentrations, not independently resolved final
postmix concentrations. Results/Figure6 say Mn-CTP whereas Methods say
MgCl2; the specific metal recipe needs reconciliation. Standard30min or
specified time courses end by95C/10min heating. The downstream5ul aliquot
assay uses LarATt0.8uM,L-lactate45mM,HEPES100mM pH7,5min50C, followed
by95C/10min and commercial D-lactate quantitation. Default P2TMN preparation
contains upstream LarB/LarE; some experiments use Q-Sepharose purification.
LarATt racemization is a downstream reporter, not LarC racemase activity.

Figure2: added10mM PPi decreases the coupled D-lactate readout;2units PPase
with PPi restores it. PPase alone does not increase control activity.
Two biological replicates use separate enzyme preparations. No isolated
LarC-binding, metal-chelation, residual-upstream-enzyme or reporter-specific
control resolves the molecular inhibition target. Rescue does not show
that accumulated PPi explains the apparent single turnover.

Figure3 timecourse0,1,3,5,10,30,60min: substrate MS feature declines, a new
m/z715.02 feature appears, and downstream LarATt activity rises. The new
feature is absent at0 and withoutCTP; Figure4 MS/MS and isotope pattern
support CMP-P2TMN with a phosphoanhydride and two sulfur atoms. Representative
single experiment, same trends in a second preparation. Relative peak
intensity is not molar amount; no P2TMN standard, NPN signal too weak for
quantitation. Retain whole-reaction scope, not an observed elementary
nickel-transfer sequence or a resolved site-to-site transport mechanism.

Figure6 at30/60min: D256A,E261A and D256A/E261A reduce the downstream
reporter; E364A is near wild type, especially at60min. All four variants
have little715.02 signal. Low accumulated intermediate therefore does not
prove abolished synthesis or necessity of any one residue for chemistry.
Folding, metal occupancy, intermediate consumption and expression effects
remain alternatives. Caption gives MS n2 technical and activity n1 despite
visible activity error bars. Caption normalization to zero-time P2TMN does
not explain all separately scaled panels; do not infer absolute amounts,
stoichiometry, rates or significance from those bar heights. The2022 text
also alternates D387A/E387A for an L. plantarum comparator, which is not
silently reassigned to M. thermoacetica.

The no-added-nickel-growth preparation remains active and yields more715.02
signal than the nickel-supplemented preparation. This supports a preparation-
condition effect on the measured feature, not proof of a nickel-free enzyme,
the intracellular nickel source, or direct inhibition of CMPylation by nickel.
