DNA-binding domain and selected for phage immunity. Table 1 lists YbjQ/b0866

self-association, not a measured native YbjQ assembly transition or stoichiometry.

insert dependence covered libraries EB099/100/101, not all fifteen libraries;
the unspecified YbjQ clone cannot be assigned those controls by assumption.

added from this incomplete construct-level evidence. Retrieving the original
