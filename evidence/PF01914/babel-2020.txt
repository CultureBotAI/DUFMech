MG1655 CGSC6300-derived stock has restored gatC, functional glrR and
REP321j variation; do not assume it equals the canonical reference.
MarC reference P0AEY1 maps PF01914 5-217, not an independently
sequenced clone. Strain953 delta-marC750::kanR,960 deletion plus

Figure4 deletion condition improves relative growth at50mM isoprenol;
Figure5 deletion improves relative growth at5g/L butanol. Fitness is
growth rate divided by the study's initial MG1655 rate in the same
condition. Three biological replicates, mean and SD,37C,M9 glucose5g/L.
No exact values are inferred from bar heights. Vanillin1.5g/L does not
show a strong corresponding benefit; no general solvent claim.
Expression of the M35stop fragment does not add significant fitness
relative to deletion, across the tested IPTG conditions. This is not
wild-type rescue or proof of the fragment's normal activity. The
paper's wording about the35th methionine is not taken literally;
M35 is the reference residue position. The frameshift/truncation and
a complete deletion are different alleles, not proof of identical
molecular effects. No measured isoprenol/butanol transport or direct
lipid mechanism. Rob/YghB and evolved multi-mutation phenotypes are
not transferred to MarC alone.
