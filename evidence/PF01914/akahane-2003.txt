Expression experiments are in heterologous E. coli, not native archaeal
membranes. AK430 is K-12 cycA::kan. pSA28 carries about2.8kb genomic DNA;
pSA17 is a smaller positive derivative containing full snatA. Figure2A
scores glycine uptake and D-alanine colony formation in deletion
constructs; pSA14 retains the full ORF but only97bp upstream and loses
both readouts. Its contrast with pSA17 localizes a needed upstream region
in this plasmid system, not a tested SnatA amino-acid deletion or direct
promoter binding. Figure4A compares pSA28 with empty pUC119; Figure2A
provides the qualitative pSA17 result. These are distinct controls, not
a pSA17 time-course experiment invented from Figure4.
Selection: 5mg/ml D-alanine minimal medium,37C,aboutoneweek.
Uptake: washed cells,37C,HEPES/TrispH7.4,MgSO4,Tris-lactate and
chloramphenicol; cell-associated radiolabeled glycine by filtration.
Figure4A FCCP10ug/ml lowers uptake. Figure4B valinomycin plus nigericin
lowers it; valinomycin alone is not inhibitory. No inhibitor-specific
cell viability or purified SnatA reconstitution is shown. Proton-motive
force dependence is a supported model, not measured H+:glycine coupling
stoichiometry. Na/K independence is reported as data not shown.
Table1 reports apparent whole-cell Kt24uM by Lineweaver-Burk from30s
uptake; do not label this a purified binding Kd.

Table2 uses pSA28/AK430,4uM radioglycine,830uM competitor,60min.
Strong competitors: L-Ala84%,L-Cys95%,L-Thr100%,L-Ser90%,L-Met65%,
L-Trp60% inhibition, normalized as the source specifies to the glycine
competitor effect. Six selected strong-competition edges are retained.
Competition is not independent demonstration of transport of every
competitor or a mapped binding residue. Weaker reported effects are
D-Ala15%,Phe37%,Pro24%,Leu35%,Ile20%,Val18%,Tyr22%,Asn20%,His14%.
Gln,Asp,Glu,Lys,Arg are0 under these conditions. They are retained here,
not labeled universal nonsubstrates or silently promoted to strong
competition edges. CycA/ACP use different concentrations/times.
Table3: WAT9 sstT-defective host+pSA28 loses colony growth relative to
empty pUC119 on5mg/ml L-serine minimal agar24h37C; both grow on40mM
lactose. Native W3133-2 and WAT9+pMST321(sstT) are separate controls.
No direct serine flux or intracellular serine assay supports the proposed
feedback relay; that explanation and SnatA dimerization remain models.
No inference to all archaeal or bacterial PF01914 proteins is made.
