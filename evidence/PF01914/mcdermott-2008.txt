Short letter read; original Figure1 Northern blot visually inspected.
AG100 mid-exponential LB cultures30C receive tetracycline or
chloramphenicol for1h. Figure1 compares untreated,5mM salicylate,
and2/5ug/ml of each antibiotic. Both antibiotic treatments give
stronger steady-state marC RNA signals. Two edges retain the5ug/ml
conditions; no fold-change or significance is invented from the blot.
RNA abundance is not direct transcriptional activation; the authors
favor stabilization. The measured start is30nt upstream of the ATG,
not overlapping the proposed MarR binding sites. AG112 marR-inactive
and AG100 show no transcript difference (data not shown); salicylate
does not increase the blot signal. Do not turn these negatives into
unsupported no-change predicates.
AG100 marC::kan, the combined marC/ychE/yhgN deletion and three
expression constructs did not increase susceptibility/resistance in
the tested panel. This is condition-specific negative evidence, not
proof of no MarC phenotype in any strain. The later 2018 TOP10 result
has a different donor allele, vector, recipient and one-dilution effect.
No direct antibiotic efflux mechanism is demonstrated by either source.
