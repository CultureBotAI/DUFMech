Important source ambiguity: Table3 caption calls reconstructed mutants
EcNR1-derived, but main Results and knockout Methods use EcHW24 or
progeny. EcHW24 is an EcNR1 MutS-negative derivative with two premature
stop-generating SNPs; EcNR1 itself has modified lambda at bioA/bioB.
The explicit comparator is EcNR1. Thus graphs name the reported
marC::kan reconstruction versus EcNR1 and do not assert a clean
marC-only isogenic causal effect. No inspected mutS repair resolves
this discrepancy. A knockout approximates evolved IS1/IS5 insertion,
not the exact same allele or proven equivalent local regulatory effect.
Adjacent marRAB regulation and general membrane changes are alternatives;
MarC transport of isobutanol was not directly measured. acrAB efflux
assays and mdh enzyme assays cannot be borrowed as MarC mechanisms.
