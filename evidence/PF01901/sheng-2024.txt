Inspected2026-10-11 UTC: Results,Methods,Table1/2 and captions; original
Figures1,2,4,5 visually inspected. No supplementary material is listed.
Minimal quotations: "single O-antigen unit"; "impaired motility".

Figure1B compares silver-stained LPS from parental ATCC43895 and wzy-Tn5.
Long-O-antigen signal is reduced and a lower band assigned to one O unit
remains. Figure1A is an expected-structure schematic,not direct chemical
determination of every linkage. Its delta-wzy label must not overwrite the
actual Tn5 genotype named in1B,Table1 and the text. LPS gel migration supports
a whole-cell assembly phenotype,not purified Wzy catalytic chemistry.

Figure2B shows reduced retained crystal-violet signal for wzy-Tn5 versus WT
at37 C in the24h microtiter assay. It is NOT complete loss:2A retains a visible,
weaker tube ring. Caption2B gives six assays/strain,mean/SD,one-wayANOVA with
Dunnett comparison and one-star p<0.01 for wzy. Do not assign an exact raw
mean from the plotted bar. Results says WT0.54,whereas the bar appears nearer
0.57; the directional comparison is retained without numerical overprecision.
SOURCE DISCREPANCIES: Methods says MSM with0.04%glucose while caption2B says
4%; tube Methods says overnight whereas2A caption/Results say48h. Do not silently
choose either as an exact matched protocol or compare its magnitude with2020.
The2024growth curves(Figure3) are for core mutants,not a wzy-specific matched
growth control for crystal-violet biomass. Biomass,attachment,dye retention
and envelope changes remain possible contributors; no direct assembly pathway
from the altered O-antigen band to this phenotype is established.

Figure4 shows a smaller but nonzero wzy-Tn5 motility halo,roughly33mm versus
WT57mm by visual reading,with a one-star difference. Caption gives three
repeats,mean/SD,two-tailed unpaired t-test,p<0.01. Methods/caption report40h,
Results says48h. The wzy lane retains a flagellin immunoblot band: the mutation
does not establish absence of flagella or direct inhibition of flagellar
biogenesis. Halo spread is a population phenotype,not single-cell motor speed.
Figure5 retains red Congo-red binding in wzy-Tn5. This does not prove every
curli property is unchanged, but rejects a claim of complete curli loss.

The2020wzy-Tn5 strain is reused. Wzy-specific rescue,exact insertion site,
mutant sequence and downstream expression controls are not established here.
No wzy invasion-reduction edge is adopted: the current invasion experiments
test core/flagella mutants and cite2020 for the retained O-antigen-mutant
invasion. Core-mutant effects do not transfer to Wzy. Family membership uses
the paper-cited reference sequence with the culture-collection discrepancy in
identity.txt. The three adopted effects are OBSERVED,INDIRECT,PHENOTYPIC.
