Inspected2026-10-11 UTC: selected Methods/Results concerning the wzy mutant,
strain table and original Figure1A-D. Other curli engineering is outside scope.
Minimal quotation: "biofilm-negative".

Figure1B compares the parental O157 ATCC43895 with named transposon mutants.
The wzy-Tn5 tube has much less crystal-violet ring staining than the parent.
The caption identifies a static37 C tube assay. The30 C plate library screen
in1A is a separate preliminary readout, not a quantitative replicate of1B.
Retain the direction of lower tube staining rather than an exact zero biomass.
The authors report insertion-junction sequencing followed by alignment, but
the exact wzy junction, mutant sequence and allele-specific rescue are not
established by the inspected material. csgB complementation is not wzy rescue.
Whole-gene disruption can alter envelope biology and neighboring expression;
it does not identify direct PF01901 chemistry or individual catalytic residues.

Figure1D shows wzy-Tn5 intracellular recovery comparable to parental43895 in
the MAC-T assay; the striking reduction is in the csgB mutant. Do not turn the
paper's broad Figure1 title into a wzy-dependent invasion-defect edge. The
authors also report retained Congo-red binding in the LPS mutants,with reduced
intensity described as data not shown. A dye assay is not specific curli mass.
The family mapping is reference-level as detailed in identity.txt,not a
sequence-confirmed assay culture. No independent wzy complementation was read.
The2024study reuses these O-antigen mutants and is not a second independently
generated wzy genotype. Its residual ring/biomass result constrains any claim
that this allele completely abolishes biofilm formation in every condition.
