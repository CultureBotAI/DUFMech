Curator's factual notes, not a transcription:
The minimal system combines recombinant holo-GrsA Phe-ATE and the first
TycB Pro-CAT module, not TycB alone or an isolated condensation domain.
TycB1 variants have C-terminal His6 tags, are expressed with gsp for
phosphopantetheinylation, and are purified by nickel affinity chromatography.
Exact complete clone boundaries and tag junctions were not reconstructed.
Table 1 OP1 suggests a cloning-associated N-terminal change, not proof that
the complete expressed sequence is identical to the current reference.

The DKP assay separately preincubates 1 uM of each enzyme with 2 mM ATP
and its substrate for 3 min at 37 C, then mixes equal volumes and incubates
45 min at 37 C. Assay buffer is 50 mM HEPES pH 8.0, 100 mM NaCl,
10 mM MgCl2 and 1 mM EDTA; purification/dialysis also specifies reductant.
The HTML reports 0.5 M phenylalanine versus 0.5 mM proline in the separate
preincubations. The phenylalanine unit remains unresolved; it is not silently
converted to mM. Extraction uses butanol/chloroform and HPLC-MS analysis.

WT joint preparations yield d-Phe-l-Pro diketopiperazine (DKP).
R62A, H147R and H147V TycB1 substitutions give no detected DKP in the
reported assay. These soluble variants retain adenylation and thiolation;
those controls do not independently prove local C-domain folding.
The orthogonal acid-stable radiolabel assay includes a no-TycB1 control
and supports loss of dipeptide formation for these variants. The HPLC-MS
endpoint is an indirect readout of condensation followed by product release;
neither every intermediate nor a histidine proton-transfer step was tracked.
Figure 4 normalizes WT DKP to 100% and uses three consecutive experiments
from the same protein batch, not three independent purifications. Values
are normalized for thiolation activity; raw counts were not reanalyzed.
No universal detection limit or absolute catalytic rate is established here.

The main text usually names D151N, but one product-results sentence says
D151A. It similarly has Y151F/Y166F inconsistencies. Figure 1 caption says
TycB1 derives from tycA, inconsistent with the main text and operon identity.
These are retained source discrepancies, not alternative adopted mappings.
No D151 edge is curated until the labeling discrepancy is resolved.
R67A/H146A/W202L were insoluble or broadly impaired and cannot demonstrate
isolated loss of condensation chemistry. Inhibitor targets were not identified.
Coupled adenylation, loading, epimerization, condensation and cyclization
prevent assigning an observed elementary catalytic mechanism from DKP alone.
