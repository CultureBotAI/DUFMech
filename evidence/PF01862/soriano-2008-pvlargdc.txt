Experimental facts, paraphrased:
Intact M. jannaschii pPRDC.19 derivatives E109Q and N47A were expressed in
E. coli B834(DE3); the N-terminal His tag was cleaved. Purification refers to
Tolbert 2003, whose full methods were not accessible in this turn.
Table 2 specific activities (pmol/min/ug): WT 1014 +/-58; E109Q 132 +/-8;
N47A 2, without stated uncertainty. Neither mutant is an absolute null.
Methods 2.5: 343 K, 30 min, 50 mM MES/NaOH pH 6.0, 50 mM KCl, 1 mM DTT,
4 mM L-arginine plus 2 ul radiolabeled L-arginine; HCl termination and
scintillation measurement of released 14CO2. Protein amount, tracer specific
activity, blank subtraction, replicate number and uncertainty definition are
not independently established in this text. These are preparation-level
specific activities, not intrinsic kcat values normalized to processed sites.

Separate crystallization experiment: 295 K, 1-2 mM arginine, hanging drops,
17-20% PEG2000, 10% MPD, 2.5% glycerol, 100 mM HEPES pH 6.7-7.3, 0.5 mM
octylglucoside/EDTA and 10 mM DTT. Both structures are at 2.0 A. E109Q has
six processed protomers with agmatine. N47A has one clearly processed protomer,
one mixed site modeled predominantly processed, and four unprocessed protomers;
agmatine occurs at two sites. Crystal populations do not quantify assay-solution
processing fractions or rates. Figure 3 shows separated PYR/Ser52 versus
continuous Ser52/Ser53 density. Figure 4 compares E109Q with WT but N47A with
S53A, not WT. Loss of Asn47 contacts and altered local conformations motivate
the proposed processing contribution, not a measured intervening rate.

Relative self-processing rates were not obtained: after 3 h at 338 K mutants
showed little processing, but the WT failed to dissociate to monomer. Do not
convert this failed comparison into an observed rate decrease. The authors
propose incomplete processing plus reduced catalytic activity as contributors
to N47A's large loss. Glu109 acid/base chemistry remains a proposal despite
the activity reduction and processed E109Q structure. No cellular experiment
in this study proves a polyamine, growth or acid-resistance consequence.
