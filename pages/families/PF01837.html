Source description

This presumed domain is about is about 360 residues long. The function of this domain is not clear. It is found at N terminus in some proteins that have two C-terminal cystathionine beta-synthase (CBS) domains, such as MJ0100 from Methanocaldococcus jannaschii. This domain can also be found in proteins that contain two C-terminal inserted Fe4S domains [InterPro reference PUB00093781]. In Methanocaldococcus jannaschii, MJ0100 and its ortholog MA1821 from Methanosarcina acetivorans are involved in Hcy (homocysteine) biosynthesis. MJ0100 CBS domains bind S-adenosyl-l-methionine (SAM) and 5'-methylthioadenosine (MTA), which induce a conformational change consistent with regulatory function. Another protein in the homocysteine biosynthesis pathway, MJ0099 and its ortholog MA1822, is involved in the reduction of the disulfide formed in MJ0100/MA1821 during the conversion of Asa (aspartate semialdehyde) to Hcy [InterPro reference PUB00078766,InterPro reference PUB00093781]. The DUF39-CBS and DUF39-ferredoxin architectures repeatedly occur together in the genomes of methanogenic Archaea, suggesting they may be of diverged function. This is consistent with a phylogenetic reconstruction of the DUF39 family, which clearly distinguishes the CBS-associated and ferredoxin-associated DUF39s [InterPro reference PUB00078766] .

DvMF_1464 insertion mutants have a methionine-rescuable fitness defect

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Pooled barcoded transposon mutants of D. vulgaris Miyazaki F, measuring relative abundance of DvMF_1464 insertion strains during lactate/sulfate growth; not a clean deletion, individual growth curve or purified DUF39 reaction.
Context
Figure 3 compares defined lactate/sulfate medium without added amino acids with supplementation, including L-methionine at 1 or 10 mM. Pools are recovered in rich medium, transferred at OD600 0.02 and assayed at saturation by barcode sequencing. Miyazaki-specific methods describe anaerobic, unshaken Hungate cultures with 1 mM sulfide and 10 mL medium; generic fitness methods say 5 mL. Exact Figure 3 volume, temperature and elapsed time remain unverified.
Family mapping
DvMF_1464 maps to NCBIProtein:ACL08412.1, CP001197.1 positions 1794909-1796081, and the identical 390-aa sequence in UniParc UPI00018940C6, with PF01837 at 22-380. Historical UniProtKB B8DLN6 is deleted because it is not in a reference proteome; it is not an unresolved protein identity. The taxon 883 archive now uses Nitratidesulfovibrio vulgaris for the same Miyazaki F strain.
Limitations
Fitness is a normalized change in mutant abundance relative to the pool, not an absolute growth rate or a wild-type-versus-deletion monoculture comparison. Nutritional rescue is not genetic complementation. Insertion polarity, cross-feeding and insertion-level contributions were not independently resolved. Partial cysteine rescue and the different homoserine-dehydrogenase locus are not proof of this protein's catalytic substrate or a direct sulfur-transfer step. No isolated-domain or family-wide mechanism is assigned.

Dde_3007 disruption reduces minimal-medium plate growth in G20

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Individual D. alaskensis G20 transposon mutant JK00771, wild type and plasmid controls in Figure 4C; separate from the 2014 and 2018 pooled-fitness assays.
Context
JK00771 Dde_3007::Tn5 and its empty-vector pMO9075 derivative are compared with G20 and JK00771 carrying pJK2. The plate image says MOLS4, whereas the caption says LS4D; the exact minimal lactate/sulfate formulation remains unresolved. General culture conditions are anaerobic 30 C, but the plate assay duration is not established. Figure 4D separately shows homocysteine rescue without establishing its dose here.
Family mapping
Dde_3007 is reference UniProtKB:Q30WZ5, sequence version 1, 421 aa, PF01837 at 53-411, and NCBI ABB39801.1 on CP000112.1 positions 2993369-2994634. The paper's sequence-verified pJK2 uses revised start 2993462, corresponding to reference residue 32. Assuming the same stop predicts a 390-aa product and interval 22-380; this calculation is not an independently retrieved full clone sequence. The taxon 207559 current name is Oleidesulfovibrio alaskensis G20.
Limitations
The endpoint is visible plate growth, not a quantitative colony count, direct reaction assay or proof of an isolated-domain function. pJK2 complementation supports gene dependence; exact insertion position and clone junctions remain unverified because supplements were inaccessible. The source's O-phosphohomoserine pathway is proposed, and later work questions homoserine participation. No such catalytic step or universal persulfide mechanism is adopted.
