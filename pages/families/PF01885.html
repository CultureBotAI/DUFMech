Source description

Tpt1 catalyses the last step of tRNA splicing in yeast. It transfers the splice junction 2'-phosphate from ligated tRNA to NAD, to produce ADP-ribose 1"-2"-cyclic phosphate. This is presumed to be followed by a transesterification step to release the RNA. The first step of this reaction is similar to that catalysed by some bacterial toxins. E. coli KptA and mouse Tpt1 are likely to use the same reaction mechanism [InterPro reference PUB00008050].

Scope
Purified full-length His6-CthTpt1 (ABN54255.1) from Clostridium thermocellum ATCC 27405, expressed in E. coli.
Context
Separate 10-mer AUCACGCUUA substrates bearing a terminal 2'- or 3'-phosphate; 0.2 uM RNA, 0.5 uM enzyme, 1 mM NAD+, 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 37 C, 30 minutes.
Family mapping
A3DJX6, kptA/Cthe_3059, sequence version 1, 182 residues; PF01885 spans reference residues 8-172. Current species name is Acetivibrio thermocellus.
Limitations
Terminal removal is approximately stoichiometric under these conditions, not demonstrated multiple-turnover catalysis. PAGE product assignment uses T4 Pnkp and CIP controls. Intermediate chemistry, endogenous substrates and domain-only sufficiency are not established here; tested Runella and human homologs lack equivalent terminal activity.

Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
