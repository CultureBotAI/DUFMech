Source description

Previously known as uncharacterized protein family UPF0015, a single member of this family O82827 (UniProtKB protein) has been identified as an undecaprenyl diphosphate synthase [InterPro reference PUB00003021].

Recombinant E. coli UppS forms long-chain prenyl products

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Untagged recombinant E. coli UppS in Apfel 1999; intact protein rather than an isolated PF01255 segment or every cis-prenyltransferase.
Context
The pUppS-wt-EC construct uses the upstream GTG start and is expressed in BL21(DE3)(pLysS), then purified by ammonium sulfate fractionation, Phospho-Ultrogel and Superdex 200. The standard radioactive assay uses 100 ul, 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 0.2 mM MgCl2, 0.05% Triton X-100, 10 uM FPP and 10 uM radiolabeled IPP for 30 min at 35 C. Companion product identification uses potato acid phosphatase and reversed-phase TLC; its precise incubation recipe is not separately specified.
Family mapping
Historical Q47675/YAES_ECOLI has demerged into several entries. Its archived UPI0000137D08 sequence exactly matches the 253-aa K12 reference UniProtKB:P60472 (ispU/uppS/yaeS), sequence version 1, and the InterPro sequence. PF01255 maps to residues 24-244 inclusive. This reference mapping does not establish the historical PCR donor strain or all clone junctions; BL21 is the expression host.
Limitations
Reported major dephosphorylated products comigrate with both undecaprenol and decaprenol; C30-C45 products also occur. TLC is described as data not shown, not independently inspected chromatography. No exclusive C55 product, stereochemical remeasurement, eight resolved condensation microsteps, isolated-domain sufficiency or cellular peptidoglycan flux is claimed. Native one-/two-Met forms and tagged preparations are not silently equated with the adopted recombinant preparation.

Triton addition increases the UppS coupled-assay signal

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Recombinant untagged E. coli UppS together with yeast inorganic pyrophosphatase in Apfel 1999 Figure 6; the observed coupled output is not assigned to an isolated UppS elementary step.
Context
Compare 0.01% Triton X-100 with omission in the coupled phosphate assay. Other standard components are 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 0.2 mM MgCl2, 10 uM IPP, 10 uM FPP and 0.003 U S. cerevisiae inorganic pyrophosphatase in 100 ul for 30 min at 35 C. Malachite-green A660 is calibrated against phosphate; Figure 6 normalizes each series to its maximum. Exact UppS dose is not given in the caption.
Family mapping
Intact recombinant UppS is mapped through historical Q47675 and its exact archived sequence to P60472, PF01255 24-244. The yeast coupling enzyme is a required assay partner, not a PF01255 member. Reference identity does not establish every historical expression junction.
Limitations
Figure 6 shows residual signal without detergent, and higher detergent concentrations lower the signal again. This is not absolute detergent dependence, monotonic activation, or a cellular membrane mechanism. Solubilization, product release, the coupling enzyme and detection chemistry are not separately resolved. The Methods color-reagent acid notation remains uncorrected; raw triplicates were not reanalyzed.

Magnesium addition increases the UppS coupled-assay signal

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Recombinant untagged E. coli UppS plus yeast inorganic pyrophosphatase in Apfel 1999 Figure 6, separate from the detergent series and product-identification experiment.
Context
Compare 0.5 mM MgCl2 with omission. Other standard coupled-assay components are 100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 0.05% Triton X-100, 10 uM each IPP/FPP and 0.003 U yeast inorganic pyrophosphatase in 100 ul, 35 C for 30 min. Phosphate-dependent A660 output is normalized to the maximum within this series; the enzyme dose and raw triplicates remain unverified.
Family mapping
Historical Q47675 maps by archived sequence identity to E. coli reference P60472, PF01255 residues 24-244. The adopted construct is the untagged recombinant protein, not M. luteus O82827 from the frozen family description or an isolated domain.
Limitations
Residual signal without added MgCl2 is visible. Both the prenyltransferase and coupling reaction can contribute to the Mg-dependent output; direct UppS metal occupancy or catalytic metal stoichiometry is not shown. The nonmonotonic concentration series is not generalized to all doses or to in-vivo growth. No edge connects this mixed-enzyme optical assay to pneumococcal essentiality.

EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
