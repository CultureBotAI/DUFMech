Source description

This is a family of bacterial and archaeal fructose-1,6-bisphosphatases (FBPases). FBPase catalyses the hydrolysis of D-fructose-1,6-bisphosphate (FBP) to D-fructose-6-phosphate (F6P) and orthophosphate and is an essential regulatory enzyme in the glyconeogenic pathway.

Scope
Whole-protein Fbp from Thermococcus kodakarensis KOD1, expressed in E. coli.
Context
Sato2004 Table2; purified preparation with 2 mM FBP at 85 C; F6P detected by coupled glucose-6-phosphate isomerase/dehydrogenase assay. FBP-minus and thermal-decomposition corrections were applied.
Family mapping
Q8NKR9 v1, 375 aa, fbp/TK2164; historical AB081839.1/BAC10571.1 and genome BAD86353.1; PF01950 residues 6-372. Historical recombinant clone was not independently resequenced.
Limitations
Whole protein, not isolated-domain catalysis. Exact assay buffer and metal concentration refer to uninspected Rashid2002 methods. The 95 C kinetic constants are not the 85 C activity endpoint. No aldolase, residue chemistry or universal family activity is inferred from this experiment.

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Rich-medium KW128 versus delta-fbp-8J cell extracts.
Context
ASW-YT plus 5 g/L sodium pyruvate, anaerobic 85 C for 15 h, early stationary phase. FBPase measured by the coupled F6P assay, not by in vivo flux analysis.
Family mapping
