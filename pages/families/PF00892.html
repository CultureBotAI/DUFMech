Source description

This family includes many hypothetical membrane proteins of unknown function. Many of the proteins contain two copies of the aligned region. The family used to be known as DUF6. Members of this family usually carry 5+5 transmembrane domains, and this domain attempts to model five of these.

Causal graphs

YdeD deletion and plasmid expression alter peroxide-stressed growth

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Whole-gene ydeD perturbations in E. coli BW25113 and its JW5250 ydeD::KmR derivative. Comparisons use pSTV29 or pYdeD; neither isolated EamA repeat is tested.
Context
Figure 1A serial dilutions of overnight cultures on LB agar with 0.5 mM H2O2, incubated at 30 C for 24 h, with peroxide-free LB controls. pYdeD carries a 1.5-kb ydeD fragment in the lac-promoter pSTV29 vector. Panel A does not specify peroxide pretreatment or an IPTG dose; panel B pretreatment is not transferred. The malformed HTML control-concentration text is resolved only to the image's LB control, not an invented 10 mM condition.
Family mapping
UniProtKB:P31125, eamA/ydeD, b1533/JW5250, is a 299-residue K-12 reference with sequence version 2. UniProt and InterPro sequences agree exactly; PF00892 maps at 7-130 and 143-286 inclusive. The matching JW5250 locus grounds the deletion, but the assay host is BW25113 and the complete pYdeD insert sequence was not independently reconstructed. No independent function is assigned to either repeat.
Limitations
Colony growth in serial spots is not a calibrated survival rate, purified cysteine flux or a resolved peroxide-scavenging reaction. Plasmid rescue supports gene involvement but does not exclude every dosage or secondary effect. Exact insert junctions, induction, dilution factors and independent replicate count remain unresolved. The pDES multi-gene experiments and proposed FliY recycling chain are not folded into this graph.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
Not available means the source did not supply that counter. A zero is a reported count. Not scored means evidence counts are unavailable.
