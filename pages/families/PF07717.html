Source description

This family is found towards the C-terminus of the DEAD-box helicases (PF00270 (Pfam entry)). In these helicases it is apparently always found in association with PF04408 (Pfam entry). There do seem to be a couple of instances where it occurs by itself - e.g. Q84VZ2 (UniProtKB protein). The structure PDB:3i4u adopts an OB-fold. helicases (PF00270 (Pfam entry)). In these helicases it is apparently always found in association with PF04408 (Pfam entry). This C-terminal domain of the yeast helicase contains an oligonucleotide/oligosaccharide-binding (OB)-fold which seems to be placed at the entrance of the putative nucleic acid cavity. It also constitutes the binding site for the G-patch-containing domain of Pfa1p. When found on DEAH/RHA helicases, this domain is central to the regulation of the helicase activity through its binding of both RNA and G-patch domain proteins [InterPro reference PUB00092307].
