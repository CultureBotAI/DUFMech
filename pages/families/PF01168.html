Intact alanine racemase interconverts alanine enantiomers

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified B. stearothermophilus (now Geobacillus) alanine racemase and K39A in the 1998 study. The two substrate directions are separate assays, not a tracked molecular cycle.
Context
1 ml with 100 umol CHES pH 9 and 30 umol D- or L-alanine, 37 C; stop at 100 C for 10 min. L-product is measured using alanine dehydrogenase/NAD+, D-product using DAAT/lactate dehydrogenase/NADH. Figure 3A varies pH in specified buffers. Exact reaction duration and enzyme amount per comparison remain unresolved.
Family mapping
P10724/ALR_GEOSE, alr/dal, 388-aa reference sequence version 2, matches InterPro and 1L6G's canonical sequence. PF01168 spans 12-229 and includes K39, but excludes Y265 in the other domain. The 1998 mutagenic primer matches residues 34-44 with K39A; complete clones and donor strain are not independently resolved.
Limitations
These are intact-enzyme product assays. Neither the isolated N-terminal domain's sufficiency nor every proton-transfer step is observed. K39A's activity loss does not by itself prove one unique chemical role; its reported absorption similarity is not a complete folding control. Older sequence conflicts and unspecified detection limits/replicates remain explicit. Coupling enzymes measure quenched products, not live-cell peptidoglycan flux.

D-alanine preincubation at pH 7 lowers retained Alr activity

MECHANISTIC

Scope
WT holoenzyme in Table I's 1998 inactivation experiment, distinct from the pH-9 racemization assay and the pH-7.2 spectral time course.
Context
10 ug holoenzyme, 0.5 umol D-alanine, 10 umol potassium phosphate pH 7, 0.01% 2-mercaptoethanol, 100 ul; 25 C for 12 h. Remaining D-to-L racemase activity is assayed afterward.
Family mapping
Intact P10724-associated recombinant Alr; PF01168 12-229 maps within the whole enzyme. This experiment does not selectively perturb or isolate that domain.
Limitations
Table I reports activity loss, while separate spectra support a PLP/PMP interpretation. This is not an observed intracellular regulatory switch or proof that physiological D-alanine always inhibits Alr. No independently reconstructed raw kinetics or exact detection threshold is available.

Joint PMP and pyruvate treatment restores racemase activity

MECHANISTIC

Scope
WT apoenzyme in Table I's distinct reactivation experiment. This is not a time-resolved reversal in the same molecules used for D-alanine inactivation.
Context
50 ug apoenzyme, 1 umol PMP, 2.5 umol pyruvate, 10 umol K2HPO4-KOH pH 10 and 0.01% 2-mercaptoethanol in 100 ul; 25 C for 12 h. Recovered activity is measured with D-alanine.
Family mapping
Full P10724-associated Alr, containing PF01168 at 12-229 plus its C-terminal region. Apoenzyme preparation is by hydroxylamine treatment/dialysis, not a gene deletion or isolated-domain preparation.
Limitations
PMP, pyruvate, enzyme and alkaline conditions are a joint intervention; none is represented as independently sufficient. Activity recovery and spectral changes do not directly identify each proton-transfer step. The separate mutant methylamine experiment has different conditions.

K39A reduces tritium release and methylamine increases it

MECHANISTIC

Scope
WT and K39A apo-Alr in the 1998 Figure 5 labeled-PMP experiment. The adopted rescue is tritium release, not a quantified return of normal alanine-racemization turnover.
Context
100 ul, 10 umol CHES pH 9, 8.7 nmol enzyme, 8 nmol randomly labeled [4'-3H]PMP and 8.5 umol pyruvate, 30 C. Add 50 umol methylamine hydrochloride for the rescue arm, giving 0.5 M. Acid-quenched aliquots are dried and remaining radioactivity measured; volatile release is calculated. Figure 5 displays up to 8 h.
Family mapping
K39 is within P10724's PF01168 12-229 interval; the mutagenic primer locally matches K39A. Full-length enzyme is expressed through pAR310 in JM109. Exact complete clones and affinity-free preparation details remain scoped to the inspected protocol.
Limitations
Tritium release is not directly equated with product concentration or racemase turnover. WT and methylamine-rescued mutant reach different plateaus; an enzyme-free methylamine control is not displayed in Figure 5. Formaldehyde in the proposed recycling scheme was not identified. Figure 4's methylamine caption implies 0.1 M while its prose says 0.5 M; Figure 5's separate Methods supplies the adopted 0.5 M. The opposite PLP-to-PMP mutant reaction was not rescued under the tested conditions. Replication and complete folding controls remain limited.
