Source description

The function of this protein is unknown. It appears to only occur in Mycoplasma pneumoniae. The crystal structure revealed that this domain is composed of two separated homotrimeric coiled-coils [InterPro reference PUB00039581].

MPN104 disruption lowers the fraction of gliding M129 cells

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
M. pneumoniae M129 Tn4001.2065 insertion mutant MPN104-29 and its filter-cloned progeny; not a clean deletion, domain-selective mutation or experiment on MPN010.
Context
Table 2 maps the insertion at genome position 134672. The screen enriched polystyrene-adherent transformants and selected altered satellite growth. Table 3 compares two filter clones with wild type using microcinematography, typically 30-50 cells per mutant and at least two experiments. Satellite growth conditions are glass, SP-4 plus 3% gelatin, 37 C; gliding analysis refers to the earlier published protocol and ordinarily uses one frame per second.
Family mapping
MPN104/MPN_104 is UniProtKB:P75565 (C09_orf104/MP050; NCBIProtein:AAB95698.1), M129 reference taxon 272634, sequence version 1, 104 aa. PF01519 maps at 9-103 inclusive. UniProt and InterPro sequences agree; the study independently identifies MPN104 as DUF16-bearing. Reference sequence mapping does not establish the mutant protein product or domain-specific action.
Limitations
No MPN104 complementation or excision-revertant confirmation is established here. Figure 5 predicts no flanking polar effect, but that is not a measured polarity control. Glass binding and resting-time means are near wild type; no independent adhesion-loss or resolved motor-activation mechanism is asserted. Table 3 unit headings conflict with Results; gliding frequency is retained as percent, not um/s.

MPN524 disruption reduces gliding and glass binding

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Separate M. pneumoniae M129 mutant MPN524-74 with Tn4001.2065 insertion at genome position 645829; not MPN104, MPN010 or isolated DUF16.
Context
Table 3 means and SEM derive from two filter clones, with microcinematography for typically 30-50 cells and at least two experiments. Glass binding uses the same two clones and is normalized to wild type. The satellite-growth screen uses glass, SP-4 plus 3% gelatin at 37 C. Exact glass-binding incubation details are referred to a prior protocol and were not reconstructed in this review.
Family mapping
MPN524/MPN_524 is UniProtKB:P75254 (G12_orf168/MP318; NCBIProtein:AAB95966.1), M129 taxon 272634, sequence version 1, 168 aa. PF01519 maps at 47-124 inclusive. Its C-terminal extension is not part of that interval. UniProt and InterPro reference sequences agree; the experimental perturbation is whole-locus insertion, not an isolated-domain assay.
Limitations
Genetic complementation, an excision revertant and mutant protein abundance are not established for MPN524 in the inspected study. Figure 5 predicts no polar effect but does not experimentally exclude it. Reduced attachment may contribute to altered gliding; no observed adhesion-to-motility causal edge is claimed. Table 3 velocity values are percent of WT despite the um/s heading; no 52.8 um/s absolute velocity is asserted. The DUF16 domain cannot be separated from other protein regions by this perturbation.

KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
