Tsr G235E increases clockwise rotation in adaptation-proficient cells

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Full-length pRR53 Tsr-G235E compared with WT pRR53 Tsr in E. coli K-12 RP437-derived UU1250, lacking all five MCP receptors but retaining CheR/CheB. Not an isolated HAMP-domain or purified kinase assay.
Context
Antibody-tethered cells under unstimulated steady-state conditions; Figure 4 displays 100 cells per strain and mutant. The five rotation categories are converted to an estimated fraction of CW time using weights 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. This is not simply a percentage of CW cells. pRR53 is IPTG-controlled; exact tethering induction, temperature and buffer are not restated in the 2008 Methods, and soft-agar conditions are not transferred.
Family mapping
UniProtKB:P02942, tsr/cheD, b4355/JW4318, is a 551-residue K-12 reference, taxon 83333, sequence version 2. UniProt and InterPro sequences agree exactly; PF00672 maps at 217-263 inclusive, containing G235. The study sequenced the entire mutant tsr coding region. Reference mapping does not imply the RP437-derived assay hosts are MG1655 or that the complete plasmid junctions were independently verified.
Limitations
The CW-locked class is a cellular reporter category, not direct measurement of CheA phosphorylation or a particular HAMP packing state. The greater-than-75% threshold is not an exact G235E value. Protein abundance of at least 50% WT was established in UU1535 for the relevant mutant set, not as a folding assay in UU1250. No raw movies, independent-replicate count or clean restoration of this endpoint is established here.

Tsr G235C reduces clockwise rotation in adaptation-defective cells

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Full-length pRR53 Tsr-G235C compared with WT Tsr in RP437-derived UU1535, which lacks the MCP receptors and the CheR/CheB adaptation enzymes. This is distinct from the UU1250 G235E comparison.
Context
Unstimulated antibody-tethered cells with unmodified Tsr retaining the QEQE major-site pattern. Figure 4 scores 100 cells into five rotation categories; the CW-time estimate is a weighted sum, not a direct kinase measurement. Exact tethering induction, temperature and buffer remain unspecified in the inspected 2008 Methods.
Family mapping
P02942 Tsr, K-12 reference taxon 83333, 551 residues, sequence version 2, has PF00672 at 217-263. Reference G235 matches the mutated connector position. Current UniProt and InterPro sequences agree; exact complete experimental plasmid sequence was not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
CCW is the default motor direction, so low CW output may reflect defective receptor-complex assembly rather than a specific native kinase-off conformation. The at-most-15% classification is not an exact per-mutant estimate or a universal zero. Expression controls do not prove local folding, and dominant heterodimer behavior is not a rescue of this rotation endpoint.

Tsr G235P loses the WT soft-agar chemotactic response

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
pRR53 Tsr-G235P versus WT pRR53 and pRR48 vector control in adaptation-proficient UU1250. The soft-agar response is a separate experiment from tethered-cell rotation.
Context
Fresh transformant colonies toothpick-inoculated on tryptone soft agar containing 50 ug/mL ampicillin, incubated at 32.5 C for 8 h, as in Figure 2. pRR53 is IPTG-inducible and 100 uM is the reported optimal WT screening level; the photographed plate caption does not independently specify its IPTG dose.
Family mapping
Full-length Tsr corresponds to P02942, sequence version 2, 551 residues, with G235 inside PF00672 at 217-263. The study verifies mutant coding sequences; the native strain background and the current K-12 reference are kept distinct.
Limitations
Colony morphology and expansion are a chemotaxis readout, not a purified serine-binding measurement or a measured growth rate. G235P retains approximately WT steady-state rotation and spreads more than the nonreversing vector control. Thus loss of this response does not establish immobility or a simple lack of kinase activity. No exact colony radius or statistical effect size is inferred from the image.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
