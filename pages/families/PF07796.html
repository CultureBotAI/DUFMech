Source description

This family contains sequences covering an approximately 270 amino acid stretch of a group of hypothetical proteins. These proteins are expressed by archaeal species of the Methanosarcina genus.

Scope
Pooled barcoded transposon mutants of Phaeobacter inhibens DSM 17395/BS107, focusing on the PGA1_c13340 row in Figure 4B; not an individual deletion, purified reaction or demonstrated B12-reactivation mechanism.
Context
The retained comparison is between the figure's ammonium-plus-sucrose and methionine-plus-sucrose conditions. The caption describes varied carbon and nitrogen sources; methods omit ammonium for nitrogen-source tests. This is not assumed to be methionine added to an otherwise identical ammonium-containing medium. General fitness methods recover pools in rich medium, transfer at OD600 0.02 into 5 mL and sequence barcodes before growth and at saturation. P. inhibens is grown aerobically with shaking; exact plotted nutrient doses, temperature and duration remain unverified.
Family mapping
PGA1_c13340 is NCBIProtein:AFO91049.1 on the reverse strand of CP002976.1 at 1377532-1378257, with a 241-aa sequence identical to UniParc UPI0001632E23. UniParc maps PF07796 at 72-230. NCBI's CDD-based interval ends at 231; these are not silently treated as identical annotations. Historical UniProt entries are inactive, but the sequence/locus identity is retained. The experiment concerns a bacterial member, not only the archaeal members emphasized in the frozen family description.
Limitations
Relative fitness is normalized mutant abundance during pooled growth, not an absolute growth rate or direct methionine flux. No clean deletion or gene complementation is established here. Operon-terminal placement argues against polarity but is not a direct polarity control; cross-feeding and insertion-level effects remain possible. Cofitness and phylogenetic co-occurrence with RamA-like proteins do not prove interaction or electron transfer. The source's B12-reactivation figure heading is broader than its explicit uncertainty about the DUF1638 molecular function.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
