Source description

The structure of tRNA(Ile)-lysidine synthetase consists of an N-terminal dinucleotide-binding fold domain (NTD), and a C-terminal globular domain (CTD). The structure of the NTD closely resembles that of a P-loop "N-type" ATP pyrophosphatase [InterPro reference PUB00034489]. These proteins belong to the PP-loop superfamily [InterPro reference PUB00014303].

Scope
Purified C-terminal His6 E. coli K12 TilS and transcribed E. coli tRNAIle2 in Salowe 2009 Table 4 reactions B/H, compared with no-amine reaction A. Intact-RNA mass and gel migration support modification products, not an independently localized atomic pathway.
Context
75 ul, 50 mM TAPS pH 8.5, 3 mM MgCl2, 0.5 mM TCEP, 0.1 mg/ml BSA, 20 uM transcript, 100 uM ATP and 500 nM TilS; 37 C for 4 h. Use 0.1 mM lysine in B or 20 mM ethanolamine in H. Preparations are frozen without further workup; product gels, intact-RNA MS and residual-substrate radiolysine assays are distinct readouts.
Family mapping
P52097/TILS_ECOLI, tilS/mesJ/yaeN/b0188, 432 aa, sequence version 1. UniProt, InterPro and cited Z50870.1/CAA90751.1 match exactly; PF01171 spans 10-197. Published primers match the coding start and terminal WQKTLS, excluding the native stop for a reported C-terminal His6 fusion. Complete vector scar and experimental clone are not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
Whole TilS also contains C-terminal tRNA-recognition domains. Neither isolated PP-loop sufficiency nor a directly tracked adenylation/nucleophilic-attack sequence follows from these data. Raw spectra, exact modification-site localization, replicate counts and uncertainty are unverified here. N-alpha-methyllysine and ornithine samples gave lysine-like products attributed to contamination, not confirmed alternative incorporation.

Modified E. coli tRNA preparations support B. subtilis IleRS

MECHANISTIC

Scope
A deliberately mixed-species biochemical system in Table 5, with E. coli TilS-treated E. coli transcript and purified B. subtilis 168 FLAG-IleRS. This is separate from the E. coli S100 experiment.
Context
Reaction B or H preparations diluted to 100 nM tRNA, 0.1 nM recombinant IleRS, 100 nM tritiated isoleucine and 100 uM ATP in the 30-ul HEPES pH 7.3 synthetase assay with 10 mM Mg(OAc)2. Two hours at room temperature; acidic SPA capture. Compare each preparation with no-amine A.
Family mapping
P52097 TilS supplies PF01171 at 10-197 and produces the tested preparations. The partner accession Q45477 identifies B. subtilis 168 IleRS, not a PP-loop-family enzyme. Its current sequence version 3 is later than the 2009 experiment; complete historical FLAG construct identity is not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
This mixed-species result is not a native cellular pathway or evidence for B. subtilis TilS activity. The B/H preparations are unrepurified and may retain reaction components. The reported 100/99 values have no supplied uncertainty establishing equivalence, and the zero control is bounded by assay detection. No codon recognition or translation rate is measured.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
