Source description

This family of proteins are related to a large superfamily of metalloenzymes [InterPro reference PUB00004994]. TatD, a member of this family has been shown experimentally to be a DNase enzyme.

Purified tagged TatD digests ssDNA in a magnesium-containing reaction

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length E. coli TatD with a C-terminal His tag, not an isolated Pfam domain or a cellular repair pathway.
Context
K-12 tatD in pET22b expressed in BL21-CodonPlus-RIPL and purified by Ni-NTA and Q-Sepharose. Figure 2B uses 40 nM 5'-32P-labeled 20-nt ssDNA, 0-0.5 uM TatD, 2 mM Mg2+, 3 mM MOPS, 75 mM NaCl, pH 7.2, 10 uL, 37 C for 1 h. Substrate sequence is CAAACTCTCTCTCTCTCAAC.
Family mapping
UniProtKB:P27859, tatD/b4483/JW5931, K-12 reference of 260 residues, sequence version 3; exact UniProt/InterPro sequence agreement and PF01026 at 2-257 inclusive. Native reference, DNA donor and expression host are distinct; the complete tagged clone sequence is not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
This is a joint enzyme/substrate/cofactor assay, not magnesium sufficiency or all-family activity. No-metal, Ca2+ and Zn2+ negatives apply only to the tested ssDNA conditions; they do not exclude AP endonuclease activity on another substrate. Proposed metal stoichiometry and catalytic microsteps remain unresolved. The source's 20 mM protease-K quench unit is questionable and not silently corrected.

Scope

Context
WT and QuikChange mutants use the same purification workflow and 20-nt ssDNA assay. Figure 5C displays a 0.5/1/2 concentration series without units in its image or caption. General methods specify 37 C in MOPS/NaCl pH 7.2, but this panel's duration, DNA dose and metal concentration are not resolved; Figure 2B values are not substituted.
Family mapping

Untagged TatD preparation incises an internal AP-site analogue in duplex DNA

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified E. coli TatD in the 2023 bacterial Figure 6 assay, not the human TATDN proteins or the 2014 tagged preparation.
Context
Synthetic tatD in modified pET-21 produces untagged protein in BL21(DE3). Reaction contains 10 uM protein, 100 nM FAM-labeled THF duplex, 5 mM CaCl2, 20 mM EPPS pH 8.0, 50 mM NaCl and 2.5% glycerol at 37 C; gel time points are 1, 30 and 120 min. Table S1 gives FAM-CGTCTCCTGAAC/(THF)/GTGCTATCGCTC annealed with GAGCGATAGCACAGTTCAGGAGACG.
Family mapping
E. coli TatD reference P27859 maps to PF01026 residues 2-257. The synthetic gene's exact sequence/version and full clone junctions are not independently supplied here; current native mapping is not a tag or strain assertion about all bacterial paralogs.
Limitations
The measured substrate contains THF, not a natural AP site. No matched no-enzyme/mock-purification control is labeled for the bacterial Figure 6 lanes; human BH110 controls are not transferred. Substrate and metal both differ between Mg/exonuclease and Ca/AP panels, so calcium alone is not shown to switch activity. Table S1 places THF after 12 bases while the manuscript Figure 5 caption says position 12. No cellular repair chain or metal microstep is asserted.

Whole-gene TatD deletion reduces colony growth under chronic peroxide

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
E. coli K-12 WT and DeltaTatD cells from the Keio collection; a whole-gene growth phenotype, not direct proof of a particular DNA-repair reaction.
Context
Serial dilution spots with chronic 1 mM H2O2, incubated overnight at 37 C in Figure 3B. Drug-free spots are the unstressed comparator; separate MMS, MMC and UV-C tests do not display the same defect. Basal plate-medium details and exact deletion stock identity remain unresolved.
Family mapping
K-12 tatD reference P27859/b4483/JW5931 contains PF01026 at 2-257, within 260 residues. Deleting the gene perturbs the whole protein; the tested stock's deletion boundaries are not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
No genetic rescue is shown for this adopted comparison. Spot growth is not a quantified survival probability, and impaired peroxide growth does not establish a direct nuclease-to-repair-to-survival chain. Acute outgrowth and the later three-paralog deletion are different experiments.
