Source description

This domain is found at the N-terminal end of alpha-(1->3)- arabinofuranosyltransferase from Mycolicibacterium smegmatis (AftD, [ec:2.4.2.47]), which is involved in the biosynthesis of the arabinogalactan (AG) region of the mycolylarabinogalactan -peptidoglycan (mAGP) complex, an essential component of the mycobacterial cell wall. It catalyses the addition of an arabinofuranosyl (Araf) residue from the sugar donor decaprenyl-phospho-arabinose (DPA) on the C-3 of an alpha-(1->5)-linked Araf from the arabinan backbone of AG [InterPro reference PUB00086410]. This domain shows mixed alpha/beta folds homologous to other solved GT-C glycosyltransferase structures [InterPro reference PUB00155574].
