IPR002902: integrated entry for Salt stress response/antifungal

Source description

This domain is often found in association with the kinase domains PF00069 (Pfam entry) or PF07714 (Pfam entry). In many proteins it is duplicated. It contains six conserved cysteines which are involved in disulphide bridges [InterPro reference PUB00052808]. It has a role in salt stress response [InterPro reference PUB00109166] and has antifungal activity [InterPro reference PUB00109167].

Scope

Family mapping

The authors report similar mutant CD spectra, but supplementary S5 was not visually accessible here; immobilized active fractions and local folding are not independently established. Static mannose contacts do not directly observe the mannobiose binding trajectory.

WT Gnk2 delays BY4741 growth, with attenuation by R93A or soluble mannan

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
S. cerevisiae BY4741 exposed to purified mature Ginkgo Gnk2; Miyakawa 2014 Figure 7A, not the delta-mnn2 culture or an in-plant infection test.
Context
BY4741 MATa his3delta1 leu2delta0 met15delta0 ura3delta0, potato dextrose broth at 25 C. Transfer pre-culture at OD600 1 into fresh medium at OD600 0.01 with 2 uM WT/R93A or no protein. Monitor OD600 until 2.55. The WT-plus-saccharide arms contain 0.5% w/v yeast mannan or 50 mM D-mannose. Methods state two repetitions; Figure 7 shows growth curves without statistical confidence intervals.
Family mapping
Purified mature A4ZDL6 residues 27-134 plus extra N-terminal Met; current PF01657 36-131 corresponds to mature 10-105. Mature R93 maps to precursor R119 inside the domain. The yeast is the assay organism, not the source of this Ginkgo protein.
Limitations
Turbidity delay is indirect physiology, not direct cell-wall damage, killing or measured spore germination. The Discussion specifies longer lag rather than reduced log-phase growth rate. R93A and mannan attenuation are not complete null effects. Saccharide-only controls are not displayed, so competitive sequestration is an interpretation, not a uniquely established causal step. Similar R93A thermal stability and mannan NMR results are author-reported supplementary controls, not visually inspected here.

WT Gnk2 reduces the F. culmorum liquid-culture endpoint more than R93A

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
F. culmorum IFM50210 exposed to WT or R93A mature Gnk2, Miyakawa 2014 Figure 8C; separate from disc diffusion and yeast lag assays.
Context
Potato dextrose broth at 25 C, initial OD600 0.005; 2 or 10 uM WT/R93A; measure OD600 after 36 h. Inhibition is relative to the no-protein endpoint. Figure 8C gives means and SD with n=3; no new statistical analysis is performed.
Family mapping
Ginkgo A4ZDL6 mature 1-108 corresponds to precursor 27-134, plus expression-derived Met. PF01657 36-131 contains R119, the precursor coordinate of mature R93. Whole mature protein is tested against F. culmorum, not an isolated domain or a fungal homolog.
Limitations
A 36-h turbidity endpoint is not a measured instantaneous growth rate, viability count or fungicidal mechanism. WT inhibits by 22.1% at 2 uM and 68.2% at 10 uM; R93A retains 7.6% inhibition at 10 uM. The reported 2-10-uM IC50 bracket is not a fitted exact value. Similar R93A stability is author-reported; no unique cellular lesion is demonstrated.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
Not available means the source did not supply that counter. A zero is a reported count. Not scored means evidence counts are unavailable.
