PF01827: FTH domain
IPR002900: integrated entry for FTH domain

Source description

This presumed domain is likely to be a protein-protein interaction module. It is found in many proteins from C. elegans. The domain is found associated with the F-box PF00646 (Pfam entry). This domain is named FTH after FOG-2 homology domain [InterPro reference PUB00101027].

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Hu 2019 Table 2: tra-2(e2020); fem-3(q95) families segregating fog-2(q71), not an otherwise wild-type population.
Context
Temperature-sensitive fem-3(q95) strains maintained at 15 C; embryos shifted to 25 C and grown to adults. Oocyte presence scored by compound microscopy, followed by PCR/sequencing of a phenotype-selected subset. Detailed strain construction retains unc-24(e138) and dpy-20(e1282) markers on IV.
Family mapping
fog-2(q71) is W148stop in the 327-aa Q2YS43/CAJ43447.1 reference, within PF01827 144-271 but truncating most of the domain and tail. Genotype perturbation is not a single-residue binding experiment. The 108-nt tra-2(e2020) deletion is in a different gene's 3-prime UTR.

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Hu 2019 Results near Figure 3: XX tra-2(nm75); fog-2(q71) double mutants compared with the reported untagged q71 phenotype.
Context
Endogenous nine-residue HA insertion four amino acids before the TRA-2 stop; CP153 derived from JK574 and CP139/CP141, with q71 sequencing and tagged-locus PCR confirmation. Default NGM culture at 20 C; 40 double-mutant animals scored for self-fertility.
Family mapping
The deficient member is FOG-2 Q2YS43/CAJ43447.1, PF01827 144-271, affected by W148stop. The intervention alters separate TRA-2, not FOG-2, its FTH domain or an equivalent family member.
Limitations
INDIRECT genetic suppression, not same-gene rescue or a direct binding/translation measurement. Precise scoring age, biological replicate breakdown and contemporaneous untagged denominator are unstated. Figure 3B says nmIs4 while Methods/caption and Figure 6 say nm75. The tagged allele is not a neutral reporter, and suppression remains partial.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.

PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
