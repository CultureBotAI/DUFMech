PF02130: Endoribonuclease YbeY
IPR002036: integrated entry for Endoribonuclease YbeY

Source description

YbeY is a single strand-specific metallo-endoribonuclease involved in late-stage 70S ribosome quality control and in maturation of the 3' terminus of the 16S rRNA. It acts together with the RNase R to eliminate defective 70S ribosomes, but not properly maturated 70S ribosomes or individual subunits, by a process mediated specifically by the 30S ribosomal subunit. It is involved in the processing of 16S, 23S and 5S rRNAs, with a particularly strong effect on maturation at both the 5'-and 3'-ends of 16S rRNA as well as maturation of the 5'-end of 23S and 5S rRNAs [InterPro reference PUB00064743,InterPro reference PUB00064742,InterPro reference PUB00096939]. The crystal structure of the protein from Aquifex aeolicus showed an overall fold consisting of one central alpha-helix surrounded by a four-stranded beta-sheet and four other alpha-helices [InterPro reference PUB00027806].

Purified E. coli YbeY cleaves a synthetic single-stranded RNA

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified E. coli YbeY acting on a 30-nt synthetic RNA containing 18 nt of the mature 16S 3' end and 12 nt of precursor trailer; not intact pre-16S maturation or isolated-domain catalysis.
Context
Figure 1C uses 5 uM YbeY and 5 uM 5'-32P-labeled RNA, with buffer control and 30, 90 and 120 second samples. Main methods specify 20 uL and 50 mM HEPES pH 7.5. The text describes efficient cleavage at 37 C; exact extended assay and construct details remain unverified because the supplement was inaccessible.
Family mapping
The paper's E. coli YbeY identifier P77385 has demerged into P0A898 (E. coli K12) and P0A899 (Shigella flexneri). The E. coli reference is P0A898, b0659/JW0656, sequence version 1, 155 residues, PF02130 at 21-144. The purified protein's exact tag and expression construct are not established by the inspected main text.
Limitations
The observed endpoint is cleavage of a short model RNA. It does not establish physiological cleavage specificity, turnover kinetics, a direct 16S maturation site, protein-substrate proteolysis or family-wide activity. Duplex resistance is condition-specific. Mutant activity loss is supportive but does not exclude folding or preparation effects; no residue-level chemical step is claimed.

Joint YbeY and RNase R treatment degrades rRNA in defective 70S preparations

MECHANISTIC

Scope
In vitro treatment of separate 70S preparations from E. coli MC4100 delta-ybeY and kasugamycin-treated MC4100; the two substrate contexts are distinct comparisons within Figure 3, not independent replications.
Context
Isolated 70S at 5.5 uM with YbeY and commercial RNase R at 5 uM each; the kasugamycin culture condition is 200 ug/mL. Main reaction methods specify 20 uL and 50 mM HEPES pH 7.5. rRNA is extracted after treatment and resolved by Synergel/agarose electrophoresis. Exact Figure 3 incubation time, temperature and additional buffer components require the unavailable extended methods.
Family mapping
E. coli YbeY maps from historical P77385 to UniProtKB:P0A898, b0659/JW0656, reference sequence version 1, PF02130 at 21-144 of 155 residues. The graph concerns the intact-protein preparation in conjunction with RNase R, not a sufficient isolated PF02130 domain.
Limitations
Neither enzyme alone nor H114A/H124A YbeY plus RNase R gives the adopted rRNA-band-loss endpoint. Matched wild-type 70S and 30S controls resist treatment. Ordered initiation and exonucleolytic microsteps remain proposed, and gel band loss does not demonstrate degradation of ribosomal proteins. Purification tag and full construct details remain unresolved; in vivo quality-control flux and polysome endpoints are not adopted here.
