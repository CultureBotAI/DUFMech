Source description

This domain is found in glutamate synthase, tungsten formylmethanofuran dehydrogenase subunit c (FwdC) and molybdenum formylmethanofuran dehydrogenase subunit c (FmdC). A repeated G-XX-G-XXX-G motif is seen in the alignment.

Causal graphs

Added NaCl lowers the measured radius of purified GltSHis particles

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Recombinant A. brasilense glutamate synthase containing wild-type alpha and C-terminally His6-tagged beta in Cottevieille 2008 Figure S3C; not an isolated GXGXG domain.
Context
Compare 1 mg/ml GltSHis after 17 h at 15 C with 1 M added NaCl or no added NaCl in 25 mM HEPES/KOH pH 7.5, 1 mM EDTA and 1 mM DTT. The plotted DLS radius is measured on the treated preparation. General DLS Methods describe a 50-ul cuvette and twenty 30-s acquisitions; these acquisitions are not independent preparations. The untagged enzyme is reported to behave similarly, but its unshown experiment is not a separate edge.
Family mapping
Alpha-subunit reference UniProtKB:Q05755 is 1515 aa, sequence version 1; PF01493 spans 1249-1496. UniProt and InterPro sequences agree. PDB 1EA0 polymer 1-1479 exactly matches reference 37-1515, establishing the mature-protein offset of 36 and a mature-domain interval of 1213-1460. The tested complex retains the other alpha domains and beta partner. The reference sequence paper identifies Sp7, but the complete 2008 clones and original donor strain were not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
DLS measures particle-size behavior, not domain-specific contact rupture or unique oligomer composition. SAXS and cryo-EM support an assembly interpretation but use separate measurements and mixture models. Some irreversible subunit loss and activity decrease occur on prolonged salt treatment. This is not salt-dependent cellular regulation, isolated-domain sufficiency or an observed electron-transfer trajectory.

Salt dilution increases particle radius after GltSHis salt treatment

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified wild-type-alpha/His6-beta GltSHis in Cottevieille 2008 Figure S3D; a separate dilution experiment, not full recovery of all original complexes.
Context
A 1.2-mg/ml sample previously incubated for 17 h with 1 M NaCl is diluted tenfold into the same HEPES/EDTA/DTT buffer without NaCl. The matched control is diluted tenfold into buffer containing 1 M NaCl. Calculated final protein is 0.12 mg/ml in both; residual NaCl in the low-salt arm is 0.1 M, not zero. DLS follows radius over approximately 500 s using 10-s acquisitions. General DLS temperature is 15 C; the caption does not separately establish the pretreatment temperature for this panel.
Family mapping
Q05755 alpha contains PF01493 at precursor 1249-1496, corresponding to mature 1213-1460 after removal of residues 1-36. The exact deposited mature sequence matches the current reference. This assay uses intact alpha/beta complexes with beta, not alpha, carrying the His6 extension.
Limitations
The two arms have matched dilution but different salt concentrations. Increased hydrodynamic size supports reassociation without proving exact stoichiometry, complete recovery, a unique GXGXG-mediated contact or a cellular mechanism. A fitted single-exponential curve and repeated acquisitions are not independent biological replications or an atomistic rate constant.

The delta40 alpha construct lacks detectable production in the reported expression attempts

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
A. brasilense gltB C-terminal truncation coexpressed with His6-tagged beta in E. coli BL21(DE3), as reported in Cottevieille 2008; an expression phenotype, not directly measured folding failure.
Context
pETGltSHis-derived T7 coproduction constructs encode either wild-type, delta7 or delta40 alpha. Supplemental Methods describe 30-C growth to absorbance about 2 followed by 25 C without IPTG, with delta40 attempts also using 25 C for 6-7 h or 15 C with 0.1 mM IPTG for 17 h. Delta7 production/purification is retained, whereas no truncated delta40 polypeptide production was observed. The main text compares with the productive full-length preparation. No plotted expression gel or detection threshold is supplied for delta40 in the inspected material.
Family mapping
Q05755 precursor 37-1515 matches mature 1EA0 residues 1-1479. The delta40 deletion maps to precursor 1476-1515, overlapping PF01493 at 1476-1496 and also removing an outside-domain tail. Reverse-complement translation of printed Oligo 3 ends at reference EEHVTETQSRF, residues 1465-1475, followed by two stops. Delta7 removes 1509-1515 outside PF01493; Oligo 2 locally matches the retained terminus. Printed nucleotide stop-position prose does not fully agree with this mapping; whole clones remain unverified.
Limitations
This is a reported negative production endpoint in a heterologous host, not a direct protein-folding, degradation or translation assay. Detection method, quantitative limits, independent clone counts and rescue are not established. The authors' exposed-hydrophobic-core explanation remains an interpretation. The deletion is not restricted to PF01493, and the unaffected delta7 is outside its match; neither result proves isolated-domain assembly function.
