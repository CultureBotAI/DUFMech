Source description

This domain of is found in several C. elegans proteins. The domain is 30 amino acids long and rich in cysteine residues. There are 6 conserved cysteine positions in the domain that form three disulphide bridges. The domain is found in the potassium channel inhibitor ShK in sea anemone [InterPro reference PUB00030746][InterPro reference PUB00033687].

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Enterokinase-cleaved recombinant Stichodactyla helianthus ShK tested on mouse Kv1.3 stably expressed in L929 fibroblasts, Chang 2012 Figure 6A/C; not every ShKT-containing protein.
Context
Refolded recombinant peptide is cleaved from thioredoxin and purified by RP-HPLC, leaving no extra N-terminal residue. Whole-cell patch clamp at room temperature uses 200-ms pulses from -80 to +40 mV every 30 s. Pipette solution contains 145 mM KF, 10 mM HEPES, 10 mM EGTA and 2 mM MgCl2, pH 7.2, 290 mOsm; bath contains 160 mM NaCl, 4.5 mM KCl, 2 mM CaCl2, 1 mM MgCl2 and 10 mM HEPES, pH 7.2, 300 mOsm. Figure 6C's caption reports 3-5 determinations per point.
Family mapping
P29187 / K1A_STIHL is the 35-aa mature peptide, sequence version 1, from taxon 6123. The published primers overlap by 21 bases and encode DDDDK followed by exact P29187 and a stop codon; this verifies the printed design, not independent clone resequencing. Current PF01549's curated description explicitly includes ShK; integrated IPR003582 maps P29187 residues 3-35 and includes the PF01549 signature. However, P29187 has no current UniProt Pfam cross-reference or direct PF01549 hit and is absent from the inspected PF01549.30 seed. This is curated-family plus integrated-domain association, not a verified current Pfam HMM interval. Mouse channel assay host is taxon 10090; exact channel clone sequence was not independently verified.
Limitations
The functional Hill-fit midpoint is not an independently measured physical binding constant. Current reduction establishes a scoped channel-blocking response, not atomic pore contacts or a complete immunosuppression pathway. Full text and captions were read, but figure images, raw currents and supplementary NMR data were not visually inspected. Other family members, channel paralogs and independent peptide batches remain unassessed.

Recombinant mature ShK decreases mouse Kv1.1 current

MECHANISTIC

Scope
The separate mouse Kv1.1-expressing L929 line in Chang 2012 Figure 6C; the example trace in Figure 6B uses synthetic ShK and is not misattributed to recombinant peptide.
Context
RP-HPLC-purified, enterokinase-cleaved recombinant ShK is applied during room-temperature whole-cell recording. Pulses run from -80 to +40 mV for 200 ms every 30 s. Pipette solution is 145 mM KF, 10 mM HEPES, 10 mM EGTA, 2 mM MgCl2, pH 7.2, 290 mOsm; bath is 160 mM NaCl, 4.5 mM KCl, 2 mM CaCl2, 1 mM MgCl2, 10 mM HEPES, pH 7.2, 300 mOsm. Dose-response values use peak current at +40 mV and 3-5 determinations per point according to the caption.
Family mapping
Tested mature ShK corresponds to sequence-version-1 P29187, 35 aa from Stichodactyla helianthus; the printed overlapping primers encode that exact mature sequence after DDDDK. PF01549's curated family description includes this peptide and its integrated IPR003582 interval is 3-35. That interval is not a current PF01549 HMM hit; the current direct Pfam query is empty and the entire inspected PF01549.30 seed lacks P29187. The mouse channel and host are separate assay entities, not sea-anemone gene products.
Limitations
The 15 +/- 5 pM functional inhibition midpoint does not prove physical affinity, absolute selectivity, a Lys22 pore-plug geometry or activity of all family proteins. Figure 6C's caption/Results were inspected but its image and raw recordings were unavailable. Exact channel clones, independent purification batches, other channel classes and native-organism function require separate evidence.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
