Source description

Members of this family are enzymes involved in a new pathway of pyridoxine/pyridoxal 5-phosphate biosynthesis [InterPro reference PUB00002305]. This family was formerly known as UPF0019.

PdxS addition increases the partner glutaminase readout

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified untagged Bacillus subtilis 168 YaaD/PdxS and YaaE/PdxT in Raschle 2005 supplement S2A. This is the same experiment retained from the partner perspective in PF01174, not an independent replication.
Context
4 uM each protein when present, 300 ul, 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 10 mM glutamine, 0.5 mM APAD, 7 U bovine glutamate dehydrogenase, 37 C; monitor A363. Individual-protein controls remain near background.
Family mapping
P37527/PDXS_BACSU is strain-168 PdxS/YaaD, 294 aa, sequence version 3; exact InterPro sequence match and PF01680 7-212. Genomic cloning, sequencing and N-terminal protein checks support the association. The full untagged protein, including residues outside this interval, was tested.
Limitations
This is a coupled glutamate readout. PdxS addition activates the joint preparation; PdxS is not thereby assigned autonomous glutaminase activity. No atomic allostery, ammonia channel or isolated-domain sufficiency is directly measured.

The joint PdxS and PdxT reaction produces identified PLP

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Raschle 2005 purified untagged strain-168 protein reaction and orthogonal product-identification assays. Shared primary experiment with PF01174, not independent support counted twice.
Context
40 uM each YaaD/YaaE, 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 37 C, 10 mM glutamine, 0.5 mM ribose 5-phosphate and 0.5 mM DHAP. Product analysis combines A414, deproteinized HPLC, chemical spectral shifts, electrospray MS and supplemental NMR.
Family mapping
Whole P37527-associated PdxS carries PF01680 7-212; partner PdxT is a separate P37528-associated protein. All proteins and substrates are a joint input, not proof of synthase-domain autonomy.
Limitations
Figure 1A/B captions state 1 mM glutamine while Methods/Results state 10 mM; no exact fitted kinetics are assigned. Supplemental NMR includes an unknown 3.75-ppm contaminant. The reported DHAP-containing reaction does not establish the molecular route of triose utilization or an intrinsic DHAP isomerase; later substrate-specificity work remains to be reconciled.

Ribose phosphate forms a Lys81-linked early adduct

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Raschle 2007 Figures 3/4, purified Pdx1-His6 and K81R/K149R derivatives. The observed covalent adduct is retained without adopting the paper's subsequently challenged imine interpretation.
Context
Figure 4 preincubates R5P with each protein for 3 h at 37 C, compared with its no-R5P spectrum; the figure does not enumerate protein/sugar doses. Separate radiolabel-guided tryptic fractionation and isotope-resolved MS/MS locate the early adduct at K81.
Family mapping
P37527-associated strain-168 PdxS, current 294-aa sequence with PF01680 7-212. The paper's modified peptide is current residues 61-83 and includes K81. Archived sequence version 2 lacked only the initial M; the paper's numbering already matches current K81/K149. 2NV1 is MAS plus P37527 plus LEHHHHHH, but every 2007 vial's complete clone was not independently sequenced here.
Limitations
K149R retains the early mass adduct and is not simply inactive in every step. The K81R comparison does not exclude folding effects by itself. Later NMR supports a C2 ketone/C1-N linkage for the prepared early species, so the exact older imine structure is not an observed edge. Neither preparation is an isolated PF01680 domain.

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Raschle 2007 Figure 2A main spectra and inset. This is a spectrally defined biochemical state, not endorsement of the original nitrogen-free structural assignment.
Context
20 uM free Pdx1, 20 uM Pdx2, 0.2 mM R5P and 10 mM glutamine; main spectra at 0-9 min. Reconstitution Methods give 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 7.5, without restating temperature. Inset compares WT Pdx2 with Pdx2-His6 H170N.
Family mapping
Pdx1-His6 derives from P37527-associated PdxS, PF01680 7-212; free preparation is cleared using DL-G3P/ammonium sulfate then dialyzed. H170N is in the separate PdxT/P37528-associated partner, not PF01680. The exact WT partner tag state in this inset is not independently resolved.

Individual Pdx2/glutamine omission controls are described in text; the H170N trace is shown. A sulfate-ammonium route is also reported, so PdxT is not universally necessary. The joint R5P reaction releases inorganic phosphate

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Raschle 2007 Figure 6C/D and the coupled phosphate-assay Methods. Phosphate release is an independent measured endpoint, not a causal consequence inferred from the optical chromophore trace.
Context
Equimolar Pdx1/Pdx2 at 10, 20 or 40 uM each, glutamine and R5P; 300 ul, 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 20 mM MgCl2 and EnzChek components; monitor A360. Assay-specific glutamine/R5P doses and temperature are not enumerated.
Family mapping
P37527-associated Pdx1-His6 contains PF01680 7-212, together with the separate Pdx2 protein. Full proteins and substrates are joint inputs, not isolated-domain or family-wide evidence.
Limitations
No enzyme-free control is shown in Figure 6C/D. Do not assign an exact completed one-to-one phosphate yield from finite time courses or transfer Figure 2A doses. This assay does not by itself distinguish elimination from hydrolysis or establish the nitrogen content of the accompanying chromophore.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
Not available means the source did not supply that counter. A zero is a reported count. Not scored means evidence counts are unavailable.
