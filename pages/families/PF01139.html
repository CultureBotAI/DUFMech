Source description

This family of RNA ligases ([ec:6.5.1.3]) join 2',3'-cyclic phosphate and 5'-OH ends. They catalyse the splicing of tRNA and may also participate in tRNA repair and recovery from stress-induced RNA damage [InterPro reference PUB00152168,InterPro reference PUB00057853,InterPro reference PUB00056744].

Purified E. coli RtcB forms a covalent guanylate adduct

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length recombinant E. coli RtcB, purified after His10-Smt3 tag removal; a protein-adduct assay, not an isolated domain or a cellular RNA-repair phenotype.
Context
The 2012 PNAS Figure 4B assay uses 10 uL, 50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 2 mM MnCl2, 10 uM alpha-32P-GTP and 5 uM RtcB at 37 C for 1 min. SDS-PAGE and autoradiography measure protein labeling. H337A/N/Q variants and the protein-loading gel are controls. GTP, manganese and enzyme are joint inputs.
Family mapping
The paper's 408-aa E. coli RtcB maps to reference P46850, sequence version 3, rtcB/yhgL, b3421, PF01139 at 29-408. The reported MS peptide 325-341 exactly matches the reference and contains H337. The reference is K12; the exact cloning-template strain and full final clone sequence remain unverified, so graph taxonomy is E. coli species rather than an asserted experimental K12 strain.
Limitations
Peptide mass and mutation evidence support the H337 assignment, but the graph does not assert a directly observed transition state. Figure 2's image and Results give m/z about 723.93 while its HTML caption transposes this to 732.92-732.94; no raw spectrum was reprocessed. The source's DNA-guanylylation assay is not relabeled as RNA chemistry or joined to this graph as a directly tracked molecule.

RtcB processing of a 5'-blocked cyclic-phosphate RNA substrate

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Tag-free E. coli RtcB acting on a synthetic 20-mer with a 5'-phosphate and a 2',3'-cyclic phosphate; not the ligatable 5'-OH substrate or native tRNA.
Context
The 2012 NAR Figure 7 plus-GTP time course uses 50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 2 mM MnCl2, 100 uM GTP, 1 uM RtcB and 0.1 uM labeled RNA at 37 C. RNase T1 digestion and urea-PAGE resolve terminal products over 0-120 s. RtcB, GTP and manganese are present together for both edges.
Family mapping
E. coli RtcB corresponds to the 408-aa P46850 reference, sequence version 3, with PF01139 at 29-408. Cited purification uses Ulp1-cleaved His10-Smt3 protein followed by nickel chromatography and gel filtration. Exact source strain and final clone sequence remain unverified; this is full-length protein, not the isolated Pfam interval.
Limitations
Starting RNA contains about 10% 3'-phosphate, and residual 5'-OH substrate accounts for a small ligation signal. The no-GTP control is labeled pRNAp in the caption but pRNA>p in the main text; neither identity is silently imposed, and the adopted edges use the plus-GTP time course. The specific 3'-ppG chemical assignment is inferred from mobility, GTP dependence and prior intermediate chemistry, not independently reidentified here. Enzyme-excess kinetics are not steady-state turnover.

RtcB seals a synthetic 5'-OH cyclic-phosphate RNA

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length tag-free E. coli RtcB and a ligatable synthetic 20-mer with 5'-OH and 2',3'-cyclic phosphate ends; net intramolecular RNA end joining.
Context
The 2012 NAR Figure 2 WT plus-GTP assay uses 50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 2 mM MnCl2, 100 uM GTP, 1 uM RtcB and 0.1 uM labeled RNA at 37 C. RNase T1 digestion reveals a six-nucleotide ligation-junction fragment. Minus-GTP and H337A panels use longer minute-scale time courses, unlike the WT plus-GTP seconds-scale panel.
Family mapping
The 408-aa E. coli RtcB is mapped to P46850 sequence version 3 and PF01139 at 29-408; experimental peptide identity and cited tag-removal protocol are retained separately. K12 is the mapped reference, not a verified experimental cloning-template strain.
Limitations
The substrate preparation includes about 7-8% 3'-phosphate ends, but this does not account for the much larger ligated fraction. The endpoint and time course concern synthetic RNA under enzyme excess, not turnover rates or physiological tRNA substrates. This graph is not connected directly to the 5'-blocked substrate graph. No family-wide, archaeal or mammalian cofactor independence is inferred.
