Source description

This is a family of metal-sensitive repressors, involved in resistance to metal ions. Members of this family bind copper, nickel or cobalt ions via conserved cysteine and histidine residues. In the absence of metal ions, these proteins bind to promoter regions and repress transcription. When bound to metal ions they are unable to bind DNA, leading to transcriptional derepression [InterPro reference PUB00041403,InterPro reference PUB00057455,InterPro reference PUB00057456,InterPro reference PUB00057457,InterPro reference PUB00035608].

Causal graphs

Copper loading reduces purified B. subtilis CsoR operator binding

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Intact purified B. subtilis CsoR compared in apo and Cu(I)-loaded states on a synthetic copZA operator; not cellular transcription or an isolated PF02583 domain.
Context
Figure 7A fluorescence anisotropy uses 4 nM labeled 30-bp operator DNA, 10 mM MES, 0.4 M NaCl, 2 mM DTT, pH 6.5 and 25 C. Protein was expressed in E. coli BL21(DE3) from a Helmann-laboratory pET16b construct. Five readings were averaged at each titration addition.
Family mapping
B. subtilis csoR, formerly yvgZ, is BSU33520 and maps to UniProtKB:O32222, reference sequence version 1, 101 residues, PF02583 at 17-97. The assay uses intact protein, not that isolated interval; exact final tag and construct sequence remain unverified.
Limitations
The Cu-loaded curve is unsaturated and fitted using the apo maximum anisotropy. The second binding constant is only an upper bound, and individual step constants are not uniquely resolved. E90A loses negative regulation, but no atomic allosteric path is established. Low-micromolar tetramer observations do not exclude nanomolar assembly differences. No family-wide copper specificity, transcription endpoint or cellular efflux is inferred.

  • copza-operator-occupancy: CsoR occupancy of the synthetic copZA operator; STATE; Ungrounded

Loss of csoR increases copZA reporter output without added copper

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Whole-gene csoR replacement in B. subtilis CU1065 derivatives carrying SPbeta(PcopZA-cat-lacZ); a cellular reporter phenotype, not direct polymerase exclusion.
Context
HB7352 csoR::spc is compared with HB7358 reporter control and HB7356 csoR::spc amyE::csoR-cat complementation. Overnight cultures were diluted 1:100 into LB and grown to mid-exponential phase at 37 C and 225 rpm. The adopted edge is the no-added-CuSO4 comparison; the figure also includes 0.5 mM CuSO4. Figure 2A states three experiments with total N=7.
Family mapping
CU1065 is a W168 attSPbeta trpC2 derivative. Its yvgZ/csoR locus corresponds to reference O32222, BSU33520, sequence version 1, PF02583 at 17-97 of 101 residues. Whole-gene replacement does not establish an isolated-domain effect.
Limitations
Beta-galactosidase activity is reporter output, not a direct measurement of native copZA RNA, polymerase occupancy or copper efflux. Ectopic complementation supports gene dependence without resolving each intervening step. The paper corrects an earlier YhdQ/CueR assignment caused by a wrong reporter in the old strain; that claim is not combined with this graph. Other family members and growth endpoints remain separate.
  • csor-null: Whole-gene csoR null allele in the CU1065 reporter strain; PERTURBATION; UniProtKB:O32222
  • copza-lacz-output: PcopZA-cat-lacZ beta-galactosidase output without added copper; PHENOTYPE; Ungrounded

EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
