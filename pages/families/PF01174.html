IPR002161: integrated entry for SNO glutamine amidotransferase family

Source description

This family and its amidotransferase domain was first described in [InterPro reference PUB00020352]. It is predicted that members of this family are involved in the pyridoxine biosynthetic pathway, based on the proximity and co-regulation of the corresponding genes and physical interaction between the members of PF01174 (SNO glutamine amidotransferase family) and PF01680 (SOR/SNZ family) [InterPro reference PUB00007176].

PdxS increases the glutaminase readout of untagged PdxT

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified untagged Bacillus subtilis 168 YaaE/PdxT with YaaD/PdxS in Raschle 2005 supplement S2A and main Results. The bovine reporter enzyme is a separate component.
Context
300 ul, 4 uM YaaE, 4 uM YaaD when present, 10 mM glutamine, 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 0.5 mM APAD and 7 U bovine glutamate dehydrogenase; 37 C. Measure the increase at 363 nm after glutamine addition.
Family mapping
P37528/PDXT_BACSU, 196 aa, sequence version 1, matches InterPro exactly with PF01174 at 6-188. The study cloned strain-168 genomic yaaE, verified constructs by sequencing and purified proteins by N-terminal sequencing. Primer ends match MLTIGVL and EYKQKALV. Untagged activity preparations differ from His6-YaaE used for pull-out assays.
Limitations
APAD reduction is a coupled glutamate readout, not direct observation of ammonia channeling. Individual-protein controls remain near background; this is an assay-specific bound, not universal absence of intrinsic activity. The 2004 SMY His6 preparations and their pH/temperature are not transferred.

Joint PdxS and PdxT reactions produce PLP

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Untagged strain-168 YaaD/YaaE with glutamine and phosphorylated carbohydrate substrates. Product identity is supported by orthogonal analyses, not only the Tris-dependent absorbance signal.
Context
40 uM each protein, 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 37 C, 10 mM glutamine, 0.5 mM ribose 5-phosphate and 0.5 mM dihydroxyacetone phosphate. A414 monitors the product-associated Tris Schiff base; deproteinized product is additionally examined by HPLC, MS, chemical spectral shifts and supplemental 600-MHz NMR.
Family mapping
YaaE/PdxT is P37528-associated strain-168 protein, PF01174 6-188; YaaD is the separate synthase. Both whole proteins and all listed substrates form a joint input, not a claim that PdxT alone synthesizes PLP.
Limitations
No directly tracked ammonia channel or atomic catalytic sequence is inferred. Figure 1A/B captions say 1 mM glutamine whereas Methods/Results say 10 mM; exact fitted kinetics are not assigned to this graph. Supplemental NMR identifies an unknown 3.75-ppm contaminant. Raw spectra and historical clones were not independently reanalyzed.

PdxT supports the ribulose-phosphate reaction with external ammonium

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Figure 1C's specific ribulose 5-phosphate comparison with external ammonium sulfate, not a universal requirement for PdxT in ammonia-supported synthesis.
Context
20 uM untagged YaaD with or without 20 uM YaaE, 1 mM ribulose 5-phosphate, 2 mM DL-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and 10 mM ammonium sulfate; 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0, 37 C; A414 time course. The ribose 5-phosphate condition is a separate specificity control.
Family mapping
Strain-168 YaaE/PdxT maps to P37528 and PF01174 6-188. The coupled product readout still requires YaaD and the listed substrates; no isolated-domain claim is made.
Limitations
Ribose 5-phosphate supports synthesis without YaaE in the same external-ammonium experiment. A414 is a product-associated readout, not direct identification of an isomerase step. A role in pentulose isomerization is a proposal, not an observed edge.

Acivicin exposure reduces the coupled glutaminase readout

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Figure 3A's 5-mM acivicin preincubation of untagged YaaD/YaaE, assayed by glutamate-dependent APAD reduction. This is a biochemical treatment, not a cellular antimicrobial effect.
Context
4 uM each YaaD/YaaE in the pH-8.0 coupled-assay buffer, 5 mM acivicin at 37 C for the displayed 0-10 min pretreatments; add glutamine to 10 mM and monitor A363. The selected figure protocol is explicit despite the Methods concentration-list discrepancy.
Family mapping
YaaE/PdxT corresponds to P37528 with PF01174 6-188. The C79-containing peptide 74-87 matches the reference and is separately assigned as an acivicin target by MS/MS; this does not make every proposed atomic step observed.
Limitations
Methods list 1/2/10 mM while Figure 3 uses 5 mM and 2/5/10 mM. Caption s^-1 conflicts with minute-based Results/time axes; no precise inactivation rate is adopted. Raw mass/adduct chemistry and reporter-specific interference controls remain incompletely assessed; covalent-modification evidence supports, but does not replace, assay scope.

Scope
Figure 3C's separate 40-uM YaaD/YaaE product assay with glutamine as nitrogen donor, not the lower-concentration glutaminase assay or an external-ammonium comparison.
Context
40 uM each untagged protein, 0.04-5 mM acivicin in 50 mM Tris-Cl pH 8.0 at 37 C; Methods describe 60 min exposure. Measure A414 synthesis with 0.5 mM ribose 5-phosphate, 0.5 mM DHAP and 10 mM glutamine, relative to untreated activity.
Family mapping
Strain-168 YaaE is P37528-associated PdxT, PF01174 6-188. YaaD supplies the separate synthase activity. Both proteins remain in the treated assay mixture.
