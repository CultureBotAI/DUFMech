Source description

The bacterial Tat system has a remarkable ability to transport folded proteins even enzyme complexes across the cytoplasmic membrane. It is structurally and mechanistically similar to the Delta pH-driven thylakoidal protein import pathway. A functional Tat system or Delta pH-dependent pathway requires three integral membrane proteins: TatA/Tha4, TatB/Hcf106 and TatC/cpTatC. The TatC protein is essential for the function of both pathways. It might be involved in twin-arginine signal peptide recognition, protein translocation and proton translocation. Sequence analysis predicts that TatC contains six transmembrane helices (TMHs), and experimental data confirmed that N- and C-termini of TatC or cpTatC are exposed to the cytoplasmic or stromal face of the membrane. The cytoplasmic N-terminus and the first cytoplasmic loop region of the Escherichia coli TatC protein are essential for protein export. At least two TatC molecules co-exist within each Tat translocon [InterPro reference PUB00011123].

Tat-positive vesicles generate protease-protected mature TorA-MalE335

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Intact E. coli Tat machinery in inverted membrane vesicles, not purified TatC or an isolated PF00902 fragment. Adopted readout is mature-product protection from proteinase K.
Context
BL21(DE3) DeltaTat produces vesicles with p8737 tatABCD. Radiolabeled TorA-MalE335 lacks 35 C-terminal MalE residues; vesicles are added 10 min after translation starts, followed by 25 min at 37 C. No-vesicle, TatAC and proteinase-K comparisons distinguish processing from protection. CCCP is 0.1 mM, compared with DMSO vehicle.
Family mapping
K-12 TatC reference UniProtKB:P69423, tatC/b3839/JW3815, has 258 residues, sequence version 2, and PF00902 at 12-221 inclusive with exact UniProt/InterPro sequence agreement. The assay uses full-length Tat proteins and native membrane components; reference taxon is not the BL21 production strain. Complete p8737 clone junctions were not reconstructed.
Limitations
Protection supports transport into vesicles, not a directly filmed microscopic pathway. CCCP perturbs membrane energetics, not TatC alone; no TatC proton-pump activity is asserted. Buffer and protease details rely on cited protocols, and raw gels, quantitative transport rates and supplement data were not reanalyzed.

TatAC vesicles process TorA-MalE335 without protecting its mature domain

MECHANISTIC

Scope
TatB-lacking inverted vesicles expressing TatA and TatC; observed precursor cleavage is separate from mature-protein transport.
Context
BL21(DE3) DeltaTat with p8737-tatAC; translation/vesicle reaction at 37 C for 25 min after vesicle addition. RR TorA-MalE335 from pPJ1 is compared with its RR-to-KK counterpart from pPJ2. Proteinase-K exposure tests accessibility, and Tat-positive vesicles provide a transport comparator.
Family mapping
TatC P69423 maps to PF00902 residues 12-221, within a 258-residue reference. This intact-protein/native-membrane preparation also includes TatA and signal peptidase. No isolated Pfam interval or all-Tat-substrate transfer is supported.
Limitations
Cleavage alone does not show transport of the mature passenger. RR-to-KK is a signal-peptide change, not a TatC mutation or direct binding measurement. Natural SufI/AmiC and linker variants have different cleavage behavior; the proposed hairpin topology remains a model. Complete clone sequences and referenced assay details remain open.

TatC as the sole Tat component supports premature precursor processing

MECHANISTIC

Scope
TatC-only refers to the supplied Tat components in native inverted vesicles, not purified TatC alone. Adopted endpoint is TorA-MalE335 processing without mature-product protease protection.
Context
BL21(DE3) DeltaTat with pFAT588 produces TatC-containing vesicles. The cited plasmid is pQE60-derived with MC4100 tatC and a C-terminal His6 tag. Figure 4A compares TatC, TatAC, Tat-positive and DeltaTat vesicles in the 37 C translation/processing reaction; Figure 1B verifies Tat-component content.
Family mapping
Native TatC reference P69423, 258 residues, sequence version 2, PF00902 at 12-221. The source identifies the pFAT588 lineage and C-terminal tag but complete 2012 clone junctions were not independently sequenced. Native reference, MC4100 DNA donor and BL21 vesicle host are distinct identifiers.
Limitations
Native membrane, resident signal peptidase and precursor remain required assay context. The result does not demonstrate purified TatC sufficiency, an atomic insertion trajectory or transport of the mature passenger. It cannot be generalized to every signal peptide, and the separate pPJ10 in-vivo construct is not this vesicle construct.
