Source description

This domain is found at the C-terminal end of the probable enoyl -CoA hydratase alpha subunit from Thermomonospora curvata (ChsH2) and similar archaeal and bacterial proteins. ChsH2 is likely to be involved in bile acid degradation. The C-terminal region of this protein is organised into two domains: an N-terminal zinc finger domain (PF12172 (ChsH2, rubredoxin-like zinc ribbon domain)) and an OB-fold domain (this entry) that consists of a five-stranded beta-barrel [InterPro reference PUB00155451]. This domain is also found in 3-oxo-4,17- pregnadiene-20-carboxyl-CoA hydratase alpha subunit from Mycobacterium tuberculosis that is involved in cholesterol side chain degradation [InterPro reference PUB00154876]. It is also found in Methanothermococcus thermolithotrophicus HMGCS, an enzyme complex comprising the thiolase and 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl (HMG)-CoA synthase, that catalyses the second reaction in the mevalonate pathway-the exergonic condensation of acetoacetyl-CoA and acetyl-CoA to HMG-CoA [InterPro reference PUB00154878].

T. curvata DUF35 addition increases Ltp2 activity

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified Tcur3479 Ltp2 and Tcur3482 ChsH2 C-terminal construct from Thermomonospora curvata DSM 43183; in-vitro mixing experiment.
Context
Ten-minute incubation at Ltp2:DUF35 ratio 1:5; activity toward 21 uM 17-HOPC-CoA measured at 25 C in 100 mM HEPES pH 7.5 with 200 uM NADH and 16 uM TTHB247.
Family mapping
D1AB77, sequence version 1, 319 residues; PF01796 spans 237-298. Deposited 6OK1 entity 2 is initiator M plus reference 188-319 and includes PF12172 at 199-234.
Limitations
The tested construct includes both domains; their individual contributions are unresolved. Ltp2 alone retains weak activity and DUF35 alone is inactive. The same experiment is retained in PF12172, not independent replication. Allostery, folding assistance and bile-acid physiology are not established by this assay.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
