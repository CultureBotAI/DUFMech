Source description

This entry includes small conductance mechanosensitive channels (MscS), which assembles to a homoheptameric complex [InterPro reference PUB00019883,InterPro reference PUB00019884,InterPro reference PUB00019885,InterPro reference PUB00101036,InterPro reference PUB00062568,InterPro reference PUB00101037]. Each monomer consists of a N-terminal transmembrane region and a large cytoplasmic segment composed of a five-stranded antiparallel beta-sheet (middle/second) domain and a C-terminal alpha/beta-domain [InterPro reference PUB00101036,InterPro reference PUB00062568,InterPro reference PUB00101037,InterPro reference PUB00064132]. This is the beta domain which adopts a Sm-like barrel structure.

G168D impairs MscS current adaptation and N117K restores it in the double mutant

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length E. coli MscS variants in inside-out membrane patches from MJF465 lacking mscK, mscS and mscL; adopted endpoint is current decline during sustained pressure, not an isolated beta-domain activity.
Context
pTRCMscS variants; bath 400 mM sucrose, 200 mM KCl, 90 mM MgCl2, 4 mM CaCl2, 5 mM HEPES at pH 7.2. Pipette solution omits sucrose; pipette voltage is +15 mV. Pressure steps are separated by at least 3 min. Figure 4 displays three pressure responses from the same patch per genotype, not three independent replicates.
Family mapping
UniProtKB:P0C0S1, mscS/yggB/b2924/JW2891, is a 286-residue K-12 reference, sequence version 1. Exact UniProt/InterPro agreement places PF00924 at 114-180, including N117 and G168. The paper's structural beta domain is 128-181 and places N117 in TM3b. HMM and structural boundaries must not be conflated; complete experimental clone junctions remain unverified.
Limitations
The 2011 protocol does not distinguish desensitization from inactivation. No direct salt-bridge formation or interdomain motion is measured; cartoons are interpretations. Pressure levels differ among traces and no exact common-pressure rate is claimed. Temperature, patch-specific induction and independent replication are not established by the adopted panels.

DTT reversibly restores pressure-evoked N117C/G168C MscS activity

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length N117C/G168C MscS in MJF429, which lacks mscK and mscS but retains MscL. The endpoint is detectable MscS channel activity, not a directly measured disulfide reaction.
Context
Figure 6B inside-out patches with the 2011 pH 7.2 bath and +15 mV pipette protocol. Control, 2.5 mM DTT and washout are compared at the illustrated pressure steps; MscL openings remain a pressure-response control. Single-cysteine channels are active in Figure 6A, but its caption does not assign their individual patches to a strain.
Family mapping
P0C0S1 reference N117 and G168 both lie in PF00924 residues 114-180. N117 belongs structurally to TM3b and G168 to the beta domain. Native full-length channel mutations, not a truncated-domain reconstitution, underlie these comparisons.
Limitations
Reversible reducing-agent dependence supports a redox-sensitive constraint but does not itself measure an S-S bond or distinguish closed from inactivated states. Complete construct sequence, temperature, independent replicate counts and folding controls are unresolved. No universal redox regulation of WT MscS is inferred.

Cytoplasmic Ficoll promotes tension-conditioned WT MscS inactivation more than G168D

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Figure 4D/E of the 2014 study, using full-length WT and G168D MscS and pulse-step-pulse availability measurements. This is distinct from the 2011 sustained-pressure adaptation protocol.
Context
pB10b expression; Methods name MJF465 and PB113, with retained MscL in PB113 used for mutant midpoint calibration, but do not assign each Figure 4 patch to one strain. Inside-out bath has 400 mM sucrose, 200 mM KCl, 50 mM MgCl2, 5 mM CaCl2, 5 mM HEPES at pH 7.4; pipette potential 30 mV. Cytoplasmic Ficoll 400 is 5% w/v, n=3 patches per genotype, with a 10-second conditioning tension between test pulses.
Family mapping
G168D changes the reference glycine within P0C0S1 PF00924 residues 114-180. The intact channel, membrane and controlled mechanical perturbation are the tested system, not isolated beta-domain sufficiency. Full pB10b clone sequence is not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
Inactivation is operationally measured through the decreased channel population available at the second test pulse. Tension is required; Ficoll alone is not assigned a sufficient cause. Pressure-to-tension conversion uses reference midpoints and a constant-curvature assumption. Cage compression, salt bridges and specific atomic trajectories are not directly observed. Panels A/B use 10% Ficoll and must not be substituted for the D/E conditions.
