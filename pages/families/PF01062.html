Source description

This entry represents animal bestrophin homologues. Bestrophin is a 68-kDa basolateral plasma membrane protein expressed in retinal pigment epithelial cells (RPE). It is encoded by the VMD2 gene, which is mutated in Best macular dystrophy, a disease characterised by a depressed light peak in the electrooculogram [InterPro reference PUB00011865]. VMD2 encodes a 585-amino acid protein with an approximate mass of 68 kDa which has been designated bestrophin. Bestrophin shares homology with the Caenorhabditis elegans RFP gene family, named for the presence of a conserved arginine (R), phenylalanine (F), proline (P), amino acid sequence motif. Bestrophin is a plasma membrane protein, localised to the basolateral surface of RPE cells consistent with a role for bestrophin in the generation or regulation of the EOG light peak. Bestrophin and other RFP family members represent a new class of chloride channels, indicating a direct role for bestrophin in generating the light peak [InterPro reference PUB00011865]. The VMD2 gene underlying Best disease was shown to represent the first human member of the RFP-TM protein family. More than 97% of the disease-causing mutations are located in the N-terminal RFP-TM domain implying important functional properties [InterPro reference PUB00011864]. The bestrophins are four-pass transmembrane chloride-channel proteins [InterPro reference PUB00096747], and the RFP-TM or bestrophin domain extends from the N-terminus through approximately 350 amino acids and contains all of the TM domains as well as nearly all reported disease causing mutations [InterPro reference PUB00109156]. Interestingly, the RFP motif is not conserved evolutionarily back beyond Metazoa, neither is it in plant members.

Bilateral calcium chelation decreases purified chicken BEST1 current

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Chicken BEST1 encoded residues 1-405 plus C-terminal EGEEF, expressed in Pichia and reconstituted in planar bilayers. Not the full 762-aa protein, human retinal cells or bacterial KpBest.
Context
2016 Figure 1B-D representative same-bilayer comparison. Symmetric 30 mM KCl, 20 mM HEPES-NaOH pH 7.6, 0.21 mM EGTA and 0.19 mM CaCl2 initially give about 300 nM free calcium; 3:1 wt/wt POPE/POPG, 22-24 C. Add 10 mM EGTA to both sides. Currents at +100 mV are averaged over the final 200 ms of a 2-s pulse; empty-bilayer currents are subtracted.
Family mapping
Historical E1C3A0 is deleted, but its archived sequence and current A0A8V0ZZU7 match exactly at 1-405; PF01062 spans 1-324 inclusive. Five full-length differences lie beyond 405. PDB 4RDQ entity 1 exactly matches current residues 2-405 plus EGEEF, consistent with the cited purification's MS/Edman-confirmed Thr2 start. Other mutant clone sequences are not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
This is a direct current endpoint after chelation, not an observed atomic gate-closing trajectory. Both channel orientations are present; bilateral chelation is not interchangeable with one-sided treatment. The construct extends beyond the Pfam profile and does not prove isolated-domain sufficiency or a universal family calcium threshold.

Trans-side calcium restores BEST1 current after orientation-selective silencing

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified tagged chicken BEST1 1-405 in the 2016 Figure 1E/F bilayer protocol. The cis-side population remains silenced during trans perfusion.
Context
Symmetric 30 mM KCl, POPE/POPG 3:1, pH 7.6, 22-24 C. After adding 10 mM EGTA to cis, trans solutions use 1 mM EGTA/calcium-EGTA mixtures to vary free calcium from near zero to 350 nM. Current magnitude at -100 mV is normalized to the 350-nM response. Three separate bilayers, SEM; two-second pulses and final 200-ms averaging.
Family mapping
E1C3A0 archived 1-405 matches current A0A8V0ZZU7 exactly; PF01062 is 1-324. The purified construct includes the EGEEF tag and processing to Thr2 established in the cited original method; it is not full-length BEST1 or an isolated Pfam fragment.

Increasing current magnitude at a fixed negative voltage does not imply a universal direction of chloride flow. No atomic motion, stoichiometric binding event or human disease mechanism is directly tracked.

Neck substitutions increase the current fraction retained after calcium chelation

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Individual F80A/F80N and joint I76A/F80A/F84A TripleA constructs of tagged chicken BEST1 1-405, compared with WT in 2016 Figure 3C.
Context
Symmetric 30 mM KCl bilayers, POPE/POPG 3:1, pH 7.6, 22-24 C, initially about 300 nM free calcium. Add 10 mM EGTA to both sides and stir four minutes. Current at +100 mV after chelation is normalized to each construct's calcium-present current. Three separate bilayers per comparison, SEM.
Family mapping
I76, F80 and F84 are verified in the identical historical/current 1-405 region of E1C3A0/A0A8V0ZZU7 and within PF01062 residues 1-324. TripleA is one joint perturbation, not three independently sufficient mutations.
Limitations
Normalized retained fraction is not absolute current, single-channel conductance or open probability; precise incorporation is unknown. F80I is described as nearly WT-like in calcium dependence relative to the larger mutant effects and is not assigned an all-or-none independence edge. Neck mutations do not substantially switch anion/cation selectivity. Structural gate and quadrupole models are interpretations, not directly observed trajectories.

Clasp substitutions reduce coupled chloride flux and TripleA restores it

MECHANISTIC

Scope
D301A/D304A compared with WT, and the joint TripleA/D301A/D304A construct compared with D301A/D304A, in 2016 Figure 4A/C/D proteoliposomes. Only the external 2-uM-calcium condition is adopted here.
Context
0.1 mg/ml protein and 10 mg/ml POPE/POPG before 100-fold dilution; internal 100 mM Na2SO4, 10 mM HEPES pH 8.2, 1 mM EGTA. External 125 mM NaCl and calcium-EGTA give about 2 uM free calcium. CCCP initiates ACMA quench coupled to anion influx. The cited 2014 method supplies 2 uM ACMA, 1 uM CCCP, 0.5 mg/ml BSA and external pH 8.1. Comparisons were purified/reconstituted in parallel and assayed on the same day.
Family mapping
D301/D304 and I76/F80/F84 lie within A0A8V0ZZU7 PF01062 residues 1-324 and the experimentally mapped 1-405 construct. The 2014 purification confirms Thr2 processing and the deposited sequence matches residues 2-405 plus EGEEF. Complete 2016 mutant clone sequences remain unverified.
Limitations
ACMA reports coupled acidification, not direct chloride concentration or a tracked molecular gate. The 2016 traces do not specify exact replicate count or recording temperature; bilayer temperature is not borrowed. Empty vesicles were prepared but are not displayed in Figure 4. Size-exclusion similarity does not eliminate every folding/incorporation alternative. Outside-only calcium activates only the appropriate orientation; this can affect WT/mutant flux comparisons.
