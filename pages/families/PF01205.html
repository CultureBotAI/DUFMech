PF01205: Impact N-terminal domain
IPR001498: integrated entry for Impact N-terminal domain

Source description

This entry represents the N-terminal domain of the Impact proteins. The Impact protein is a translational regulator that ensures constant high levels of translation under amino acid starvation. It acts by interacting with Gcn1/Gcn1L1, thereby preventing activation of Gcn2 protein kinases (EIF2AK1 to 4) and subsequent down-regulation of protein synthesis [InterPro reference PUB00161305,InterPro reference PUB00158485]. It is evolutionary conserved from eukaryotes to archaea [InterPro reference PUB00046141].

GST-Yih1 overexpression lowers starvation-induced eIF2alpha phosphorylation

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Saccharomyces cerevisiae H1511 expressing N-terminal GST-Yih1(2-258) from pES187-B1 versus GST alone in Sattlegger 2011 Figure 5C/D. This is a whole-protein overexpression phenotype.
Context
Galactose-induced exponential cultures are treated with 10 mM 3AT for 1 h before harvesting. Immunoblots measure Ser51-phosphorylated eIF2alpha relative to total eIF2alpha; at least two independent transformants and SE are reported. Plate-growth conditions are separate.
Family mapping
P25637/YIH1_YEAST is YCR059C, 258 aa, sequence version 1; the cited GI 6319904 resolves to matching NP_009985.1. PF01205 maps to 144-251 in the C-terminal ancient domain, despite the preserved Impact_N label. PF05773 RWD is separately at 6-108. The 2-258 fusion retains both; exact historical junctions remain unverified. P40512/PCI8 is an unrelated YIH1 synonym.
Limitations
Figure 2 confirms overexpression, not physiological dosage. RWD-only 2-132 lacks PF01205 yet inhibits, whereas 133-258 retains PF01205 but has little effect. This does not establish isolated PF01205 inhibition, direct eIF2alpha dephosphorylation or increased total translation. Binding and modeled actin switching are not an observed causal chain.

GST-Yih1 overexpression reduces H1511 growth under 3AT starvation

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
H1511 with endogenous YIH1 and plasmid-borne GST-Yih1(2-258) versus GST-only control in Figure 5A.
Context
Spot 5 ul of tenfold serial dilutions of overnight cultures on synthetic minimal galactose medium with or without 3AT. The inspected 2011 caption does not specify plate 3AT concentration, incubation temperature or duration; the liquid 10-mM/1-h protocol is not transferred.
Family mapping
The YCR059C fusion maps to P25637 with PF01205 at 144-251 and separate RWD at 6-108. Endogenous Yih1 remains present in this host. Current reference identity does not reconstruct every historical host or plasmid sequence.
Limitations
Growth is an indirect integrated phenotype, not direct kinase activity, viability loss or a quantified growth rate. Nonstarved plates constrain starvation specificity. This is not a normal-expression loss-of-function assay or an isolated ancient-domain effect.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.

Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
