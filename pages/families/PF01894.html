Source description

This family includes YjbQ from Escherichia coli, its orthologue from Pyrococcus furiosus and similar sequences. These proteins have been shown to have sufficient thiamine phosphate synthase activity to complement thiE mutants. However, it is presumed that this is a secondary activity, and the primary function of the YjbQ family enzyme remains unknown [InterPro reference PUB00101019,InterPro reference PUB00101020].

Expressed YjbQ supports delta-pdxB colony growth

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Kim2010 TableII, supplementary TableIII and relevant Methods; BW25113-derived delta-pdxB/JU283 on M9/glucose at37 C.
Context
Supplementary TableI identifies JU283 as a selected JWK2317 single colony. M9 contains glucose0.2% and NH4Cl0.1% w/v. ASKA hits were reintroduced and independently cloned in pTrcHisB; confirmation Methods specifies five PBS washes, IPTG0.1 mM, kanamycin20 ug/ml and ampicillin100 ug/ml. TableII compares no supplement and a defined mixture without pyridoxine; TableIII separately tests serine1 mM. FigureS1 caption instead names chloramphenicol35 ug/ml with pTrcHisB; that antibiotic discrepancy remains unresolved.
Family mapping
Whole E. coli K12 YjbQ b4056/JW4017 corresponds to P0AF48/NP_418480.1 reference138 aa with PF01894 at6-138. Both expression systems add N-terminal His6; ASKA also adds five C-terminal residues. TableV printed pTrcHisB and pET45b forward primers differ in the YjbQ start region. The reference sequence match does not independently establish the experimental insert sequence or isolated-domain sufficiency.
Limitations
TablesII/SIII +++ means1 mm colonies after3-5 days, not a directly measured specific growth rate or20% catalytic activity. pdxB complementation is faster at1-2 days. FigureS1 labels YjbQ at4 days and vector at7 days, not matched-time growth rates. Failure to rescue separate serC,pdxA,pdxJ,pdxH mutants bounds the effect but is not a matched double-knockout causal chain. Proposed OHPB entry is inferred; YjbQ dehydrogenase activity was not assayed because soluble protein was unavailable. JU283 was selected from heterogeneous Keio stocks for its30 C phenotype; transfer to all delta-pdxB isolates is not established.
