Source description

This family includes four different proteins from E. coli alone. One of them, YdgG or TqsA, has been shown to mediate transport of the quorum-sensing signal autoinducer 2 (AI-2). It is not clear if TqsA enhances secretion of AI-2 or inhibits AI-2 uptake. By altering the intracellular concentration of AI-2, TqsA affects gene expression in biofilms and biofilm formation [InterPro reference PUB00076990]. TsqA belongs to the AI-2 exporter (AI-2E) superfamily [InterPro reference PUB00076989].

Whole-gene ydgG deletion lowers extracellular AI-2 reporter activity in LB

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
E. coli K-12 BW25113 and its complete ydgG::KmR replacement, Herzberg 2006 pp. 589-590; not a purified TqsA transport assay.
Context
Overnight donor cultures are diluted 1:100 in LB and harvested at OD600 0.6. Filter-sterilized supernatants are assayed with V. harveyi BB170 luminescence normalized per reporter cell. The cited detailed reporter protocol was not independently inspected; no absolute AI-2 concentration or reporter incubation time is invented.
Family mapping
P0AFS5 / tqsA/ydgG / b1601 is the 344-aa K12 reference, sequence version 1, with PF01594 17-328. The paper identifies b1601/ydgG and verifies the complete BW25113 deletion by five PCRs. Reference sequence and InterPro match exactly, but the historical assay strain/clone was not independently sequenced. Deletion removes the whole gene, not only the domain.
Limitations
Reporter activity is not a direct chemical concentration or membrane flux. Pleiotropy, polarity and altered signal production/uptake remain possible. This assay cannot distinguish increased export from decreased uptake; the later structural study did not demonstrate TqsA transport with its attempted methods.

Induced ydgG complementation restores extracellular reporter activity

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
BW25113 ydgG::KmR with pCA24NydgG+ and empty-vector controls, Herzberg 2006 extracellular complementation experiment.
Context
Cultures receive IPTG and 30 ug/ml chloramphenicol, are harvested at OD600 0.6, washed twice for 10 min at 8820g to remove antibiotic that inhibits the Vibrio reporter, and resuspended in 20 ml LB plus 0.2% glucose. Supernatants are sampled after growth to OD600 0.9. The retained endpoint is 1.5 mM IPTG, compared with the uncomplemented/vector context and normalized to WT with pCA24N.
Family mapping
The historical pCA24NydgG+ expresses the b1601/ydgG gene corresponding to P0AFS5, PF01594 17-328. Full historical tag/linker sequence is not established. Gene-level trans-complementation does not isolate the domain or rule out every overexpression-mediated effect.
Limitations
Restoration concerns the extracellular BB170 bioassay only; it is not direct export, restoration of intracellular lsr output or a universal rescue of all deletion phenotypes. Washed OD600-0.9 cultures must not be merged with the plasmid-free OD600-0.6 comparisons.

Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized.
