Source description

This is the second beta-propeller domain of ER membrane protein complex subunit 1. ER membrane protein complex subunit 1 is a component of the ER membrane protein complex (EMC, composed of EMC1, EMC2, EMC3, EMC4, EMC5 and EMC6). In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the EMC seems to be required for efficient folding of proteins in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) [InterPro reference PUB00061987]. EMC1 contains two eight-bladed beta-propellers and stabilises the entire EMC complex, contacting six of the remaining eight subunits [InterPro reference PUB00096682]. The beta-propellers are in the lumen and may serve as a platform for recruitment of co-factors at the site of nascent protein insertion [InterPro reference PUB00096682].
