Source description

Members of this family belong to a large family of nucleotidyltransferases [InterPro reference PUB00005745]. This family includes kanamycin nucleotidyltransferase (KNTase) which is a plasmid-coded enzyme responsible for some types of bacterial resistance to aminoglycosides. KNTase in-activates antibiotics by catalysing the addition of a nucleotidyl group onto the drug.

GlnD HD substitutions reduce GlnB demodification outside the PF01909 domain

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length E. coli GlnD H514A/D515A and H514Q/D515N variants compared with full-length reference enzyme.
Context
Figure 4 native-PAGE endpoint after 60 min at 30 C, 40 uM fully modified GlnB, 0.1/0.5/2 uM GlnD, no UTP. Mg-UR uses 50 mM MgCl2, 0.5 mM ATP, 0.5 mM alpha-KG and 2.5 mM glutamine. Mn-UR uses 1 mM MnCl2 and 10 mM glutamine, omitting MgCl2, ATP and alpha-KG.
Family mapping
P27249 sequence version 2 carries PF01909 at 85-144, but H514/D515 lie in PF01966 at 468-601. These whole-protein controls distinguish activities and do not assign demodification to PF01909.
Limitations
Figure 4 plots remaining UMP on an inverted axis; the encoded outcome is extent of removal, not the plotted ordinate or an initial rate. Protocols differ in several components, so comparisons are within each protocol. Figure 5 retained transfer activity argues against completely inactive preparations but does not exclude local structural changes. No metal-binding geometry is established.

Glutamine lowers GlnB uridylylation under magnesium-only assay conditions

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Matched no-glutamine versus 10 mM glutamine conditions for 2 uM full-length, HD-AA or HD-QN E. coli GlnD.
Context
Figure 8A; Mg-only UTase protocol with GlnB, UTP, ATP and alpha-KG, 30 C/60 min endpoint. Every edge compares glutamine addition within the same enzyme construct and cofactor condition.
Family mapping
Full-length P27249 sequence version 2 includes PF01909 at 85-144 and HD region 468-601. HD-AA/QN alter residues 514/515 outside PF01909; glutamine response is a whole-enzyme property, not a demonstrated ligand-binding function of PF01909.
Limitations
HD variants lack detectable UR in the separate Figure 4 assays, helping distinguish inhibited transfer from enhanced removal. Exact binding site and allosteric relay were not measured. Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
