Source description

This domain is a CTP-dependent riboflavin kinase (RFK), found in archaea, that catalyses the phosphorylation of riboflavin to form flavin mononucleotide in riboflavin biosynthesis [ec:2.7.1.26]. Its structure resembles a RIFT barrel, structurally similar to but topologically distinct from bacterial and eukaryotic examples. The N-terminal is a winged helix-turn-helix DNA-binding domain, and the C-terminal half is most similar in sequence to a group of cradle-loop barrels. O28174 (UniProtKB protein) has this domain attached to PF00325 (Pfam entry).
