IPR000631: integrated entry for NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase

Source description

Hydration of NAD(P)H to NAD(P)HX, which inhibits several dehydrogenases, is corrected by an ATP-dependent dehydratase and an epimerase. The ATP-dependent dehydratase has been identified as the product of the vertebrate Carkd (carbohydrate kinase domain) gene [InterPro reference PUB00073185,InterPro reference PUB00100876]. Variants of this enzyme leads to deficient NADHX repair mitochondrial dysfunction in humans, associated with a lethal neurometabolic disorder in early childhood [InterPro reference PUB00100876]. In E. coli, it is found as the C-terminal domain of a bifunctional enzyme (YjeF) that also includes the epimerase and uses ADP instead of ATP [InterPro reference PUB00060794]. These enzymes are widespread in eukaryotes, prokaryotes, and archaea.

General assay conditions use 25 mM HEPES, 2 mM MgCl2 and 0.05-0.2 mg/ml BSA; nucleotide concentration is varied as plotted rather than assumed to equal the general 0.2-1 mM range. Methods permit mixed stocks or 70-85%-pure enriched fractions; Figure 2C does not specify which stock or which spectrophotometric wavelength was used. Spontaneous epimerization and mixed-species expression do not establish intracellular flux. The plotted ATP response and reciprocal ADP result are condition-specific, not a universal nucleotide rule for PF01256. No phosphorylated intermediate or residue-level catalytic trajectory is directly observed.

Intact YjeF and ADP convert an R-enriched NADHX mixture to NADH

MECHANISTIC

Scope
The bifunctional E. coli YjeF preparation in Marbaix 2011 Figure 2D; this is a whole-protein net conversion, not an isolated dehydratase acting directly on pure R-NADHX.
Context
HPLC time course at pH 8 and 30 C with 2.1 ug/ml YjeF, 5 mM KCl and an R-enriched NADHX preparation, with or without 0.1 mM ADP-Mg. Equal-volume 8 M urea/10 mM EDTA stops reactions. Figure 2D gives means and SD for three replicates; the caption also reports little change in no-enzyme controls with ADP-Mg. HPLC separates epimers and NADH by retention and UV criteria; the full incubation buffer composition is not independently specified for this panel.
Family mapping
The 515-aa P31806 reference maps to PF01256 at 258-496 and separate PF03853 at 40-199. Both domains remain in the recombinant pET100/D-Topo His fusion; no domain deletion or isolated-domain assay was performed here. Reference/primer matches do not reconstruct every historical clone or template strain.
Limitations
The starting fraction contains both epimers, not pure R substrate. Without ADP, YjeF accelerates epimer interconversion without comparable NADH formation; with ADP both epimers decline as NADH increases. These observations are not split into independently sufficient domains or atomically tracked molecules. ADP-to-AMP stoichiometry is reported as unshown data and is not added as a separate observed edge. Cyclic NADHX repair is not inferred.

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length His-YjeF in Marbaix 2011 supplemental Figure S4A, with both its N-terminal epimerase region and C-terminal PF01256-containing region retained.
Context
At 30 C and pH 8, 10.5 ug YjeF is added to a 1-ml reaction containing 0.5 mM ADP-Mg and about 12 uM NADPHX in a nominal 60:40 S/R mixture. A340 follows NADPH production against a no-enzyme control.
