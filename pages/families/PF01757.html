PF01757: Acyltransferase domain
IPR002656: integrated entry for Acyltransferase domain

Source description

This family includes a range of acyltransferase enzymes. This domain is found in a wide range of acyltransferase enzymes, including, mainly, bacterial proteins which catalyse the transfer of acyl groups, other than amino-acyl, from one compound to another, such as Glucans biosynthesis protein C (OPGC) or protein OatA from Listeria monocytogenes serovar 1/2a and Staphylococcus aureus, an integral membrane protein which is responsible for O-acetylation at the C6-hydroxyl group of N-acetylmuramyl residues, forming the corresponding N,6-O-diacetylmuramic acid of the peptidoglycan, a modification that determines lysozyme resistance [InterPro reference PUB00097284,InterPro reference PUB00097283]. This domain is also present in eukaryotic proteins, namely O-acyltransferase like protein (OACYL) from mouse and RHY1 (Regulator of hypoxia-inducible factor 1) [InterPro reference PUB00097285] and NRF6 (Nose resistant to fluoxetine protein 6) from Caenorhabditis elegans [InterPro reference PUB00097286].

Hypothesized Tyr206-to-Ser453 acetyl relay in full-length SaOatA

HYPOTHETICAL

Scope
Jones 2021 Figure5 and Discussion proposal for full-length SaOatA; not a directly observed sequence of intermediates or a family-wide mechanism.
Context
Proposed membrane-associated donor acquisition from acetyl-CoA, followed by intramolecular transfer from an acetylated Tyr206 to the cognate C-terminal Ser453. Full-length OatA supplies both sites; these are joint protein/substrate states, not independently sufficient residues.
Family mapping
PF01757 19-344 in the matched Q2FV54/A0A0H2XFE4/ABD21303.1 references includes H36,E154,Y206 but excludes S453 and the isolated445-601 partner. E357 is also outside current PF01757 and must not be confused with proposed E154. Topology-library ATCC6538 and purified-clone provenance remain distinct.
Limitations
Both edges are HYPOTHESIZED and directness UNRESOLVED. Deposited treated and untreated S453A samples both contain acetylated Tyr206; the search reference includes K495A/K496A. This is not treatment-specific flux proof. Synthetic acetyl-Tyr donor use by isolated C-domain does not observe an intramolecular relay. No atomic OatA structure or proton-transfer trajectory is established by Figure5; OafB modelling is not SaOatA experimental validation.

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
Not available means the source did not supply that counter. A zero is a reported count. Not scored means evidence counts are unavailable.
