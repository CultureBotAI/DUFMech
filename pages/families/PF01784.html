Source description

This family includes the DUF34/metal-binding protein/NIF3 proteins, which are widely distributed across superkingdoms. They were previously annotated as GTP cyclohydrolase 1 type 2 [InterPro reference PUB00070175] and, recently, through a comprehensive literature review and integrative bioinformatics analyses it was revealed that annotations for these members were misleading as they were based on a single set of in vitro results examining the NIF3 homolog of Helicobacter pylori [InterPro reference PUB00070175]. Actually, they have varied phenotypes with the unifying functional role as metal-binding proteins [InterPro reference PUB00100327]. This entry contains several NIF3 (NGG1p interacting factor 3) protein homologues. NIF3 interacts with the yeast transcriptional coactivator NGG1p which is part of the ADA complex, the exact function of this interaction is unknown [InterPro reference PUB00002954,InterPro reference PUB00019927].

Context
Liquid LB with 0.3 mM deoxythymidine, starting OD600 0.1, 37 C; growth followed for 40 h with five replicates. The adopted comparison is the lower double-mutant OD600 during approximately 5-15 h in Figure 8b.
Family mapping

Limitations
The result is conditional on folE deficiency and liquid-medium timing; ybgI deletion alone was similar to wild type. No ybgI rescue of this liquid-growth endpoint was established, and indirect or allele-context effects remain possible. Later prose describing better double-mutant growth on dT is unresolved and not generalized over the inspected curve. No metal-insertion or GTP-cyclohydrolase mechanism is assigned.
