Source description

This transmembrane domain is found in metal transporter proteins such as cyclin M 1/2 (CNNM). CNNMs are integral membrane proteins that are conserved from bacteria to humans. CNNM family members influence metal ion homeostasis through mechanisms that may not involve direct membrane transport of the ions. Structurally, CNNMs are complex proteins that contain an extracellular N-terminal domain preceding a transmembrane domain, a 'Bateman module', which consists of two cystathionine- beta-synthase (CBS) domains PF00571 (Pfam entry), and a C-terminal cNMP (cyclic nucleotide monophosphate) binding domain [InterPro reference PUB00088668,InterPro reference PUB00088669,InterPro reference PUB00088670,InterPro reference PUB00088671]. This entry describes the CNNM transmembrane domain which contains four hydrophobic regions and forms a dimer through hydrophobic contacts between TM2 and TM3, in which each chain is composed of three transmembrane helices (TM1-3), a pair of short helices exposed on the intracellular side, and a juxtamembrane (JM) helix that forms a belt-like structure [InterPro reference PUB00088669,InterPro reference PUB00098586]. The homodimer adopts an inward-facing conformation with a negatively charged cavity containing a conserved pi-helical turn in TM3 that coordinates a Mg2 ion [InterPro reference PUB00098586].

Reconstituted TtCorB supports magnesium accumulation in proteoliposomes

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length Tepidiphilus thermophilus CorB 1-445 with C-terminal His6, Chen 2021 Figure 6a and Supplementary Figure 8a/b; not Thermus thermophilus or isolated CNNM transmembrane domain.
Context
POPE/POPG 3:1 vesicles contain 50 uM mag-fura-2 in 20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.5, 100 mM KCl, 50 mM NaCl and initially 0.5 mM EDTA. UDM-purified protein is reconstituted by Sephadex G-50 detergent removal, one freeze/thaw and 0.8-um extrusion. Dilute 10 ul vesicles into 190 ul EDTA-free buffer at 25 C. Following a 1-min baseline, add 5 mM MgCl2 for the WT/empty and protein-loading comparisons. The separate MgCl2 series uses 0.75, 1.5, 3 and 5 mM. Monitor 330/369-nm excitation and 509-nm emission for 10 min; Triton/EDTA calibration yields intravesicular magnesium. Captions report three independent measurements, mean +/- SEM.
Family mapping
A0A0K6IWT9 sequence version 1 is 445 aa and maps to PF01595 at 19-189. UniProt and InterPro sequences exactly match. The paper identifies this accession and intact pET29a construct. CBS/CorC regions are also present; the isolated Pfam domain was not tested.
Limitations
Directness concerns magnesium accumulation in purified-protein vesicles, not an observed atomic transport cycle. Orientation and active fraction are not measured, so influx into these vesicles is not native cellular import/export direction. Independent measurements are not stated to be independent purifications. Empty controls receive matched 0.05% UDM buffer. The concentration/loading series are separate interventions, not a serial causal chain or a fitted Michaelis constant. Ion counter-substrates and exchange stoichiometry remain unresolved.

TtCorB cavity substitutions increase or decrease liposomal magnesium accumulation

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Separate single substitutions in intact His6-TtCorB, compared with WT under Chen 2021 Figure 6d/e conditions.
Context
POPE/POPG 3:1, protein/lipid 1:30 wt/wt, 5 mM added MgCl2, pH 7.5, 25 C, 100 mM KCl and 50 mM NaCl, mag-fura-2 excitation 330/369 nm and emission 509 nm. The 10-min time courses use empty-liposome and WT comparisons, three independent measurements, mean +/- SEM. Supplementary Figure 8c/d supplies size-exclusion and reconstituted-protein SDS-PAGE controls.
Family mapping
Reference A0A0K6IWT9 S26, S30, N76, S117, E118, P121 and K122 were sequence-verified and all lie inside PF01595 19-189. Each mutation is in the full 445-aa protein, not an isolated-domain construct; domain position does not isolate its causal microstep.
Limitations
All plotted loss-of-response mutants retain accumulation above empty controls. E33A and N80A are described as near WT, not zero-effect proof. Several variants, especially S26A/S30A/N76A, have earlier or broadened SEC peaks. Strong incorporation bands do not establish equal folding, oligomerization, orientation or active fraction. Directness means the net purified-vesicle effect; an elementary metal-coordination or pi-helix-motion mechanism remains unresolved.

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.

Repeated annotations may not be independent.
