Added sodium lowers the ethidium fluorescence endpoint in NorM-VC-expressing cells

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
His10-tagged V. cholerae NorM-VC expressed in L. lactis NZ9000 delta lmrA delta lmrCD, Raturi 2021 Figure 2A; not a native Vibrio phenotype or all MatE proteins.
Context
Cells are de-energized with 0.5 mM 2,4-dinitrophenol for 40 min at 30 C, washed three times, diluted to OD660 0.5 and loaded with 2 uM ethidium in 50 mM KPi, 5 mM MgSO4, pH 7.0 at 30 C. Glucose initiates efflux; Methods specify 12.5 mM glucose but Figure 2 specifies 20 mM. The comparison is 1 mM added Na+ versus no added Na+, with the 400-s fluorescence endpoint and three independently prepared cell batches, mean +/- SEM.
Family mapping
Q9KRU4 / norM / VC_1540, V. cholerae O1 N16961, has current PF01554 repeats at 17-176 and 243-404 in the 457-aa sequence version 2. The paper's AAF94694.1 reference is 461 aa; its residues 6-461 exactly match current 2-457, with different N-terminal initiation. This reference reconciliation does not independently resequence the N-terminal His10 assay plasmid. Intact protein is tested, not isolated repeats.
Limitations
Intracellular ethidium-associated fluorescence is an efflux proxy, not an absolute mass-flux measurement. Sodium addition also changes cellular energetics; the edge does not establish an isolated sodium-binding step or ion stoichiometry. Nonexpressing controls lack the comparable sodium response. The glucose discrepancy remains unresolved. No universal sodium requirement is inferred from this pH-7 comparison.

Sodium enhances low-concentration ethidium binding in WT but not three NorM-VC variants

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Purified N-terminal His10-tagged NorM-VC WT and single substitutions D36N, E255Q and D371N, Raturi 2021 Figure 4A; paper residue numbering retained.
Context
Protein is purified from L. lactis membranes using DDM and Ni-NTA. Titrations contain 2 uM ethidium, 50 mM KPi pH 7.0, 0.2 mM KCl and 0.05% DDM, with 0.036-1 uM protein and either no added sodium or 0.5 mM Na2SO4 (1 mM Na+). Fluorescence anisotropy uses 500/580 nm excitation/emission after about 2 min equilibration and matched elution-buffer baseline. Three independent protein preparations are reported, mean +/- SEM.
Family mapping
Historical AAF94694.1 D36/E255/D371 correspond to current Q9KRU4 D32/E251/D367, verified by sequence comparison. D32 lies in PF01554 17-176 and E251/D367 in 243-404. Current protein begins MHRY whereas the historical reference begins MENSVHRY; current sequence is M plus historical residues 6-461, not simply historical residues 5-461. Mutant labels refer to the paper's numbering, not current UniProt coordinates.
Limitations
Directness describes the purified-protein binding response, not an observed atomic sodium-coordination or transport step. WT sodium enhancement is strongest at low protein concentrations; the curves converge at high concentration. Single mutants retain binding. Apparent two-component fits do not independently count sites. Supplementary expression/purity controls were not inspected, so equivalent folding is not established. Li+, Cs+ and Rb+ do not mimic the WT sodium response in this assay.

Raising pH increases ethidium-binding anisotropy of purified WT NorM-VC

MECHANISTIC

Scope
WT His10-NorM-VC in the separate constant-protein pH titration, Raturi 2021 Figure 4B.
Context
No added sodium; 0.2 uM purified WT protein and 2 uM ethidium in the DDM/KPi binding assay. Buffer pH is varied from 6 to 9 in 0.2 steps with concentrated KOH. Anisotropy uses 500/580 nm and about 2 min equilibration; three independent protein batches, mean +/- SEM. This is not the glucose-energized cell assay.
Family mapping
Intact historical 461-aa NorM-VC maps to Q9KRU4 sequence version 2 with the verified initiation difference and four-position internal numbering offset. Both current PF01554 intervals, 17-176 and 243-404, belong to the tested protein context; neither repeat was isolated.
Limitations
The double-sigmoid response is observed, but assigning fitted midpoints 6.9 and 8.0 to specific carboxylates is an inference. Buffer alkalinization by KOH is the experimental intervention, not an independently controlled change of only one residue's protonation. This binding response is not an observed transport cycle or universal pH rule for the family.
  • raise-buffer-ph: Raise the no-added-sodium binding buffer from pH 6 to 9; PERTURBATION; Ungrounded
  • wt-ph-series-anisotropy: Ethidium-binding anisotropy at 0.2 uM WT NorM-VC; ACTIVITY; UniProtKB:Q9KRU4

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
