PF01732: Mycoplasma immunoglobulin protease
IPR022382: integrated entry for Mycoplasma immunoglobulin protease

Source description

This is the serine protease domain of MIP (Mycoplasma immunoglobulin protease), a protein that is related to pathogenicity of animal and human pathogenic Mycoplasmas [InterPro reference PUB00098027,InterPro reference PUB00098028,InterPro reference PUB00098029,InterPro reference PUB00151017]. This protein functions together with the Ig binding protein M (MIB) to evade host Ig-mediated defense against the MIB-MIP system, which is a novel mechanism that protects mycoplasma against host immune response [InterPro reference PUB00098028,InterPro reference PUB00151017]. MIB tightly interacts to the antigen-binding fragment (Fab) and recruits the MIP protease that is activated and cleaves the IgG heavy chain, disrupting the antigen binding site. Therefore, the MIB-MIP system appears to be crucial to mycoplasma immune evasion [InterPro reference PUB00151017].

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Nottelet 2021 Figure 5B/C/E/F, GM12-derived Mmc WT versus deletion of all four MIB-MIP pairs; the single retained MIB83-MIP82 pair is a separate positive comparator, not a rescue.
Context
Cleavage assays use about 1e9 washed late-exponential cells in 15 ul SP5 without FBS, 100 ng/ul purified serum IgG or 2% albumin-depleted D12 goat serum, 30 min at 37 C, supernatant immunoblot. Agglutination is a different overnight static 37 C assay; Methods give 4% serum and caption 2%. D0 serum does not produce the same cellular IgM cleavage/agglutination response.
Family mapping
The deleted cluster includes MIP82/MMCAP2_0582, ACU78687.1 with PF01732 290-776, but also three other MIPs and four MIBs. Whole-locus effects cannot be attributed exclusively to this protein or its domain. Main Figure 5B establishes the design; exact strain lineage and nucleotide breakpoints in inaccessible supplements remain unverified here.

INDIRECT genotype effects. Single-pair retention is not reintroduction complementation. Anti-HRP IgG is not detectably cleaved by cells despite purified-system activity. OD310 measures settled aggregates, not growth. Results panel cross-references are offset; actual Figure 5C/E/F is used. Low-resolution visual inspection supports qualitative direction, not numeric effect sizes or a domain-specific mechanism.
