Causal graphs

Slr2013 Y294Stop suppresses D2-T192H photosynthetic defects

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 Rg2 suppressor retaining psbDI-T192H and deletion of psbDII, compared with the T192H parent. The additional slr2013 Y294Stop allele was identified by suppressor mapping; this is not an isolated DUF58 assay or a clean full-gene deletion.
Context
PS I-retaining cells grown in BG-11 at 30 C and 40 umol photons per square meter per second; liquid culture at 120 rpm. Photoautotrophy omits glucose. Oxygen evolution uses intact cells at 5 ug chlorophyll/mL, a Clark electrode, 0.1 mM 2,5-dimethyl-p-benzoquinone and 0.5 mM potassium ferricyanide, illuminated above 580 nm at 1 mmol photons per square meter per second. The oxygen-assay temperature is not explicitly specified and parental maintenance atrazine is not assigned to this assay. Table 2 reports means and SD from three independent experiments.
Family mapping
Slr2013 is UniProtKB:P73233 (NCBIProtein:BAA17260.1), a 435-residue Kazusa reference, taxon 1111708, sequence version 1. UniProt and InterPro sequences agree; PF01882 maps at 195-385 inclusive and reference residue 294 is Y. The stop predicts retention of residues 1-293, including DUF58 residues 195-293, and loss of residues 294-435, including the remaining domain and a C-terminal extension. This is neither a whole-domain deletion nor proof of domain sufficiency. The laboratory PCC 6803 substrain is not equated with the complete Kazusa reference genome.
Limitations
Genomic-fragment mapping and transfer of a PCR-amplified mutant region support the suppressor attribution, but an independently reconstructed single-base allele and reciprocal full-length Slr2013 rescue are not established here. The mapped region contains additional genes and possible cotranscription. The predicted 293-residue product was not directly measured here. Restoring WT D2 while retaining Y294Stop yields near-WT growth and oxygen evolution; other tested D2 mutants were not rescued. Chaperone and protease interpretations remain alternatives, not demonstrated direct chemistry.

Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized.
