Source description

This entry, previously known as UPF0016, represents the budding yeast Divalent cation/proton antiporter GDT1 and its homologue, the human TMEM165 that are functionally related and form a new group of Golgi-localised cation/Ca(2+) exchangers. Budding yeast GDT1 is a Golgi-localised calcium transporter required for stress-induced calcium signalling and protein glycosylation [InterPro reference PUB00079030]. TMEM165, also a Golgi protein, transports Ca2+ and Mn2+, which are both required for proper protein glycosylation in cells [InterPro reference PUB00079031,InterPro reference PUB00152812]. Most members of the family contain two copies of a region that contains an EXGD motif. Each of these regions contains three predicted transmembrane regions.

GDT1 expression increases the alkaline-pulse pH response

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Yeast GDT1 expressed with sfpHluorin in Lactococcus lactis DML1, compared with the sensor-only control in Deschamps 2023 Figure 2A. This is a whole-cell response, not an isolated transporter assay.
Context
Two-hour nisin induction; 2 ml cells at OD600 10 in 100 mM KCl, 1 mM EGTA, 1 mM MgCl2 and 20 mM MOPS pH 7.4, 28 C, 300-rpm stirring. Pre-treat control with 10 mM HCl for 4 min to match initial intracellular pH, then add 20 mM NaOH to control or 10 mM to GDT1 cells. Blank-corrected 390/470-nm sfpHluorin ratio, emission 507 nm; N=5-7 biological replicates.
Family mapping
P38301/GDT1_YEAST is the 280-aa S288c reference, sequence version 1, with PF01169 repeats at 42-127 and 193-277. UniProt and InterPro sequences match. The 2023 operon has a reported silent NcoI-site change; full clone/junction sequences are unresolved. DML1 is the expression host, not the Gdt1 source species.

Directness is UNRESOLVED for expression-to-pH attribution because endogenous transport or altered membrane integrity can contribute. Counterion experiments support the exchange model but do not measure single-protein stoichiometry or orientation. Figure 1's calibration caption says digitonin while Methods and supplement S1 specify nigericin. The distinct tagged delta23 construct from 2016 is not silently assigned to this experiment.

Higher external pH increases calcium and manganese uptake readouts

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Separate Fura-2 calcium and manganese assays in GDT1-expressing DML1 cells, comparing pH 8.0 with 6.8 in Figure 3. DIRECT describes the gradient intervention on the transport readout, not exclusive attribution to purified Gdt1.
Context
Load 10 uM Fura-2/AM with 1.7 mM probenecid for 2 h at 28 C. Wash in 100 mM KCl, 1 mM MgCl2, 1 mM EGTA and 50 mM Tris at the chosen pH; resuspend in corresponding EGTA-free, calcium-sponge-treated buffer. Add 25 uM CaCl2 or, separately, 10 uM MnCl2. Measure Ca-associated 340/380 ratio or Mn-associated 360-nm quenching, with 510-nm emission; N=3 biological replicates.
Family mapping
Yeast P38301-associated GDT1 expressed from pNZ8048 in DML1; both PF01169 repeats, 42-127 and 193-277, belong to the tested protein. Exact 2023 clone/junction sequence is not independently resolved.

Exact recording temperature and membrane orientation remain unverified; 28 C is explicitly the loading temperature.

Scope

Context
100 OD600 units of induced cells washed twice in 50 mM Tris pH 7 and once in 1 mM Tris pH 7, resuspended in 20 ml weak buffer. After 2 min baseline add 20 mM CaCl2; pH-meter recording with stirring. Compare acidification slopes during recording minutes 5-10; N=3-7 biological replicates for slope comparisons.
Family mapping

GDT1 deletion changes compartment pH after glucose re-addition

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
BY4742-derived gdt1::KanMX4 versus WT in Figure 6, with separate cytosolic and Golgi pH-probe cultures. This is whole-gene physiology, not an isolated repeat or directly measured reverse transport.
Context
Low-fluorescence defined medium with 50 mM MES pH 5.0. Wash three times, deprive glucose for 60 min at 28 C, record 2 min baseline and add glucose to 2%. Compare cytosolic pH at +120 s (N=3) and Golgi pH at +720 s (N=5). Yeast pHluorin readout uses the cited 400/480-nm excitation, 507-nm emission protocol, distinct from bacterial sfpHluorin.
Family mapping
Native S. cerevisiae GDT1/YBR187W, represented by P38301 and its two PF01169 repeats, is deleted as a whole gene. BY4742 is distinct from the S288c reference annotation; full strain resequencing is not claimed.
Limitations
Cytosolic final pH at +720 s is not different; steady-state Figure 5 shows no significant GDT1-deletion pH effect. The +120 s Golgi comparison is borderline and not an adopted edge. Calcium signaling and other pumps can mediate the effects; no Figure 6 complementation or simultaneous ion-gradient measurements establish the proposed reverse-transport model.
