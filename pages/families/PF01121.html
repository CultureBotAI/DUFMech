Source description

This family catalyses the phosphorylation of the 3'-hydroxyl group of dephosphocoenzyme A to form Coenzyme A [ec:2.7.1.24]. This enzyme uses ATP in its reaction.

Recombinant E. coli CoaE supports preparative CoA formation

MECHANISTIC

Scope
W3110 coaE/yacE expressed in BL21(DE3) using pET28b(+), with an engineered Ala after the start codon and the measured processed N terminus ARYIVALTGGIG. Not the mycobacterial tagged fusion protein.
Context
2001 preparative reaction contains 3 umol dephospho-CoA, 0.1 umol ATP, 1 umol MgCl2, 1 umol KCl, 3.5 umol phosphoenolpyruvate, 5 U pyruvate kinase and 2 U recombinant CoaE in 5 ml water adjusted to pH 8, 30 min at room temperature. HPLC-purified product is compared with authentic CoA by proton NMR. ATP regeneration is part of the joint reaction.
Family mapping
K-12 reference P0A6I9, coaE/yacE/b0103/JW0100, 206 aa, sequence version 1, exactly matches InterPro and contains PF01121 at 3-177. Ala plus native residues 2-12 explains the measured N-terminal sequence after initiator removal. The modern record cites this W3110 study; complete plasmid sequence was not independently reconstructed.
Limitations
Product identity is reported from NMR, but raw spectra and a preparative no-enzyme control are not supplied in the inspected material. Separate ADP and thioester-coupled assays corroborate activity under different conditions. This graph does not establish an elementary catalytic base, isolated-profile sufficiency or the products of minor alternative-substrate reactions.

Mycobacterial CoaE substitutions alter reported DCoA-series turnover

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length hexahistidine-tagged mycobacterial CoaE and individual R140A/R140K/D32A/L114A substitutions in the 2011 study. The R140A loss claim is explicitly CONTESTED by inconsistent source presentations.
Context
Single-coupled alpha-ketoglutarate-dehydrogenase assay, 25 C, five-minute A340 monitoring, no-DCoA blanks subtracted. DCoA varies with 10 mM ATP, 10 mM MgCl2, 20 mM KCl, 50 mM Tris pH 8, 2 U coupling enzyme, 2 mM alpha-ketoglutarate, 0.3 mM NADP and the source's 0.3 mM pyrophosphate cofactor. Exact CoaE dose is unreported. Table 2 gives apparent kcat; Figure 5 states two independent experiments in duplicate. Separate ITC kinetics use 20 C and different buffer, not this same reaction.
Family mapping
The cited P63826 demerged into P9WPA3/Rv1631 and P9WPA2/MT1667; both 407-aa sequences exactly match archived UPI0000127CE6. P9WPA3 PF01121 spans 4-178 and contains R140/D32/L114; the intact protein also has PF04229. The 2009 source names Rv1631 but its printed forward primer mismatches the reference N terminus, so complete clone and donor-strain reconstruction remain unresolved. Two identical successors do not imply two experiments.
Limitations
Effects concern reported fitted turnover in this substrate series, not binding Kd or observed catalytic chemistry. Text/Table 2 report R140A no reaction while Figure 5A plots its Km as 240 uM. Other Km, ratio and fold-change inconsistencies prevent precise affinity/efficiency claims. Structural controls do not establish identical folding for every mutant. The printed cofactor name thymine pyrophosphate is not silently corrected, and raw kinetic fits remain unreviewed.

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Full-length tagged mycobacterial CoaE L114A versus WT in the 2011 Table 2 ATP-varied kinetic comparison, not the DCoA-varied series.
Context
Coupled A340 assay at 25 C, 50 mM Tris pH 8, fixed 0.5 mM DCoA and varying ATP, 10 mM MgCl2, 20 mM KCl, 2 U alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase, 2 mM alpha-ketoglutarate, 0.3 mM NADP and 0.3 mM the source's pyrophosphate cofactor. Limitations

No elementary contact, folding equivalence or family-wide behavior is proved. The assay cofactor's printed name and raw fit details require clarification.

EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
