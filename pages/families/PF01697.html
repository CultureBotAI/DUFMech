Source description

Members of this family act as galactosyltransferases, belonging to glycosyltransferase family 92 [InterPro reference PUB00075505,InterPro reference PUB00075506,InterPro reference PUB00075507]. The aligned region contains several conserved cysteine residues and several charged residues that may be catalytic residues. This is supported by the inclusion of this family in the GT-A glycosyl transferase superfamily.
