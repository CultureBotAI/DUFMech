Source description

TTR-52 was called family 2 in [InterPro reference PUB00001984], and has weak similarity to transthyretin (formerly called pre-albumin) which transports thyroid hormones. The specific function of this protein is as a bridging molecule in apoptosis cross-linking dying cells to phagocytes. TTR-52 bridges by cross-linking surface-exposed phosphatidylserine (PtdSer) on apoptotic cells to the CED-1 receptor, a transmembrane receptor, on phagocytes. TTR-52 has an open beta-barrel-like structure [InterPro reference PUB00109168].

The sm211 V43M allele increases embryonic corpse persistence

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Whole-animal C. elegans ttr-52(sm211) phenotype compared with N2 Bristol, not an isolated-domain biochemical reaction or a gene-null allele.
Context
2010 Figure 1C; sm211 backcrossed four times to N2. Thirty-three corpses from three WT embryos and 32 from three mutant embryos were followed. Four-dimensional microscopy of 300-min embryos uses 118 mM NaCl/48 mM KCl egg buffer, 26-um stacks with 1.3-um spacing, every 5 min for 150 min. Displayed mean durations are 28 +/- 2 versus 55 +/- 6 min, SEM.
Family mapping
G5ED35 ttr-52/F11F1.7 has 135 residues, sequence version 1, exact UniProt/InterPro agreement and PF01060 at 28-112 inclusive. The study's deposited HM130527.1 translation ADO60153.1 matches G5ED35 exactly. V43 lies inside the match; sm211 is V43M, not the distinct tm2078 deletion.
Limitations
Corpses are nested within six embryos, not 65 independent embryo replicates. Exact imaging temperature and handling of the >110-min histogram bin remain unresolved. Gene mapping and corpse-count rescue support the target but do not separately rescue every lifetime endpoint. Altered localization can mediate V43M effects; no unique ligand-contact chemistry or increased apoptosis is inferred.

The sm211 allele decreases CED-1 ring labeling around corpses

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
CED-1::GFP localization in 1.5-fold C. elegans embryos carrying smIs34, compared with smIs34;ttr-52(sm211), in the 2010 Figure 4B experiment.
Context
Pced-1CED-1::GFP marks corpses identified by Nomarski morphology. Serial optical sections span 20 um at 1-um spacing. Fifteen embryos per stage and genotype, SEM; the adopted 1.5-fold comparison is approximately 64% versus 34% labeled corpses with reported P < 0.0001. Comma-stage data are a separate comparison.
Family mapping
sm211 changes V43 to M in G5ED35, within PF01060 28-112. Reference sequence version 1 matches the study's HM130527.1 protein translation exactly. Intact secreted TTR-52 is the implicated protein, not a purified 28-112 fragment.
Limitations
Reporter rings are a cellular localization endpoint, not a molecular binding constant or a directly tracked recruitment-to-engulfment chain. V43M can change secretion/localization and is not interchangeable with a deletion. Separate pull-down and co-IP experiments show association, not automatic receptor activation. No endpoint-specific rescue or raw image reanalysis is claimed here.

Scope

Context
With versus without transgene expression, scored in 1.5-fold embryos after 1 h heat shock at 33 C and recovery at 20 C, examined 3.5 h after treatment. D51A, D80A and N85A reduce TTR-52 binding to PtdSer on lipid strips

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Separately affinity-purified WT and substituted full-length TTR-52-mCherry-Flag secreted from transfected 293T cells, 2012 Figure 4. The mammalian production host does not make this a mammalian TTR assay.
Context
P-6001 lipid strips; 25 mM Tris-HCl pH 7, 150 mM NaCl, 0.1% Tween 20, 0.1 mM ZnSO4 and 0.2 mM CaCl2. Block 1 h at 22 C with 1% dried milk; protein incubation overnight at 4 C without milk, three washes, anti-Flag detection. Anti-TTR-52 blot checks protein presence. Two independent experiments; exact protein dose is not specified.
Family mapping
D51, D80 and N85 match G5ED35 sequence version 1 and lie within PF01060 at 28-112. The study's earlier cDNA deposit matches this reference exactly. Full-length secreted fusion preparations are not isolated domain fragments; cleavage and fusion junctions remain construct-specific uncertainties.
Limitations
Directness concerns the measured immobilized-lipid binding endpoint, not atomic ion coordination, bilayer insertion or receptor activation. Blot presence is not exact equimolar loading or a folding control. Other lipid signals remain visible in mutant strips. The crystallized six-alanine M5 protein and modeled WT coordinates do not directly show a WT binding trajectory. The 2010 strip protocol has tenfold higher cation concentrations and is not merged here.
