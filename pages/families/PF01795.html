Source description

Members of this family are probably SAM dependent methyltransferases based on P18595 (UniProtKB protein) [InterPro reference PUB00017338]. This family appears to be related to PF01596 (Pfam entry).

RsmH reconstitutes N4 methylation in a 30S assay

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Kimura and Suzuki 2010 Figure 3: purified His-tagged E. coli RsmH, alone or jointly with the distinct RsmI enzyme, and substrates from BW25113-derived rsmH/rsmI double deletion cells.
Context
100-ul reactions at 37 C for 1 h; 100 mM NH4Cl, 20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.6, 10 mM magnesium acetate, 6 mM beta-mercaptoethanol, 1 mM SAM when supplied. Methods specify 10 pmol recombinant proteins RsmH and/or RsmI; 5 pmol 30S or tightly coupled 70S versus 2.5 pmol naked 16S. AdoHcy treatment and dialysis precede assays; RNA extraction, RNase T1 digestion and LC/MS detect products.
Family mapping
313-aa K-12 RsmH P60390, b0082/JW0080, equals versioned MG1655 NP_414624.1 and parental BW25113 WIA74303.1; current PF01795 5-310. Old seed accession P18595 is demerged. ASKA pCA24N N-terminal His-tagged clone is not independently resequenced, and exact terminal additions remain unverified. RsmI is a separate enzyme, not a PF01795 alias.
Limitations
DIRECT means net purified-preparation effects, not atomic methyl transfer or isolated PF01795 sufficiency. RsmH-alone product is low. The naked-RNA comparison changes both substrate state and dose; the displayed negative comparisons use both enzymes. No universal inactivity, methylation order, required enzyme-enzyme interaction, absolute detection limit or late assembly intermediate is established.

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Kimura and Suzuki 2010 Table 1: BW25113 WT versus rsmH single and rsmH/rsmI double deletions at 37 C.
Context
LB 1-ml cultures in 96-well format, OD600 measurements every 15 min. Three independent experiments; mean +/- SD doubling times in minutes: WT 20.4 +/- 0.3, rsmH 23.5 +/- 0.8, double deletion 26.3 +/- 2.5. Other temperatures are retained in the ledger but not represented by these edges.
Family mapping

Limitations
INDIRECT whole-genotype growth effects without adopted rescue. Longer doubling time means slower growth, not increased growth rate. Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
Not available means the source did not supply that counter. A zero is a reported count. Not scored means evidence counts are unavailable.
