Causal graphs

TusA increases purified IscS sulfide-production activity

MECHANISTIC

Scope
Recombinant E. coli TusA and IscS in the 2024 purified-protein assay; not all PF01206 members, an isolated domain or intracellular sulfur flux.
Context
Figure 1 uses gel-filtered precomplexes formed at a 1:3 IscS-to-partner ratio for 20 min at 30 C in 50 mM Tris-HCl, 200 mM NaCl, pH 8. Activity is measured over 10 min at 30 C with 1 mM DTT and 1 mM L-cysteine by methylene-blue sulfide quantification against Na2S standards. IscS alone and TusA alone are controls. The paper reports BL21(DE3) expression and untagged TusA/IscS for interaction studies; exact Figure 1 protein doses and complete clone sequences remain unverified.
Family mapping
E. coli K12 reference UniProtKB:P0A890 (tusA/sirA/yhhP, b3470/JW3435), sequence version 1, 81 aa, maps to PF01206 at 10-79 inclusive. InterPro and UniProt sequences agree. The cited 2013 protocol lists pJD34, tusA in pET11b; the 2024 cloning template strain is not independently verified. BL21 is the expression host, not an inferred template identity.
Limitations
The readout is sulfide release in a reducing biochemical assay. It does not resolve allosteric activation versus sulfur-acceptor turnover, a specific residue's chemistry, intracellular sulfur partitioning or tRNA/Moco synthesis. TusA alone has no detected assay activity. Mixed IscS2/IscU/TusA complexes occur in the study, so mutually exclusive occupancy of an entire IscS dimer is not assumed.

The purified IscS-TusA preparation produces sulfide at about three times the activity of IscS alone under the stated conditions. This is a directly tested protein-dependent biochemical effect, without assigning an elementary catalytic or cellular regulatory mechanism.

Equimolar TusA reduces the IscU Fe-S assembly-associated optical signal

PHENOTYPIC

Scope
Concentration-dependent effect of E. coli TusA in the reconstituted IscS/IscU system, retaining the observed optical outcome separately from the proposed sulfur-diversion mechanism.
Context
Figure 5A/D compares 0, 1, 5, 10 and 50 uM TusA with 50 uM IscU, 25 uM Fe2+, 1 uM IscS and 250 uM L-cysteine in anaerobic 50 mM Tris-HCl, 100 mM NaCl, pH 8. Reductants are either 2 mM DTT or the conjunction of 1 uM Fdx, 1 uM Fpr and 100 uM NADPH. Following 30 min preincubation, IscS and cysteine initiate the reaction. A456 is recorded every 5 min for 40 min in independent triplicates; assay temperature is not specified in the inspected caption.
Family mapping
TusA is mapped to the 81-aa E. coli K12 reference P0A890, PF01206 residues 10-79, not to all TusA-like paralogs. Full experimental clone sequence and template strain remain unverified. IscU was His6-tagged then thrombin-cleaved according to the 2024 Methods.
Limitations
A456 is a biochemical assembly readout, not direct atom tracing or identification of a inhibited elementary reaction. Lower TusA doses do not show the same clear reduction. Protein competition and sulfur diversion are explanatory models, not additional observed edges. The B/E spectral caption's yellow-line reference is inconsistent with the no-TusA red-line key; only the A/D concentration time courses are adopted.

  • assembly-associated-a456: Fe-S assembly-associated absorbance at 456 nm over time; PHENOTYPE; Ungrounded

Status and evidence

Seed status
A name-based classification of frozen InterPro/Pfam metadata. UNKNOWN CANDIDATE means the metadata explicitly says the function is unknown. KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF means a DUF name matched without that wording; this heuristic is not proof of a known function and does not establish experimental characterization. EX DUF means Pfam renamed the family away from a DUF/UPF name, as recorded in Pfam's previous identifiers; a rename usually follows published work on some members but is naming history, not proof of function for every member. Some EX DUF families' current names or descriptions still say the function is unknown; their classification basis lists this, and scoring does not treat them as characterized. FALSE POSITIVE TEXT HIT means the naming rules did not match.
Characterization
A separate evidence-scoring result. UNSCORED means no score has been calculated; it is not evidence that the family lacks a known function. Missing scores are not negative evidence. PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED has partial support; KNOWN HISTORICAL DUF can inherit the seed label or reflect a known-function signal. Neither label implies experimental validation.
Evidence counts
Source-specific signals, not unique proteins, publications, or confidence scores. Known counts Rhea reactions and experimentally supported GO molecular-function annotations. Partial counts functional/domain assignments. Context counts structural, environmental, orthology, interaction and neighborhood signals; context alone does not assign function. Repeated annotations may not be independent.
Curation status
Reported separately from seed classification and characterization. SNAPSHOT ONLY means no curated metadata was supplied for this build. SEEDED denotes a source-derived record, not an expert-reviewed functional claim.
Missing counts
